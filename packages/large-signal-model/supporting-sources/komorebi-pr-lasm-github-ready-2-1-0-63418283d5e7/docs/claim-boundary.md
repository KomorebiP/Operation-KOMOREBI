# Claim boundary

## Established in the finite implementation

- Exact reconstruction in every declared coordinate atlas.
- Canonical synthesis of the weighted redundant multi-atlas frame.
- Exact synthesis for complete mixed-depth packet-tree prunings.
- Bayesian comparison of a finite typed operator library.
- Explicit within-program and between-program predictive uncertainty.
- Deterministic state-action graph bookkeeping and replay safety checks.
- Controlled mechanism-identification and deterministic synthetic planning tests.
- Static audit machinery for user-local ARC environment trees.

## Not established

- Completeness of the operator grammar for arbitrary physical or computational worlds.
- Universal program induction or planner optimality.
- Infinite-dimensional convergence of the learned model.
- Guaranteed solution of arbitrary ARC environments.
- An official ARC-AGI-3 benchmark score.
- General intelligence or AGI.
- Historical priority over all related signal, wavelet, Bayesian, world-model, or planning systems.

Numerical results are certificates for the bundled finite systems. They
must not be generalized beyond their stated inputs, seeds, dimensions,
priors, and program families without additional analysis.
