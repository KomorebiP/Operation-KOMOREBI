# Proof and continuation protocol

CONTINUE_HERE.md and AUDIT.json define the live state. Historical controls remain
immutable history. A checked theorem requires actual successful compilation under
the pinned runtime, warnings as errors, no placeholders/custom axioms/native proof
shortcuts, and an all-declaration axiom audit. Check source, attempted-source,
log, compiled-output and direct-dependency hashes. Run small bounded modules.
A timeout or incomplete receipt is never a pass. Finite tests are not universal proofs.

Preserve all inherited checked files and semantic boundaries. Distinguish rounded
gain execution, exact preprocessing/budget arithmetic, outward verification and
hardware arithmetic. Do not confuse a computed sufficient bit policy with a fixed
precision convergence guarantee. Preserve the explicit fixed-precision counterexample.

The v12 output arrays provably enclose the parameters of the mathematical Joseph
Gaussian at the returned gain. Their radii obey the output tolerance. This does
not certify a Gaussian defined by their centers or an executable sampler.

The v13 selective branch updates gains with exact rational arithmetic and fixed
output packets. Its geometry/scan guards, contraction and termination are checked.
Only unselected output packets have a locality guarantee; input packets can grow.
Do not transfer the v11 rounded full-update proof to selective or approximate
selection dynamics without a new argument. Dense scanning is not a speedup proof.
