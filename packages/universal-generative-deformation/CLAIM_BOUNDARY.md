# Claim boundary

## Proved in the paper

- exact reachability of the raw path forest
- free rooted path property
- well-defined quotient under extension-compatible relations
- exact raw and quotient counts for the integer walk
- propagated continuous-cover error bound
- convergence under summable step bounds
- free operadic evaluation of multi-input expressions
- Catalan recurrence for repeated binary substitution shapes
- exact route-cylinder projector refinement
- regular-tree partition-function threshold
- inductive soundness of a family certificate
- finite-depth completeness of unrestricted breadth-first exploration
- fairness consequence for countable direction enumerations
- recursive stability bound for perturbed deformation systems

## Verified computationally

- 50 of 50 independent tests
- 18 of 18 experiment checks
- 20 Python-generated PDF/PNG figure pairs
- 11 finite explored realizations
- exact route and quotient accounting for integer, analytic, polynomial, sequence, Gaussian, qubit, matrix, shape, graph, and congruence-lifting systems
- 24 explicitly audited invalid analytic operations

## Not claimed

- historical novelty for every component or for the combined framework
- one fixed finite grammar that reaches all mathematical objects
- literal finite enumeration of an uncountable direction space
- automatic identification of exact equivalence from floating-point similarity
- proof that a generated symbolic family is absent from existing literature
- infinite completeness from a finite node count
- universal sparsity or efficient finite representations for every object
