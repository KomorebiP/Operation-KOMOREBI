# Source and claim map

## Exact mathematical results

### Local singular models

For a fixed support, unfolding, and rank, truncated SVD minimizes Frobenius error over every model of that rank.

### Exact tree optimization

For the declared finite dyadic tree, local unfolding family, rank range, additive local squared-error objective, and integer parameter cost, the Pareto recursion returns the best complete pruning at every budget.

### Global refitting

Once a tree has proposed a span, orthogonal projection gives the least-squares reconstruction in that span and cannot increase error.

### Block gain

After orthogonalizing a proposed block against the active span, its residual-energy reduction is exactly the squared norm of the newly projected coefficients.

### Portfolio nonregression

The final finite selector retains all three unchanged seeds. Selecting the best completed candidate cannot be worse than any seed in the portfolio.

### Stability

A fixed local singular subspace is stable under perturbation when the retained and discarded singular values have a positive gap larger than the perturbation scale.
