No new license is selected or granted by this release-preparation step. The owner has not yet approved the public distribution terms for the project-specific source and prose in this candidate.

Before public distribution, the owner must approve the project terms and confirm the required third-party notices for the selected files. This document records an outstanding decision; it is not a substitute license or legal opinion.
