# Komorebi: representations, obstructions, and verified computation

*Announcement text for the approved release. This local candidate has not been published.*

Komorebi is an independent human–machine research program concerned with how mathematical objects can be transformed, corrected, and represented without losing the behavior being studied.

Research Preview 1 presents a concrete development in prime-power Cartier systems. From admissible integer polynomial masks, it constructs a unique normalized formal series and an exact finite machine for its coefficients. The mathematical development then removes unnecessary layers, coefficients, divisibility freedoms, linear relations, and states invisible to future outputs. The result is canonical up to a unique structure-preserving linear isomorphism and has minimum cardinality among finite linear realizations of the same all-word behavior over the fixed ring and alphabet.

The package brings the theorem statement, Lean source, verification records, and runnable reference examples together. It states the hypotheses and limitations alongside the results. The reference software is not presented as an efficient formally verified compiler, and the linear minimum is not confused with a minimum deterministic automaton.

A Future Research section describes the next questions: verified execution, state-level compatibility across precisions, inverse-limit machines, and broader filtered systems. Those possibilities are not results already claimed, nor conditions that must be fulfilled before the finite-precision work can be examined.

Publication makes the work available for scrutiny, use under its approved terms, independent reproduction, and correction. It does not declare the wider Komorebi program finished.

— Komorebi P.
