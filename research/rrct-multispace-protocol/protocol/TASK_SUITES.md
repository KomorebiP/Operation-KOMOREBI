# Frozen Primary Task Suites

The primary holdout contains 720 cases, with 80 cases from each of nine families. Development, calibration, and holdout use disjoint concepts, relations, templates, numbers, and surface forms.

## T1 — silent choice and later report

The model chooses a single-token member of a category but reports it only after an unrelated delay. Readouts before the report are evaluated, and donor-coordinate swaps test whether the report follows the changed state.

## T2 — directed modulation under surface work

The model holds or silently computes a concept while copying unrelated text. The concept may not leak into the copied output. Persistence, later report, and matched nonverbal controls are measured.

## T3 — ordered intermediate reasoning

Two-hop and arithmetic problems contain an unspoken intermediate. Only baseline-solved trials count toward conditional causal success, while unconditional coverage is also reported. Intermediate donor swaps must redirect the downstream answer without merely injecting the answer direction.

## T4 — flexible multi-consumer reuse

One entity state is routed to at least three context-specified queries such as location, action, quantity, or category. A shared intervention must be interpreted correctly by each downstream operation.

## T5 — routine-processing selectivity

Matched copying, grammar, punctuation, local anomaly detection, and adjacent-token tasks test whether candidate-workspace intervention preserves automatic processing. Retention uses a preregistered equivalence margin rather than a nonsignificant difference.

## T6 — capacity, displacement, and competition

Related and unrelated item streams test occupancy, saturation, displacement, and context shifts. Capacity must be estimated relative to matched random dictionaries and must not be inferred from a display top-k.

## T7 — ambiguity and graded fuzzy organization

Natural clue mixtures and controlled embedding interpolations track whether fuzzy packets organize before a later local cone or balanced state sharpens. The fuzzy model must beat capacity-matched hard, dense, shuffled-gate, and random-gate controls.

## T8 — source formation and factorial mediation

The four central conditions are clean, upstream packet transplant, transplant plus clean mediator restoration, and direct donor-mediator patch. Sign-reversed donors and covariance-matched non-consensus directions are negative controls. This suite tests necessity, sufficiency, and mediation.

## T9 — relational binding beyond a bag of words

Minimal pairs preserve the same concepts while exchanging roles or relations. Four-way interaction contrasts discover relational residuals without the lens. Interventions must change binding while leaving concept inventory approximately fixed. A readout that only identifies the correct words but not their roles fails.

## Counterbalancing

- Donor and recipient roles are balanced.
- Single-token validity is frozen before holdout.
- Template-control tokens and user-content tokens are separately labeled.
- Every condition uses matched layers, positions, ranks, and intervention energies.
- Candidate ranks are selected by each estimator family's own development objective, never by overlap with V.
- Field seeds are optimization repeats, not independent model samples.
