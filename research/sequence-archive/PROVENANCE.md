# Provenance, originality and boundaries

This extension was prepared on 2026-11-15 as a continuation of the previously packaged original Komorebi OEIS research collection. `../originals/` retains the previous **43 recovered original artifacts without modification**. `../adjacent_sources/` adds six unmodified research-source files recovered specifically to explore possible new integer-valued extracts.

## Relation to existing work

- The exact filtered exponential block normalization and the classical sequence anchors come from `../originals/Filtered_Exponential_Block_Calculus_for_Generalized_Euler_Families.tex`, `../originals/v10_sequence_catalog.csv`, `../originals/Komorebi_OEIS_v1.0.0_Guide.pdf`, and `../originals/OEIS_Connection_Atlas.tex`.
- The Smith/RLC inspiration comes from `../adjacent_sources/ENGINEERING_NOTES.md` and `../adjacent_sources/Smith_Chart_Extended_Seven_Reactive_Exact.md`. The polynomial recurrence used in this extension is a **new illustrative construction** and should not be attributed to those files verbatim.
- Totient-prefix statistics and prime-power exponent slack use `../adjacent_sources/Carmichael_Komorebi_Attack.md` as topic/source context. The finite-prefix statistics were defined for this extension and do not prove any global preimage theorem.
- Collatz jump statistics use the user's dyadic-jump research theme and the recovered Collatz checkpoint notes. They were defined here as classical exact functions of a nonnegative index; no global Collatz claim follows.
- The finite 24-element Clifford word-count model is **newly specified in this extension**, inspired by the program's quantum/circuits strand. It is not copied from or represented as a theorem in a historical monograph.
- Cyclic coset counting uses the exact safe-residue-retention argument recorded in the Smith analysis, but the resulting sum-of-divisors sequence is **already known**.

## What the numeric tests establish

Tests establish agreement with 162 terms of the recovered generalized Euler catalog, several independent exact formal power-series calculations, and elementary combinatorial and group-theoretic controls. These are implementation checks, not full independent audits of the original research papers. The included formula-level integrality argument is a general mathematical deduction, distinct from tested numerical cases.

## What they do not establish

They do **not** establish novelty or absence from OEIS, universal p-adic valuation formulas, a Collatz theorem, a Carmichael theorem, a physical Smith-circuit realization for the formal recurrence, or any computational-chemistry breakthrough. The published and classical source lineage must be investigated before attribution or submission. Some manuscripts were written under earlier names; their metadata is preserved in `originals/` as a historical archive, while this extension is presented under **Author: Komorebi P.**

OEIS data and official record text are not bundled. The optional matcher consumes user-supplied copies under their OEIS Foundation data terms, separate from this archive's research source code.
