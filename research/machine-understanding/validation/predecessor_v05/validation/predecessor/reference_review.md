# Targeted reference review

**Review date: 8 October 2026.** This is a targeted primary-source check, not an exhaustive survey or priority audit. Publication metadata, abstracts and selected passages were inspected as specified below. Access to a full text is not a claim to have reread it in full.

## [1] Komorebi P. (2026), Machine Societies v1.0.0

Conversation attachment

Read cover, contents, conventions and the first-edition review record through Files. Used only to locate the companion within the project. The full 106-page monograph was not independently re-audited.

## [2] Jacovi and Goldberg (2020), Towards Faithfully Interpretable NLP Systems

https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.386/

Official ACL record and abstract reviewed. Used for the faithfulness distinction, not quantitative empirical claims.

## [3] Shalizi and Crutchfield (2001), Computational Mechanics

https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1010388907793

Author-hosted publication material and abstract reviewed for predictive-state foundations. No comprehensive proof or historical-priority audit claimed.

## [4] Givan, Dean and Greig (2003), Equivalence Notions and Model Minimization

https://engineering.purdue.edu/~givan/papers/mm.pdf

Author-hosted full text available; introduction and state-aggregation context inspected. The draft supplies its own narrower proof.

## [5] Tishby, Pereira and Bialek (1999), The Information Bottleneck Method

https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0004057

Author preprint metadata and abstract reviewed. Conference date and later preprint posting distinguished.

## [6] Beckers and Halpern (2019), Abstracting Causal Models

https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/halpern/papers/abstraction.pdf

Author-hosted primary text inspected for declared intervention families and abstraction compatibility. Not cited as proof of the whole proposed framework.

## [7] Geiger et al. (2025), Causal Abstraction

https://jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html

Official JMLR publication record, complete author list and abstract reviewed. Broad interpretability and causal-abstraction foundation only.

## [8] Hewitt and Liang (2019), Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks

https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/

Official ACL metadata and abstract reviewed. Used for the distinction between probe performance and a representation claim.

## [9] Sutter et al. (2025), The Non-Linear Representation Dilemma

https://arxiv.org/html/2507.08802v1

Version 1 theory, definitions and complexity discussion reviewed. Cited as a qualified criticism of permissive alignment assumptions, not a blanket refutation of causal abstraction. Preprint status retained.

## [10] Miller (2019), Explanation in Artificial Intelligence

https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.07269

Author preprint abstract and author publication metadata reviewed. Published date and journal range checked separately from the 2017 preprint date.

## [11] Gentner (1983), Structure-Mapping

https://groups.psych.northwestern.edu/gentner/papers/Gentner83.2b.pdf

Primary scanned PDF. Opening page inspected as a screenshot because parsed text was imperfect. Abstract and relational-mapping framework checked.

## [12] Angluin (1987), Learning Regular Sets from Queries and Counterexamples

https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~dawnsong/teaching/s10/papers/angluin87.pdf

Primary paper in academic-hosted copy and publisher metadata checked. Query/counterexample framework inspected. The draft does not claim its elementary finite elimination bound is Angluin’s full algorithm.

## [13] Blackwell (1953), Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments

https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032

Bibliographic metadata verified. Full-text requests returned a publisher iframe and did not yield the article. Used only as background context for comparison of experiments; no unreviewed Blackwell theorem is needed for a draft proof.

## [14] Rischel and Weichwald (2021), Compositional Abstraction Error

https://proceedings.mlr.press/v161/rischel21a.html

Official PMLR metadata, abstract and compositional scope reviewed. Our finite intertwining/error arguments are stated separately.

The Blackwell full-text fetch did not return readable article text. That failure is retained above rather than represented as a successful full-text review. No third-party full text or scraped source is included in the source package.
