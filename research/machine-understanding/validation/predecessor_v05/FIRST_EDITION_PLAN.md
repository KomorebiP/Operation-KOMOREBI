# Toward the first edition

Version 0.5 is a release identifier, not a percentage estimate. Its mathematical scope is already substantial enough to consolidate into a first edition without claiming a general solution of machine understanding.

## Proposed first-edition core

A finite realization contract, a reusable representation, explicit source access and recipient background, acquisition with physical effects, evidence-preserving correction, and a joint feasibility account. Every use of "understanding" remains a declared operational research proposal. It does not collapse subject competence, faithful mechanism attribution, social usefulness and subjective experience into one test.

## Work before freezing 1.0

**Consolidate the claim boundaries.** Make the common contract easy to follow across deterministic acquisition, noisy posterior updating, and recipient decision guarantees. Retain negative cases where individually satisfactory components cannot be combined. Avoid implying that the stochastic and deterministic joint problems have already been unified.

**Tighten the resource account.** The graph theorem allows fixed designable decoders and counts message symbols. The decision-tree theorem counts experimental cost within a horizon. A first edition should foreground what is and is not charged, with one worked implementation cost ledger. It need not solve optimal decoder design in every computational language.

**Finish reference and editorial review.** Expand the itemized primary-source audit where only metadata or abstracts were accessible. Review terminology, running notation, repeated limitations and reading paths. A separate visual guide may be useful, but is not required to keep extending the paper indefinitely.

**Recheck the frozen implementation.** Preserve a small independent-algorithm comparison for each load-bearing executable construction, regenerate figures, and repeat the fresh build and artifact checks after the final source is frozen. Formal verification and independent review would add evidence, but must not be claimed before they occur.

## Research beyond the first-edition boundary

Jointly optimal noisy acquisition and multi-recipient explanation, learning or calibrating unknown likelihoods, endogenous candidate-model expansion, economical decoder discovery, stochastic state evolution with hidden history, and empirical attribution to an actual learned solver. These can remain explicit open research problems without preventing a coherent first theoretical edition.
