# Claim status and exact boundaries

## Internally proved Resolution Theory results

The manuscript proves the finite corner calculus, representation and refinement naturality, countable and unbounded completion, weighted inverse path theory, nonlinear packet calculus, finite and countable atomic Resolution Logic, and the declared Fourier, wavelet, polyphase, quantum, arithmetic, and Galois realizations under their stated hypotheses.

## Galois embedding proved in the manuscript

The following statements are proved directly after importing the standard underlying Galois theorems where indicated.

1. For a finite unitary group action, subgroup averaging is the orthogonal projector onto the fixed subspace.
2. For a finite Galois extension with a chosen embedding of the base field into the complex numbers, the subgroup to fixed projector assignment realizes the fundamental Galois correspondence as an order reversing resolution correspondence.
3. Permutable subgroups correspond to commuting subgroup projectors. Nonpermutable subgroup charts are retained through mixed corners rather than forced into a false common refinement.
4. Normal subgroups correspond to central averaging idempotents, globally commuting fixed projectors, invariant fixed ranges, and quotient compression.
5. Finite group actions on unital C star algebras give faithful conditional expectations onto fixed point algebras.
6. Field trace is linear packet aggregation. Field norm is a homogeneous nonlinear packet transfer. Both respect towers.
7. Finite abelian Galois actions admit a Fourier character chart. Classical Galois resolvents are the character packet coordinates.
8. A normal basis identifies the field Galois module with the regular group module and makes the group and character charts explicit.
9. A nonnormal intermediate field gives an atlas of conjugate fixed projectors. The subgroup core and normal closure give the canonical global envelopes.
10. Mackey double cosets are exact transition packets between subgroup symmetry charts.
11. The lower ramification groups give a nested projector filtration. The Artin and Swan conductors are weighted unresolved ranks of that filtration.
12. Haar averaging extends fixed point projectors to compact and profinite groups. Shrinking open normal subgroups reconstruct a continuous unitary representation strongly.
13. Nonabelian Hilbert 90 makes linear semilinear packet descent effective.
14. The relative Brauer class is the exact obstruction to lifting projective descent data to linear descent data.
15. Finite etale Galois categories have canonical permutation resolution realizations.
16. A neutral Tannakian category with a faithful exact tensor fiber functor reconstructs an affine symmetry group scheme, and declared packet idempotents become tensor compatible concrete projectors.

## Imported foundations

The manuscript imports the classical finite Galois correspondence, normal basis theorem, Mackey decomposition, local ramification and conductor formulas, Hilbert 90, the Galois cohomological Brauer obstruction, the fundamental theorem of Galois categories, and neutral Tannakian reconstruction. The contribution is the resolved synthesis and the exact preservation statements, not a claim to have rediscovered those classical theorems.

## Not proved

The manuscript does not prove:

1. a semisimple projector embedding for modular representations when the group order is not invertible in the coefficient field
2. a replacement for finite flat group schemes, inseparable extensions, or wild integral descent
3. a complete operator realization of all nonabelian Galois cohomology
4. a monoidal fiber functor for every resolved tensor category
5. measurable descent for arbitrary packet atlases
6. a Hopf Galois or quantum Galois reconstruction theorem in full generality
7. new arithmetic estimates from the formal resolution calculus alone
8. global spectral invariance for every weighted transfer algebra
9. a universal algorithm finding the optimal resolution for every operator family

## Exact interpretation

The strongest defensible statement is:

> Finite semisimple Galois theory embeds as a distinguished realization of Resolution Theory. Resolution Theory then extends the fixed object, symmetry, descent, and obstruction architecture to noncommutative, approximate, multiscale, countable, nonlinear, and logical settings.

This is not a claim that Resolution Theory replaces all of modern Galois theory.
