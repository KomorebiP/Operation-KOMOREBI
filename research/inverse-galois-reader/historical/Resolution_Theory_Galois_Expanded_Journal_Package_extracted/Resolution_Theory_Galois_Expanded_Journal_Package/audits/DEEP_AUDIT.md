# Deep mathematical audit

## 1. Coefficient and semisimplicity hypothesis

The finite field embedding is stated for a finite Galois extension with a chosen embedding of the base field into the complex numbers. This makes the complex Hilbert realization explicit. Averaging is valid because the group order is invertible. The modular case is excluded and appears as an open problem.

## 2. Fixed point projectors

For a subgroup H, the Reynolds operator

\[
P_H=|H|^{-1}\sum_{h\in H}U_h
\]

is self adjoint and idempotent, and its range is the H fixed subspace. Nesting of subgroups reverses the order of projector ranges. No faithfulness is needed for this theorem.

## 3. Injective Galois correspondence

Injectivity of the subgroup to projector map is asserted only in the faithful field or regular realization. The normal basis theorem identifies the field Galois module with the regular module. Distinct subgroups then have distinct fixed fields and distinct fixed ranges.

## 4. Lattice operations and commutation

The formulas

\[
L^{\langle H,J\rangle}=L^H\cap L^J
\]

and

\[
L^{H\cap J}=L^HL^J
\]

are the classical meet and join identities. The equivalence between subgroup permutability and projector commutation is checked in the regular realization by comparing the supports of the products of subgroup averages. The manuscript does not assert this equivalence for an arbitrary nonfaithful representation.

## 5. Normality and quotient compression

Conjugation carries the subgroup average for H to the subgroup average for the conjugate subgroup. Centrality, commutation with the full action, invariant fixed range, normality of H, and Galois normality of the fixed field are therefore equivalent in the faithful Galois realization. The quotient action on the fixed range is well defined because H acts trivially there.

## 6. Noncommutative fixed point algebras

The finite group average of star automorphisms is a faithful unital completely positive conditional expectation. A group invariant state implements this expectation by the subgroup fixed projector in the GNS representation. This is a resolved fixed point construction, not a claim that every fixed point inclusion is a Hopf Galois extension.

## 7. Trace, norm, and Fourier charts

Trace is the unnormalized subgroup average. Norm is the product over the subgroup orbit and is treated as a nonlinear homogeneous transfer. In the abelian case, character idempotents form an orthogonal Fourier chart, and summing the characters trivial on H recovers P_H. The normal basis theorem connects the group coordinate and character charts.

## 8. Nonnormal fields and atlas structure

A nonnormal intermediate field produces conjugate fixed fields and conjugate projectors. These form an atlas rather than one global invariant chart. The subgroup core gives the smallest Galois field containing the intermediate field. The normal closure of the subgroup gives the largest Galois subfield contained in it. The naming follows the subgroup operations and the fixed field order reversal.

## 9. Mackey transition packets

The restriction of an induced representation decomposes by double cosets. Each summand is an exact transition packet between two subgroup charts. This is a resolved interpretation of the classical Mackey theorem, not a new proof of induction theory beyond the finite setting.

## 10. Ramification filtration

The lower ramification groups are nested normal subgroups. Their fixed projectors therefore form an increasing resolution. Differences of adjacent nested projectors are orthogonal detail projectors. The Artin conductor formula becomes a weighted sum of codimensions, hence a weighted unresolved rank. Reversing the finite sums yields the detail rank formula. Upper numbering is correctly treated as a reparameterization of the same subgroup family.

## 11. Profinite completion

For a strongly continuous unitary representation of a compact group, Haar averaging is the fixed point projector. For a shrinking neighborhood basis of open normal subgroups in a profinite group, uniform continuity on each vector gives strong convergence of the averages to the identity. Continuous finite dimensional complex representations have finite image because a compact totally disconnected Lie group is finite.

## 12. Hilbert 90 and Brauer obstruction

A semilinear GL cocycle is trivialized by nonabelian Hilbert 90, so its twisted fixed packet descends to a vector space over the base field. A PGL cocycle may fail to lift. The connecting H2 class is exactly the relative Brauer obstruction. The manuscript distinguishes effective vector descent from projective descent rather than treating every atlas as globally linearizable.

## 13. Galois categories and Tannakian reconstruction

Finite etale covers become finite continuous fundamental group sets, then coordinate permutation resolutions in finite Hilbert spaces. Neutral Tannakian reconstruction requires a faithful exact tensor fiber functor. The manuscript uses this to identify the monoidal boundary already present in Resolution Theory. Ordinary corner representability alone does not reconstruct a group scheme.

## 14. Historical novelty boundary

Most underlying Galois statements are classical. Potential novelty lies in the integrated resolution dictionary, the use of noncommuting projector atlases for nonnormal symmetry charts, the conductor as a resolution defect statistic, and the common theorem architecture spanning operator, nonlinear, arithmetic, and Galois realizations. Historical novelty for those formulations requires specialist comparison.
