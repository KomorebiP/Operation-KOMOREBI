# Journal readiness assessment

## Ready components

The manuscript is structurally complete, reproducible, and suitable for specialist circulation. It has a self contained abstract, a coherent theorem dependency chain, full internal proof parity, a complete bibliography, vector figures, and explicit claim boundaries.

## Strength of the Galois addition

The Galois chapter is integrated into the master theorem architecture. It does not merely append a dictionary. It proves how fixed objects, lattice operations, normality, quotient actions, trace, norm, Fourier coordinates, nonnormal atlases, ramification, profinite limits, cohomological descent, and monoidal symmetry reconstruction enter Resolution Theory.

## Editorial risk

A seventy six page submission spanning several mature fields may be better received as a memoir or monograph style article than as a standard journal paper. The mathematics is organized to permit later division into a foundational operator paper and a Galois realization paper without altering theorem statements.
