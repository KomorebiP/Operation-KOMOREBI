# Publication visual audit

## Inspection scope

All seventy four pages of the mathematical core were rendered at publication inspection resolution and viewed through contact sheets. The seventy six page submission, one page references, and one page disclosure were separately rendered and inspected.

## Galois section inspection

The new Galois material begins with the fixed point dictionary and proceeds through normality, Fourier coordinates, conjugate atlases, ramification, profinite completion, cohomological descent, and Tannakian reconstruction. The eight new Galois figures were checked for legibility, consistent mathematical notation, and vector rendering.

## Results

- no blank terminal page
- no clipped text or figures
- no overlapping floats
- no malformed equations
- no broken reference codes
- no raster image objects
- all fonts embedded
- no Type 3 fonts
- clean rebuild renders agree page for page

Minor underfull spacing messages remain and do not create visible defects.
