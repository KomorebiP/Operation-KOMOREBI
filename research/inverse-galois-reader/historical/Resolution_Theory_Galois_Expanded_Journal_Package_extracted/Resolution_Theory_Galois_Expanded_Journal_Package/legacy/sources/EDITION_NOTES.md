# Edition notes

## Canonical subject works

- **Mathematics as a Resolved Practice: A Companion to Projector Calculus and
  the Langlands Program** - the largest philosophy-of-mathematics companion in
  the collection.
- **Resolution Theory: Complete Foundations, Proof Paths, and Beyond the
  Finite** - integrated foundations and proof-path context.
- **Resolution Theory: Mathematics, Computation, Statistics, Technology, and
  the Life of Proof** - the practice-centered Resolution Theory edition.
- **Beyond the Finite** - methodological companion on countable and unbounded
  mathematical practice.
- **Proofs as Compressed Discovery Fields** and methodology v10 - proof paths,
  compression, archives, meaning, collective noise, and auditability.
- **Abstraction as a Resolved Practice** - philosophy of abstraction and
  representation.
- **Intelligence as a Resolved Practice** and **Mathematical Pathways** -
  philosophy and mathematical architecture of intelligence.
- **Emergent Structures in Resolution Theory** - emergence, human
  collaboration, open interfaces, and robust bounds.
- **Code as a Human Practice - Consolidated Edition** - philosophy of computer
  science, automation, maintenance, and the continuing role of human coding.

## Overlapping and historical editions

The archive retains earlier editions when they have a distinct structure,
pagination, or editorial history.  They should not be added together to infer a
unique total page count for the underlying ideas because substantial material
is repeated across editions.

## Source availability

The archive includes PDFs and the TeX sources that were available.  The full
proof-path methodology source bundle includes its Python models, figures,
tables, and result ledger.  The coding-philosophy model and tests are also
included.  Supporting code for every other historical manuscript was not
available as one complete source tree at packaging time.
