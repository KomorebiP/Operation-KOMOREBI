# Collection scope

This archive contains all distinct philosophical and methodological artifacts
that were available in the active workspace or recoverable from the persistent
file library during this packaging pass.

Included domains:

- philosophy of mathematics and mathematical practice;
- proof paths, discovery fields, compression, auditability, and memory;
- philosophy of abstraction and representation;
- philosophy and mathematical architecture of intelligence;
- emergence, collaboration, field-relative value, and open interfaces;
- philosophy of computer science, programming, automation, maintenance, and
  human coding;
- integrated Resolution Theory editions where the philosophical material is
  inseparable from its mathematical framework.

Excluded domains:

- primarily technical appendices without a sustained philosophical or
  methodological purpose;
- benchmark-only archives;
- mathematical application papers whose philosophical content is already
  represented in one of the included companions;
- exact duplicate files, except where an older edition has a genuinely
  different structure or pagination.

The archive is a collected preservation package, not a new merged manuscript.
Substantial overlap exists among several integrated editions.
