not silently interpret left multiplication as left-module linearity.

## Generated formal proofs

All 202 unchanged v0.2 matrix-certificate specifications have generated Lean
proofs: exact all-word quotient, output preservation, actual Smith image,
every-competitor state injection, and the necessity of each accepted test.
They evaluate finite matrix and trace indices, never source-state tables.
Seventeen fixtures have zero encoding coordinates, hence a singleton image.

`GeneratedLarge` handles the actual eight-coordinate source modulo 65536. The
separate `LargeCardinality` module proves a bijection between its image and
Fin(2^30), the minimum finite sufficient-state cardinality, and exactly two full
residue coordinates. The zero/one-coordinate exclusion permits even nonlinear
competing encoders and transitions under the same all-state/all-word contract.

## Exact boundary

- The universal numerical minimum FREE LINEAR coordinate theorem remains written,
  not ported to this adapter. Generic state-space injection and actual image
  factorization are formal; these are different claims.
- Python discovery, JSON parsing, exporter, Smith routine and independent checker
  are not verified executable programs. Literal-data agreement is independently
  tested, not assumed to be a semantic specification theorem.
- The large fixture is synthetic and inherited, not held-out mathematical novelty.
- No learned AI policy, comparative speedup study, arbitrary-program lower bound,
  p-adic corpus extension, or universal representation-invention result.
- All proof interpretation review is self-review, not an independent referee.
