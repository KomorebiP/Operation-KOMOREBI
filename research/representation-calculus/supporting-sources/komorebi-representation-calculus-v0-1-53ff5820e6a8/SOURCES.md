# Source and literature boundary

## Project anchors

- `source_records/BLOCK15_THEOREM_NOTE.md` and `BLOCK15_FORMALIZATION_COVERAGE.md` are byte copies of the corresponding attached Block15 files. The actual obstruction is in polynomial coordinates after dividing out the common unit seed, not in consecutive raw partition coefficients. Historical Lean status was read, not rerun here.
- Library semantic retrieval located `operator_symbolic_ai_language_v0.4.pdf` and `operator_symbolic_ai_language_v0.5.pdf`, with excerpts on typed operator language, observational recovery, provenance and proof. Only returned excerpts were used. This release does not audit those drafts or claim the present architecture first originated here.
- The binary-two-color fixed spectrum and model-relative minimality were available in the current conversation's full Block08 and Block09 theorem extracts. The precision-profile calculation in the new script independently evaluates the arithmetic consequence of that specified spectrum. It is not a new replay of those formal proofs.

## Primary public sources directly opened

1. MLIR official overview: https://mlir.llvm.org/
   Used for the existing concept of reusable, extensible, multi-level compiler infrastructure and explicit non-goals. No claim that MLIR itself solves mathematical representation discovery.
2. egg project: https://egraphs-good.github.io/
   Used for e-graphs representing equivalent programs and the optimization/synthesis setting. No claim that an arbitrary enclosure or approximate map is equality.
3. Patrick and Radhia Cousot, author-hosted POPL 1977 abstract and publication page:
   https://www.di.ens.fr/~cousot/COUSOTpapers/POPL77.shtml
   Used for the existing abstraction/fixpoint viewpoint. Only the author-hosted HTML was read; the linked PDF was not analyzed.
4. Lean, Theorem Proving in Lean 4, Axioms and Computation:
   https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/Axioms-and-Computation/
   Used for the distinction between proofs, classical choice, noncomputable constructions, and executable code. Not a claim of a new Lean check in this branch.

## Retrieval limitations

Broad web queries initially returned irrelevant or root-page-only matches. Those results were not used as factual support. Direct retrieval of the four primary HTML sources above succeeded. The literature scope is conceptual positioning, not a theorem-priority or comprehensive novelty audit. No third-party paper PDF is copied into this package.

The quotient, intertwining, finite refinement, and first-order lifting arguments in FORMAL_NOTE.md are written directly, including their hypotheses. They are standard kinds of arguments and are not advertised as historically new. The untested claim is the value of integrating them into an AI-assisted representation-discovery architecture.
