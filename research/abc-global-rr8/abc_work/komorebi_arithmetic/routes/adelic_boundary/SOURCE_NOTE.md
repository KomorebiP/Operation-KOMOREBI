## External primary leaf

J. S. Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, version 3.08, 19 July 2020,
<https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANTc.pdf>.

The normalization preceding Lemma 8.6 and Theorem 8.8, printed pp.137-138
(PDF indices 138-139), were checked directly on 25 October 2026. They
use the ordinary square modulus at a complex place and the residue-norm
valuation at a finite place, and give the product formula for nonzero
elements of a number field. The exact hypothesis is diagonal global
field membership; a general norm-one idele is not asserted to be diagonal.

Authentication, extraction and the use for genuine global sources are
verified. The full background proof is not independently rederived here.
No full external source text is retained or redistributed. The weak
adelic family's balances were calculated separately, so no converse of
Theorem 8.8 is invoked.

No novelty search was undertaken and no priority claim is made. The
counterexample refutes only the displayed weak gluing contract. It does
not refute integer or number-field ABC, IUT, or Komorebi Space's actual
relative realization theorems.

In particular, the contract balances the three specified coordinates.
It does not impose a product formula on every element of an entire common
field generated across the places. Its weak realization locus is not
asserted to be a field. Strengthening to such a field-level contract is
a different possible continuation and is not excluded by this example.
