## Positive constructions

seed and carry obstruction, then a mixed arithmetic diagram with one exact
normalized realization. Its precision maps commute. The separate digit
views reconstruct the complete local ring Z_p. This is a written general
construction, not a claim that the predecessor's small Lean example proves
these new theorems.

The integer_space route constructs bounded residue views modulo N! and
proves that their coherent sections are exactly ordinary positive primitive
additive triples at that budget. Thus the intended integer source is
identified by an explicit theorem. A factorial family proves that allowing
a different unbounded budget at every level adds non-integer coherent
points.

The global_coercivity route proves a joint-observable criterion and a
bounded factorization criterion for a height functional. It also proves
the exact integer stopping rule eta<1/B at a known height bound c<=B.
