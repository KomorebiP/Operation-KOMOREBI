# Arithmetic reconstruction in Komorebi Space

This program pursues the arithmetic comparison and global height ambition
independently inside Realization Calculus. The ambient realization theory is
Komorebi Space: declared views and restrictions, coherent families, protected
extension obstructions, intended-source comparisons, and quantitative repair
with an explicitly chosen cost. No disputed IUT comparison is assumed.

The target remains the ordinary integer ABC conjecture. For every epsilon>0,
we seek a constant C_epsilon independent of the primitive positive a+b=c such
that

    log c <= (1+epsilon) log rad(abc)+C_epsilon.

This is a research target, not a consequence already obtained from the
general realization framework. The every-epsilon uniformity is essential.

the obstruction quotient is legitimate, from a mixed Set-valued diagram

linear correction conclusions by notation. The prime-support observations
are also treated as nonlinear data.

## The first direct comparison test

The multiplicative power presentation retains rad(abc) and changes log c to
s log c. Its additive law is transported, however. The comparison with
ordinary addition has residual

    D_s=c^s-a^s-b^s,
    D_2=2ab,                  D_3=3abc.

Protecting a^s and b^s forces the ordinary additive repair c_s=a^s+b^s.
The exact cost calculation in FIRST_COMPARISON.md shows that, on
(1,t,t+1), its logarithmic correction cost is asymptotic to
(s-1)log(t+1) for fixed s. This is the size of the formal height increment.
The candidate does not give a free conductor-controlled height gain under
this declared ordinary correction norm. Its repaired radical must also be
recomputed. This is an executed candidate test, not a model asserted to be
equivalent to the full IUT link.

## Why the global realization contract matters

The budgeted integer theorem gives a positive global reconstruction at a
supplied B. It also supplies an exact arithmetic escape example: the
coherent residues of (1,sum_(k<N)k!,1+sum_(k<N)k!) have an ordinary primitive
integer lift at every finite level, while their required budgets diverge.
Thus a uniformly bounded realization and a sequence of finite realizations
with changing budgets are different achievements.

The adelic route tests a stronger weak gluing proposal. Over the fixed
field Q(i), exact local sums, coherent finite reductions, fixed prime
support and the product formula on the three chosen coordinates admit
families with unbounded ambient height that are not diagonal global
points. A three-view protected obstruction proves their missing common
global marking. The countermodel does not impose a full-field product
formula on every rational function of the coordinates; that stronger
contract is not excluded by this test.

These statements explain what must be retained during an arithmetic
comparison. They do not assert ABC violations by intended integer or
number-field points.

## The next genuinely arithmetic theorem

The remaining positive input must exploit the exact integer relation across
the multiplicative channels. It must construct a comparison whose cost is
controlled by radical/conductor data while the output remains an intended
global arithmetic realization. Three tested replacements are insufficient:
zeroing the carry, forgetting the global marking, and deriving height from
a small dimensionless additive residual.

The existing Komorebi repair theorem controls distance to a coherent locus.
The new joint-coercivity calculation states the additional condition needed
to control size along it. Applying that calculation to ABC requires a real
arithmetic estimate: the nonlinear radical channel is not a linear map P,
and the every-epsilon coefficient is not supplied by closed range alone.

The program therefore has concrete objects and failed candidate mechanisms,
with an explicit open quantitative leaf. It is no longer conditional on
interpreting an unspecified IUT output family. It is still an unsolved
arithmetic research program.
