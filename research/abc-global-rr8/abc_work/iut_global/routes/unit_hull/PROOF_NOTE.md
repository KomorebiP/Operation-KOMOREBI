# A local test of a proposed repair by units or integral basis changes

Checkpoint: ABC-20261025-RR5, based on the RR4 archive with SHA-256
689847eefd2cf75b9804b574d6613c13d10606e6e805a979cd74facb998ea8ae.

This note tests one explicitly defined construction. It does not identify
these lattices with the full IUT multiradial representation, its three
indeterminacies, or its holomorphic hull. It cannot establish or refute
IUT III, Theorem 3.11 or Corollary 3.12.

## Contract

Let K be a nonarchimedean local field, O its valuation ring, pi a
uniformizer, and q the finite residue-field cardinality. Normalize additive
Haar measure by mu(O)=1 and product measure by mu(O^k)=1. Fix integers
m>=1, k>=1, and s_1,...,s_k>=1. Set

    L_theta = direct_sum_j pi^(s_j m) O,
    L_q     = direct_sum_j pi^m O.

The candidate repair permits changes M in GL_k(O), followed by an O-lattice
H containing both M L_theta and L_q. Its normalized enlargement cost is

    E(H,M) = [log mu(H)-log mu(M L_theta)]/k.

The target tested here is whether this construction can give
E(H,M)=o(m log q) as m tends to infinity, with k and s_j fixed and
their average greater than 1.

## Exact determinant and enlargement cost

For every such M and H,

    E(H,M) >= (average_j s_j - 1) m log q.                 (1)

Proof. The measure of L_theta is q^(-m sum_j s_j). Since det(M) is
an O-unit, M preserves additive Haar measure. Containment H superset L_q
implies mu(H)>=mu(L_q)=q^(-km). Taking logarithms and subtracting gives
(1). All lattices have finite positive measure, so these logarithms exist.
Equality is attained at M=identity and H=L_q, because every s_j>=1
implies L_theta subset L_q. This also proves optimality over the stated
class of constructions. No classification of IUT hulls is asserted.

In the scalar case k=1, multiplication by a unit u leaves each ideal
pi^(sm)O unchanged. Consequently the O-submodule generated by any
nonempty unit orbit of pi^(sm)O is the same ideal. Roots of unity are
units: if zeta^r=1, then r v(zeta)=0, hence v(zeta)=0. Thus varying roots
of unity alone cannot bridge pi^(sm)O to pi^m O. The same statement
holds after passing to any fixed finite extension, with its own normalized
valuation and measure.

For the illustrative square weights s_j=j^2, j=1,...,k,

    average_j s_j = (k+1)(2k+1)/6.

For k>=2, the minimum normalized enlargement cost is a positive multiple
of the original contact height m log q. In particular it is not o(m log q).
For k=1 that coefficient is zero and no obstruction is asserted. As a
scalar check, pi=3, s=4, m=1 requires enlargement from 81 Z_3 to 3 Z_3,
with index 27 and log-volume increase 3 log 3.

## What this rules out, and what it leaves open

This refutes only the specified proposal: unit changes or integral
invertible mixing cannot preserve the enlarged numerical gain at sublinear
enlargement cost in these lattices. Keeping that cost explicit produces
a linear term rather than a free gain. Merely enlarging enough to include
the desired input is therefore not a quantitative repair in this model.

The actual IUT construction uses more structure. Its indeterminacies may
change regions in ways not represented by GL_k(O), and its procession and
determinant normalizations must be derived from their definitions. A
nonlinear or differently normalized construction lies outside the present
claim. To apply this diagnostic to IUT, one must first prove an exact
dictionary, not infer one from the symbols q and theta or from the j^2
weights. No such dictionary is proved here.

## Evidence and continuation

Evidence: the measure computation and containment argument above are a
universal elementary proof. The numerical example is illustrative, not
load-bearing computational evidence. No finite experiment or proof
assistant establishes an additional statement.

Disposition: the unit/integral-lattice candidate is refuted within its
stated scope. The full IUT repair route remains open. Its executable next
obligation is to derive, from the actual IPL/SHE/APT and hull/determinant
definitions, a numerical witness or approximate witness in the common
normalization, while tracking the enlargement error globally. The current
note supplies neither that derivation nor a global ABC bound.
