# IUT comparison question: primary-source record

Checked 2026-10-25 from the RR4 checkpoint pinned in INPUTS.json.

## SS-2018

Peter Scholze and Jakob Stix, *Why abc is still a conjecture*, author-hosted
[PDF](https://www.math.uni-bonn.de/people/scholze/WhyABCisStillaConjecture.pdf),
internally dated July 16, 2018, 10 pages. Relevant locations: §1.3, equations
(1.4)–(1.6), printed pages 3–4; §2.1.8–2.2, printed pages 8–10.

Extraction: their objection concerns degree normalization when identifying
abstract pilot objects with concrete divisors. The concrete theta degrees carry
factors j². In their simplified interpretation, maintaining consistent real
coordinates removes these factors from the decisive estimate, leaving no height
bound. They acknowledge simplifying the IUT objects. This verifies what their
objection says, and does not independently verify its application to every
construction of IUT.

## IUT3-2020

Shinichi Mochizuki, *Inter-universal Teichmüller Theory III: Canonical Splittings
of the Log-theta-lattice*, author-hosted
[PDF](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Inter-universal%20Teichmuller%20Theory%20III.pdf),
internally dated May 2020, 199 pages. Relevant locations: Corollary 3.12,
proof Step (xi-d)–(xi-g), printed pages 183–185, especially (xi-e)–(xi-f),
printed page 184.

Extraction: the author constructs possible output log-volumes bounded above by
−|log(Θ)|, linked to the input prime-strip and expressed in the 1-column
arithmetic structure. He then infers that the input value −|log(q)| belongs to
that lower interval. This is the specific implication to reconstruct.
