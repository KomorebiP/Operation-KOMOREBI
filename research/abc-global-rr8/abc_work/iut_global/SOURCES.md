# Sources and exact verification limits

Checked 2026-10-25. Read source text at the listed primary locators. External
theorem statements and attribution were extracted; the complete IUT argument
and the complete analytic counting proofs were not independently verified.
No full external source text is redistributed in this checkpoint.

* C. Bernert, T. Browning, J. D. Lichtman and J. Teravainen, *Bounds on the
  exceptional set in the abc conjecture*, arXiv:2410.12234v2, revision submitted
  9 May 2026. [Version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12234v2),
  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.12234v2), Proposition 1.1 and Theorems
  1.2-1.3. Pin v2 rather than the different internal display dates. Our
  epsilon dictionary is lambda=1/(1+epsilon). Source statement checked;
  whole proof and optimization appendix not audited here.
* P. Scholze and J. Stix, *Why abc is still a conjecture*, PDF dated
  16 July 2018, especially section 2.2, pp. 9-10.
  [Author PDF](https://www.math.uni-bonn.de/people/scholze/WhyABCisStillaConjecture.pdf).
  Their objection and stated simplifications were read. Their simplified
  presentation is not certified here as a complete model of current IUT.
* S. Mochizuki, *Inter-universal Teichmuller Theory III: Canonical splittings
  of the log-theta-lattice*, author PDF dated May 2020, 199 pages, proof of
  Corollary 3.12, Step (xi-d)-(xi-g), printed pp. 183-185.
  [Author PDF](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Inter-universal%20Teichmuller%20Theory%20III.pdf).
  The exact numerical inference was read; its full dependencies remain
  unverified in this checkpoint.
* S. Mochizuki, September 2018 comments on the August Scholze-Stix report;
  October 2025 IUT report; and April 2026 preliminary formalization report.
  Versions, locators, byte hashes and checked passages are recorded in
  routes/response/SOURCE_RECORDS.json. The April report describes skeletal
  ongoing work and does not provide a completed public formal ABC proof.
