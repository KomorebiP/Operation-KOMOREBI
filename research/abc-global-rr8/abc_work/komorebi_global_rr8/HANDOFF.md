raw integer data: radical-30 triples pass every specified finite depth
with nonzero ordinary defect. Their relative archimedean residual need
not be small. A single-prime high-precision perturbation likewise defeats
each fixed finite translated norm profile outside a supplied budget.

The initial weighted-precision statement used c>=max(a,b)>0 where its
subsidiary conclusion required 0<a,b<=c. An isolated reviewer supplied a
negative-coordinate counterexample. The correction is explicit, the
original is preserved, and the positive-source CRT theorem is unaffected.
