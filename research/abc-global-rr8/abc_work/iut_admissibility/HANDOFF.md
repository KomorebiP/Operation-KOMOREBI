## What failed and why

* Choosing S to match a raw volume is insufficient. Equal raw volumes can
  have different hull volumes even among compact-open sets. Taking the
  hull after forgetting the representative is not defined on that quotient.
  This does not refute a source construction retaining representatives.
* Rescaling does not erase hull overhead. Both quantities shift together,
  while containment and the discrete scalar group impose further constraints.
* Hull approximants Phi(P) cannot exceed the already fixed hull. Their
  existence does not prove the native value is under that cap.
* Arbitrary permutations of separate j^2 coordinates are not supplied by
  the source's within-stage tensor-factor action. A new typed comparison
  would be needed to make that proposed construction admissible.
