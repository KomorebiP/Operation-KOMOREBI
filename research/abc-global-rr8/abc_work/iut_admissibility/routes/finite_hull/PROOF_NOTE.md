# Finite certificates for a bounded union of local hulls

Checkpoint ABC-20261025-RR6. Base: RR5 archive SHA-256
79351e014266a936a96a3511d859957d2e00a6f25c3a18e6e3c987f761dafa01.

This is an elementary local algebra result and a conditional certificate
interface. It does not construct admissible IUT output regions or assert
that every local witness can be obtained from IUT's specified data.

## Setting and exact hull

Let A=product_(i=1)^d K_i, d>=1, where each K_i is a nonarchimedean
local field with normalized discrete valuation v_i, valuation ring O_i,
uniformizer pi_i and residue cardinality q_i. Put O=product_i O_i.
For a bounded set U in A with nonzero projection to every K_i, define

    a_i(U)=min {v_i(x_i): x in U, x_i!=0}.

These minima exist: the sets of valuations are nonempty subsets of Z
bounded below. Define

    H(U)=product_i pi_i^(a_i(U)) O_i.                         (1)

Then H(U) is the smallest fractional O-ideal containing U. To see this,
the displayed ideal contains U by the valuation criterion. Any fractional
O-ideal containing U has, in coordinate i, an exponent at most a_i(U),
so contains (1). In fact H(U) is the O-submodule generated by U: select
one x^(i) in U attaining the ith minimum and apply the ith central
idempotent of O to isolate x_i^(i). That element generates the required
coordinate ideal. Thus at most d points of U generate H(U) as an
O-module. No measurability assumption is needed for this assertion.

## Hull of an arbitrary bounded family

Let {U_beta}_{beta in B} be a nonempty family, whose union U is bounded
and whose members each have nonzero projection to every K_i. Then

    a_i(U)=min_beta a_i(U_beta),
    H(U)=sum_beta H(U_beta).                                (2)

Every minimum in (2) is attained, even when B is infinite. Choose beta_i
attaining the ith minimum. The at most d selected indices satisfy

    H(U)=H(U_(beta_1) union ... union U_(beta_d)).             (3)

This follows from (1) coordinate by coordinate. Repeated indices are
allowed. This finite generation statement is about a bounded local hull,
not an enumeration of all admissible global choices.

For a target fractional ideal Q=product_i pi_i^(b_i)O_i, equations (1)-(2)
give the exact equivalence

    Q subset H(U)
      iff, for every i, there is beta in B and x in U_beta
             with x_i!=0 and v_i(x_i)<=b_i.                 (4)

The witnesses in different coordinates need not come from a single
U_beta. They must all belong to the one bounded family for which H(U)
is defined and estimated. One cannot substitute outputs from different
inputs or incompatible numerical realizations.

## Numerical consequence with explicit weights

For fixed positive real weights w_i, define on fractional ideals

    ell_w(product_i pi_i^(a_i)O_i)
          = -sum_i w_i a_i log(q_i).                        (5)

It follows from (4) that Q subset H(U) implies

    ell_w(Q)<=ell_w(H(U)).                                  (6)

Ordinary product Haar log-volume is the special case w_i=1. Other
positive weights are allowed in (5), but no assertion is made that
exp(ell_w) is an additive Haar measure on arbitrary regions. The precise
IUT packet and procession weights must be supplied separately.

Containment is sufficient and is stronger than the single weighted
inequality. For example in A=Q_p times Q_p, let H have exponent vector
(0,2) and Q have exponent vector (1,1). With equal weights their
log-volumes agree, yet Q is not contained in H. Thus (4) gives a usable
stronger certificate, not a necessary condition for a numerical comparison.

## Why a single output can miss the available hull

Fix m>=1 and let

    U_1=pi^m O_K times pi^(4m)O_K,
    U_2=pi^(4m)O_K times pi^m O_K.

Both are compact open regions of measure q^(-5m). Their union has hull

    H(U_1 union U_2)=pi^m O_K times pi^m O_K,

of measure q^(-2m). The union itself has measure
2 q^(-5m)-q^(-8m), since the intersection is pi^(4m)O_K squared.
These are three different quantities: individual-region measure,
union measure, and hull measure. Replacing the last by the supremum of
the first would give a wrong numerical bound.

This example is not an admitted cross-stage permutation in IUT. The
source check in the original_iut route explicitly distinguishes
within-stage tensor permutations from such a coordinate exchange.

## Conditional use for the IUT comparison

IUT III, May 2020, Remark 3.9.5(i), defines nonarchimedean hulls after
decomposing the packet into local-field factors as the smallest full
fractional ideal of this type. Corollary 3.12 uses the hull of the union
of the possible output images. These definitions motivate the present
calculation. Reading those definitions does not authenticate all of the
theory producing the family of images.

In a fixed nonarchimedean packet, IF the actual admissible output family
is the bounded family {U_beta}, IF the native q-pilot has the target
fractional-ideal realization Q, and IF the same numerical maps and
positive weights are used, then witnesses as in (4) prove the local
numerical comparison (6). At any fixed finite collection of such packets,
their sum of factor counts bounds the number of witnesses needed for
these local containments. This does not produce the witnesses, cover
archimedean terms, prove compatibility of their global realization, or
supply the final global height/conductor estimate.

There is consequently a second concrete repair route besides proving
exact equality of the eta maps: derive native-pilot containment in the
actual bounded union hull, with its original quantitative bound retained.
This is a sufficient route to the relevant numerical inequality, subject
to all the stated dictionaries and remaining places. No claim is made
that it is easier or that it has been completed.

## Evidence and continuation

The proofs above use only the product-of-DVR description and bounded
integer valuation sets. No finite computation or proof assistant is
load-bearing. The unbounded-family case is outside (2)-(4): no finite
proper fractional hull is then guaranteed. A zero projection would also
leave the full-rank hull contract and is excluded explicitly.

Next discriminating task: specify the actual family at one nontrivial
packet, retain its weights and fixed input, and either construct its
coordinate witnesses from the allowed operations or demonstrate why a
proposed witness violates those operations. This route remains open at
that source-dependent witness construction. The elementary finite-hull
certificate is proved, not the actual IUT or ABC conclusion.
