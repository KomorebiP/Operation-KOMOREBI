# Claim ledger

## Proved (elementary arguments in PROOF_NOTE.md)

1. Exact radical ceiling for `1+hQ` when `h` is squarefree and coprime to `Q`.
2. Counting lemma for `rad(1+hQ)<=T` via quadratic roots modulo `Q`.
3. Uniform ABC-hit exceptional-set bounds for bounded input prime alphabet.
4. Quality-`1+epsilon` exceptional-set bounds in the range `Q<=H^A`.
5. Quadratic progression with discriminant `4m` and irreducibility for squarefree `m>1`.
6. Perfect-square prime-multiplier cutoff `h<=Q+2`.
7. Fixed offset `a+hQ` extension.

## Finite checks only

- Exact prime-multiplier censuses for specified `Q,H` pairs.
- Exact `epsilon=1/10` comparisons and prescribed congruence tests.
- `m=10`, `Q=243` quadratic progression with one ABC hit and one non-hit at prime multipliers.

## Not proved

- A pointwise bound of ABC strength on all prime multipliers.
- A prime-value theorem excluding exceptional irreducible quadratic progression points.
- An effective globally uniform radical bound for `r(m*u)` in the high-power input regime.
- Lean compilation or external independent peer review.

## Context

These estimates combine classical squarefree-kernel arithmetic and congruence counting. No claim is made that the bounds are historically original. They are stronger in some regimes than the preceding internal fractional-moment argument; in other regimes they are complementary.
