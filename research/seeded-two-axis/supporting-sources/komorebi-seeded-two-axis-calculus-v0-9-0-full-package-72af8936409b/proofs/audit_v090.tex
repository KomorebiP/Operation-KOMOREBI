\section{Audit and verification record for v0.9.0}\label{app:audit-v090}

\subsection{Authority and evidence status}
The supplied v0.8.0 PDF, LaTeX source, and full package are preserved unchanged in the new package. Their hashes identify the base. The continuation adds K53--K61, with written proofs and an authoring-session self-review. It is not a new independent audit of every inherited theorem. There is no Lean proof package and no claim of historical novelty.

Three routes were executed. The kernel and invariants route produces explicit coefficient boundaries and a sharp lifting window. The fully derived regular-base route proves exact commutation, ruling out a putative defect in that regime. The finite nonperfect-base route produces a genuinely nonzero canonical exchange and a separate one-step group-action obstruction. Transitivity of the canonical comparison then gives a positive theorem on coefficient-lifted inputs.

\subsection{Proof boundaries checked}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The boundary uses torsion-freeness of the source lattices and of the target of the linear constraint. The residual at precision $n$ is an annihilator group, with transition multiplication by $p^{m-n}$, not an arbitrarily identified constant system.
\item Sharp lifting delay concerns solutions for the same fixed integral constraints. It does not grant a lift of constraints or group actions that were supplied only at finite level.
\item Ordinary invariants are not an exact functor. Their cokernel residual is not silently substituted into an exact-functor theorem.
\item Fully derived invariants commute with a coefficient module perfect over the source. At finite singular coefficients that perfectness can fail. The exact counterexample is calculated in enhanced unbounded derived categories, not inside perfect fibers.
\item The $\mathbb Z/p^2$ example assumes $p$ odd and $J$ invertible modulo $p$. Explicit tests outside both hypotheses have nonzero positive cohomology and do not satisfy its asserted quasi-isomorphism.
\item The source of the derived comparison is computed from a bounded-above free resolution of its degree-zero invariant module. Tensoring the original nonnegative invariant complex termwise would give the wrong answer.
\item The vanishing degree-zero comparison follows from naturality of evaluation. The cofiber's cohomology in all integer degrees follows from its long exact sequence, not extrapolation of a truncated numerical calculation.
\item Nonzero derived exchange obstructs a coefficient lift. Its converse is not proved. The separate matrix relation calculation rules out the specified lift from level two to level three.
\item Killing the unbounded target residual kills every perfect $k$-complex. To conclude that it kills all of $D(k)$, the exact functor must also preserve coproducts.
\end{enumerate}

The full group-action enumeration is exactly $\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_3)$, $\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_5)$, and $\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_7)$. Twelve verified Smith certificates compute their finite cohomology sizes, and eight chain composites vanish modulo the coefficient modulus. This agrees with the periodic computations. It is an independent implementation check within one authoring session, not independent mathematical review.

The infinite-degree counterexample and the general exactness, lifting, and completion assertions have separate proofs. The test suite does not infer an unbounded derived tensor product from a bounded truncation. The JSON report records exact families, environment, command, counts, and script hash.
