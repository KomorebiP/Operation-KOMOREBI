## J. S. Milne, Class Field Theory

Primary author source: https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf
Version 4.03, 6 August 2020. The title page/version metadata and the cited PDF pages were checked directly.

- Chapter II, 1.9, printed p.61 (PDF page 70): coefficient short exact sequences produce functorial long exact sequences in group cohomology.
- Chapter II, 1.20, printed p.66 (PDF page 75): for a cyclic group with generator sigma, H¹ is ker(norm)/(sigma−1)M.
- Chapter II, 1.21, same page: explicit connecting cocycle obtained by lifting a cochain and taking its differential.
- Chapter II, 3.4, printed p.81 (PDF page 90): the cyclic difference/norm sequence and periodicity. The proof records the underlying integral freeness that permits coefficient change.

Application dictionary: O=Z_p, M a finite free O-lattice; apply the coefficient boundary to 0→M --p^n→ M→M/p^n→0. Our cocycle is (gm−m)/p^n, with the same coboundary sign gb−b. Transition multiplication by p^(m−n) and the precision-window statement are derived explicitly in the manuscript.

For the finite-ring example, the cyclic resolution is tensored from the integral one. Its integral short exact constituent sequences split as abelian groups, so coefficient change preserves exactness. Applying Hom gives the nonnegative cochain complex with alternating A−I and norm. This source does not itself assert our canonical derived base-change counterexample or a representation-lifting theorem.

Classification: classical machinery and explicit consequences/reorganization. No novelty verdict is inferred for the combined package.

## Stacks Project, Derived tensor product, Tag 06XY

Primary source: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/06XY
Section 15.60, checked 27 September 2026.

Used for the K-flat/bounded-above free model of derived tensor and its exactness. The manuscript does not treat an arbitrary bounded-below complex of free modules as K-flat. The cyclic invariant complex in K59 is first replaced by its degree-zero module, and derived tensor is computed with a bounded-above free resolution of that module.

The exact base-change comparison and its perfect-coefficient argument are proved using evaluation mates and thick generation. They are not quoted as a theorem from this page.
