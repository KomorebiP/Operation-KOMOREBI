# Visual review

Final PDF: 95 pages. Rendered every page with PyMuPDF 1.26.7 at 105 dpi. Inspected all six 16-page contact sheets and detailed 145-dpi pages 57–62 containing the cocycle, counterexample, averaging, finite horizon, conjugacy, and profinite proofs. Page 56 and page 63 were inspected in context on the contact sheet; title, contents, ledger, references, and new audit were also included in the full scan.

No visible clipped equations, overlapping text, missing glyphs, or broken cross-references were found. The geometry check found no words within 10 points of the physical page edge. That test is only a coarse safeguard; the mathematical pages were also inspected visually.

The initial 96-page rendition had a short table-of-contents spill page. Hiding appendix subsection entries from the contents, while retaining their sections and all manuscript text, removed that spill. Final rendering and LaTeX checks were repeated after the change.

Contact sheets are included. Individual high-resolution page renders were used for inspection but are not included in the package. This is a layout review, not an independent mathematical review.
