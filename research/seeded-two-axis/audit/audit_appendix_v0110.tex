\subsection{Executed routes and mathematical boundaries}
The continuous-obstruction route retains the actual profinite source and proves the finite-quotient inflation criterion, including an explicit carry cocycle killed by source refinement. It does not infer that all finite-quotient obstructions eventually vanish. The free-source route constructs lifts without source relations beyond those of a free pro-$p$ group. The abelian route retains commutativity, exposes its normalized residual, and gives a complete criterion on the cyclic normalized stratum, including every rank-two input. The example with $E_{12},E_{21}$ proves the uniform $2n-1$ horizon sharp for $n,r\geq2$.

The self-review checked the following distinctions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Continuous $H^2$ here has a finite discrete coefficient module. No statement about arbitrary topological coefficient theories is substituted for that definition.
\item A continuous repair cochain may require a finer finite source quotient. It is not required to factor through the initial quotient.
\item The cohomology-vanishing theorem gives a sufficient condition and the finitely branching tree gives a compactness criterion. Neither supplies a general numerical horizon for all profinite groups.
\item In the cyclic test, the normalized generator must be cyclic modulo $p$. Rank two makes that condition automatic after minimal scalar-depth normalization. No such assertion is made for arbitrary higher rank.
\item Cancellation of $p^{2a}$ takes place in torsion-free integral matrices for necessity, or is translated into the stated lower-modulus congruence for a finite witness. It is not division by a unit in $R_n$.
\item The positive construction preserves the given reduction, but not extra determinant, ramification, or geometric conditions. The constructed matrices are units over $\mathbb Z_p$, not necessarily unimodular over $\mathbb Z$.
\item The continuous-powers argument includes $p=2$. The explicit carry example retains its separate odd-prime hypothesis.
\item Source inflation and coefficient reduction have opposite variance. A preliminary draft description treating both as unrestricted coarsenings was replaced by \cref{prop:mixed-source-variance}; reversing inflation would require descent through the smaller quotient.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Exact computations and usable certificates}
All six regression suites passed with Python 3.13.5. The inherited five suites were rerun at their previously recorded bounds. The new standard-library suite uses exact integer and modular arithmetic with no random sampling or floating tolerance.

The source-inflation checks cover 495 cocycle triples, all 3,107 refined-quotient pairs for $p=3,5,7$, and 83 repaired matrix values. The centralizer checks cover 1,017 cyclic matrices and 982,827 candidate centralizer pairs in the explicitly listed finite rings and companion-matrix family. Complete rank-two tables contain all 10, 33, 832, and 29,889 valid continuous two-generator actions over $\mathbb F_2$, $\mathbb F_3$, $\mathbb Z/4$, and $\mathbb Z/9$, respectively. Each positive case is checked against an exactly commuting integral polynomial certificate.

An orthogonal enumeration of all 1,048,576 ordered pairs of admissible generator matrices modulo $8$ gives 59,392 commuting pairs. Their 664 distinct reductions modulo $4$ agree exactly with the positive set from the normalized criterion. All 4,096 rank-two tangent triples over $\mathbb F_2$ are also checked. The sharpness suite includes 24 prime/precision families and all 72,353 correction pairs in three stated finite fixtures. Higher-rank checks use 54 constructed three-generator fixtures, not an exhaustive higher-rank population, with 162 recovered polynomial certificates and 648 finite continuity checks. Exact ranges and nonclaims are in \texttt{checks/profinite\_results.json}.

The included command-line tool \texttt{tools/lift\_abelian\_rank2.py} validates the matrix input and returns either exact integral generators or a nonzero normalized-commutator certificate. It applies only to the unconstrained rank-two $\mathbb Z_p^r$ problem. Its positive and negative examples are preserved in \texttt{examples/}. The universal lifting and convergence statements follow from the written proofs, not from these bounded computations.
