# Smith / RLC consolidation and gap closure

Author: Komorebi P.

Base checkpoint: published commit `3e4090141cc88dac723d87c7c504a9b1076a2caf`.
This work resumes the authenticated 12 November handoff. Original archives,
proof files and evidence remain unchanged.

The delivery target is one canonical Smith research reader, with one contents
structure, current statements in the main argument, superseded work identified
as history, and existing reading URLs leading to the corresponding sections.
The laboratory remains a separate interactive instrument linked to that reader.

The mathematical target is to close concrete gaps while preserving the full
finite positive ordinary uncoupled transformer-free RLC model. It does not
promise an arbitrary-target minimum-synthesis algorithm or an optimized margin.

Routes:

1. **Universal-margin audit.** Reconstruct resistor elimination, compact
   sample equations, physical boundary reduction, rational identity and the
   effective-minimum theorem application. Check both external sources directly.
   A counterexample or missing hypothesis restricts every dependent claim.
   A successful written audit remains self-review, not independent peer review
   or Lean verification.
2. **Deterministic family synthesis.** Specialize the existing uniformly
   certified cubic coefficient box at exact rational targets. Peel the explicit
   endcap inverse, reconstruct the physical composition, and pass its independent
   acceptance checker. Accept a target only with a bound exact witness. Outside
   this family or resource scope, return an appropriately scoped result.
3. **Falsification and boundary controls.** Attack the reductions with opens,
   shorts, floating components, repeated placements, malformed certificates,
   targets just beyond the family box and the five-reactive zero-error Hughes
   control. Finite checks can refute implementations; a pass does not replace
   the universal written argument.

Evidence labels and route dispositions are recorded in `STATE.json`. New
arguments receive a separate adversarial self-review before integration.
