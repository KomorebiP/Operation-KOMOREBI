## Contracts

1. **Universal proof assurance.** Formalize actual finite-graph reductions and
   their boundary semantics, not just the displayed scalar identities. Full
   closure requires the compact-model and packet-transfer chain, its external
   leaves and an explicit axiom audit. Compilation of selected lemmas closes
   only those lemmas. A new author pass is self-review, not peer review.
2. **Useful Hughes separation.** Produce an explicit usable positive margin
   with an all-topology certificate, or an independently checked global
   four-reactive exclusion. Better-looking numerical fits and timeouts are not
   exclusions. Any reduced topology list needs a coverage proof.
3. **General synthesis.** Return physical positive ordinary uncoupled
   transformer-free circuits with exact target binding and a global reactive
   lower bound. A complete target subclass is a useful theorem but does not
   settle arbitrary degree. Resource failures remain `UNKNOWN`.
4. **Resistor optimality.** Prove lower bounds on actual resistor branches,
   allowing arbitrary extra reactive branches unless a restriction is stated.
   The existing reactive-degree bound does not supply this conclusion.
5. **Robustness.** Prove quantified parameter-error guarantees in an explicit
   frequency domain. State parasitic and model assumptions. Analytic guarantees
   do not replace physical measurements; no laboratory access is assumed.
6. **Priority.** Authenticate primary sources, compare exact statements and
   record established overlap. No finite search establishes global novelty.

dependencies of synthesis, resistor optimality and robustness, rather than
three unrelated experiments.
