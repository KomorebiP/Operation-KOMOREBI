# Source and claim map

## Internal framework

The manuscript uses the project grammar

```text
seed -> address -> operate -> project -> transfer -> refine -> constrain -> complete -> reconstruct
```

and the exact parent-child interpretation of polyphase refinement. Those principles motivate the cycle-frame and correspondence-packet organization. They are used as an internal coordinate language, not as external evidence for the Hodge conjecture.

## Directly proved in this manuscript

- master Hodge obstruction projector for nested algebraic and Hodge subspaces
- primitive Lefschetz reduction
- positive cycle-frame range and kernel identities
- exact degree-residue frame refinement
- child-kernel intersection criterion
- finite stabilization of nested bounded-degree cycle spans
- approximation-to-exact membership in a fixed finite-dimensional cohomology space
- verification of a proposed support through restriction to the complement
- rational multiplicity reconstruction from a verified support
- divisor-lowering transfer theorem with invertible algebraic return operator
- positive multi-channel completeness criterion under the stated adjoint/correspondence hypothesis
- Cayley-Hamilton reconstruction of the inverse transfer
- propagation of an invertible relative transfer through a connected smooth family
- nonlinear elimination of the range component of a Dolbeault defect under the stated contraction hypotheses
- exact Adams degree projector
- finite computational certificate forms for a proposed correspondence family

## Conditional statements

- a rational Hodge packet satisfying the divisor-lowering transfer hypotheses is algebraic
- a small Dolbeault defect with vanishing harmonic obstruction and a bounded right inverse can be corrected to an integrable structure
- a proposed multi-incidence family proves the targeted Hodge statement once its total positive transfer is shown invertible

## Open statements retained explicitly

- a universal effective bound on the degree needed for algebraic-cycle saturation
- generation of a supporting codimension-`p` algebraic subset for an arbitrary primitive Hodge class
- vanishing of every endomorphism-valued harmonic Dolbeault obstruction packet from Hodge-type data alone
- primitive codimension-two saturation for a general projective fourfold
- a new family in which several incomplete incidence channels become jointly complete
- the general rational Hodge conjecture

## Negative controls

- trace moments of a nilpotent obstruction can vanish while the obstruction itself is nonzero
- a sum of span projectors is not a valid replacement for the positive frame operator when child spans overlap
- numerical rank or spectral checks are scouting tools until converted to exact rational certificates
