## Harmonic theta modularity
- Authors: Tomoyoshi Ibukiyama and Brandon Williams.
- Title: Hermitian modular forms and algebraic modular forms on SO(6).
- Exact inspected preprint: arXiv:2505.23497v1. Header date 29 May 2025; internal PDF date June 12, 2025. These differing visible dates are retained rather than silently harmonized.
- Locator: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.23497
- Exact passage: section 3.4, pages 18–19, especially the assertion that a harmonic homogeneous polynomial of degree nu on a rank-n lattice gives a vector-valued theta form of weight nu+n/2 for its Weil representation.
- Translation: rank n=4, lattice sqrt(5) A4, polynomial degree 10+d. The project supplies its own regular-coset/sign-line argument to deduce full level one.
- Scope: none of the article's conjectural Hermitian/orthogonal correspondences is assumed. Only the established theta statement is used.

## Level-one structure and finite vanishing
- Author: William Stein.
- Title: Modular Forms: A Computational Approach, online chapter Modular Forms of Level 1.
- Locator: https://wstein.org/books/modform/modform/level_one.html
- Exact statements: Theorem 2.11 (valence formula), Theorem 2.14 (multiplication by Delta), Corollaries 2.15–2.16, Theorem 2.17.
- Use: a nonzero holomorphic weight-k full-level form has order at infinity at most k/12; cusp spaces in weights20 and22 are one-dimensional.
- Normalization: the project's E4 and E6 have constant coefficient1. The cited chapter often scales Eisenstein series differently. Dimension/vanishing arguments are unaffected; the moment identities explicitly use constant-one normalization.
