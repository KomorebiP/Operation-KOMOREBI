# Exact failures and limits preserved at final closeout

1. **Proposed valuation equalities are false.** The equalities
   v_ell(tau(p))=v_ell(p+1) fail for ell=2,3,5. The minimal prime examples
   and independent integer checks are in computations/valuations001.
   The 5-adic error can have either sign. This closes those equalities,
   not every possible valuation bound.
2. **Interpolation on one shell does not imply universal nullness.** The
   explicit interpolants at 7 and 31 fail already at shell 1. Harmonicizing
   does not preserve shell values, as s2*D shows. No interpolation-to-null
   theorem was proved.
3. **A fixed finite nonzero-residue sieve cannot cover all primes.** The
   Deligne–Chebotarev theorem gives infinitely many simultaneous survivors,
   including every primitive local exponent. This does not refute adaptive
   moduli or imply an integer zero.
4. **Nonhomogeneous finite symmetric polynomial nulls do not escape content
   conservation.** PC-ALL establishes this universally. Nonsymmetric or
   nonpolynomial functions are outside its scope and were not classified.
5. **Local admissibility does not exclude trace zero.** The explicit
   filtered Frobenius object is compatible with the local constraints.
   A global obstruction is still missing.
6. **The retained arithmetic axioms are insufficient even together.** The
   synthetic-model theorem preserves arbitrary finite data and fixed
   congruences along with the recurrence, bounds and square theorem, yet
   has a prime zero. It is not a counterexample to Lehmer.
7. **Source access failures.** The Rutgers candidate lee14b.pdf could not
   be retrieved and was not used. The direct versioned BCO PDF fetch failed
   during audit; the unversioned full PDF succeeded and its internal v1
   marker was checked. No source gap was filled from a search snippet.

All official computations completed successfully; there was no timeout or
failed arithmetic assertion in the recorded runs. Their finite scopes do
not prove universal statements. No possible future global route is declared
exhausted merely because this final push did not solve the conjecture.
