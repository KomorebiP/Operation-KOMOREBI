# Sources checked on 26 September 2026

1. B. K. Moriya and C. J. Smyth, *Index-dependent divisors of coefficients of modular forms*, author manuscript dated 1 May 2013, accepted 28 April 2013. Sections 2.1-2.3 and 7. Source for the size-bound/divisibility observation, nonordinary-prime discussion, and Niebur identity.
   https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~chris/papers/Moriya_Smyth_final.pdf

2. N. Lygeros and O. Rozier, *A New Solution to the Equation tau(p) = 0 (mod p)*, Journal of Integer Sequences 13 (2010), article 10.7.4, published 9 July 2010. Sections 4-6. Source for the Eichler-Selberg moment system, explicit Catalan inverse, Hurwitz moment formulas, and reduced-form convention. These constructions are classical and are not claimed as new here.
   https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL13/Lygeros/lygeros5.pdf

3. J.-P. Serre, *Divisibilite de certaines fonctions arithmetiques*, Seminaire Delange-Pisot-Poitou, volume 16 (1974-1975), expose 20, pages 1-28. Section 1, example 3, printed p. 20-02: for each fixed modulus, tau(p) vanishes modulo that modulus on a positive-density prime set. This page was visually checked.
   https://www.numdam.org/article/SDPP_1974-1975__16_1_A15_0.pdf

4. T. J. Fonseca, *Higher Ramanujan equations I: moduli stacks of abelian varieties and higher Ramanujan vector fields*, arXiv:1612.05081. Introduction and Section 6. Source for the classical differential identities for E2, E4, E6. The retrieved PDF carries arXiv v1 and an internal date of 5 November 2018; no assumption about the chronology beyond those visible labels is made. The differential derivation is verified directly in the included symbolic script.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.05081

External mathematical theorems are dependencies, not conclusions of the finite audit. This was not a historical-priority review. No third-party papers are redistributed in the bundle.
