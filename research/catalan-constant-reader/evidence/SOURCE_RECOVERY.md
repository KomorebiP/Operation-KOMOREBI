dated 23 September 2026. The parent publication process owns materialization

unrelated Fuss–Catalan figure files. Searches for Zudilin, Eskandari,

also checked in four plausible content-search candidates. The only matches
were Catalan-number constructions, not Catalan's constant.

“Personal context is unavailable for this conversation.” No earlier prose
or unpublished proof is reconstructed from that unavailable source.

uniform asymptotic bound or irrationality. Any written arguments newly
derived from those definitions in the present edition should be identified
as current exposition and checked against authenticated literature where
