\subsection{An independent primitive census}
A new exact enumeration covered every primitive positive triple with $a\le b$ and $2\le c\le10000$. It contains 15,198,743 triples. A separate Euler totient sieve gives the same count. Exactly 121 satisfy $c>R$, with membership decided by the integer inequality rather than a floating quality threshold.

Within that declared domain, the largest displayed quality is approximately $1.567887$ at $1+4374=4375$, with $R=210$. The example $2+109\cdot3^{10}=23^5$ lies outside the census. This computation supplies neither a uniform conjectural constant nor a conclusion about unsearched triples.

\subsection{Two controls on delicate local boundaries}
The CRT lift criterion was checked against complete finite brute force in 2,928 cases. Every individual coordinate interval must be nonempty. The sum-of-endpoints condition alone can incorrectly accept a target when one coordinate interval is empty. The preserved implementation already had the individual guard. The new theorem statement now includes it.

The strict good-prime boundary was also tested with justified infinite-series tails. For rank two and $p=5=3r-1$, the raw determinant at $t=10$ has valuation four, while its depleted version has valuation three. For $p=7$ and $t=14$, both have valuation three. The modular precision levels were 6, 8, 12 and 16. The tail bounds in the computation report authenticate these named valuations. Thus the strict condition $p>3r-1$ cannot be weakened to equality in the universal exact-valuation theorem.

At a small prime, a raw lower bound can even coexist with zero. For rank one at $p=2,t=2$, the standard convergent logarithm identity gives $\Li_1(2)=-\log_2(-1)=0$. Finite zero residues support this identity but do not prove it by themselves. The depleted moment at that argument has valuation one. This is why the all-prime budget theorem explicitly uses $v_p(0)=+\infty$ and does not promise raw nonvanishing at every small prime.

\subsection{What the seven jet scripts actually formalize}
The original jet file has now been built with its exact Lean 4.19.0 and mathlib v4.19.0 versions. All seven theorem axiom checks passed. Its original source produces two style-linter warnings, so a warning-as-error elaboration fails. A separately preserved checked copy changes only the two tactic separators from \texttt{<;>} to \texttt{;}. That copy builds and elaborates with warnings treated as errors. The statements, coefficients, quantified domains and imported library are unchanged.

The seven statements cover rational cancellation of the balanced factor, two-node and three-node polynomial Gram identities, a rank-two finite truncation formula, its cleared rational expression, its integer remainder and a rational coordinate change. Their standard logical axiom dependencies are recorded individually. There is no custom axiom or unfinished proof in the inspected outputs.

These scripts do not formalize convergence, general infinite Hankel determinants, positivity, the all-rank valuation theorem, the residue-class minimum proof or the global $abc$ criterion. The Gram identities alone have no positivity hypotheses. The integer remainder identity is a polynomial equality, rather than a completed prime-divisibility theorem. The formal scope is finite algebra, distinct from the written analytic and arithmetic proofs.

\subsection{The separate checked KTG interface}
The recovered KTG Block 03 is a different development with a different source history. Its exact Lean 4.34.0-rc2 clean replay passed all 24 build jobs and all 22 direct source elaborations with warnings treated as errors. A fresh axiom inspection covered 42 named theorems. Forty-one use no axioms, and one generic logical independence theorem uses the standard proposition-extensionality axiom.

Its proved theorem is conditional. Given abstract typed data, realization and normalization certificates, a supplied witness-preservation theorem and a monotone observable, a hull containment yields the scalar observable bound. The source witness for an actual IUT instantiation is still missing. A finite Boolean model demonstrates that the earlier abstract fields do not logically imply the missing containment. That is an independence result inside the defined interface, rather than a counterexample to IUT or $abc$.

Separate type fields distinguish roles but do not require pairwise unequal types. Its positive natural-number rank field does not enforce rank greater than one. Its grade-transfer equations check the chosen abstract definitions, rather than authenticate their interpretation as actual IUT log-volume or normalization objects. Those interpretation obligations remain mathematical work outside compilation.

The historical jet verifier first encountered a float at the rank-one empty product. The exact identity test detected it, and the accepted historical implementation corrected it. The latest replay uses the corrected exact source. The false unbalanced $Q$ target and the failed rational witness are mathematical corrections retained as explicit theorems.

The runtime recovery had host-specific compiler startup and extraction failures, corrected by a disclosed compatibility layer for the process's own executable-path lookup. Compiler and proof bytes were unchanged. The exact commands, logs, changes and hashes are preserved in the evidence report.

None of these successful finite or formal checks supplies the global arithmetic realization. The final missing implication remains the one stated in Section~\ref{sec:bridge}.
