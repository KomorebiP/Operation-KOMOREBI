# ABC reader edition 1.0: authoring and verification

Komorebi P. · Operation Komorebi · Conjecture Series 04 · 8 October 2026

The release contains a 71-page mathematical paper with 24,435 extracted words and a separate 25-page visual guide. The guide has a cover and 24 illustrated spreads. The paper develops the recovered project routes, their precise local results, counterexamples to overstrong targets, and the remaining uniform global implication. General abc is not established by this work.

## Content and scope

The reader states the all-epsilon quantifiers, distinguishes radical and conductor quantities, proves the characteristic-zero Mason–Stothers theorem, and explains the conditional asymptotic Fermat consequence. It develops the prime-packet, CRT, modular, integral-projector, filtered-contact, Kummer, polylogarithmic, coherence, sparse-spectral, and jet/Hankel routes. It separates published IUT claims, differing assessments, and the project's abstract formal interface.

The local jet and Hankel arguments include exact contact order, nonvanishing, real positivity, raw and depleted p-adic valuation formulas, and the fixed small-prime denominator budget. Rational truncation is shown to lose the intended output-prime contact. The final bridge is an explicitly conditional theorem. Arithmetic realization, local output contact, and a uniform normalized global height estimate remain obligations.

## Corrections and editorial decisions

The audit corrected the summed CRT interval criterion by retaining the per-coordinate nonempty-interval condition. It clarified empty sectors and the unit example, formal versus analytic smoothness, integral Smith and carry scopes, modular parametrizations and differential multipliers, metric normalization, polylogarithmic branches, and the need for full local Tate scalarity. It also corrected the cubic coefficient of the first jet series to −1/9.

The unbalanced target involving a subpolynomial or small-power bound on Q = abc/R was retracted. The paper proves its obstruction and the necessary power cost B ≥ 2s. The balanced identity Q/(ab) = c/R is used in the corrected global theorem. The strict good-prime threshold and p-adic indices were checked independently.

The exact high-quality example is 2 + 109·3^10 = 23^5. The initially proposed identity without the factor 109 failed its integer assertion and is recorded in the development evidence. The second guide annotation fix moved the copper label on the small-prime-budget figure away from the p = 3 column.

Six opening section page breaks were removed after the mathematical text was frozen. The independent reviewer checked these as exact formatting-only diffs. The final paper has 71 pages. All mathematical source and evidence pins still match the reviewed revision. The build wrapper was changed to preserve TeX console bytes after a non-UTF-8 bibliography character exposed a decoding failure. The TeX source and mathematical output were unaffected. Transient acquisition, execution, and authoring failures and their corrections remain in the evidence reports.

## Mathematical and computational verification

The internal independent review reconstructed the stated local proofs and the conditional global theorem. All 44 source/evidence pins and the report pin were checked again after the final layout edits. This is an internal review, not external peer review or a complete audit of IUT.

The unchanged September verifier replay passed 149,306 assertion evaluations, including 504 exact modular valuation comparisons and 361 stored triangle rows. The canonical historical audit and nine implementation groups were replayed. The complete integer census through c = 10,000 covered 15,198,743 primitive triples with a ≤ b and found 121 cases with c > R. Quality values are floating display values. Radical comparisons and census membership use integers. A further 2,928 brute-force CRT comparisons checked the guarded search criterion. The guide data received 429 independent comparisons.

The formal replay used the exact pinned compilers. The KTG interface passed 22 strict source elaborations and 42 theorem axiom checks with Lean 4.34.0-rc2. It remains an abstract conditional interface and finite independence model. The actual IUT witness instantiation is open. The seven elementary jet identities compiled with Lean 4.19.0 and mathlib v4.19.0. A separate checked copy changed two tactic separators to satisfy warning-as-error. Its statements and coefficients are unchanged. These seven identities do not formalize the infinite analytic, Hankel, p-adic, or global abc arguments. Runtime workarounds, dependency pins, original and corrected sources, and failed logs are documented in `evidence/FORMAL_STATUS.md`.

The finite outputs support precisely their tested ranges. They do not establish a universal bound. The general results in the paper have written proofs with separate dependency and hypothesis review.
