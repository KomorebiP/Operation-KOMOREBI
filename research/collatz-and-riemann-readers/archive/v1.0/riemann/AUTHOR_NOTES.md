# Riemann monograph consolidation notes

## Source corrections

The original parity report contains a display that upper bounds the full signed infinite sum by V_{Q,R}(M), a mass on one finite tail interval. That inequality does not follow and is generally false. The new manuscript defines the full triangle majorant A_Q(M), for which |c|<=A is valid, and lower bounds A by a positive finite interval contribution.

The original interval mass sums over every coprime base and omits |mu(m)|. A lower bound for that larger mass does not lower bound the actual triangle majorant, because nonsquarefree bases have mu(m)=0. The new manuscript counts squarefree coprime bases and proves a uniform density formula. The resulting revised barrier includes an extra factor 1/zeta(2) in its displayed constant. The exponent conclusion remains the same.

Both repairs preserve the original reports unchanged under recovered/. The reviewer independently derived and confirmed the squarefree density formula and revised constant before inspecting final text.
