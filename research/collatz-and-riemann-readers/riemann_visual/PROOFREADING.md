# Riemann visual guide proofreading

Date: 2026-10-08. Scope: cover, twenty four spread texts, generated figure labels, and computation report. Mathematical reference: the companion Riemann manuscript and its source register. This guide explains the proof architecture. It does not replace its proofs or add a new RH claim.

Checked notation: centered z = rho − 1/2, positive heat parameters, amplitude exponent δ versus energy exponent 2δ, multiplicity in Laplace residues, signs and prefactors in the explicit formula, annihilator 1/4 − partial_u², normalized Gaussian overlap, Hermite scaling, Poisson squared coefficients, and the ground pairing constant.

Checked arithmetic: the finite c_K expression and K = 0 endpoint convention, exact four and eight corner signs, remaining base sign μ(m), squarefree zero support, partial sum versus full sum, full absolute majorant versus signed amplitude, and all quantified asymptotic boundaries.

The independent review requested four precision edits which were applied: explicit C > 0 and t0 > 0, retained modes through index R_J rather than an ambiguous count, prime coordinates including primes in divisor b rather than dividing the already indexed argument, and the full Gram matrix of five modes rather than five entries. It also requested that the q chart respect its strict domain. That chart now begins above α.

An independent numerical check recomputed all thirteen c_K values with Machin series π and a different exact Bernoulli recurrence. It found a maximum difference of 4.66 × 10⁻⁷⁷. The shared review preserves its implementation and results.

The hypothetical quartet is geometric. No phase free cosine and hyperbolic cosine identity is asserted. Every off line geometry, energy model, count scale, and barrier power curve is explicitly labeled as a model. The map contains the two separate rigorous RH interfaces and no invented direct Möbius to heat transfer.

Prose uses short connected paragraphs, with each spread under 160 prose words. Titles and takeaway lines are concrete. Publication attribution is Komorebi P. The guide contains no new theorem certification claim. Historical certificates are referred to through the companion source bundle.
