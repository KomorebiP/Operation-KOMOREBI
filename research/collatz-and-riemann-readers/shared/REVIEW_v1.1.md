## Corrections and limitations

1. The Collatz orbit figure initially pointed its largest odd state label at time 17. The true maximum 3077 occurs at time 32. The final source derives the annotation coordinate from the actual maximum index.
2. The merger diagram's positive multiple implication now explicitly states `n>m`. Divisibility alone would not imply a positive lower bound for an unordered or negative difference.
3. The Collatz density spread now explicitly declares integer block parameter `ell>=1` and integer deficit bound `D>=0`.
4. The RH compression spread now declares `C>0` and `t0>0` and says to retain modes through the inclusive cutoff. The manuscript separately treats zero Poisson mean before using the positive-mean Chernoff optimizer.
5. The RH coefficient spread records the `K=0` convention. Prime-cube coordinates include selected primes in the divisor argument, and the Hermite report refers to the Gram matrix of the first five modes.
6. The RH rate chart initially included `q=alpha` while its title stated `q>alpha`. Its interval now starts at `q=1.001` for `alpha=1`. The rate formula itself extended to the boundary, but the revised chart matches the declared strict choice.
7. One report sentence called the zero rows 25 and 49 squarefree. They are nonsquarefree. Their actual Möbius values and plotted support were already correct. The report wording has been corrected.
8. Native Collatz figure inspection found footer and axis-label overlap. Production removed redundant plot footers while preserving their notes in the figure data and adjacent guide text. Final assembled layout is reviewed separately.

These repairs leave the two global conjectures open. There is no fresh Lean replay, unrestricted archive verification, global novelty certificate, or new asymptotic arithmetic estimate in this review. The earlier source acquisition boundaries in `LITERATURE_CHECK.md` remain applicable.
