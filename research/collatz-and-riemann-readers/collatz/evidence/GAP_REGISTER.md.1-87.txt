# Gap register — corrected after v0.19 and the serious audit

## Ultimate target G0

\[
\forall n>1\text{ odd},\;\exists r:\ A^r(n)<n.
\]

**Status:** open. The equivalence with `OddCollatz`, strong-induction reducer, and existence of an infinite self-floor obstruction on failure are checked. No additional conjecture is installed as an axiom.

## G1 · A self-floor obstruction with a coefficient crossing

**Required output:** eventual descent below the original source for any infinite self-floor candidate that has a contracting coefficient prefix. Descent at precisely the first coefficient crossing is stronger than necessary.

### G1a · Full-floor half-danger classification

The auxiliary target is

\[
2\,3^q\le2^J,\quad\mathsf{SelfFloor}(p;n,y)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n\le2.
\]

The current formal formulation uses the canonical source; the canonicality theorem connects positive non-descending half-contractions to that form. A least failure has a low-lift even-child parent in the strict coefficient annulus with full source floor, doubled endpoint, and the known source/odd-count/depth constraints.

For an appropriate upper vector, doubling requires

\[
\mathcal L\le U-(2^{J+1}-3^q)n.
\]

**A closing result:** every remaining actual witness forces enough ordered loss to reverse this inequality, or a correction collision. v0.19 proves soundness of supplied correction chains and concrete slice exclusions. It does not prove that all residual witnesses are excluded.

**Useful finite subtask:** a sound-and-complete finite correction generator under explicit sorted-bound, fixed-count, and horizon hypotheses. Its termination would be finite algorithmic completion, not a Collatz proof.

### G1b · Residual crossing behavior

Even proving G1a does not automatically handle a source-floor path whose contracting prefixes stay in \(1/2<\lambda<1\), or prove eventual descent after a first crossing. A stronger common-floor theorem or another sufficient global descent argument is still needed.

## G2 · Permanent no-crossing

**Hypothesis:** a positive odd actual orbit with \(2^{S_r}\le3^r\) at every accelerated time.

**Proved consequences:** state injectivity and \(x_r\to\infty\); unbounded deficit on every tail; exact record/reset arithmetic; constrained non-descending strong resets; arbitrarily large tail minima and valid finite floor-density certificates.

**Missing contradiction:** no theorem excludes simultaneous satisfaction of all these conditions.

### G2a · Oscillatory deficit / common-floor excursion control

The reset statements do not cover all excursions: a reset may descend relative to a large local peak while staying above the original seed. The useful object is the composed growth/reset equation

\[
x_v=\lambda_r\lambda_g n+\lambda_r c_g+c_r.
\]

A closing bound must compare the whole expression with the **same** original floor. Another duration or local-gain inequality alone is not such a bound.

### G2b · Persistent deep deficit / positive-integer support

In the possible subcase \(d_r\to\infty\), the normalized coefficient \(\rho_r\to0\). This is compatible with divergence. A successful route needs an integer-support exclusion, a well-founded invariant, or a joint height/valuation contradiction. Local norm contraction and sparse cylinders do not independently establish one.

### The packing gate

Any proposed density contradiction must name a common height \(H\), prove that enough distinct required states lie below \(H\), and prove there are fewer admissible states in that same interval. The linear envelope used under bounded deficit is not available for free here.
