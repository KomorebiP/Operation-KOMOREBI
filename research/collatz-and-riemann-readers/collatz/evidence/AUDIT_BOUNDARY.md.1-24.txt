# Audit boundary — v0.17

## Established by final Lean builds

- A compressed Collatz exit lifts uniformly to an explicitly computed arithmetic progression.
- Explicit sufficient base/slope conditions give exact first strict-descent time j+1 on that progression.
- The concrete prior seeds satisfy the conditions and give infinite admissible progressions, including infinitely many inputs in the 4095 residual class.
- A certified terminating computed exit transfers termination to its source with an explicit added j+1 steps.
- For any globally Lipschitz full-state field, the actual normalized v0.16 dyadic quadrature converges to its Bochner interval integral.
- The error is at most L*2^(-n)/2, and the constant is attained on nonconstant affine fields.
- The old canonical geometric-defect limit is identified with the integral.

## Not established

- Applicability of these run certificates to every odd input, termination of every computed exit, a global cover, a new residue-coverage percentage, or Collatz.
- A physical source for arbitrary Lipschitz regularity, identification of the analytic coordinate as spacetime, an interacting continuum QFT, or replacement of the sup norm by a physical Hilbert norm.
- Fresh rebuilding of all inherited records or independent revalidation of the compiler and trusted Mathlib binary caches.
- New semantic equivalence or definitional equality. Pretty-print hashes are presentation fingerprints only.

The inequality involving the run-exit slopes is a sufficient explicit hypothesis, not a universally true assertion about all compressed runs. The supplied global Lipschitz premise in Physics is not a theorem about arbitrary fields.

Body dependencies and type-only dependencies are stored separately. Transitive internal edges traverse only the freshly compiled twelve-module source closure. Historical theorem IDs are linked only after exact source-hash matching. Missing original v0.14 full archive bytes remain missing; this package preserves the actual original v0.16 full ZIP once.

Package verification checks bytes, manifests, hashes, receipts and ledger consistency. It is not a claim of mathematical originality, formal verification of all English prose, or replay of every old theorem.
