# Komorebi Collatz: source, implication, and gap audit

**Audit date:** 11 September 2026.  
**Frozen baseline:** `Komorebi_Collatz_Congruence_Correction_v0.19.0_Checked.zip`.  
**Additional comparison:** the two different archives labeled v0.18.  
**Conclusion:** the rebuilt source closure passes. No complete Collatz proof or half-danger classification was found. Several advertised endgame branches are redundant or vacuous; a useful theorem from the parallel v0.18 branch can be recovered without changing the baseline.

This is an audit companion, not a new Collatz proof checkpoint. The original archives and mathematical source snapshots are retained unchanged. Supplemental results proved during this audit are clearly separated from inherited results, finite tests, and mathematical deductions not formalized here.

## 2. One consistent mathematical language

For the shortcut map,

\[
T(n)=\begin{cases}n/2&n\text{ even},\\(3n+1)/2&n\text{ odd},\end{cases}
\]

a length-\(J\) parity packet with \(q\) odd inputs and offset \(C\) satisfies

\[
2^J y=3^q n+C.
\]

`Realizes` includes actual parity-compatible integer dynamics. `RealizesFloor(m,p,n,y)` additionally requires every intermediate state, including the endpoint, to be at least \(m\). The accelerated map is the terminating odd-part recursion applied to \(3n+1\); that recursion does not assume termination of Collatz iteration.

For an accelerated odd orbit,

\[
2^{a_r}x_{r+1}=3x_r+1,\qquad S_r=\sum_{i<r}a_i,
\]

\[
2^{S_r}x_r=3^r n+C_r,\qquad
C_r=\sum_{j<r}3^{r-1-j}2^{S_j}.
\]

Set \(B_r=\lfloor\log_2(3^r)\rfloor\). Under the permanent no-cross hypothesis \(2^{S_r}\le3^r\), the natural-number definition \(d_r=B_r-S_r\) agrees with ordinary integer subtraction. Outside that hypothesis, Lean's natural subtraction truncates; it must not be silently read as a signed deficit.

For interpretation only, define

\[
\rho_r=2^{S_r}/3^r,\qquad Z_r=\rho_rx_r
=n+\frac13\sum_{j<r}\rho_j.
\]

The Lean reset theorems use exact cross-multiplied integer statements rather than floating-point logarithms or ratios.

## 3. What the program has proved, grouped by mathematical role

### 3.1 Exact infrastructure

The rebuilt closure gives the actual-map bridge, affine packet laws, exact addresses and endpoint equations, odd-position offset sums, chronological prefix extraction, and source congruence transport. These establish that the later inequalities concern Collatz itself rather than an unrelated auxiliary recurrence.

A canonical source is the least positive representative of a packet's dyadic source class. Canonicality is a uniqueness or smallness result, not a descent theorem.

The code's `paradoxicalThreshold` is the **natural-number quotient** `offset / gap`. For a negative-coefficient packet it is \(\lfloor C/(2^J-3^q)\rfloor\), while the associated real affine fixed point is \(C/(2^J-3^q)\). Their integer non-descent tests are equivalent, but the two quantities should not be identified as equal real numbers.

### 3.2 A genuine global regime exclusion: bounded no-cross deficit

The v0.6 theorem, present in the rebuilt closure, proves

\[
\left[\forall r,\ 2^{S_r}\le3^r\right]
\Longrightarrow
\forall D,N\ \exists r\ge N:\ 2^{D+1}2^{S_r}\le3^r.
\]

Thus permanent no-crossing cannot remain in any fixed finite deficit strip. Its proof uses exact high-odd packet counting, injectivity, and a linear height envelope supplied by the bounded-deficit assumption. This is a real exclusion, not merely a new coordinate identity.

It does not prove \(d_r\to\infty\), only unbounded deficit on every tail. It does not exclude an unbounded-deficit no-cross orbit.

### 3.3 Records, resets, and conditional occupation

The critical staircase increments by one or two. Deficit rises by at most one, and a rise by exactly one forces the staircase increment two and the actual accelerated valuation one. Record levels are consequently attained without upward skips.

For a reset \(d_u=d_{u+k}+K\), with local dyadic depth \(A=S_{u+k}-S_u\),

\[
2^{-K-1}<\frac{3^k}{2^A}<2^{1-K}.
\]

The offset/duration/profile theorems quantify what is needed **if the endpoint also satisfies \(x_{u+k}\ge x_u\)**. Peak-to-first-hit hypotheses yield heavy occupation of one profile level and an internal more-than-doubling excursion. These implications remain valid after the audit.

They do not apply automatically to every reset of a global non-descending trajectory. A local reset may descend from a large peak while staying above the original seed.

### 3.4 Canonicality and finite strong-reset dictionaries

Half-contracting non-descending packets must start at their canonical source. This, together with injectivity, prevents the same strong surviving packet from occurring twice in a no-cross orbit. Every fixed finite dictionary of such packets is eventually absent.

The scope is important: these are **strongly contracting packets whose endpoints do not descend from their own starts**. This does not eliminate arbitrary switching among a finite set of growing record words, nor all finite alphabets of local transitions.

### 3.5 Conditional half-danger witness reductions

The v0.16–v0.18 reductions analyze a least failure of the auxiliary full-floor half-danger classification. The full-floor hypothesis removes the odd primitive branch. The remaining parent is a low-lift even-entry packet in

\[
3^q<2^J<2\cdot3^q,
\]

whose actual canonical source \(a>2\) remains a floor and whose endpoint is at least \(2a\). The linear budget and earlier finite descents imply \(q\ge55\), \(2a<q\), and consequently \(a\ge27\), \(J\ge88\).

These are restrictions on the least failure **if the classification is false**. They do not prove that a failure exists or that the residual annulus is empty.

### 3.6 Ordered offsets and finite source-congruence correction

For odd positions \(t_0<\cdots<t_{q-1}\),

\[
C=\sum_{i<q}3^{q-1-i}2^{t_i}.
\]

The relaxed latest positions \(f_i\) yield an upper envelope \(U\), and \(L=U-C\) is the exact arrangement loss. The prefix-power relaxation has a constructed maximizing word. That word need not be realized from the specified integer; the source contains exact counterchecks to the envelope-only approach.

If \(i_0\) is the first displaced position, v0.19 proves

\[
\nu_2(U-C)=t_{i_0}.
\]

It then proves that every checked finite correction chain preserves all compatible ordered candidates and decreases the envelope. Endpoint divisibility \(2^b\mid y\) legitimately extends precision from \(J\) to \(J+b\). A corrected envelope below the doubling budget, or a bit collision, is a sound finite exclusion certificate.

None of these theorems says that every residual witness must admit an excluding certificate.

## 4. Finding A: two later reset branches were already impossible

**Status: proved in new supplemental Lean audit theorems.**

The v0.7 reset inequality already gives

\[
2^K3^k<2^{A+1}.
\]

For any natural \(h<K\), it follows that

\[
2^h3^k<2^A.
\]

If \(x_u>0\) and \(x_{u+k}\ge x_u\), multiplication yields

\[
\boxed{2^h3^kx_u<2^Ax_{u+k}.}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{Z_{u+k}>2^hZ_u\quad\text{for every }h<K.}
\]

In particular, the gain exceeds \(2^{K-1}\). No occupation hypothesis, peak condition, first-hit condition, actual step recurrence, or state injectivity is needed for this implication beyond the endpoint coefficient and reset data.

This resolves the advertised gain-versus-occupation alternatives:

- for \(K\ge4\), the eightfold gain branch of v0.11 is automatic;
- for every \(1\le h<K\), the gain branch of the v0.12 dichotomy is automatic;
- a surviving reset with normalized gain at most \(2^h\), \(h<K\), is inconsistent.

The disjunction theorems still compile and are true. The proposed low-gain fallback is **vacuous under the combined hypotheses**. It must be removed as a distinct endgame research branch. The unconditional heavy-occupation theorem of v0.9 is not thereby invalidated or made vacuous.

New proved declarations:

- `CollatzSeriousAudit.reset_gain_alternative_always_holds`
- `CollatzSeriousAudit.reset_low_gain_cap_is_inconsistent`

Their source and fresh compiler/axiom logs are included in `companion/` and `evidence/companion/`.

## 5. Finding B: divergence and dictionary escape do not need to be rediscovered

The source already proves no-cross trace injectivity. An injective sequence in \(\mathbb N\) has only finitely many indices in each finite initial interval, so it tends to infinity. The final divergence theorem uses this route directly; it does not need the v0.6 unbounded-deficit conclusion.

Therefore both oscillatory and persistently deep deficit under permanent no-crossing already have \(x_r\to\infty\). A proposed later theorem proving state divergence only after \(d_r\to\infty\) is redundant.

Similarly, canonicality plus injectivity already implies that any fixed finite strong surviving-reset dictionary can occur only finitely often. This useful reduction is not a further exclusion of the whole no-cross orbit. An orbit can keep using different words.

The v0.6 theorem remains substantive: ordinary state divergence by itself does not imply the specific coefficient/deficit conclusion proved there.

## 6. Finding C: the duplicate v0.18 labels hid an unmerged theorem

The archive named `Ordered_Floor` (48 modules) and the archive named `Ordered_Floor_Budget` (47 modules) are different branches. v0.19 inherits the former byte-for-byte, not the union of both.

The shared filenames `CollatzOrderedOffset.lean` and `CollatzV018Audit.lean` have different contents in the two branches. They must not be merged by blindly overwriting same-named files. The complete branch comparison is in `evidence/BRANCH_COMPARISON.json`.

One useful result existed only in the Budget branch:

\[
2^b3^q\le2^J,\qquad y\ge2^g n,\qquad b+g>0,
\]

with positive source and every odd input at least \(m>0\), imply

\[
\boxed{q>2m(b+g).}
\]

The ordinary v0.19 lineage retained the one-factor specializations, but not this general arbitrary-\(b+g\) declaration. The module `CollatzFloorGainBudget.lean` imports only shared baseline dependencies. Its exact side-branch source was recovered, freshly compiled against the rebuilt v0.19 workspace, and all 12 explicit declarations were axiom-audited (11 theorems/lemmas and one definition).

This repairs a real coverage/lineage gap. It supplies a stronger reusable necessary condition. It does not turn that condition into universal descent. The recovered module is supplied separately rather than silently inserted into the frozen baseline.

## 7. Finding D: the two showcased v0.19 slices were already simple descent cases

The correction theorems are universal soundness results and retain value. The two example certificates, however, should be classified as **regression demonstrations**, not newly exposed global counterexample regimes.

For source 3,

\[
T^4(3)=2<3.
\]

Therefore no full-floor trajectory from 3 can have depth at least four. This already excludes the showcased \((n,J,q)=(3,16,10)\) slice.

For source 27, a fresh finite Lean check proves

\[
T^{59}(27)=23<27.
\]

The existing floor-prefix cutoff theorem therefore excludes **every** full-floor packet from 27 of depth at least 59, independent of its odd count. In particular, it excludes the \((27,130,82)\) slice more strongly and directly than the new envelope endpoint estimate.

Three supplemental Lean declarations certify these observations:

- `shortcut_27_at_59`
- `no_full_floor_27_at_depth_ge_59`
- `no_full_floor_3_at_depth_ge_4`

The source-27 computation is new to this audit; no claim is made that the exact numeral theorem already existed in the baseline. The reusable logical cutoff did exist. The examples show the repair mechanism can detect known impossible slices; they do not measure proximity to a uniform theorem.

## 8. Finding E: finite generator completion is much closer than global closure

**Status: an elementary mathematical deduction here, supplemented by bounded checks; not newly formalized as a general Lean termination/completeness theorem.**

Consider the deterministic generator, with a sorted upper vector of length \(q\), entries below the precision, no optional early budget exit, and explicit collision/no-gap rejection. At each genuine correction:

1. the current lowest nonzero residue bit is \(v\);
2. the first upper position \(f_i>v\) is replaced by \(v\);
3. the envelope decreases by an odd multiple of \(2^v\), clearing that bit;
4. the next nonzero pivot is strictly larger than \(v\);
5. a later genuine correction must use a strictly later coordinate index.

Thus there are at most \(q\) genuine corrections. Collision or absence of a suitable gap contradicts compatibility by v0.19. At zero residue the final sorted vector itself solves the finite congruence problem, and zero-residue uniqueness identifies it as the only compatible dominated vector.

This supplies a direct proof route for a terminating, sound-and-complete **finite congruence-feasibility** generator. It is not a theorem that every fixed input is rejected, and it is not Collatz termination. The at-most-\(q\) argument applies to the strict deterministic generator, not arbitrary supplied checker lists that may contain useless steps on an empty candidate class.

The separate direct-itinerary audit checked 20,481 cases with \(0\le J\le10\), \(1\le n\le2^J\), and \(0\le q\le J\). The generator accepted 245; acceptance and its decoded vector agreed with an independently computed shortcut itinerary satisfying the upper-position caps. The remaining 20,236 were rejected by collision or no gap. All runs respected the correction bound.

A particularly useful boundary example is \((n,J,q)=(27,59,37)\). The generator reaches zero residue after 33 corrections, and the scalar terminal inequality also passes. The actual endpoint is 23, so full-floor realization from 27 is false. Correctly solving the finite residue problem therefore does not settle the floor condition or global survival question.

## 9. Finding F: a forbidden blanket descent statement was already refuted in the source

The imported core contains `packet7` with the actual trajectory

\[
7,11,17,26,13,20,10,5,8.
\]

It has \(J=8\), \(q=5\), \(C=347\), coefficient \(243/256<1\), and canonical source 7. Its real affine fixed point is \(347/13\), and the code's integer threshold is 26. Its endpoint is 8, not below 7.

It is even in the scaled nonresonance class:

\[
3^5-2^5=211<32(256-243)=416.
\]

Thus a general target saying all canonical nonresonant contracting packets descend is false. The full-floor restriction excludes this example because the state 5 occurs before the endpoint. Every future source-versus-threshold theorem must retain the correct floor or chronological restrictions. This is an existing exact counterexample to an overbroad auxiliary statement, not a counterexample to Collatz.

For literature context only, Rozier and Terracol, *Paradoxical behavior in Collatz sequences*, arXiv:2502.00948v5 (17 May 2026), distinguishes this phenomenon from Terras's coefficient-stopping-time equality. This audit makes no claim that the packet-affine or ordered-swap ingredients are new to the literature.

## 10. The inference gaps that the valid proofs do not bridge

### 10.1 Soundness is not universal successful exclusion

The main correction theorem has the form

\[
\operatorname{ActualCandidate}(p)\land\operatorname{ValidCertificate}(c)
\Longrightarrow C(p)\le U_c.
\]

Consequently an actual candidate cannot survive a valid certificate that independently yields a strict budget contradiction. The missing statement is that every hypothetical residual candidate must admit such an excluding certificate, or a different arithmetic contradiction.

Terminating a finite generator leaves an accepting possibility. A sound checker cannot reject an actual compatible witness merely because the witness is undesirable. The arithmetic reason that the bad family is empty remains to be proved.

### 10.2 The loss budget is a restatement until loss is bounded independently

Since \(C+L=U\),

\[
(2^{J+1}-3^q)a+L\le U
\]

is exactly

\[
C\ge(2^{J+1}-3^q)a.
\]

The formulation identifies the right quantity to bound, but it is not itself a tightening. Strict source-dependent corrections can tighten it. The unproved part is a lower bound on mandatory loss that exceeds the entire survival budget uniformly in the residual family.

### 10.3 Local descent is not descent below the original floor

The global condition \(x_r\ge n\) permits \(x_v<x_u\) at many local resets. Occupation and canonicality results assuming \(x_v\ge x_u\) cannot be applied to those resets. An entire growth/reset excursion must be compared to one fixed reference floor. The existing results do not prove that sufficiently strong local descents accumulate to global descent.

### 10.4 Sparse cylinders need a matching height estimate

v0.6 could turn sparse cylinders into a contradiction because it had a linear envelope for the distinct states at every relevant late time. Unbounded deficit removes that envelope. An infinite sparse set can contain the states of one rapidly growing orbit. A proposed packing contradiction must give both the number of required states and the number of admissible states below the same proven height.

### 10.5 The two adic descriptions do not automatically supply independent constraints

The source congruence modulo \(2^{S_r}\) and the endpoint congruence modulo \(3^r\) are reductions of the same affine equation. Their combination may be useful, but it is not automatic extra independence. Branchwise 3-adic contraction compares the same chosen exponent/word. Real growth and 3-adic contraction are compatible.

To get a local-global contradiction, a nonzero integer error and an independent strict height bound must actually be exhibited. None of the inspected theorems supplies the proposed universal squeeze.

### 10.6 Rebased tails and slope quantifiers require care

A tail minimum gives a full-floor tail, but not automatically a permanently no-cross tail relative to its new starting index. Its local coefficient is the ratio of two global coefficients.

Likewise, obtaining a sufficiently large tail minimum for each rational density certificate gives a statement of the form \(\forall\epsilon\,\exists u\). It does not permit choosing one finite \(u\) that works for all arbitrarily sharp certificates. Tightening rational slopes alone does not close the limiting critical case.

### 10.7 Predicate names are not stronger hypotheses

`Packet.FirstCrossDanger p` says the immediate parent is coefficient-nonnegative and its even child is dangerous. It does not itself require every earlier prefix to be coefficient-nonnegative. A chronological first crossing supplies stronger history. Arguments using a chronological minimum must retain that additional hypothesis explicitly.

## 11. Revised proof map

The minimal global goal is

\[
\forall n>1\ \exists j\ge1:\ T^j(n)<n.
\]

Strong induction then proves eventual arrival at 1. Conversely, a least nonconvergent positive integer would have no smaller value in its orbit, so it supplies a genuine full-floor obstruction.

There are three useful targets, which must not be conflated:

**H: finite full-floor half-contraction exclusion.** Every positive full-floor packet with coefficient at most one half has source in the trivial range. This is the current auxiliary target. It remains unproved. If established, repeated nontrivial cycles would be excluded, but the weakly contracting annulus and divergent no-cross branch would remain.

**F: coefficient-crossing route to eventual descent.** If a coefficient crossing occurs, prove that the orbit eventually drops below its original source. Requiring descent exactly at the first coefficient crossing is the stronger CST timing target; it is not required merely to prove Collatz.

**G: exclusion of a permanent no-cross positive orbit.** The baseline shows that any such orbit is injective, divergent, and has unbounded deficit on every tail. It does not show those properties are impossible. Oscillatory and escaping deficit can be useful subdivisions, but neither is closed by the current surviving-reset restrictions.

One sufficient completion route is F plus G followed by strong induction. H is an intermediate theorem within the coefficient-crossing lane, not a substitute for both F and G. A different universal descent invariant could bypass the current split; the audit is not asserting these are the only conceivable proof routes.

## 12. What to retain, retire, and do next

**Retain as established:** exact actual-map algebra; bounded-deficit exclusion; record/reset identities; the unconditional conditional-on-survival profile theorems; canonicality; no-cross injectivity/divergence; floor-conditioned primitive normal forms; ordered offset maximization in the stated relaxation; finite correction soundness and exact certificate examples.

**Retire as supposed open branches:** low normalized gain after a surviving K-reset when the cap is below \(2^{K-1}\); proving divergence only after persistent deficit; repeatedly classifying the two showcased finite examples as new global exclusions; unrestricted canonical-contraction descent; treating duplicate v0.18 outputs as sequential additive progress.

**Recover:** the independently compatible general floor–gain module from v0.18B, without overwriting the distinct Ordered_Floor lineage.

**Near-term formal completion:** prove the strict finite generator's increasing-pivot/increasing-index invariant and its finite completeness. That supplies a reliable finite decision procedure for the precise congruence class and should be labeled accordingly.

**Genuinely closing work:** establish a uniform source-dependent correction loss beyond the survival budget, or a full-floor coefficient/margin invariant that independently forces descent. In parallel, supply a height-controlled integer-support argument for permanent no-crossing. Additional local constraints are worthwhile only when they make one of these missing inferences demonstrably closer.

## 13. Audit outputs and failures

Supplemental Lean: two reset-vacuity consequences and three finite-slice consequences were newly checked. The recovered floor–gain module was copied unchanged from v0.18B and checked against v0.19. No convergence axiom, admitted proof, or finite-test-to-universal promotion was used.

The initial attempt to request an interactive container session was unsupported. The normal process invocation worked. A draft branch-comparison helper assumed both archives had the same folder layout; this was corrected before reporting results. The first draft of `ResetAudit.lean` omitted the namespace containing `localDepth` and failed elaboration; the import/open scope was corrected, the final file compiled, and the failure log is retained. These are execution/elaboration issues, not mathematical counterexamples. The fresh baseline 54-module replay itself had no failed module. Historical runtime/compile failures are not relabeled as successes.

Machine-readable status, source census, branch hashes, all-module rebuild records, expanded axiom table, finite audit scripts/results, supplemental sources, compiler logs, and exact original input archives accompany this report. Original `.olean` and external package caches are omitted from the distributable evidence bundle.

**Final assessment:** the audit validates the formal core while materially correcting the earlier progress narrative. One real regime exclusion and a substantial exact proof framework are established. Some later branches were repetitions or vacuous alternatives. No hidden completed proof was found. The useful next advance must close the universal implication from exact source constraints to descent, or eliminate a permanently no-cross positive integer orbit.
