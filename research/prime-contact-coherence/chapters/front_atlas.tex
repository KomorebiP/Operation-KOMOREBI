\section{Exact theorem dependency chain}
The core exact implication chain is:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item Pairwise coprimality of a primitive triple separates prime sectors.
\item The prime-packet differential produces one additive row.
\item The arithmetic Wronskian produces one transverse row.
\item Local valuation divisibility gives $abc/R\mid W$.
\item Smith reduction gives the exact obstruction torsion and the lattice extension.
\item Sector compression and CRT reduce exact search to one moving integer parameter.
\item The continuous relaxation gives the exact stable height and finite stopping.
\item The no-escape identity closes the first-order search as a proof route.
\item Filtered contact restores the carry data lost by split shell packets.
\item Kummer theory globalizes the cyclic contact packet with logarithmic loss.
\item The additive relation yields a Fermat point and exact character bank.
\item Polylogarithmic distribution compresses the complex branch bank but leaves integral gain.
\item Simultaneous local logarithms produce the coherence character and exact final factorization.
\end{enumerate}

Every unproved global theorem occurs after this chain.

\section{Normalization rules}
Several normalization choices are mathematically essential.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item Remove the common gcd of the additive relation before constructing prime sectors.
\item Preserve the dyadic sign layer separately from the principal-unit logarithm.
\item Remove common Kummer powers before measuring local logarithmic degeneration.
\item Divide local rows by their common coefficient depth before forming coherence.
\item Carry exact synthesis norms through every polyphase refinement.
\item Distinguish the marked Frey representative from its rational newform or minimal twist.
\item Distinguish a finite torsion packet from the real regulator of an extension containing it.
\item Use arbitrary-precision integers before every multiplication or determinant.
\end{enumerate}

Failure to respect these rules produces double counting, false rank obstructions, lost integral depth, or numerical overflow.

\section{Four recurring proof patterns}
The proofs repeatedly use four patterns.

\subsection{Local-to-global divisibility}
Prove a prime-power divisor at every active place. Pairwise coprimality then lets the local factors multiply.

\subsection{Compress-then-lift}
Use Smith form to reduce a large prime block to a few aggregate channels. Retain an exact local lift gauge for reconstructing integral coordinates.

\subsection{Analysis-synthesis normalization}
Whenever an analysis map lowers coordinate size, include the norm or determinant of exact synthesis. This prevents coordinate compression from being mistaken for intrinsic height reduction.

\subsection{Finite packet plus archimedean metric}
A finite torsion packet alone cannot carry a real regulator. The correct global object is often an extension, determinant line, or metrized lattice combining finite and infinite realizations.

\section{Two principal conjectural bounds}
The theory has two direct arithmetic endpoints.

\begin{conjecture}[Combined logarithmic--coherence bound]
For every $\eps>0$,
\[
 WC\le C_\eps R_aR_bR^\eps.
\]
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Integral adelic regulator]
There exists a canonical global object carrying the balanced contact packet whose finite kernel and integral archimedean synthesis cost are bounded by $R^\eps$ and $R_aR_bR^\eps$, respectively.
\end{conjecture}

Either the combined bound or the stated integral-regulator theorem, together with the exact factorization and logarithmic Kummer bound, yields strong $abc$.

\section{Logical economy}
All layers use one normalization and one chain of maps. Negative theorems are retained only when they impose a necessary condition on the final object. Computational evidence is separated from uniform arithmetic statements, and every conditional implication identifies its missing estimate explicitly.
