# Proof boundary v0.8.0

## Completed

All 26 predecessor mathematical modules and 290 theorem declarations are unchanged. Seven new modules add 88 declarations, for 378 total.

The internal constraint class now uses an internal relation table, actual internal assignment graphs, a total scope graph, internally witnessed smallness and internal locality. It contains no compactness conclusion as an admissibility field.

From the encoded ZFC axioms the project constructs internal products, all internally existing functions between two internal sets, bounded definable relations, fibers, local solution sets, selectors, restriction and composition graphs, injections proving image-smallness, and definable Replacement image maps.

A complete internal ultrafilter selects a member of any small internal cover, and a unique large fiber of any internally coded function with small value domain. Complement and fiber families are internal sets and are proved internally small before completeness is applied.

From an internal fine complete ultrafilter on the internal small-subsets set, local satisfaction yields a globally satisfying graph inside the same model. The pointwise choices are assembled by Separation. Full-scope agreement uses an internally coded Replacement image family and its proved smallness.

The resulting fine-to-compactness implication is encoded as an actual closed sentence in the original membership language. Every model of the explicit encoded ZFC theory satisfies this sentence. This is a proved semantic consequence, not only a decoding identity.

## Assumptions and trust

AxiomSemantics is unchanged. Every closure used here is derived from its actual finite-parameter schema instances or fixed axioms, not added as a new field. The ultrafilter and its completeness/fineness are explicit hypotheses, never manufactured by external choice.

Quantification over functions and families is internal, through elements of M and its membership relation. External cardinality, powerset correctness and external well-foundedness are not assumed.

Lean's ambient logical axioms, the compiler/kernel, pinned Mathlib dependency source/cache and platform remain trusted. The clean audit rebuilds project products, not the dependency cache. Named-theorem counts include all supporting lemmas and interpretation identities. Checksums establish provenance, not a second proof of Lean soundness.
