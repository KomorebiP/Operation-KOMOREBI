# Block 08: internal fine-ultrafilter compactness

## Research decision

The missing comparison could not be advanced safely by another external ultrafilter proof. The decisive step was to reproduce the complete forward construction inside arbitrary models of the explicit first-order ZFC theory.

## Completed proof chain

1. Build internal products and internal function spaces using powersets and real Separation formulas.
2. Collect solution fibers and use the encoded Choice axiom to construct an internal selector for every small subsystem.
3. Build coordinate functions by bounded relation comprehension.
4. Construct the internal fiber family and prove it small using an internal injection obtained from a section of a surjection.
5. Apply internal ultrafilter completeness to an internal small complement family, obtaining a unique large coordinate fiber.
6. Assemble these coordinate values into an internal global function graph using a first-order formula.
7. Use functional Replacement to collect the agreement fibers over one small scope. Prove this family small and apply internal completeness to obtain simultaneous agreement.
8. Intersect this agreement set with the fine cone of the target constraint. A member of the intersection supplies a local assignment satisfying that constraint and agreeing with the global assignment on its scope.
9. Use the declared locality condition to transfer satisfaction to the global assignment.
10. Compile the independently stated fine and compactness predicates into the original membership language and prove the closed implication in every encoded ZFC model.

## Logical status

The internal forward implication is proved. It is stronger than a formula-interpretation lemma: the hypotheses force a new internal global assignment, whose existence is constructed in the proof.

No ultrafilter existence theorem is asserted. The remaining classification arrows are internal extension-to-fine and internal compactness-to-extension. Full equivalence with the original Theta is therefore not claimed.
