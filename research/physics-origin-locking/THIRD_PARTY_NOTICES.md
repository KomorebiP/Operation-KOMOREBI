# Third-party and inherited material

Python, NumPy, SciPy, SymPy, mpmath, matplotlib, TeX, and their dependencies are external software. They retain their own licenses and are not redistributed. No standalone font files are included. Fonts embedded by the PDF renderer remain part of the document under their applicable terms.

The frozen benchmark, inherited event trajectory and settings, selected source ledgers, and extracted event snapshots are original open Komorebi Physics project materials. Their identities are recorded in `PROVENANCE.json`. The original monograph and the full preceding study archive are not duplicated in this package. Preserved event data are not presented as new integrations.

Research articles in the bibliography are references, not redistributed article files. Existing classical conservation laws and established adiabatic methods are attributed. No empirical endorsement or independent external review is implied.
