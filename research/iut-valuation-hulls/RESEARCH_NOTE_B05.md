## 2. A local multiplicative valuation model

Let `K` be a discretely valued field, `v:K^×→Z` a normalized valuation, `O={x:v(x)≥0}∪{0}`, and `U={u∈K^×:v(u)=0}`. For `R⊆K^×` define the two closures

\[
U R=\{ur:u\in U,r\in R\},\qquad
O^\bullet R=\{cr:c\in O\setminus\{0\},r\in R\}.
\]

**Theorem A (exact level criteria).** For any `q∈K^×`,

\[
q\in UR\iff\exists r\in R:\,v(q)=v(r),\quad
q\in O^\bullet R\iff\exists r\in R:\,v(q)\ge v(r).
\]

*Proof.* In the forward direction, use `v(ur)=v(u)+v(r)` and either `v(u)=0` or `v(u)≥0`. Conversely, if the corresponding relation holds, put `c=q/r`. Then `v(c)=v(q)-v(r)`, and `q=cr`. QED.

Both directions, including existence of the quotient multiplier, are proved in `KTG/ValuationHulls.lean` against an abstract `ValuationShell` with only the required multiplication, quotient, and valuation laws. It has a nontrivial concrete witness `Int×Int`, where the first coordinate is depth and the second is unit phase.

**Important limitation.** `O^\bullet R` is a model of nonzero local *integral-multiplier* closure. For a discretely valued field it also describes the nonzero part of the `O`-ideal generated by `R`, since principal ideals are nested. It is **not** the full IUT holomorphic hull of a region in a tensor packet of log-shells. The IUT construction uses additional arithmetic and categorical data. These theorems cannot be applied to its output without building a source-faithful dictionary.

## 3. Unit indeterminacies cannot cross a missing valuation level

**Corollary B.** If no member of `R` has the same valuation as `q`, then `q∉UR`. If `v(q)<v(r)` for every `r∈R`, then `q∉O^\bullet R` as well.

This is formalized as `unitSaturation_absent_level` and `integralSaturation_excludes_shallower`.

It rules out the *specific* repair 'apply arbitrary valuation-zero units to create the missing lower-valued q-pilot' in this model. It does not rule out a source construction based on nonlinear theta evaluation, log-Kummer correspondences, or other permitted IUT mechanisms.

## 4. Minimal quantitative enlargement cost

Let `π` be a uniformizer and consider ideals `I_a=π^a O`, `I_b=π^b O` for nonnegative integers `a,b`. The smallest ideal containing both is

\[
I_a+I_b=I_{\min(a,b)}.
\]

The enlargement index of `I_a` inside this joint ideal equals `|k|^{a-\min(a,b)}`, where `k` is the finite residue field. Its logarithmic cost is

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{vol}}=\bigl(a-\min(a,b)\bigr)\log |k|.
\]

**Theorem C (optimality).** Every integral ideal `I_c` containing both `I_a` and `I_b` has `c≤min(a,b)`, hence `a-c≥a-min(a,b)`. Equality is achieved by the joint ideal. The bound is sharp.

The algebraic inequality and attainment are proved in `KTG/ValuationCost.lean`. In particular, if `a=sm`, `b=m`, `s≥1`,

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{vol}}=(s-1)m\log |k|.
\]

For independent diagonal coordinates of weights `s_1,…,s_k≥1`, the exponent cost is

\[
E(m)=m\sum_{j=1}^k(s_j-1).
\]

Lean proves this exact list identity and that for any positive coefficient the total cost is unbounded as `m→∞`. For weights `(1,4,9)`, the cost is `11m`, or `33` exponent units when `m=3`; dividing by three summands gives an average `11m/3`.

The preceding ABC/IUT RR5 audit additionally proves the lower bound persists under `GL_k(O)` mixing via determinant preservation and Haar measure. **That broader matrix claim is referenced here, not independently formalized by the B05 Lean code.**

## 5. Why the sign of the log-volume matters

Define a discrete surrogate for the IUT negative log-volume by `L(a)=-a`. Then

\[
L(b)\le L(a)\iff a\le b.
\]

If `b<a`, the unmodified q-pilot-to-theta comparison `-b≤-a` is false. Enlarging the theta ideal to `I_{min(a,b)}` makes the resulting bound true, but it requires the exact cost calculated above.

**Crucial:** forming this joint ideal uses the q-pilot to choose the enlargement. IUT's output hull is defined by its own construction. We have **not** shown the source's output hull makes this enlargement or is allowed to substitute it. Thus 'enlarge until the pilot fits' is not itself a proof of the Step (xi-f) inclusion.

`KTG/IUT312DiscreteWitness.lean` provides a separate explicit approximation contract. If a source-certified output candidate has normalized value `v≤B` and also `q≤v+E`, then `q≤B+E`. Consequently `E≥q-B`. A branch-A witness cannot be combined with a branch-B output bound unless they share the same normalized source context. The Lean code provides positive and negative branch tests.

The actual source uses **real** log-volumes; the earlier RR5 numerical bridge observes that exact containment may be replaced by *upper cofinality* of output values, a weaker sufficient condition. B05 formalizes a discrete attained-with-error witness, not the full real cofinality criterion.

## 6. Finite reconstruction with an additional additive anchor

The previous B04 model over `F5` showed that multiplication does not determine the labelled addition. The new `F17` construction strengthens this.

Let

\[
T(0)=0,\quad T(x)=x^9\text{ for }x\ne0.
\]

Since `F17^×` is cyclic of order 16, `gcd(9,16)=1` and `9²≡1 (mod 16)`, `T` is an involutive multiplicative automorphism. It fixes every element of the order-eight subgroup generated by `2`, as `T(x)=x·x^8=x` whenever `x^8=1`.

Pull back the ordinary addition as `x⊞y=T^{-1}(T(x)+T(y))`. Multiplication remains ordinary multiplication. Then

\[
1\boxplus1=2=1+1,\qquad 1\boxplus2=14\ne3=1+2.
\]

Thus the two labelled field additions agree on the entire multiplication table *and* on the anchor `1+1=2`, but a second additive observation distinguishes them. The classification theorem from B04 continues to explain why these are isomorphic as fields, not distinct abstract fields.

Lean proves `T` is a multiplicative involution, fixes the eight-element subgroup, verifies the additive witness, and proves that a reconstruction function of the coarser public view cannot return `1+2` for both structures. Generic abstract transport lemmas and the ordinary `Fin17` ring laws establish additive associativity, commutativity, zero, inverses, both distributive laws, and nonzero multiplicative inverses. Unlike the first aborted implementation, the final proof avoids kernel-exhaustive checks of all `17³` transported triples.
