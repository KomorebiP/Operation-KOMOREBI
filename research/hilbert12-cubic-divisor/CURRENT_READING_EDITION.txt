OPERATION KOMOREBI — STANDARDIZED READING EDITION 1.0
Komorebi P. | 8 October 2026

This release coordinates 89 current reading documents: 31 traditional papers,
29 visual guides, 25 formal guides, one machine-native paper and three essays.
It is the selected recent publication collection, not a census of every
historical Operation Komorebi artifact or a claim of 89 new research papers.
Open index.html for the catalog. All documents are in pdfs/.

Publication identity: Komorebi Botanical covers; Classical Monograph proof
and formal interiors; Data Atlas visual interiors; Editorial Magazine essays.
The authoring account is centralized in How This Work Was Made. Scientific
AI methods, model details, hypotheses, unsuccessful gates and formal coverage
boundaries remain with the relevant papers. Original authoring paragraphs
and their source paths are preserved in the editable release.

Paper X has coordinated traditional, visual and Lean editions. Its checked
conditional completion produces a transported target family and a recovered
carrier packet under their separate supplied inputs. It does not establish
an unrestricted arithmetic carrier or identify the two outputs. The later
stronger 19/20 attempt has no pinned Lean source and does not retract the
checked conditional result. Dated evidence is identified in the paper.

This publication migration performed document builds and source comparisons,
not new Lean kernel runs or a new mathematical proof audit. 4,830 existing
Lean files and 436 numeric data files were byte-identical to their baselines;
117 edited TeX files retained identical theorem/definition/proof spans.
Every page of all 89 PDFs was rendered. Current selected crosswalks were
refreshed against exact TeX labels and final PDF named destinations.

Paper V's original source archive was truncated. Intact members and additional
exact-hash copies were recovered; 17 historical verification files remain
missing. Its current publication sources and all three reading editions
are intact. The exact missing-file inventory is preserved in the release.
Historical verification logs and old manifests retain their original dates;
the current publication manifests identify the formatted editions.
