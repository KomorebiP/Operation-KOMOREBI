# Primary-source check

## Hyperbolic Fourier cohomology

Abdellatif Zeggar, *Equation cohomologique d'un automorphisme affine hyperbolique
du tore*, arXiv:1902.04849v1, 13 February 2019.
https://arxiv.org/html/1902.04849

Read Sections 3.3, 4.3–4.6 and 5 in the primary HTML. The source proves the
smooth cohomological equation can be solved exactly when its invariant Fourier
orbit distributions (including the constant mode) vanish; it constructs the
inverse by one-sided Fourier sums and uses adapted hyperbolic norms. This is a
known mechanism, not a novelty claim for Komorebi. Its frequency convention is
M^{-T}; ours is M^T, the inverse map on the same orbit sets. The recurrence and
its signs are derived directly in our proof. The global holomorphic extension
on (C*)^3 is justified by our explicit exponential estimate, not by assuming the
source states a global holomorphic theorem.
