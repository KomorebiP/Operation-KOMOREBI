# Failures, changes, and trust boundaries

## Executed mathematical tests

- The proposed assertion that the full threefold product polarization gives the
  Gauss additive phase is FALSE. Its exponent is 63 modulo seven, so its value
  is one. The retained useful pairing is the explicitly chosen one-dimensional
  elliptic coordinate pairing, whose exponent is three. Both statements are
  exact and included as positive/negative controls.
- Extending the twisted Hodge obstruction to the untwisted M would be FALSE.
  The untwisted fixed H^3 has rank eight and its Hodge-allowed part has rank six.
  The final theorem retains the B=omega M hypothesis.
- Topological cancellation and a finite invariant-class group do not identify
  the actual gamma descent, its chosen equivariant structure, or its value.
  No failed exact gamma comparison is concealed: no such comparison was
  obtained or asserted in this block.

## Runtime and proof development

- An exploratory Python decompression route could not import zstandard.
  The installed zstd executable and tar were used successfully instead.
  The complete combined seven-part runtime SHA-256 was subsequently recomputed
  and matched its pin. Only the toolchain/Std restoration is needed here.
- A preliminary tar listing was intentionally piped through head, producing
  the expected upstream SIGPIPE (141). It was a preview, not the extraction
  command and not a passed scientific test.
- The first Lean normal-form draft did not simplify the coordinate projections
  sufficiently before omega; the conjugation involution also required an
  explicit arithmetic goal. Its source and diagnostic are retained.
- Direct elimination of all twenty equations in one omega invocation exceeded
  its heartbeat bound. Raising the bound then exceeded a 60-second execution
  limit. That strategy was replaced by eighteen explicit integer elimination
  certificates. The fixed-space theorem was not weakened.
- A later warning-as-error rejected one unused simp argument in the single-term
  last certificate. Removing only that redundant simp argument resolved it.
- The exact-checker prototype looked for an `index`/`i` field instead of the
  durable certificate's actual `coordinate` field, causing an invalid None
  matrix index. The parser was corrected to use the explicit recorded schema.
  The complete checker was then run successfully from the final source.
- One draft bibliography label conflated two separate elliptic-gamma papers.
  Reading the short account's title page fixed its title and authorship before
  release; SOURCES.md gives the actual version and theorem locator.

## Source retrieval

An initial guessed Galbraith filename did not fetch; the author's actual
pair-over-C.pdf was found, opened and visually checked. A Numdam attempt to
retrieve a Cartan-theorem source timed out. The vanishing theorem was instead
checked in Wehler's official LMU notes, Theorem 6.22, with the actual page
rendered. Unrelated search results were not used as mathematical evidence.

## Accepted verification

All three final Lean modules compiled; all 51 theorem axiom lists were covered
by the audit. The false arithmetic control failed as required. The final
exact checker passed 58 assertions and five negative controls. The source
handoff was extracted to a fresh directory and both checks repeated there.
The inherited 216-theorem project was not replayed; its unchanged archive and
previous evidence remain under prior/. No new interval or floating-point gamma
comparison was performed.
