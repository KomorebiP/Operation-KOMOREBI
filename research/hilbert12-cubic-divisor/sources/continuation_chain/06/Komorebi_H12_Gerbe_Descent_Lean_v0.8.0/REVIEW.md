# Adversarial self-review

This is a second pass by the same assistant, not independent human review.

1. **Hodge sign and saturation.** The three-form coefficients were independently
   derived from exterior products. Projection times D^3 gives (3b,-3a), agreeing
   with (a*omega-b)/D. The coordinates on triples (0,1,2) and (0,1,5) are a,b,
   so the invariant kernel basis is saturated over Z, not merely a Q-basis.
2. **Action variance.** Pullback on H^{0,2} is computed in the cyclic form basis.
   The actual identity P W2=C_R P checks the inverse, transpose and conjugation
   choices. The correct action is omega M^{-1}, whose inverse is omega^2 M.
   Replacing it by omega M fails the exact matrix control.
3. **Twist hypothesis.** The obstruction uses invariance under omega M. Under
   the untwisted M, six Hodge-allowed invariant topological directions survive.
   No assertion about the actual gamma's additional twist invariance is made.
4. **Class versus evaluation.** The group with 252 elements consists of fixed
   holomorphic gerbe isomorphism classes. Chosen equivariant isomorphisms,
   sections, and normalized scalar evaluation are not determined by this count.
5. **Smoothing degree.** The domain/codomain lattice bases differ. The map z->5z
   has matrix diag(1,1,5), determinant five. Using degree 125 from scaling all
   three coordinates would be wrong. DD cancellation is necessary but does
   not give the missing compact descent data.
6. **Intermediate cover.** X=C^3/Z^3 really is Stein. Its map to the triptic
   quotient is an equivariant map to a stack. It is not a descended map to an
   ordinary elliptic curve. Cartan vanishing proves trivializability of the
   pulled-back smoothed gerbe but gives no preferred scalar normalization.
7. **Pairing distinction.** The first-coordinate elliptic pairing has exponent
   three and matches the fixed additive character. The full product
   polarization pairing has exponent 63 and is trivial. This was not repaired
   by changing orientation: the two genuinely different pairings are retained.
8. **Arithmetic boundary.** The Gauss sum and xi already belonged to the known
   arithmetic side. This block identifies geometric sources for its phase but
   does not identify any gamma transport multiplier with that finite pairing.
9. **Formal scope.** No gerbe or complex analytic cohomology object is postulated
   in Lean. All hypotheses and operators there are concrete coefficient data.
   The analytic interpretation is ordinary sourced mathematics. The 216 old
   theorem inventory is not advertised as a fresh cumulative replay.
