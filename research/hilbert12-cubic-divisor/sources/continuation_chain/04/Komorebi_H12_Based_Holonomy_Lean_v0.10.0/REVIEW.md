# Self-review and current proof obligations

## Checks against overclaiming

- The raw commutator is gauge-dependent even with all b_lambda(0)=1 and all
  b_n=1 on the real sublattice. The compensating imaginary/imaginary entry
  has zero total Hodge projection; ignoring it would falsify the class test.
- The six-element quotient concerns lattice characters only. The unit value
  supplies an independent C^* factor in the semidirect-product character group.
- The holomorphic counterexample is an actual M-action on a trivial line over
  the Stein cover, not a counterexample to the gamma identity or its full
  functional equations. It explicitly passes origin and inverse-point tests.
- Compactness is used essentially in the positive line theorem, both through
  topologically trivial flat presentations and the bounded modulus of a lift.
- No invariant Chern class survives because no product of two unit eigenvalues
  is one. This is checked independently by the exact exterior determinant.
- The based multiplier divides the action at p by the action at 0. Forgetting
  that denominator reintroduces the unrestricted unit scalar.
- The rational-point theorem needs a flat equivariant line. A fixed GERBE CLASS
  alone does not provide it or identify a numerical gamma value with its holonomy.
- A return under M^3 has different lattice coinvariants. The conductor theorem
  still applies, but the exponent of the compared scalar must be proved, not guessed.

## Active route and first unproved implication

The actual rank-three gamma cochains must be extended/compared in the additional
Eisenstein directions with their normalization retained. The class selector is
ready but has not been applied to such a gamma extension.

A successful endpoint would construct a genuine compact equivariant comparison
line and prove that its based conductor multiplier equals U_Gamma/U_alg. The
compact-line theorem and conductor lemma then give the cube equality, and the
inherited branch certificate selects equality. No item here supplies the missing
comparison by definition.

This is a self-review, not an independent-author or proof-assistant review of
all the analytic geometry.
