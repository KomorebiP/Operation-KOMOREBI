# Blocks 29–30 proof boundary

Scope: Goal I phases A and B of the roadmap, not completion of Goal I as a whole.

Block29 proves membership in coordinate p-power ideals, capped order and capacity,
generator transfer, least invariant coordinate-ideal products, finite N*r closure,
full-product invariance, and exact initial/transition/ordered-word embeddings into
Block28. Its actual mask instance uses the already certified full summed linear
transition and the previously constructed canonical Mahler series.

Block30 proves an additive equivalence ZMod(p^c) ≃+ p^(r-c) ZMod(p^r), the scalar
compatibility with reduction, product normalization, removal of zero-capacity
singleton factors, exact transition conjugacy and a coordinate formula using
canonical integer representatives. It proves the all-coefficient output identity,
precision compatibility at outputs, and exact ambient cardinality p^(sum_i c_i).

Generic generator and ideal results use positive p; the subtraction form of
transfer via a p-adic valuation requires prime p. Capacity is bounded by r for
normalization; r=0 and zero capacity are included. Actual masks retain prime p,
r>0, p not dividing B(0), and A(0)=B(0) in ZMod(p^r).

NO inversion of a nonunit in the residue ring: decoding divides a divisible
canonical NATURAL representative. The equivalence is additive with scalar law,
not a unital ring isomorphism. Leastness is for coordinate-ideal products, NOT
arbitrary correlated submodules, reachable-orbit support, minimal automata or
canonical transition matrices under arbitrary basis changes. The correlated
ZMod5 example continues to show that relations can allow further reduction.

The normalized machine remains noncomputable Lean mathematics at inherited
interfaces. No sparse runtime speed/memory improvement or verified Python-to-Lean
code refinement is asserted. Cross-precision output compatibility does not
identify minimized state modules across precision. Canonical module invariant
factors do not automatically select a canonical basis.
