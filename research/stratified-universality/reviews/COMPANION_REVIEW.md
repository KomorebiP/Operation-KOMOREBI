## Source constraints sent to companion authors

1. The universal transport theorem has complete finite split lane systems and explicit preservation profiles. It does not automatically extend to arbitrary resolutions or analytic/infinite limits.
2. Relational composition supplies a support bound. Additive cancellation can make the actual composite support smaller.
3. A nonempty pair orbit can supply a compatible selector without that selector acting nontrivially on every morphism. Orbit support and nonzero selector action must remain distinct.
4. Observer packet updates need finite branching and the publication edition's declared empty-fiber conventions. The unamended source omitted these conventions; the final traditional author owns their recorded amendment.
5. Convex-hull value equivalence does not preserve all witnesses, multiplicities, branch identity, or history. Singular admissible continuations can reveal previously invisible provenance.
6. The inspected Lean normalization is over a commutative ring and modules, with finite index types. The resolved lane space is the submodule whose coordinate `i` is fixed by projector `P_i`, not unrestricted functions `i -> M`.
7. The general additive-category statement and its module normalization are different levels of scope. No correspondence to uninspected general categorical, geometric, semantic, or analytic declarations should be asserted.
8. The inspected core has 30 theorem declarations, 12 definitions, 2 structures, and 18 `#check` commands. These are textual inventories, not fresh build results.
9. Reconstructed inspected Lean bytes have the published byte count, but provenance states that no upstream hash comparison is available. The local SHA256 identifies the recovered bytes and is not an independent upstream authentication.
10. The 75 concordance entries comprise 52 internally proved components, 12 classical imports/reinterpretations, 2 conditional components, 6 conjectural components, and 3 definitions/framework entries.
