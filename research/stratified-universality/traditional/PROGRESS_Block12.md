# Block 12 progress record

## Scope

Block 12 develops the finite Galois, profinite, and neutral Tannakian stratum. It places subgroup-fixed-field correspondence, Reynolds projectors, nonnormal conjugate atlases, Mackey packets, ramification details, semilinear descent, projective Brauer obstruction, profinite finite-level resolution, and tensor-fiber reconstruction into the same typed passport and descent language used by the preceding blocks.

## Closed in this block

1. For a fixed finite Galois extension, subgroups, intermediate fixed fields, and faithful Reynolds projectors form mutually reconstructible order-reversing charts.
2. Two subgroup averages commute exactly when the subgroups permute. In that case their product is the Reynolds projector of the product subgroup.
3. Normality is equivalent to centrality of the subgroup idempotent, commutation with the global action, invariance of the fixed packet, and Galois quotient compression.
4. A nonnormal subgroup generates a conjugate projector atlas. Its core and normal closure give the smallest Galois overfield containing all conjugates and the largest Galois subfield common to all conjugates.
5. Double cosets are the exact transition addresses in the Mackey restriction-induction decomposition.
6. A lower ramification filtration gives nested fixed projectors. Their orthogonal differences form detail packets, and the Artin and Swan conductors are weighted unresolved ranks.
7. Haar averaging extends fixed-packet projection to compact groups. Shrinking open normal subgroups converge strongly to the identity projector, or to a declared closed-normal fixed projector.
8. Every continuous finite-dimensional complex representation of a profinite group factors through a finite quotient.
9. Finite semilinear vector descent is effective on objects and morphisms. A retained global frame makes each normalized fixed-rank fiber contractible.
10. Projective descent has a canonical scalar two-cocycle. Its relative Brauer class vanishes exactly when the projective cocycle lifts to linear vector descent.
11. The real quaternionic example has scalar defect `-1`, while the split example has defect `+1`.
12. A finite etale fiber functor gives a profinite permutation-resolution chart.
13. Neutral Tannakian symmetry is reconstructed only after a faithful exact tensor fiber functor has been supplied.
14. Faithful additive, Hilbert, or corner realization alone is not promoted to a Tannakian fiber functor.
15. The finite semisimple Galois and neutral tensor charts form one typed assembly theorem under the printed hypotheses.
