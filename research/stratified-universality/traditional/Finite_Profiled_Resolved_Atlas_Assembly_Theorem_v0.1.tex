\begin{abstract}
We isolate the smallest cross-stratum theorem needed by the Komorebi stratified-universality program.  The theorem is formal once a finite profile-indexed atlas has been constructed: its descent category is the finite pseudolimit, maximal subgroupoids commute with descent, pseudonatural directional bridges induce global functors without becoming invertible, equational proofs transport exactly through the common preservation profile, and coherent native invariants descend.  Global completeness additionally requires effective descent of the local isomorphisms supplied by local completeness.  This separates the formal assembly theorem from the hard verification of the actual Komorebi strata.
\end{abstract}

\section{Finite profiled resolved atlases}

\begin{definition}[Finite profiled resolved atlas]
Fix a small resolved theory $\mathbb K$.  A finite profiled resolved atlas consists of a finite Cech indexing category $\check{\mathcal A}$, a pseudofunctor
\[
\mathfrak R:\check{\mathcal A}^{\mathrm{op}}\longrightarrow\Cat,
\]
a declared preservation profile $\Pi_U$ on every chart and overlap category $\mathfrak R(U)$, coherent equivalences on reversible overlaps, and a pseudonatural semantic realization $\operatorname{Sem}:\mathfrak R\Rightarrow\mathfrak O$.  Noninvertible quotients, truncations, completions, compressions, and constructors belong to a separate directed transformation category unless an inverse has been proved on a named overlap.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Descent category]
The category $\Desc(\mathfrak R)$ has pseudocones over $\mathfrak R$ as objects and modifications as morphisms.  Concretely, an object is a compatible family of chart objects with invertible overlap comparisons satisfying the cocycle law, and a morphism is a compatible family of chart morphisms.
\end{definition}

\section{Assembly theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Finite profiled resolved atlas assembly]
Let $\mathfrak R:\check{\mathcal A}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\Cat$ be a finite profiled resolved atlas.  Assume the chart and overlap categories are small, or locally small in one fixed universe, the reversible transitions and comparison $2$-cells satisfy the pseudofunctor coherence equations, and every construction used below belongs to the verified profile of every participating chart and bridge.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item $\Desc(\mathfrak R)$ exists and has the universal property of the finite pseudolimit of $\mathfrak R$.
\item There is a canonical equivalence
\[
\Core\bigl(\Desc(\mathfrak R)\bigr)
\simeq
\Desc\bigl(\mathfrak R^{\simeq}\bigr).
\]
\item Every pseudonatural transformation $T:\mathfrak R\Rightarrow\mathfrak S$ induces a functor $\Desc(T):\Desc(\mathfrak R)\to\Desc(\mathfrak S)$.  If every $T_U$ is an equivalence, then $\Desc(T)$ is an equivalence.  Pointwise noninvertible $T$ remains directional.
\item If two global morphism expressions are built only from constructors in the common verified preservation profile and their canonical equality is proved in the resolved syntax, then, after every domain-specific hypothesis is verified locally, their realizations are equal in $\Desc(\mathfrak R)$.
\item A coherent pseudonatural invariant family $I:\mathfrak R\Rightarrow\mathfrak V$ induces $\Desc(I):\Desc(\mathfrak R)\to\Desc(\mathfrak V)$.
\item If every local invariant is complete on its normalized chart and the groupoid of local invariant-compatible isomorphisms has effective descent, then $\Desc(I)$ is complete on the corresponding global normalized category.
\end{enumerate}
If the semantic pseudonatural transformation is effective for objects and morphisms, $\Desc(\operatorname{Sem})$ identifies descent data with the declared global semantic objects.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Objects and morphisms of $\Desc(\mathfrak R)$ are finite compatible families.  Identities and composition are componentwise, and the pseudofunctor coherence equations preserve compatibility.  This direct construction has the pseudocone universal property.

A descent morphism is invertible if and only if every component is invertible.  Componentwise inverses satisfy the overlap equations obtained by inverting the original equations.  Therefore the maximal subgroupoid of the descent category equals the descent groupoid of the chartwise cores.

A pseudonatural transformation acts on objects, overlap comparisons, and morphisms componentwise; its coherence cells supply the target cocycle laws.  Pointwise equivalences have pointwise quasi-inverses and hence induce an equivalence on descent categories.

For theorem transport, the equational realization principle transports the proof to every chart because every constructor used by the proof is preserved.  Bridge naturality makes the transported equalities compatible on overlaps.  Equality in the descent category is componentwise.  No unverified analytic, topological, positivity, finiteness, convergence, or integrality hypothesis is created by this argument.

Invariant descent is the same pseudonatural-transformation construction.  If descended invariants are isomorphic, local completeness supplies local isomorphisms.  The added isomorphism-effectivity assumption glues them to a global isomorphism.  Without that assumption, holonomy, a cokernel class, a Brauer class, or a higher coherence defect may obstruct the gluing.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Exact status]
The theorem is conditional but proved.  The remaining Komorebi problem is to construct the profile-indexed resolved envelope, define the actual chart and overlap categories, verify the bridge naturality and higher coherence equations, and prove effectivity for a selected heterogeneous atlas.
\end{remark}

\section{Verified finite model cases}

\begin{proposition}[Rational affine-Mahler chart groupoid]
Fix $d\ge0$.  In characteristic zero, the affine Mahler coefficient maps $A_{m,s}^{(d)}$ are invertible and satisfy
\[
A_{n,t}^{(d)}A_{m,s}^{(d)}=A_{mn,mt+s}^{(d)}.
\]
For coprime $m,n$, the two paths around every declared Chinese-remainder diamond are equal.  Hence any finite diagram generated by these charts is a coherent finite chart groupoid with trivial holonomy on those diamonds.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Integral split Mahler packet descent]
Let $P_{m,S}^{(d)}:\mathbb Z^{d+1}\to\mathbb Z^{|S|(d+1)}$ have an integer left inverse $D$.  Then, with $A=P_{m,S}^{(d)}$, $L=D$, and $\Pi=AL$,
\[
LA=I,\qquad \Pi^2=\Pi,\qquad
\mathbb Z^{|S|(d+1)}=\operatorname{im}A\oplus\ker L.
\]
Thus the source chart and the normalized packet core $\operatorname{im}A$ are equivalent, while $\ker L$ is a named residual.  For the prefix of length
\[
L_{\mathbb Z}(m,d)=\min\bigl(d+1,B(m)\bigr),
\qquad
B(m)=\max\bigl(\{1\}\cup\{p^{v_p(m)}:p\mid m\}\bigr),
\]
Core-Transfer Addendum 04 proves existence and cardinality minimality of such a decoder.  The split identity survives every base change.
\end{proposition}
