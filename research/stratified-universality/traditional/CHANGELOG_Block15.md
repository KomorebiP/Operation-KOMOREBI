## Clarified after proof audit

- Residue projectors are the direct additive realization of the theorem.
  The factorial step-product is an exact multiplicative companion transported
  through a specified multilinear multiplication map; it is not an additive
  idempotent splitting.
- Complete changes of lane resolution are lossless coordinate equivalences;
  loss begins only after a selector, quotient, statistic, summary, or fold.
- Ordinary global lanes require trivial address holonomy. With nontrivial
  holonomy, only invariant orbit sums or a separately defined local-system
  lane doctrine can be expected to descend.
- Kernel descent and split-losslessness are separated: compatible left
  inverses descend directly, while kernels require explicit preservation and
  coherent comparison data.
