## Decisive evidence and corrected inherited versions

The rank theorem uses algebraic `V_w^T`, not a conjugate transpose. Full Vandermonde column rank, an invertible diagonal and a surjective right factor establish rank exactly. This avoids an unjustified inference from an arbitrary product of full-rank matrices.

For supplied noisy order selection, the exact gate is `epsilon < t < sigma_r(H0) - epsilon`, with `epsilon < sigma_r(H0)/2`. Rank zero and full rectangular rank are handled separately. The native largest-ratio rule is not implied by this theorem. The manuscript's valid periodic three-jump witness has nodes `(1,i,-1)`, real amplitudes `(100,-99,-1)` summing to zero and a four-by-four Hankel starting at index four. Adding moment noise `(1/10)(-i)^n` changes its singular values from `(400,396,4,0)` to `(400,396,4,2/5)`. The perturbation norm is `2/5 < 4/2`, yet the largest ratio selects order two while a supplied threshold in `(2/5,18/5)` selects order three. The dedicated current suite also reproduces the native rectangular implementation's failure.

The root theorem requires simple distinct roots, same-degree monic polynomials, disjoint disks and strict domination on each whole boundary. Its explicit upper bound has weights `(|z_l|+rho)^k`; its lower bound is `rho` times the product of pairwise separations minus `rho`. These are true-model conditional quantities. Normalization gives chord error at most `2rho` and angular error at most `2 arcsin(rho)` when `rho<1`; a disk radius is not itself an angular error. Repeated-root individual Lipschitz stability is not claimed.

The corrected periodic mean-zero edge bound is

$$
\|j-\widehat j\|_1\le \frac T4\sum_\ell|a_\ell-\widehat a_\ell|+2\sum_\ell|\widehat a_\ell|d_\ell(1-d_\ell/T).
$$

It follows directly from the sawtooth's L1 norm `T/4` and translation cost `2d(1-d/T)`. For `j=1_[0,T/2)-1/2` and `jhat=1_[T/4,T/2)-1/4`, the exact error is `3T/8`, refuting the inherited coefficient-one bound `T/4`. The corrected location term is attained. Fourier truncation's L1 norm grows logarithmically, and SG-38 retains that growth. Estimated amplitudes are made real and zero-sum before canonical synthesis, preserving their conditional error order.

The collision witness compares two distinct admissible fixed-order packets, not an undefined location metric at zero order. The self-calibration budget is a sufficient `3L_max+1` clean high coefficients plus low reconstruction modes, under a supplied noncolliding decimation. The conditional rates use selected scales with uniform conditioning and strict branch margin; tail decay alone establishes only the moment-defect rate.

Ideal orthogonal refitting and the native positive-ridge refit are separated. Ridge obeys `M^T r=lambda c`; even the full pixel basis leaves `lambda*y/(1+lambda)`. Clipping is nonlinear, with truth-error contraction only for truth in the declared box. Complete residual storage is exact because it retains whatever the approximation missed. These facts do not establish sparse adaptive convergence or recovery from fewer measurements.
