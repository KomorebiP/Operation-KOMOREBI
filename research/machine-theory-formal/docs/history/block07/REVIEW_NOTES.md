## Mathematical review

The state support is finite and nonempty. Permission and terminal acceptance must hold uniformly on that support. Reports are functions of the pre-action state; the successor support is their filtered image through the declared transition. Origin information is not recovered by resetting the current state.

A literal plan has one root experiment and optional child trees indexed by reports. It cannot select the root using the hidden case. `Valid` requires exactly the possible branches and rejects both missing live children and invented impossible children. The terminal must satisfy every fixed recipient on every remaining case.

Online resource coordinates are nonnegative integers. Maxima are taken separately by coordinate, while complete storage is summed across all present branches. `profile_records` identifies that storage with an independent structural count. `execution_sound` bounds primitive modeled executions when terminal charges are conservative. `worst_coordinate_attained` proves exact maxima when terminal charges are exact support maxima; it does not assert one case attains all coordinates simultaneously.

The recurrence is proved sound and lower-cofinal in all valid tree profiles. Those properties establish exact Pareto minimality and joint-budget equivalence. Every retained profile has a complete witness. The proof does not use separate resource minima or omit unsuccessful possible branches. `compute_eq_solve` connects the computable finite recurrence to the mathematical one.

The source tradeoff is checked by the actual recurrence at depth3. A finite table covering every four-state support is checked to be a backup fixed point. General induction plus horizon padding then proves the same two-point frontier for every horizon3+n and excludes the fictitious(3,5) budget at every finite horizon. This is stronger than finite-depth testing but does not promote Core5.3's separate general leaf-count theorem.

The sensor retains the inherited eight worlds and the two exact installed XOR recipients. The terminal acceptance table is proved equivalent to their unchanged acceptance predicate. One display observation, two total recipient arithmetic operations and one broadcast bit yield hard online coordinates(1,2,1) and three stored records. Model work units and literal records are not elapsed time, peak host memory or program bytes.
