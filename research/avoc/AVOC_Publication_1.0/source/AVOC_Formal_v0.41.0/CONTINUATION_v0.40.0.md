## Closed mathematical obligation

Given a finite rational primitive conditional source law for each identity, a
fixed finite schedule and deterministic quote memberships, the explicit reveal
process and optional planner have unconditional uncertified-issuance mass at
most the total allocated error. Binomial count and observed-prefix premises are
derived. Shared identity histories and arbitrary outcome-dependent traces and
selectors are supported. The subsequent episode keeps its quote bands fixed.

## Exact remaining gaps

1. Real-process applicability: stationarity, accepted-source independence,
   fault interpretation, source/outcome authenticity, and completeness of logs.
   The one-attempt retention theorem alone is not a universal multi-attempt
   process refinement. The Python oracle tests an exact bounded family only.
2. A universal adapter refinement that combines operation-ID freshness, replay,
   unresolved intents, and observation generation into the reveal machine.
   Fresh checked/fault steps are proved; arbitrary host logs are not.
3. Updating bands during an episode and accumulating reward/error/accounting
   across episodes. The present policy guarantee is frozen within each episode.
4. Empirical setup/gross cost and savings bands, actual workload measurements,
   and calibration against authenticated real service runs.
5. Wider artifact workloads, robust storage/authentication, and universal host
   correctness. The current propositional, Boolean, and affine backends retain
   their defined scope.
6. Arbitrary real parameters, infinite horizons or stopping schedules, drift,
   sharper intervals, and computational scaling.

A concrete next formal block is a recorder/reveal refinement with unique
operation IDs and durable intent gates, followed by a fixed finite multi-episode
error/accounting composition. It must preserve no-retry and exact-identity rules.
Data collection or real paid operations require an actual authorized workload;
none occurred in v0.40.
