## New obstruction requiring attention

initiation-probability interpretation when outcomes affect completion order.
With two fair initiation outcomes, success-first completion makes the first
stored outcome succeed with probability 3/4. With 20 fair attempts, first 10
success-first completed reports can exclude 1/2 at nominal allowance 1/10 on an
event of mass 308333/524288. This is exact finite review evidence, outside the
source-generative assumption. Preserve it when proposing a physical coupling.
