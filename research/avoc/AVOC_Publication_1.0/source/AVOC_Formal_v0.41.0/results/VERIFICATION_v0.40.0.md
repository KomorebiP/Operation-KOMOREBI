## Counterexamples preserved

Lean proves that equal marginals do not imply independent/binomial outcomes,
that success-only consuming censoring biases a fair attempt parameter, and
that a predictable completion gate can destroy the conditional binomial law.

The actual interval procedure also fails if its nominal error bound is asserted
conditional on completion: in a separate exact 1,024-path example, conditional
miscoverage is 1/2 for allowance 1/10, while unconditional bad issuance is 1/1024.
The completed-interval example is executable evidence, not an extra Lean claim.

## Remaining gaps

authenticated complete logs and universal recorder/host refinement; changes of
bands during an episode and cumulative reward/error/cost accounting; empirical
cost/savings and real workload validation; broader artifacts; real parameters,
drift, unrestricted stopping, and scalable/tighter methods.

The one-attempt censoring theorem is separate from the multi-attempt source
assumption. Fresh recorder-to-reveal steps require freshness and next-bit
agreement. No universal Python/SQLite refinement or completion-rate guarantee
is claimed. Quotes remain fixed within each policy episode, and the inherited
regret comparator uses conservative upper-setup reservations.
