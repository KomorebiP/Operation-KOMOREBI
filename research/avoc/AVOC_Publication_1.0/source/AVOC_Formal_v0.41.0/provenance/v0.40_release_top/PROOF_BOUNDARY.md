# Exact proof boundary — AVOC v0.40.0

## New kernel-checked chain

Primitive conditional Bernoulli source laws imply product-vector probabilities,
binomial prefix counts, and finite scheduled calibration. A recursive reveal
machine preserves per-identity report prefixes through arbitrary allocation and
fault steps. Completed fixed prefixes feed an optional quote planner. Its
unconditional bad-issuance probability is at most the total allocated error
budget; every plan inside the good event satisfies the inherited frozen-episode
certificate with specified true probabilities and deterministic values.

Count-law and observed-prefix correspondences are derived in the final
shared_stream theorems. They are not supplied as additional premises there.
Multiple checkpoints share actual per-identity report lists. No independence
between identities or checkpoint counts is required by this formal bound.

Kernel-checked obstructions cover outcome-dependent consuming censoring,
completion-conditioned binomial laws, and the insufficiency of equal marginals.
The larger interval-specific completion counterexamples are exact computations.

## Remaining supplied assumptions

1. The finite latent source for each identity satisfies the stated conditional
   fresh-outcome law with a stationary rational p. Neither a parsed log nor the
   theorem proves that an external service has this law.
2. Report generation follows the reveal model: next outcomes cannot be skipped,
   checked rejection consumes a source position, and a fault consumes no latent
   accepted report. A fault's actual operational meaning needs separate evidence.
   The separate equal-retention attempt theorem does not by itself establish
   the multi-attempt accepted-stream model.
3. The finite schedule, sample sizes, grids, and error allocations are fixed
   before the data used by them. Historical precommitment is not authenticated.
   Warm/cold selectors must select the prescribed true probability strata.
4. Identity/outcome labels, complete logs, and deterministic setup/gross band
   memberships are supplied. Real provider provenance, empirical cost/savings
   calibration, and authentication remain outside the proof.
5. Quotes are frozen within each policy episode. No cumulative cross-episode
   accounting or in-episode band update theorem is added.
6. The formal probabilities are rational and the probability spaces finite.
   No arbitrary real-parameter, infinite-stream, or drift guarantee is claimed.

## Exact interpretation of the conclusion

Coverage is an outer probability over data and process outcomes. It is not a
posterior confidence claim for the particular observed interval. The result is
P(issue and uncertified) <= total alpha. Conditioning on issue/completion can
invalidate that bound; the counterexample is part of this release. The result
also holds if the process never issues a plan, so it gives no completion-rate,
performance, or productivity guarantee.

Inside the good event, regret is an expected finite-horizon policy-value
difference under a fixed compatible realization. It is not realized sample-path
loss. The comparator retains conservative upper-setup reservation guards;
runtime spent counts those reservations. There is no automatic refund or
identity with physical spending, and no exact-cost enlarged-action-set claim.

## Operational refinement boundary

Fresh checked/fault recorder operations are related to reveal transitions by
one-step theorems. Replay/conflict handling is inherited, and must gate whether
a fresh reveal step runs. The machine's trace itself has no operation identifiers.
There is no universal refinement of arbitrary Python/SQLite logs to the trace,
and supplied next-bit agreement is not authenticated from a real observation.
The exact oracle tests a finite family of adapter interactions only.

The new formal planner is optional and requires both selected samples. The
existing executable also blocks unresolved intents before refresh. This release
does not merge or weaken those gates or authorize automatic paid retries.

## Evidence and runtime trust

Every explicit named theorem/lemma is included in the exact axiom audit, including
inherited private helpers. Only propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound are
allowed. Current local project source is rebuilt with warnings as errors;
external dependency caches are pin checked rather than rebuilt from all source.
Lean/compiler/kernel, Python, SQLite, OS, and protected filesystem remain trusted.
No alternative kernel, hostile rollback defense, or power-loss proof is added.

Independent exact finite computations compare latent-table and attempt-tree
models, calibration arithmetic, and actual parser behavior. Those executions do
not establish universal adapter correctness or empirical stationarity.

arbitrary Lean/mathlib proof ingestion. Boolean and affine backends have their
stated finite/declared-cost scope; no universal program-minimization claim follows.
Representation theorems characterize sufficiency rather than discover encodings.
Bayesian posterior confidence does not itself admit a proof artifact.

v0.38 example descriptions remain valid and preserved. All inherited non-audit
