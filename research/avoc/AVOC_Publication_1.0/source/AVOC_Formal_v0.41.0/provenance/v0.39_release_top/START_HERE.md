exact finite-sample probability intervals, finite-schedule coverage, and a proved
learned-band-to-frozen-policy guarantee, plus executable calibration and replanning.

results/VERIFICATION_v0.39.0.md summarizes fresh evidence and inherited test repairs.
examples/calibration/README.md specifies the log interface and statistical assumptions.
