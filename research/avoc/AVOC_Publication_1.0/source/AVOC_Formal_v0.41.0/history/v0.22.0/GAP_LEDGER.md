## G20: costed retry decisions

CLOSED FOR THE DECLARED SINGLE-OPERATION PERSISTENT-RELIABILITY MODEL.

The previous paid-retry ledger bounded costs but did not optimize retries. The
new executable advisor computes the model's exact finite-horizon optimal value.
A proved failure-posterior variance inequality yields a cheaper horizon-independent
threshold for action selection under the persistent-type hypotheses. Resource
eligibility is connected to the existing paid-attempt gate; the scientific
checkpoint is unchanged by retry advice/application.

Still open: real likelihood/benefit calibration, full physical costs, repair/backoff
or alternative modes, optimal handling of operational/scientific correlations,

fault-inclusive research-project performance.
