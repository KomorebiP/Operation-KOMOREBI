# AVOC v0.25.0 — targeted gap update

No already-closed theorem scope is reset. Prior ledgers are retained under history.

## G29. Bounded candidate generation and protected promotion

**FIRST CONCRETE GENERATED-PROGRAM PROPOSER CLOSED IN A RESTRICTED GRAMMAR.**

The accepted candidate is now an enumerated AST rather than one of three supplied
complete named versions. The checker is sound and complete over all three-Boolean
inputs. Search terminates at the stated grammar/prefix/budget bounds, never degrades
node count or semantics, and achieves the examined-pool minimum. Generation and every
examined candidate are charged. An actual instance of the old prepaid upgrade gate
checks and promotes the generated AST, which is persisted and invoked after reopening.

Remaining: scalable synthesis/deduplication, useful new languages, candidate proof
construction beyond exhaustive finite semantics, and evidence for benefits on workloads
not built around these known Boolean identities.

## G28. Durable paid promotion history

**EXTENDED TO GENERATED ASTS IN A NEW ONE-SHOT PROTECTED CAMPAIGN.**

The generated-program authority is fixed by the reference and declared workload. The
SQLite wrapper reserves its envelope before generation and publishes the checked journal,
charges and active AST in one result transaction. Incomplete search retains its envelope
and cannot dispatch. Reopening invokes the preserved program after replay checking.

This does not port the complete earlier retry/recovery protocol to synthesis or migrate
older stores. Arbitrary source deployment, protected-store authenticity, complete physical
costs and universal filesystem refinement remain open.

## G25/G26. Contract-preserving improvement

**PRESERVED; AN ACTUALLY GENERATED AST NOW INSTANTIATES THE AUTHORITY.**

The complete candidate semantics are checked, not selected from a whitelist. The retry
adapter transports equivalence through precomputed decision flags under NoRevival.
This is not a newly discovered threshold rule, nor a proved speedup over the existing
native threshold. Lower expression visits are a restricted metric.

## G27. Recursive improvement of the improver

**STILL OPEN.**

The deterministic enumerator does not alter itself, and no LLM is used. No controlled
multi-generation experiment shows an accepted upgrade improving the next improvement
round relative to a frozen proposer. Persistent generated-program dispatch is progress
on the mechanism, not evidence for that stronger feedback claim.

## G20/G21. Net resources and empirical usefulness

**GENERATION/TRIAL TARIFFS NOW INCLUDED; FULL PHYSICAL-COST OPTIMALITY STILL OPEN.**

The smaller-program negative-net example shows that semantic correctness and component
improvement do not prove profitable search. The exact tariff ledger exposes search
costs, but omits many real execution costs. Call counts and utility are declared data.
No production LLM calibration, autonomous discovery benchmark or novel-algorithm claim.

## Other interfaces

Arbitrary Lean/mathlib artifact backends, dependency-bearing live scientific jobs,
provenance replay, learned uncertainty bounds and the broader empirical program remain
at their previous boundaries. This release neither closes nor reopens them.

## Next focused decision

Use a concrete workload to choose between (a) proving and measuring a deduplicating or
counterexample-guided proposer against the frozen enumerator under a complete matched
budget, and (b) extending the certificate language to a genuinely useful component.
Keep the equivalence checker, workload and promotion authority fixed during that test.
Do not call a single enumeration/promotion run recursive improvement.
