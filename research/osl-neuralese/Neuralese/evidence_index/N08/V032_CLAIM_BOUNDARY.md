## Design limits that matter

1. **Adaptive-developmental class.** The scientific profile was prospectively
   frozen, but the endpoints arise from an adaptive research lineage. This is
   not an independent confirmatory replication.
2. **Two checkpoints.** Sixteen coordinate episodes do not create sixteen
   independently trained models. They are useful deterministic coordinate
   probes, not population units or held-out task-family support.
3. **Finite cones.** The cone module stops at history length 8 and suffix
   horizon 8. A positive finite clearance is not global closure.
4. **Finite long-path transversal.** Relation and matched claims use the
   registered 64-case base transversal per episode and suffixes only through
   `H=8`, not every recurrent state, prehistory, or continuation.
5. **Deterministic control bank.** There are no sampled-word p-values or
   word-population claims. Sixty aliases correspond to 54 distinct words.
6. **Imperfect feature equality.** The 48 controls are exact-length and
   best-score selected, not fully feature-matched or exchangeable.
7. **Cross-endpoint controls.** Only the distinct identity control holds the
   semantic endpoint fixed. `R²`, `FR`, and `FR³` change endpoints, so `X0` and
   `X3` cannot isolate path mechanism from endpoint or decoder-facet difficulty.
8. **Spectral diagnostics.** Primitive spectra are ambient mechanistic
   candidates. They do not prove orbit-wise behavior or quotient stability.
9. **Lineage context.** Results using the older episode seeds 930000/931000 are
   exploratory context. The fresh 940000/941000 endpoints remain
   adaptive-developmental and nonconfirmatory.

## Predicate semantics

- Integrity and eligibility gates are validity checks.
- `H0`–`H3` and `X0`–`X4` are scientific outcomes, not pass/fail gates.
- `H0` and `H1` are complements.
- `X4` is exactly `X0 and (X2 or X3)`; it is false because `X0` is false.
- `full_finite_factorization=true` is a preregistered composite, not a
  conjunction requiring every `H` and `X` predicate to be true.
- `H2` requires one exit somewhere in each checkpoint × episode-half stratum;
  it does not require every episode to exit.
- “Replicated” for `X1`–`X3` means the condition holds in both checkpoint
  aggregates, not in every episode and not with a population p-value.
- Registered severity is `[failure cases, reveal cases, −minimum margin]` in
  lexicographic descending order. Strict-error count is not in this tuple.

## Execution provenance

The v0.3.1 bootstrap read checkpoint raw bytes solely for SHA-256
authentication. It aborted before its run-start ledger, checkpoint
deserialization, and endpoint execution. The v0.3.2 wrapper correction
preserved the frozen scientific profile. Its single execution produced the
registered ledger and result.

One reproducibility caveat remains: exact spectral unique-dominance and grouped
ray tolerances are represented in frozen code and outcomes, but are not both
serialized numerically under `relations.design`. Reproduction therefore
requires the frozen source, not the result JSON alone.
