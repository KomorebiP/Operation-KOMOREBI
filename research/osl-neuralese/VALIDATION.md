This record separates (1) manuscript derivations, (2) current executable checks, (3) historical scientific outcomes, and (4) archive/build integrity. ## Source preservation

`SOURCE_LEDGER.json` binds 15 original archives, 85,794,776 bytes in total. The initial recursive audit inspected 26 archives including nested archives, checked their CRCs, and found no standalone font binaries. The sources were not reserialized or altered. Selected reading capsules are copied byte-for-byte and linked to exact archive members.

The final edition manifest binds all delivered files except the manifest and its external digest file. Verification after clean ZIP extraction is documented in the delivery validation record supplied beside the package. The manifest and hashes establish consistency with this edition; they are not cryptographic author authentication.

## Manuscripts and layout

The final manuscripts are 18 pages (OSL), 21 pages (Neuralese), and 8 pages (visual/verification guide). They compile with `latexmk`/pdfLaTeX. The final logs contain no undefined references, missing characters, overfull or underfull boxes, or LaTeX warnings.

The first guide build failed because a line-break command was mistyped as a math delimiter. A later guide diagram exceeded the text width. The line break was corrected and the diagram repositioned. Narrow table columns were changed to ragged-right layout to remove stretched word spacing. The final PDFs were rendered for inspection; detailed page/font/text-bound results are in `validation/pdf_preflight.json`.

## Environment

Python 3.13.5; NumPy 2.3.5; pytest 9.0.2; PyTorch 2.10.0+cpu; Linux x86-64. All fresh work was CPU-side. The fresh checks do not establish replay equivalence on the user's Windows GPU environment. No external pretrained-model checkpoint was downloaded.

## What did not happen

No neural retraining, new population-level replication, learned-bank consumption, repeated one-shot endpoint, production deployment, public repository push, license reassignment, or Lean proof check occurred. These limits are part of the edition's status, not hidden qualifications to an undifferentiated PASS.
