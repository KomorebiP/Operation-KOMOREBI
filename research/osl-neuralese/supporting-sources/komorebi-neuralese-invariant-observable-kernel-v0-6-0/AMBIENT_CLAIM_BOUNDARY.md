# Ambient packet claim boundary

## Developmental target

The strongest result this diagnostic lineage could eventually support is:

> In the declared finite operator task and continuation interface, independently separated ambient projective states form robust task-relative fibers whose heldout causal continuations agree, whose successors are representative-independent, and whose semantics survive preregistered null-loop, switched-dynamics, off-manifold, and cross-context transport challenges.

This would be evidence for a finite-audit semiconjugacy

\[
\pi_c(\widehat M_{c,g}[h])=g\cdot\pi_c([h]),
\]

not evidence that raw activations are identical or that every null path returns to one exact ray.

## Retrospective and calibration evidence

Version 0.2 established or diagnosed the following before this preregistration:

- positive scale is an exact gauge for the bias-free linear recurrence
- normalized recurrence separates radial overflow from angular drift
- the centered decoder is instantaneously rank seven
- finite future observability is full ambient rank eight
- no fixed nonzero additive future-null subspace was detected
- projective terminal training greatly improved long-depth structured execution
- learned null cycles could retain labels while accumulating ray displacement
- an engineered exact semantic chart solves the finite task with bounded dynamics

These observations motivated the ambient-packet hypothesis. Read-only calibration on the same two inherited checkpoints also informed the v0.3 metrics and thresholds. Consequently, v0.3 is mechanistic development evidence rather than an outcome-independent prospective test.

## Claims explicitly withheld

No finite packet experiment can establish:

- an infinite-horizon equivalence class
- observer-independent or task-independent meaning
- a fixed additive nuisance subspace when future observability is full rank
- a smooth semantic manifold merely because a decision cell has interior
- harmless holonomy from endpoint label agreement alone
- switched stability from individual spectral radii or bounded normalized norms
- global ambient algebra from behavior on state anchors alone
- abstract operator discovery from a supplied transition-table task alone
- spontaneous Neuralese from an operator-shaped or normalized architecture
- architecture-independent emergence
- the mechanism of a frontier language model
- awareness, experience, intention, or consciousness

## Four distinctions that must remain visible

### Argmax packets versus additive nullspaces

Full future-observability rank rules out a permanently invisible nonzero linear direction under equality-sensitive observation. It does not prevent multiple states from sharing the same argmax sequence. Argmax equivalence defines open task-relative decision cells, not a decoder-null vector space.

### Finite signatures versus semantic congruence

Two states can agree for every suffix through depth \(K\) and diverge at \(K+1\). A finite packet is therefore a candidate cell. A quotient operator is supported only when different representatives map into the same successor class on disjoint, longer continuation banks.

### Intra-cell motion versus harmless holonomy

A null loop may move a ray inside a broad cell and still preserve the current label. It is harmless only relative to the declared interface and only while every allowed future continuation remains equivalent with positive robust margin. Systematic drift that eventually crosses a continuation boundary is a failure, even if short-loop endpoint accuracy is perfect.

### Exact execution versus learned algebra

The exact semantic chart, lookup-table executor, and nearest-anchor reset model can all produce excellent task behavior. The exact chart is a positive control. Lookup and reset systems are mechanistic baselines. None demonstrate that an abstract ambient algebra emerged from terminal supervision.

## Invalidating observations

The corresponding interpretation must be withdrawn or narrowed if:

- same-discovery-packet states disagree on frozen heldout suffixes
- applying one generator makes the successor class depend on the chosen representative
- nontrivially separated same-packet states cannot be constructed
- packet inradius collapses as continuation horizon increases
- a delayed-observability coordinate returns after the construction horizon
- repeated null loops reduce margin to zero or alter a later continuation
- adversarial switching amplifies angular error beyond the available packet margin
- operator images approach zero so the projective map becomes ill-conditioned or undefined
- correctness is confined to anchors and fails under certified off-manifold perturbations
- context transport fails on heldout states, operators, conjugacies, or families
- shuffled or random controls perform comparably to the claimed transport
- a reset or lookup model satisfies every metric used to claim emergent algebra
- the claimed margin can be arbitrarily inflated by changing decoder temperature or latent coordinates

## What a pass would and would not mean

A complete developmental pass would justify saying that the learned system has robust finite-audit semantic fibers for this task. It would strengthen the case that its useful state is an equivalence class of causal continuations rather than a unique raw vector.

It would still leave three open problems:

1. whether the nested finite packets converge to nontrivial infinite-horizon fibers
2. whether their structure persists when the explicit permutation-table scaffold is weakened
3. whether the same quotient organization emerges in generic recurrent or pretrained architectures

Any failed gate remains part of the result. It may localize the semantics to a shorter horizon, narrower neighborhood, structured family, or anchor manifold without erasing the causal execution already established in v0.2.
