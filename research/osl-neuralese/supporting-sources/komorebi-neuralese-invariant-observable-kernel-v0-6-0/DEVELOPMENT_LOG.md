## Numerical audit correction

The first stored observability report centered the float32 decoder before promoting it to float64. The rounding residue made the centered decoder appear rank eight even though common-logit invariance limits it to rank seven. Version 0.2.0 now promotes before centering and uses a relative singular-value tolerance. The centered decoder is rank seven, while the finite future-observability stack remains full rank eight. The scientific conclusion—no detected fixed additive future-null packet—therefore remains unchanged.

## v0.6.0 invariant-observable-kernel development

The v0.5 posthoc spectrum had its decisive gap after seven directions, not
eight. This is not an arbitrary cutoff: centered eight-class logits have exact
rank seven. The v0.6 geometry was therefore frozen as

\[
H=S_7\oplus L_1\oplus K_4,
\]

where \(L\) is the common-logit gauge and \(K\) is the typed ambient fiber.
The total centered-observation kernel has rank five.

An initial design proposed learning a global orthogonal frame inside an exact
block model. Adversarial review showed that this frame cancels from terminal
behavior and is unidentifiable pure gauge. The implementation instead uses a
fixed seeded dense frame. The ambient operator encoder is separately seeded
and frozen; its curvature is a behaviorally inert calibration, not learned
memory.

The inherited anchor-conditioning objective exposed a second issue. Seven
centered dimensions alone cannot contain eight orthogonal state anchors. Each
hard-model anchor now uses the exact homogeneous completion

\[
h_i=\sqrt{7/8}\,s_i+1/\sqrt8\,u_L,
\]

with zero \(K\) component. When the \(s_i\) form a regular simplex, the full
anchors are orthonormal and the common component remains behaviorally null.

The separate learned-intertwiner arm leaves the full \(O(12)\) recurrence
unrestricted and learns a row-orthonormal \(7\times12\) map only through
explicit centered-simplex Gram, centered-readout, intertwining, and induced
semantic relation losses. The Gram objective constrains
\(B^\top\Pi^\top\Pi B\) without selecting a basis in the observed quotient.
The observer is initialized only from the instantaneous centered-readout
rowspace, without operators, targets, or continuations. It cannot support a
spontaneous-emergence claim.

A seed-73 forty-step integration probe found that the hard split already had
rank seven, one-step leakage near \(10^{-7}\), and suffix leakage near
\(10^{-6}\). The learned-intertwiner model correctly failed extraction at
that early point: its suffix observer was still rank twelve and its
bidirectional leakage remained near one. This was a pipeline diagnostic, not
an efficacy result.

A subsequent seed-887 single-arm development diagnostic exposed two flaws
before sealing a screen. First, the inherited per-step projective
normalization allowed an injected null component to change centered-logit
scale even when raw linear products did not leak. Second, entrywise MSE
normalization concealed near-unit spectral leakage. All four polar arms now
share a direct linear rollout, which is equivalent on natural orthogonal
trajectories, and the quotient losses are dimensionless and bidirectional.
Seed 887 is consequently marked touched; the replacement untouched screen
seed is 947.

Before touching replacement seed 947, objective review found that the v0.6
relation auxiliaries still used only the first accepted episode in each
training batch. That single-context estimator could confound algebraic pressure
with one episode's visible coordinate realization. The sealed design now uses
the first eight accepted rows without replacement at every step, paired across
all six arms. Each row supplies all nine anonymous relation cells, for 72
context-cell observations per step. Losses are means over cells and contexts,
not sums, so the correction reduces coordinate-sampling variance without
changing the intended coefficient scale. This correction was made before any
run of seed 947; the executable plan identifier advanced from `dev1` to `dev2`.

### Final dev2 rehearsal and stop decision

The complete dev2 recipe was frozen at 3,200 steps for every evidence-bearing
arm, with all v0.6 auxiliaries held at zero through completed step 800 and
ramped to full weight at completed step 1,600. The learned-intertwiner weights
were fixed at 0.1 for basis-free anchor geometry, 1.0 for centered-readout
containment, 10.0 for bidirectional intertwining, and 0.3 for anonymous
semantic relations. No registered threshold was relaxed.

Exactly one final implementation rehearsal was then run on the already retired
seed 887 for the two decisive arms. This was a non-branching check: its stated
purpose was to catch implementation failure and adjudicate whether the hard
calibration was adequate, not to select another coefficient.

The learned-intertwiner arm preserved perfect robust accuracy through depth 128
and reached 0.8203125 at depth 512. Its local measurements were encouraging:
centered-readout leakage was 0.003077, generator-leakage p95 was 0.020217, and
intertwining p95 was 0.009937. The finite-horizon audit nevertheless rejected
the proposed quotient. The sealed suffix observer remained rank 12 with
\(\sigma_8/\sigma_1=0.049954\); length-64 leakage had median 0.112718, p95
0.356866, and maximum 0.655633. Thus a good one-step intertwiner did not create
a fixed forward-invariant future-null space.

The hard-kernel semantic arm cleanly isolated the registered
\(7\oplus1\oplus4\) split: generator leakage was at most
\(1.86\times10^{-7}\), length-64 leakage had median
\(1.08\times10^{-6}\), and the suffix observer had rank seven with
\(\sigma_8/\sigma_1=4.11\times10^{-7}\). Its ambient fiber remained strongly
curved (median defect 1.3743), as intended. However, the learned semantic
connection was not flat enough: median/p95 semantic defect was
0.05110/0.08794, depth-128 robust accuracy was 0.85547, and depth-512 robust
accuracy was 0.39844. This fails the frozen hard-calibration minima and
maxima.

Accordingly, v0.6 stopped as `CALIBRATION_FAILURE_STOP_BEFORE_SCREEN`. Seed
947 remains untouched, no v0.6 screen or pilot was run, and the frozen weights
will not be tuned. The failure localizes the next problem: the ambient exact
sequence and radial isometry can be correct while small quotient connection
errors accumulate as angular/holonomy drift. A later version must enforce or
derive representation-level composition, rather than rely on another scan of
local residual weights.

A posthoc no-training diagnostic then separated construction from learning.
The exact quotient lift and the quotient-curved ambient lift both retained
1.0000 robust accuracy at depths 512, 4,096, and 16,384 on 128 retired-seed
episodes per depth, with zero boundary decisions. The quotient-curved control
therefore demonstrates directly that large ambient path curvature is compatible
with indefinitely stable task semantics when the quotient action is an exact
representation. This diagnostic did not reopen the frozen v0.6 screen; it
localizes the observed failure to approximate quotient functoriality.
