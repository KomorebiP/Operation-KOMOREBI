# Claim boundary — v0.4.0

Version 0.4 is a prospective developmental fresh-seed paired pilot. Its
architecture, objective panel, workloads, seeds, deterministic evaluation-bank
recipes, and gates are frozen before the official pilot is run. The concrete
evaluation banks are reproducibly materialized from those sealed recipes only
after the checkpoint panel is serialized and hashed. The workload and
thresholds were informed by development, so this is not an outcome-independent
confirmatory study.

## Claims a successful primary pilot may support

Subject to execution and assay validity, a complete primary pass may support
the bounded statement that:

- the hard-polar `polar_cr` model preserves positive held-out D4 continuation
  margin and packet membership at the registered prefixes;
- its hard-isometric recurrent action remains defined under the finite
  exhaustive switched-word assay;
- it meets the registered D4 depth-128/256/512 execution curve;
- its hidden state and operator channel pass matched causal patch tests; and
- the paired \(2\times2\) panel reports per-metric contrasts for outcomes whose
  four finite cells are all defined; an undefined metric remains explicitly
  unavailable rather than suppressing other valid contrasts.

Hard-polar isometry is a mathematical fact of the architecture, up to audited
floating-point error. It must never be described as learned stability.
Likewise, successful D4 behavior would show finite tested closure, not an
infinite-horizon theorem.

The held-out packet bank is an endpoint-action firewall, not a fully
length-unseen split. CR arms receive unlabeled finite-return structural rollouts
at lengths 2, 4, and 8, although terminal supervision, construction endpoint
supervision, and composition scales stop at four. Every scheduled return path
and nonempty prefix remains outside the two reserved endpoint-action groups.

## Separately reported outcomes

C8 and mixed-cycle accuracy are zero-shot cross-family transfer tests. Their
status is secondary and does not enter the primary D4 closure claim.

Ambient-\(Q\) attribution is descriptive and causal. \(Q\) is the rank-four
Euclidean complement of the learned anchor plane in the ideal float64
construction and is separately checked after the float32 cast actually used by
the intervention. The matched random control is compared against the float32
\(Q\) removal using the two actually removed vectors before normalization;
ideal construction error is bounded by \(10^{-8}\), while applied projector and
energy-match error is bounded by \(10^{-6}\). Energy
in \(Q\) does not establish computational use. Undefined or nonfinite causal
rollouts cannot support attribution, and a failure to outperform the
rank- and energy-matched random ablation does not invalidate projective
closure.

The CR-objective effect is a separate paired descriptive result. Its registered
direction checks use the hard-polar-stratum simple effect
`polar_cr - polar_supervised`, not a CR main effect averaged across both
architectures. The estimands use the worst packet membership, tenth-percentile
margin, and null closure across the full audit grid for each seed. Null closure is the
minimum of baseline packet membership, conditional retention of baseline
members, and absolute post-loop membership, preventing turnover from hiding
erosion. With three
seeds, the sign rules are developmental direction checks, not inferential
evidence or population-level effect estimates. CR benefit is not required for
the primary `polar_cr` closure status.

State and operator causal specificity is evaluated only among same-bank cases
where the native rollout is correct and the counterfactual target is distinct;
all relevant outputs must be finite. It is evaluated against two controls each.
The state controls match recipient angle on the unit sphere or remain on a
wrong reachable same-prefix state. The hard-polar operator controls preserve
relative operator conjugacy geometry or come from an independent encoder
context using a visible slot balanced by frozen bank-row index and flipped only
to exclude exact concrete-table collision with either the original or the main
alternate. Relative
operator geometry does not guarantee a matched state-space effect size, and the
independent control is not distance matched, so no stronger claim is made.

The archived eleven-arm learned-metric screen is developmental evidence. It
is not pooled with the official four-arm panel and cannot satisfy a pilot
gate. The tested learned-\(G\) formulation failed developmentally; that is not
a general impossibility result for learned metrics.

## Claims withheld

The package does not establish:

- spontaneous or open-ended Neuralese;
- an exact learned group representation;
- infinite-horizon, universal, or adversarial stability;
- invariance under arbitrary \(GL(12)\) reparameterization—the official fixed
  Euclidean gauge is naturally equivariant only under orthogonal coordinate
  changes;
- a fixed additive nuisance or observational-null subspace;
- necessary ambient-complement use outside the registered causal assay;
- architecture-independent emergence;
- the mechanism of a frontier or pretrained language model;
- subjective experience, intention, awareness, or consciousness.

Scientific failures remain results. Execution or assay failure blocks the
scientific interpretation; mechanism, capability, transfer, or ambient-use
failure is retained without relabeling.
