# Lineage

## v0.1.1 — recurrent operator workspace

- randomly conjugated finite operator systems
- terminal-only and relation-regularized linear recurrence
- GRU, one-shot, and token-only comparisons
- causal state and operator interventions
- cross-seed behavioral transport
- discovery of severe raw hidden-norm growth at depth

## v0.2.0 — stable quotient

- proved positive radial scale is an exact gauge of the bias-free recurrence
- separated overflow from directional failure by normalized replay
- introduced projective terminal-only training
- demonstrated large long-depth improvements on D4, C8, and mixed-cycle systems
- ruled out a fixed global additive future-null subspace at the tested tolerance
- added metric-polar, anchor-closed, and canonical semantic-chart controls
- localized the remaining failure to angular drift, operator inference, and path holonomy

## v0.3.0 — ambient packets

- generalizes exact anchor action from \(BPB^{-1}\) to weighted monomial transport \(BP\Lambda B^{-1}\)
- decomposes state-dependent radial gauge from off-ray leakage
- defines oriented-projective continuation packets by pulled-back future decoder boundaries
- measures packet membership, radius, erosion, and heldout continuation
- measures ordered projective Jacobian growth under operator switching
- separates radial, fiber, and semantic holonomy
- adds per-context observability and delayed-return controls
- preserves integrity, assay-calibration, and learned-hypothesis outcomes as separate result classes

Version 0.3 reuses the frozen v0.2 projective checkpoints for mechanistic diagnosis. It performs no new training and makes no confirmatory emergence claim.

## v0.4.0 — ambient packet closure

- enlarges the hidden space from eight to twelve dimensions so the learned
  rank-eight anchor plane has a nonzero rank-four ambient complement
- records an eleven-arm learned-metric factorial as a developmental failure
  of that tested formulation rather than official evidence
- fixes the metric to \(G=I\) and replaces the learned-metric proposal with a
  paired raw-versus-hard-polar architectural comparison
- crosses both architectures with matched supervision versus demonstration-
  aligned composition plus finite-path complement-return pressure
- rejects all loss-bearing D4 endpoints in the identity and \(r^2\) action
  groups, then materializes the exhaustive held-out terminal-action bank only
  after checkpoint sealing
- audits hard-polar isometry, pre-polar conditioning, held-out packet margins,
  null-loop retention, finite switched products, causal state and operator
  patches, and deep D4 behavior
- reports zero-shot C8/mixed-cycle transfer and causal ambient-complement use
  separately from the primary D4 claim

Version 0.4 is a fresh-seed developmental pilot whose design and thresholds
were informed by seed-71 development. It is not a confirmatory emergence
study, and hard-polar stability is supplied by the architecture.
