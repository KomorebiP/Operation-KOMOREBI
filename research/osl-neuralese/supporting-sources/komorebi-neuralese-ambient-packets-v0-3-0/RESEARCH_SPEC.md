# Ambient-packet research specification

## Question

Does the frozen terminal-only projective recurrence realize task semantics on robust continuation-stable regions of its ambient positive-projective state space, and which ambient defects explain its eventual deep failure?

## Formal hierarchy

Let \(H=\mathbb R^d\), let \(F_g\) be the normalized recurrent action, and let \(R\) be the declared output interface.

1. Raw state: \(h\in H\).
2. Positive ray: \([h]\in\mathbb P_+(H)\).
3. Finite continuation signature:
   \[
   \sigma_K([h])=(R(F_u[h]))_{|u|\le K}.
   \]
4. Finite continuation packet: a level set or robust neighborhood of \(\sigma_K\).
5. Infinite semantic congruence: a representative-independent equivalence preserved by every allowed generator.

The v0.3 audit measures levels 2 through 4. It does not assume level 5.

## Hypotheses

### H1: state-dependent scale is a legitimate anchor-ray gauge

On an anchor basis \(B\), projective correctness permits

\[
B^{-1}M_gB=P_g\Lambda_g
\]

with positive diagonal \(\Lambda_g\). The older global-scalar diagnostic may therefore overstate error. The residual

\[
E_g=B^{-1}M_gB-P_g\Lambda_g
\]

measures off-ray leakage in that chart.

### H2: statewise gauge does not automatically solve ambient transport

Weighted monomial equivalence is exact on anchor rays. Off-anchor mixtures can still change. Statewise gauge reduction and continuation-packet robustness must therefore be reported separately.

### H3: continuation margin predicts deep failure

For target \(\tau_u(i)\), competitor \(j\), and

\[
n_{uij}=M_u^{\mathsf T}(w_{\tau_u(i)}-w_j),
\]

the normalized signed distance

\[
\gamma_{uij}(h)=
\frac{n_{uij}^{\mathsf T}h}
{\|n_{uij}\|_{G^{-1}}\|h\|_G}
\]

is a future-boundary margin. The packet radius is its minimum over the declared continuation bank. Radius erosion should appear before aggregate accuracy collapse if ambient drift is the operative failure.

### H4: unit norms do not imply switched projective stability

The relevant local derivative is

\[
D\widehat F_g([h])
=
\frac{\Pi_{\widehat{M_gh}}M_g}{\|M_gh\|}.
\]

Ordered products of these derivatives can amplify angular error even when every recurrent state is normalized and every raw generator has a small spectral radius.

### H5: null-loop displacement is harmless only conditionally

A semantic null relation may generate radial or fiber holonomy. It is harmless for the declared task only if repeated application preserves all audited continuation signatures with positive margin. Endpoint agreement alone is insufficient.

### H6: full future observability and finite argmax packets are compatible

Full equality-sensitive observability rules out a fixed nonzero additive nuisance subspace. It does not rule out full-dimensional regions sharing the same discrete continuation signature. Per-context observability and packet geometry answer different questions.

## Evidence classes

### Integrity

- exact checkpoint schema, configuration, and SHA-256
- deterministic context and suffix banks
- explicit canonical-to-visible operator alignment
- float64 geometry and native float32 behavior channels
- finite, canonical JSON with source and input hashes

### Assay calibration

- exact semantic-chart execution
- global positive-scale invariance
- rejection of negative scaling as gauge
- exact recovery of synthetic statewise gauge
- monotone response to injected off-ray leakage
- detection of a nonnormal switched counterexample

### Learned-model hypotheses

- fraction of global defect removed by statewise scaling
- packet membership and margin versus reachable prefix depth
- switched angular gain and switching enhancement
- per-context observability profile
- null-loop margin retention and first failure

Learned-model hypothesis failures are scientific outcomes, not execution failures.

## Controls

| Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Exact semantic chart | Existence and harness positive control |
| Globally scaled exact action | Positive radial-gauge invariance |
| Statewise-scaled exact action | Missing gauge diagnostic sensitivity |
| Negative-scaled action | Oriented-ray specificity |
| Norm-matched off-ray leakage | Leakage sensitivity |
| Synthetic nonnormal switch | Product-level stability sensitivity |
| Constant-generator products | Separates generic angular expansion from switching enhancement |

Lookup and reset executors remain important future baselines. Their likely success would show that behavioral packet transport alone does not establish abstract operator discovery.

Delayed-observability and relation-grouped suffix-split constructions are tested at the module level, but they are not recorded v0.3 pilot gates. They belong in the fresh-seed confirmatory block.

## Interpretation rules

- A construction-bank packet inclusion is descriptive, not independent evidence.
- A finite packet is not an infinite-horizon fiber.
- Current-output margin is not continuation margin.
- A full-dimensional packet does not provide a literal tangent/normal manifold split.
- Statewise gauge on anchors is not a global ambient gauge.
- An individual spectral radius is not a switched stability certificate.
- An exact semantic chart is engineered, not emergent.
- D4 heldout words test path consistency, not unseen transition functions, because short words already realize the finite group actions.

## Immediate next decision

If packet radius erosion and switched gain jointly explain failure, the next learned arm should combine:

\[
\boxed{
\text{projective recurrence}
+\text{anonymous multiscale composition consistency}
+\text{continuation-margin preservation}
+\text{delayed-return control}
}
\]

Any structural loss must be labeled as structural supervision. A later emergence claim requires weaker operator bias and freshly frozen training seeds.
