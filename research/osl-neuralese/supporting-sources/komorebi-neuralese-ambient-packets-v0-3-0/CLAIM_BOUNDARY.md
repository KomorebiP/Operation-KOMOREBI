# Claim boundary

Version 0.3 is a developmental, frozen-checkpoint mechanistic audit. It introduces an ambient continuation-packet formalism and tests it on the two projective models selected and trained in v0.2.

## Supported parent facts

The retained v0.2 evidence established that positive scale is an exact behavioral gauge for the bias-free linear recurrence, projective normalization substantially extends stable execution, causal hidden-state and operator edits remain effective, and an engineered semantic chart solves the finite task exactly.

## Claims this block may support

Depending on its recorded outcomes, v0.3 may support bounded statements about:

- how much anchor error is radial gauge versus off-ray leakage
- whether reachable states lie inside finite continuation packets
- how packet membership and margin change with recurrent depth
- whether switched angular amplification accompanies deep failure
- whether tested null-loop holonomy remains continuation-safe
- whether equality-sensitive observability and discrete packet structure coexist

## Claims withheld

The package does not establish:

- spontaneous Neuralese
- an infinite-horizon semantic quotient
- observer-independent or task-independent latent meaning
- a fixed additive nuisance subspace
- a global ambient group representation
- universal arbitrary-permutation generalization
- robust off-manifold fibers beyond the tested perturbations
- architecture-independent emergence
- the mechanism of a frontier language model
- awareness, experience, intention, or consciousness

The exact semantic chart and table-derived controls are engineering positive controls. They establish solvability and assay sensitivity, not learned discovery.

See `AMBIENT_CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md` for gate-by-gate interpretation and explicit invalidating observations.
