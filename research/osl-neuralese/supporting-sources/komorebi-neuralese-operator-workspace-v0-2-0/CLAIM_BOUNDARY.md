# Claim boundary for v0.2.0

## Supported

The primary evidence supports this bounded statement:

> In an explicitly matrix-operator recurrent architecture trained on complete
> finite transition tables, a learned latent metric plus correct anonymous
> composition pressure substantially improves finite-horizon execution on
> exactly held-out operator tables, suppresses explosive transients, and permits
> high-accuracy affine alignment between independently trained state spaces.

Correct anonymous targets outperform matched shuffled targets on D4 and C8
long-depth behavior and on operator closure residuals.

## Not supported

The package does not establish:

- nonvanishing or asymptotically stable recurrent dynamics
- an exact hidden group or semigroup representation
- spontaneous discovery of operators without architectural bias
- generalization without full transition-table access
- context-independent latent state semantics
- transport richer than the decoded eight-state label codebook
- the mechanism inside any frontier or proprietary language model
- open-ended Neuralese, autonomous improvement, awareness, or consciousness

## Reclassified v0.1.1 evidence

V0.1.1 exhausted its D4 coordinate orbit during training, so its D4 evaluation
was word-depth holdout rather than coordinate holdout. Its donor-state and
operator-code tests are native splice consistency checks. Its transport assay
largely aligned an eight-state codebook and lacked the decode–re-embed ceiling.
Those results remain useful positive-control evidence but must not be cited as
independent proof of unseen-coordinate causal semantics.

## Why the primary is a failure

The protocol is conjunctive. Six gates failed, including the nonvanishing norm,
absolute closure, and absolute cross-context patch gates. Passing 20 other gates
does not override those failures.
