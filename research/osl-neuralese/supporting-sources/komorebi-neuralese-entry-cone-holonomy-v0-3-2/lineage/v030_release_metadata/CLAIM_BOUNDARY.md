# Claim boundary

## Registered results

This adaptive-developmental audit covers two frozen projective checkpoints,
eight fresh coordinate-resampled D4 episodes per checkpoint, every ordinary
history through `L=8`, every suffix through `H=8`, and the registered repeated
relator family. Coordinate resampling is fresh for this audit but remains
inside the reconstructed table-pair support.

- `E0_execution_integrity`: **pass**
- `H0_hard_rectangle`: **fail**
- `H1_downset`: **pass**
- `S0_soft_exact_collapse`: **fail**
- `D0_diagonal_action`: **fail**
- `W0_relator_hard_closure`: **fail**
- `W1_relator_soft_flatness`: **fail**
- `C0_nonidentity_control`: **fail**

The full cumulative `(L=8,H=8)` surface contains 33,423,488 decisions and
eight errors. All eight are the same frozen checkpoint-seed-0 depth-zero
anchor defect: semantic label 2 decodes as 6 with margin `-0.784008`. Seed 1
passes all 81 cumulative hard cells in every episode; seed 0 passes none
because every cumulative cell retains that failed root.

Exact probability and centered-logit collapse fails in every episode as soon
as multiple histories are present. Exact equality was the registered rule;
no post-hoc tolerance changes that verdict.

Repeated identity paths also fail strict closure. Across 336
relation/budget/environment records, hard closure passes 316, soft flatness
passes none, and the matched nonidentity control passes 317. At 64 inserted
tokens, every relation family fails somewhere. The control failures show that
most long-depth fragility is broader than identity-loop algebra alone.

## Post-hoc localization

The following findings are conjunctions or localizations of registered rows;
they are not replacement gates.

- Every exact ordinary history shell with `1 <= L <= 8`, under all futures
  through `H=8`, passes for both checkpoints and all episodes:
  33,358,080 of 33,358,080 hard decisions, with minimum target margin
  `2.397187`.
- All 1,792 diagonal generator-cells whose target horizon is at least one
  pass. The 128 seed-0 target-`H=0` cells fail because that coarse quotient
  retains the anchor collision; all 128 seed-1 target-`H=0` cells pass.
- The earliest hard relator counterexample has total observed history depth
  38 (`frfr`, 32 inserted tokens) and is first revealed after a suffix of
  length 3. Of 2,193 hard-failing relator cases, 1,697 require a nonempty
  continuation to reveal the defect.

## Supported interpretation

The evidence supports a finite, task-local, post-transition hard
decision-cone code over the exhaustive registered ordinary-history window.
It also shows that the continuous realization retains history-dependent soft
microstate and that sufficiently long redundant paths can drive that
microstate across decision boundaries. The raw depth-zero interface and the
recurrent post-transition regime should therefore be modeled separately.

The data support delayed observability: a path can remain correct at its
endpoint and fail only under a later continuation. They do not establish that
all observed long-path failure is specifically algebraic holonomy, because
matched nonidentity paths usually fail in the same configurations.

## Claims not supported

This package does not establish:

- a causal completion that includes depth zero for both checkpoints;
- exact probability, centered-logit, or full-law quotient collapse;
- a stabilized same-space operator action;
- recurrence beyond the registered finite domains;
- an invariant metric, joint spectral stability, or normally attracting semantic fibers;
- held-out task-family generalization or population-level reliability;
- loop-specific excess risk against independently sampled endpoint-matched controls;
- a coherent global semantic atlas or architecture-independent transport;
- spontaneous or universal Neuralese;
- any claim about frontier language models, consciousness, or awareness.

Near-perfect pooled accuracy must not replace the registered worst-case gate
verdicts. A relator failure is a valid finite counterexample; passing the
registered relator family would not have proved exhaustive behavior at the
same total depth.
