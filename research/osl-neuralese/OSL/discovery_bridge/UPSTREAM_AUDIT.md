OSL v0.5 was verified with 66 source programs, 61 focused tests, 1,149 independently reconstructed receipts, 70 proof terms, 2,330 finite abstract states, and 47,958 attempted transitions. Those counts are finite evidence, not a universal proof.

## Why a discovery boundary is necessary

The v0.6 geometry pilot found that answer-only training produced weak formal alignment. Explicit state supervision and equivalence regularization improved alignment and equivalent-path retrieval, but random recurrent and lexical controls matched or exceeded the trained probes. The untrained recurrent transition score also exceeded the answer-only score. A decodable feature or fitted affine map is therefore insufficient for admission as an operator.

The pilot's causal endpoint also contains a semantic defect. The executable world defines `observe(a,f)` as changing both status to observed and origin to `a`. The two intervention scripts instead required the previous communicated origin to remain unchanged. Their reported coherent-conversion rate is not a valid observe-operator endpoint. The strict audit also had only 12, 17, and 13 baseline-correct cases out of 660 for the three main arms. This does not rescue the emergence claim, but it prevents treating the near-one-percent conversion result as a clean causal measurement.

The HuggingFace v0.3.1 package has no model result. It supplies a 24-slot ontology and typed event operands, so even a future success would test scaffolded execution rather than autonomous operator discovery. Its causal-bank generator also discards `blocked_alpha`, leaving alpha-isomorphic separation unverified.

## Consequence for OSL

Neural geometry should remain an untrusted proposal. It should not acquire memory, planning, or execution authority merely because a probe decodes it. The bridge implements the missing independent synthesis and admission boundary.

The next neural experiment should compile predicted typed calls through OSL-Core itself, replay receipts independently, and compare exact post-state, write set, acceptance or rejection, and unaffected-state preservation. State-equivalent paths and receipt-distinct paths must remain separate representations.
