- autonomous invention of the protected semantic ontology;
- arbitrary nonlinear, stochastic, variable-length, or information-losing path correspondence;
- natural-language or production-model Neuralese interpretation;
- a faithful linguistic transcript of latent reasoning;
- evaluator or witness self-improvement;
- external independent replication;
- open-ended recursive self-improvement, indefinite acceleration, unrestricted safety, consciousness, or sentience.

## Construction boundary

The benchmark uses commuting symmetric intervention operators, orthogonal foreign gauges, finite-dimensional latent packets, and author-specified gauge policies. Hidden source-target pairing exists in the audit harness so that a final external witness can score correspondence, but it is excluded from construction and selection. These are controlled positive, ambiguity, partial-support, and no-go fixtures rather than a theorem about arbitrary learned representations.
