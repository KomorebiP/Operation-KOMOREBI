## Scope and residual limits

The new compiled module proves eleven generic ordinary-cochain and affine-linear statements. In particular, its complete null criterion explicitly assumes `ker N = im A`. The assigned guide and reader pages do not claim that Lean constructed the actual arithmetic primitive, target vector, 15 × 10 matrix or full left-null basis. The restricted primitive existence theorem uses the stated residual hypotheses and finite-S arithmetic duality, with that proof boundary kept separate from the generic formal source.

Guide physical page 12 was inspected after the primewise-bound rebuild. Its bounded-contract wording is consistent with the paper figure and with the later distinction between restricted Stage P and the remaining upper ramification problem.

No unresolved page-layout or local mathematical-scope finding remains in the assigned pages. Coverage is guide physical pages 10–18 and paper physical pages 25–36 only. Other pages are outside this independent visual inspection.
