# B74.1. A local Galois probe that kills all units and detects valuation

Fix F=Q_7(sqrt5), a separable closure, and compatible primitive roots of unity.
Write kappa_j(a) for the Kummer cocycle of a relative to7^j-th roots, with values
in mu_(7^j). On an element fixing mu_(7^j) its value is independent of changing a
chosen root by a root of unity. The statement below specifies an element, not a
new global representation or a Frobenius at an arbitrary chosen prime.

For EVERY j>=1, there exists tau_j in G_F such that

    chi(tau_j)=1 mod7^j,
    kappa_j(phi)(tau_j)=kappa_j(3)(tau_j)=0,
    kappa_j(7)(tau_j)=1 mod7^j.                          (1)

The last equality uses the fixed primitive root to write Kummer values additively.
Consequently, for every a in F*,

    kappa_j(a)(tau_j)=v_F(a) mod7^j.                     (2)

This is a valuation probe, not a claim that inertia at seven has only one coordinate.

## The unit basis

The units of F have prime-to-seven torsion of order48, and the logarithm identifies
1+7O_F with7O_F. Direct exact arithmetic modulo49 gives, with omega^2=5 in the BASE
field,

    (phi^48-1)/7 = 5omega mod7,
    (3^48-1)/7 = 6 mod7.

Their determinant is nonzero. Nakayama, or an explicit digit-lifting argument for
the logarithm lattice, shows that phi and3 generate the pro-seven completion of
the unit group as a free rank-two Z_7 module. Together with the uniformizer7 they
form a free basis of F*/F*^(7^j) for every j. There are2352 units in O_F/49:
49^2-7^2=2352. Each of the49 logarithmic images has48 preimages. The new code checks
that complete count; it must not be replaced by2304.

## Restriction to the cyclotomic field does not destroy the first-level classes

Put L_j=F(mu_(7^j)). Its Galois group over F is abelian of order6*7^(j-1), since
F/Q_7 is unramified and the cyclotomic extension is totally ramified. We DO NOT
call this order prime to seven.

The inflation kernel of

    H1(F,mu7) -> H1(L_j,mu7)

is H1(Gal(L_j/F),mu7). Let P be the seven-primary subgroup and Delta the order-six
quotient. P acts trivially on mu7; Delta acts on it by the nontrivial cyclotomic
character, while Delta conjugates P trivially because the cyclotomic group is
abelian. A Delta-equivariant homomorphism P->mu7 must therefore be zero.
Averaging over Delta (6 invertible modulo7) gives H1(Gal(L_j/F),mu7)=0.
For j=1 the same conclusion follows directly from the prime-to-seven order.
Thus the three classes phi,3,7 remain independent modulo seventh powers in L_j.

## Full7^j independence, not only the mod7 calculation

Suppose a relation among phi,3,7 modulo7^j has coefficients not all zero. Extract
7^s, the largest common seven-power in the coefficients, with s<j. The relation
implies

    phi^a 3^b 7^d = z^(7^(j-s)) * zeta,  zeta in mu_(7^s),

where at least one of a,b,d is not divisible by7. Since L_j contains mu_(7^j),
every such zeta is a seventh power in L_j when s<j. The right side is a seventh
power, contradicting the preceding independence. Hence their span modulo7^j is
(Z/7^j)^3.

The Kummer pairing therefore identifies

    Gal(L_j(phi^(1/7^j),3^(1/7^j),7^(1/7^j))/L_j)

with(Z/7^j)^3. Choose the element dual to(0,0,1) and lift it to G_(L_j). This proves
(1). Every unit class is generated by phi,3, proving(2).

are the written argument above. No numerical Galois-field construction is claimed.
