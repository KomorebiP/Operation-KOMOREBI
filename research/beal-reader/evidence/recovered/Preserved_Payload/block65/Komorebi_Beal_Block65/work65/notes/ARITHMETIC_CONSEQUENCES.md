# Arithmetic consequences and complete-support boundaries

Every new conclusion keeps TARGET_CONTRACT.md. Let U be the preserved ten-prime
set and let V65=U union W65. W65 is defined in CONDUCTOR_EXCLUSION.md.

1. At depth one, if beta descends as a chi^-1 cohomology class to Q, normalize
   a5=1. Then alpha=(v*w,r,1), v,r in F7^*, and beta has an F7-valued representative.
   No finite conductor bound is needed. This is not lattice descent.
2. At depth one, beta must ramify outside W65. The theorem only rules out that
   COMPLETE beta-conductor class. The six U-primes already excluded geometrically
   then show beta must ramify outside V65 as well.
3. If the complete conductor is among the25 primes P2000, some actual ramification
   occurs at673,1163,1723 or1933. There is no assertion for a conductor enlarged
   by further primes without their columns.
4. Under the old COMPLETE H(C) subset U hypothesis, the inherited result gives
   n=1. Its supporting prime outside V65 necessarily has h=1 and prime multiplicity
   with v7(v_q(C))=0, and must satisfy phi and75A seventh-power residue tests.
   The whole H(C) class is still open because unbounded low-h support is allowed.
5. For the full geometrically eligible divisor set E(C) from Block64,

       COMPLETE E(C) subset V65 => no retained reducible representation.

   All new primes of W65 outside U have h=1. Thus any nontrivial highest eta layer
   would be supported above U, impossible by Block58. Full eta then vanishes.
   At n>=2 beta support needs h>=2, reducing to the previously excluded four-prime
   full-eta case. At n=1 use B65.4. Extra divisors that fail eligibility are allowed.

The ramifying outside-support prime is not automatically2549. Divisibility of C,
ramification in beta, and ramification in the highest eta layer remain distinct.
No upper bound for arbitrary E(C), H(C), or C is proved. The entire reducible
branch, higher levels, CM branch, full signature and Beal remain unresolved.
