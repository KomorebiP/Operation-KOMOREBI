# A reduced global reciprocity presentation for the actual diagonal

Block54, v5.4.0. This is a necessary actual-character presentation, not a
realization theorem for every character of the presented group.

## B54.7. The five-row quotient

Let S be the full set of variable prime ideals dividing the hypothetical C,
outside2,3,5,7, with h_v=v_7(Nv-1)>0. S is finite for any fixed C but is NOT
bounded in advance. Choose a surjective seven-primary residue logarithm

    ell_v : (O_K/v)^* -> Z/7^h_v.

Put c_v=max(h_v-1,0), reduce each logarithm modulo7^c_v, and set

    D^-_S = direct_sum_(v in S) Z/7^c_v,
    R^-_S = D^-_S / <ell(phi),ell(2),ell(3),ell(sqrt5),ell(7)>.       (R)

Zero-capacity summands are omitted. Then the ACTUAL eta_n factors through
R^-_S. So does the additive theta_n in Block53's square-zero parametrization.

Proof. Eta has odd seven-power image. At q!=7, wild inertia is pro-q, so its
seven-primary conductor is at most the first power of the prime. It is
unramified at7 and locally trivial at all fixed places. Odd-order characters
kill real inertia. Since Cl(K)=1, the seven-primary ray group for S is the
product of residue seven-primary quotients modulo the image of global units.
The unit group of K is generated by -1 and phi; -1 has zero seven-primary
logarithm. This gives the phi relation.

For a direct elementary check of the unit generator, multiply any real positive
unit by a power of phi to place its first embedding in [1,phi). If its norm is1,
the coefficient b=(u-1/u)/sqrt5 of phi lies in [0,1), so b=0 and u=1. If its
norm is-1, b=(u+1/u)/sqrt5 lies strictly between0 and1, impossible. Adjust a
sign first when necessary. Thus no additional unit generator is missing.

Full local triviality at the fixed primes requires their Frobenius ideal
classes to vanish. Those primes are principal: (2),(3),(sqrt5),(7). Under the
ray sequence their classes give the four remaining rows in (R), up to the
common reciprocity sign convention. B54.2 says that the image of each cyclic
inertia group has exponent at most7^(h_v-1). Quotienting that cyclic group by
its subgroup of order7 is precisely reduction of the logarithm modulo7^c_v.
This proves (R). All rows refer to the SAME global character. QED.

Changing a local logarithm generator multiplies its whole column by a unit.
It does not alter the quotient or its exponent. The independent implementation
checks the columns up to such unit multiples, not by forcing identical logs.
The five rows must not be solved separately in unrelated local bases.

The corresponding unreduced group R_S uses c_v=h_v and the same five rows.
It classifies the abelian seven-primary characters with the fixed-prime
splitting conditions before the new inertia restriction. Both groups can be
nonzero. Neither classifies Frey realizations, beta, or the Kummer ratio.

## B54.8. Exponent-to-depth bounds

Suppose the finite group R^-_S has exponent7^e, with e=0 for the trivial group.
Every eta_n value has order dividing7^e. The principal-unit order formula and
Block53 give

    eta_n = 1 mod 7^max(m,n-e).                              (1)

At any beta-ramified place, B54.3 gives order7^s for inertia, so s<=e and

    n=t+2s <= t+2e.                                         (2)

The factorization F=I+(x,y)^T(p,r) gives w(p)>=n-s>=n-e. Also yr=F_22-1
has valuation at least n-e by (1). Hence

    h>=n-e,             n<=h+e.                             (3)

The inequality remains valid when n-e<=0, in which case it is merely weak.
When R^-_S=0, these statements specialize to

    eta_n=1 mod7^n,        s=0,       n=t,       h>=n.         (4)

They still do not kill beta. In particular, high natural monodromy multiplicity
can accommodate s=0 and n=t.

## Exact finite presentations

Let P0={11,13,19,29,31,43,71}; fixed primes2,3,5,7 are not in the variable
support. The eligible places over P0 are 13(inert), both places over29,
43(inert), and both places over71. All six have h=1. The other P0 places are
ineligible. Define C_d to mean a cyclic group of order d.

| Assumed rational support | Unreduced R_S | Reduced necessary R^-_S |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | C7 x C7 | 0 |
| P0 union {491} | C7^3 | 0 |
| P0 union {491,1471} | C7^4 x C49 | C7 |

Here491 and1471 split in K and have v_7(q-1)=2. These are precisely stated
finite-support cases, NOT a theorem that arbitrary C has such support.
The last row prevents the false inference that reciprocity always kills eta.

The primary computation uses phi-basis finite fields and seven-primary
projection logs. The independent one uses a sqrt5 basis, enumerates full
multiplicative cyclic groups, and computes independent discrete logs. Integer
Smith reduction returns elementary unimodular operations; a separate
interpreter replays those operations. The reduced exceptional cases are also
checked by exhaustive enumeration of all1,49,2401 coefficient vectors.

For P0, in the fixed primary coordinate order
(13,29:phi=6,29:phi=24,43,71:phi=9,71:phi=63), the full five-row matrix is

    1 6 1 0 4 3       phi
    0 1 1 1 1 1       2
    0 5 5 6 2 2       3
    0 4 4 5 0 0       sqrt5
    0 5 5 0 6 6       7.

It has rank4 overF7, not rank6. The unrestricted kernel therefore really is
nonzero; the new reduction kills it by removing h=1 inertia capacity, not by
misreporting the rank. A modulo7 rank alone does not determine higher exponent
in a mixed C7/C49 quotient; the integral Smith presentation is essential.

## Local and global lifting remain different questions

The actual eta_n factors through (R), but the global character lift proved in
GLOBAL_CUP_VANISHING need not factor through the same R^-_S, preserve its fixed
local values, or share the same conductor. The theorem does not assert that
every character of R^-_S admits a lift with these stronger restrictions.
