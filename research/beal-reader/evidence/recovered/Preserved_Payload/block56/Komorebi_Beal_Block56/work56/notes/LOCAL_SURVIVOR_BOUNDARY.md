# Why a direct local 97/293 contradiction does not work

## B56.6. Exact local models retain the old abstract survivor

The two inert primes have h=2. With column normalization ell(phi)=1, their
five-row columns are both (1,0,0,0,0). Every rational unit has seven-primary
logarithm zero because 7 does not divide q-1. The same holds for sqrt5,
whose square is rational. Direct seventh-power tests confirm that phi is
not a seventh power at either place.

Thus lambda=(6,1) satisfies the reciprocal unit and fixed-prime relations.
The normalized natural vector alpha=(0,1,1) vanishes at both places.

Even imposing trivial diagonal Frobenius at these inert places does not
contradict local toric algebra. The following exact matrices have n=3:

At 97:

    N=[[-49,7],[-343,49]],
    F=[[9409,0],[65856,1]].

At 293:

    N=[[-294,42],[-2058,294]],
    F=[[85849,0],[600936,1]].

At both places:

    N^2=0,  FN=Q N,  NF=N,  det F=Q,
    v_7(content N)=1,  v_7(N21)=3,  F21 in 7^3 O,
    F12=0,  F22=1.

The inverse lower inertia diagonals modulo 343 are respectively 295 and 50.
Writing eta(I)=1+49 lambda gives lambda=6 and lambda=1. Their order is seven.
The local Frobenius diagonal correction is one. Beta is ramified in these
local endpoint models and the natural local class is zero.

These are only local representations. No common global two-dimensional
representation, Frey Jacobian, or Diophantine solution is constructed.
They show that an attempted contradiction using just these ramified local
matrices and the highest-layer reciprocal line would be unsound.

For an abelian character with conductor only at these two places, reciprocity
on the rational uniformizers 97 and 293 gives trivial values for the
corresponding chosen local Frobenius lifts: the other residue component is
a rational unit with trivial seven-primary character. This observation uses
the specified complete conductor. With more high conductor primes, such
values cannot be imposed without recomputing reciprocity.

## Resolution achieved, and remaining route

The actual obstruction found in this block is at a DIFFERENT prime where
that complete high-support branch would have good reduction. Good 491 rules
out n>=2. This invalidates an actual realization under H(C) subset {97,293}
without invalidating the ray character or the local matrices.

If C also contains 197 and 491, the new good-prime tests no longer apply at
those places. An actual first layer still supported at 97/293 is not excluded
by this block alone. That is the next strengthened continuation boundary.
