# Komorebi Local–Global Reuse Audit

**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Scope:** Existing Komorebi records relevant to the Paper XI local-field/Weil/base-change program and the later local–global attachment program.

## Executive conclusion

The immediate unramified tower and conductor-base-change work should not be rebuilt from scratch. Paper XI v0.29–v0.33 already contains most of the required residue-tower, Henselian field-tower, degree, étale, Frobenius-pullback, and conductor-transport machinery. The principal new work is to reconcile and instantiate that older ambient Henselian branch with the newer standalone spectral local-field branch developed in Blocks 36–40.

## Directly reusable Paper XI machinery

### v0.29 — Unramified residue base change

- Residue Frobenius changes by the expected power.
- The residue-fixing pullback is identified with the integer-degree subgroup `deg^{-1}(fℤ)`.
- Lower ramification groups, fixed spaces, normalized conductor summands, Swan sums, and Artin sums are preserved under the unramified restriction.

### v0.30 — Henselian lift and actual field tower

- `HenselianLiftTower.field_tower` constructs compatible nested fields from finite residue towers.
- `HenselianFieldLift.field` constructs the actual finite intermediate field generated by multiplicative Hensel lifts.
- `ValuationFixingField.decompositionEquiv` identifies the actual decomposition group over the lifted field.
- `HenselianWeilAttachment.sourceEquiv`, `embedding_range`, and `degree_formula` identify the field-fixing Weil pullback with the degree-divisible subgroup.
- `HenselianConductorTransport` transports every lower group and conductor summand.

### v0.31 — Exact degree and full residue field

- Proves `[E:K]=[s:k]` for the constructed field.
- Proves the field is Galois under the stated hypotheses.
- Constructs an actual residue-field equivalence, not only a cardinality identity.

### v0.32 — Étale and ramification-index criterion

- Finite free local extension plus equality of rank and residue degree implies maximal-ideal equality.
- With finite presentation and separable residue extension, this gives `Algebra.Etale`.
- The ramified counterexample explains why rank=residue-degree is load-bearing.

### v0.33 — Standalone unramified realization

- Builds the complete upper DVR directly from a monic lift of a residue-field primitive polynomial.
- Proves locality, maximal-ideal equality, DVR structure, étaleness, ramification index one, completeness, and the residue-field equivalence.

## Reuse for later local blocks

### Finite tame comparison

Existing Paper XI finite comparison machinery already computes, from actual operators and spaces:

- dimension and inertia support;
- the full Frobenius characteristic polynomial;
- the determinant on inertia invariants;
- finite Swan and Artin expressions;
- finite-field Gauss phases;
- tensor interaction decompositions.

It explicitly distinguishes the full Frobenius polynomial from the invariant-space local polynomial.

### Gauss and epsilon-phase infrastructure

Existing Gauss-sum results prove:

- transport under finite-field automorphisms;
- the exact additive-character scaling law;
- normalization and nontrivial/trivial character magnitude cases.

These are the correct finite ingredients for a later epsilon/gamma-factor attachment, once additive-character and measure conventions are connected to the actual local field.

### Local factor identification

Existing comparison modules separate:

1. existence of a local realization;
2. equality of the relevant local factors;
3. uniqueness or characterization within the admissible class.

This separation should be retained when instantiating actual local-field and Weil-group data.

## Reuse for global blocks

### Paper XII local-condition pushouts

The archive contains the completed-tensor quotient law, simultaneous local-condition factorization, and a global base-change pushout formulation. These are the correct formal interfaces for imposing several local conditions on a represented global deformation problem.

A version-status inconsistency exists: one older roadmap marks the global pushout as unformalized, while a later theorem register marks it internally proved. The exact latest source must be reconciled before importing it as checked evidence.

### Frobenius spines and compatible systems

Paper X and the Langlands Completion Atlas contain:

- common algebraic Frobenius spines;
- finite determinant realization levels;
- compactness from finite satisfiability;
- strict compatibility at unramified places;
- one-member propagation once two compatible families and one attached member already exist.

These are global assembly tools. They do not create the missing companion or automorphic attachment.

### Cyclic descent / Strong Artin sector

The Strong Artin records provide a concrete restricted target:

- Galois extension torsors and automorphic descent torsors under the same quotient-character group;
- finite local attachment sets computed by extension spectra;
- a global cyclic attachment criterion given by nonempty intersection of all local attachment sets;
- determinant/central-character selection when the quotient prime is coprime to the rank;
- a finite-Satake pseudocharacter realization gate.

This makes a cyclic/monomial finite-image sector the best first restricted local–global theorem, rather than an unrestricted general LLC target.

## Revised block order

### Block 41 — Actual unramified field tower

Reuse v0.30 `field_tower`, v0.31 degree/residue results, and v0.32 étale criterion. Connect them to the current Blocks 36–40 spectral local-field realization.

Target:

- actual maps `K → E_s → E_t`;
- `[E_t:E_s]=[t:s]`;
- residue equivalence and ramification index one;
- compatibility with the current canonical/spectral local-field instances.

### Block 42 — Weil restriction equals degree dilation

Reuse v0.29/v0.30 pullback equivalences and degree formulas.

Target:

- construct the actual map on unramified Weil models;
- identify its image with `deg^{-1}([t:s]ℤ)`;
- prove geometric Frobenius maps to the lower Frobenius raised to `[t:s]`;
- identify the map with Block 40's internal degree dilation.

### Block 43 — Conductor transport across the actual tower

Reuse `UnramifiedConductor` and `HenselianConductorTransport`.

Target:

- lower-group equivalences;
- fixed-space equality;
- termwise conductor equality;
- Swan and Artin invariance under unramified restriction.

### Block 44 — Actual tame local-factor attachment

Instantiate the existing finite comparison package with the constructed local field and Weil objects. Prove that the finite conductor/local-polynomial outputs agree with the standard local-field definitions in the stated tame class.

### Block 45 — Epsilon/gamma attachment

Use the existing Gauss-phase transport and additive-scaling results. Add the actual local additive character and Haar/self-dual measure normalization. Keep the trivial-character exception explicit.

### Block 46 — Conditional local comparison

Instantiate the existing local-factor characterization and atomic comparison interfaces. Keep realization existence, factor comparison, and uniqueness as separate contracts.

### Block 47+ — Restricted global attachment

Use Paper XII local-condition pushouts, Paper X common Frobenius spines, compatible-family propagation, and the cyclic attachment sets from the Strong Artin records.

Best first bounded goal: a cyclic/monomial finite-image local–global attachment theorem, not unrestricted reciprocity.

## Verification boundary

This audit inspected existing project records and materialized the v0.29–v0.33 archives. It did not freshly replay their Lean modules. Historical verification records remain inherited evidence until the exact reused dependency closure is rebuilt against the current branch.
