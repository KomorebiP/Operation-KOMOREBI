## Exact positive packets with the new symmetry

Let eta=(1+w)/2 in the COEFFICIENT ring, with norm-1, and set C=eta I. Then
C sigma(C)=-I. Entrywise sigma is an automorphism of any sigma-stable matrix group,
and C sigma intertwines that automorphism with the tautological representation.
It preserves every standard endpoint lattice.

For each retained unit coefficient v, each local orientation epsilon, and c>0,
put kappa=v/(2epsilon) (use its integral unit lift),

    R_tau=I+7c*kappa E12,        R_g=I+7b E21,

where b is the nonzero frozen883 inertia coefficient. Both are honest invertible
integral matrices with determinant1. With j acting by sigma, the exact square
relation, endpoint preservation, conjugate traces, and the existing mixed trace
formula all hold:

    tr(R_tau R_g)-tr(R_tau)-tr(R_g)+2=49c*kappa*b.

Conjugating the whole packet by one nonrational integral endpoint-preserving P
gives C'=P eta sigma(P)^-1, usually non-scalar. This supplies finite positive
controls with nontrivial lower and diagonal entries in the probe. All changes
are made in one frame, not independently at each word.

These matrices can be regarded as a tautological representation of the compact
matrix group they generate, with its sigma automorphism. They are NOT asserted
representations of the arithmetic Galois group, actual Frey realizations, global
period solutions, or integer triples. They show only that the new symmetry and
trace identities are not, by themselves, inconsistent with any of the eight
residual patterns. Arbitrary c can be represented at the matrix-constraint level.

## Invalid strengthenings

1. The curve map does not square to identity: f iota(f)=[-1]. Discarding this
   sign fails already for the displayed twist and its hyperelliptic involution.
2. Norm+1 is not the same as norm-1: C=I fails C sigma(C)=-I in the normalized j.
3. An ordinary E-linear intertwiner is not interchangeable with a sigma-semilinear
   one. At nonzero u the normalized Kummer rows(u,r,1),(-u,r,1) are not proportional.
   At u=0 this obstruction disappears, so nonvanishing is genuinely used.
4. Integral semilinearity does not make rho Q7-valued. The sigma-stable group
   generated by U(1),U(w),L(7),diag(3,1) has the comparison C=eta I but contains
   U(w)L(7) with trace2+7w, not in Q7. No change of E-basis changes that trace.
5. Without separated residual characters the unique-segment argument is invalid:
   the scalar residual representation fixes all50 lines overF49, not only two.
6. The symmetry does not force nu7=0. The positive packets realize all seven
   residual c*kappa values while preserving the equations of this block.
7. No statement here repairs a candidate that violates any prior finite trace,
   residue, period, or global cohomology condition. All previous exclusions and
   negative controls remain preserved in the original archives.
