# B74.4. Three labelled traces replace the complete trace/inertia fibre hypothesis

Consider two actual depth-one representations satisfying the stated geometry, in
compatible natural residual frames. Suppose they have the same residual natural
class and the same normalized beta cocycle. Choose ONE local probe tau_3 and ONE
g in the common residual kernel with beta(g)!=0.

If their three rank-two traces at tau_3,g,tau_3 g agree modulo343, then

    nu7(rho')=nu7(rho).                                 (9)

No equality of all inertia matrices is needed. No equality of the complete trace
functions is needed. Apply(6) to the two representations. This proves(9) without
first proving that their global difference is upper-only.

If the residual coefficient v is the same, their Diophantine valuations c,c' obey

    c/epsilon = c'/epsilon' mod7,
    hence c^2=(c')^2 mod7.

The signs are local coefficient orientations. This is not a theorem that c=0
mod7 or that either representation exists.
