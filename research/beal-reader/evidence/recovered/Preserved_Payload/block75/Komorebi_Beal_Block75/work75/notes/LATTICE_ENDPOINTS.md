# B75.1. Unique oriented endpoints on the actual stable-lattice segment

Let O be a complete DVR with residue characteristic 7, fraction field E and
uniformizer 7. Suppose rho preserves L=O^2, its residual representation is a
nonsplit extension of 1 by a nontrivial character chi, and its opposite class at
finite depth n is nonzero. The hypotheses are those in G3, not a classification of
arbitrary residually scalar representations.

## A separating element and absolute irreducibility

Choose h with chi(h)!=1 modulo7. The two roots of the characteristic polynomial of
rho(h) lift to distinct O-roots, since they are distinct modulo7. Its eigenprojectors
are integral polynomials in rho(h), with denominator the unit difference of these
roots. Diagonalize rho(h) in a natural O-basis whose first vector lifts the chi-line.
The lower component of that first eigenvector is in7^n O. The change of natural
frame preserves the endpoint exponent n. On the lower divided cocycle it induces
a nonzero scalar and a coboundary; consequently it cannot make a nonzero beta
class zero. In this basis the ideal generated by all upper entries is O, since
alpha is nonsplit. The ideal of lower entries is exactly7^n O: it is contained
there by construction, and containment in7^(n+1) would make beta zero.

Both off-diagonal ideals are nonzero. Any invariant line over an algebraic closure
of E must be an eigenline of rho(h), hence one of its two E-defined eigenlines.
Neither is invariant under all of rho. Therefore rho is absolutely irreducible.
This is why a nonzero intertwiner used later is invertible.

## All stable lattices

Every O-lattice stable under rho is stable under the two integral eigenprojectors
of rho(h). It therefore splits as fractional ideals on its two eigenlines.
Up to E-homothety, it is

    L_i = O e1 + 7^i O e2.

In this basis a matrix [[a,b],[c,d]] becomes [[a,7^i b],[7^-i c,d]]. The upper and
lower entry ideals above show that it is integral for every group element exactly
when 0<=i<=n. Thus these n+1 lattices are the complete stable segment.

At i=0 the residual extension is nonsplit with subcharacter chi. At i=n it is
nonsplit with subcharacter 1. Interior vertices have split residual representation.
Hence the natural endpoint is the UNIQUE homothety class having the nonsplit
chi-subline orientation. The opposite endpoint is likewise unique in its opposite
orientation. A rational intertwiner preserving these characters must preserve
the corresponding endpoint homothety classes.

If such an intertwiner is integral unimodular on L_0 and preserves the homothety
class of L_n, it preserves L_n itself: [L_0:L_n] has unchanged O-length n, so the
homothety has valuation zero. In a natural frame it belongs to

    Gamma_0(7^n) = {C in GL2(O): C21 in7^n O}.

It then preserves every L_i, 0<=i<=n. This is an integral statement, not only a
rational representation isomorphism.

## Scope of the standard input

This elementary argument is the residually multiplicity-free segment case of the
stable-lattice theory. Ophir-Weiss, arXiv:2111.01559v3, section1.1 and Lemma2.3,
confirm the segment and lattice/line dictionary. We give the argument here to pin
orientation and integrality. We do not use a theorem asserting that an arbitrary
trace-compatible lattice has the required orientation.

Without distinct residual characters the projector step can fail and the stable
graph can branch. Without nonzero beta the finite opposite endpoint and absolute
irreducibility need not hold. These are not dispensable hypotheses.
