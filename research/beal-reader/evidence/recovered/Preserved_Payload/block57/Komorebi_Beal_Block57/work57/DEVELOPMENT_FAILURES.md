# Failures, rejected strengthenings, and evidence limits

1. Execution failure: the first session-based container launch returned
StreamingExecNotEnabledContainerError before starting a process. An observed
subprocess replay then completed successfully. The failure and replacement
PID/argv/status are preserved in verification/Block57_Startup_execution.json.

2. The former97/293 ray line and its two local toric models remain valid under
their old hypotheses. They fail the NEW actual Frobenius rows at forced bad
places. This is not a retrospective claim that the older exact arithmetic
was wrong or that those local models never existed.

3. The attempted universal-zero strengthening of the eleven-row system fails.
Two explicit nonzero compatible ray/Kummer patterns remain in the declared
universe. Their coefficients, supports and exact vectors are preserved. They
are not Frey realizations and are not counted as global counterexamples.

4. The strengthening 'every bad prime makes eta locally trivial' is false.
The exact n3,Q29 matrices in LOCAL_DECOMPOSITION have eta inertia trivial but
eta Frobenius nontrivial of order7. The h=0 condition cannot be dropped.

5. A highest-layer support restriction is not a complete divisor restriction.
The proof of n<=3 uses H(C) subset U. Omitting that assumption would allow
additional high places to support beta, so no such stronger conclusion is made.

6. No new Lean compilation or independent mathematical review was performed.
The independent arithmetic verifier does not prove inherited geometric leaves.
All earlier failures and retractions remain inside the byte-identical predecessor.

7. A document-generation helper emitted an invalid-escape SyntaxWarning for a
set-difference notation string. The helper was corrected. Generation completed,
and no production arithmetic script or theorem conclusion depended on it.
