## B57.1. Distinct residual toric characters force local triviality

Let R=O/7^n O and let chi,eta:G->R^* be characters, with eta=1 mod7.
Suppose for every g in G,

    chi(g) eta(g)+eta(g)^(-1)=chi(g)+1.                 (1)

If chi modulo7 is nontrivial on G, then eta is the trivial character.

Proof. Multiplication of (1) by eta(g) gives

    (eta(g)-1)(chi(g) eta(g)-1)=0 in R.                (2)

Whenever chi(g)!=1 mod7, the second factor is a unit, so eta(g)=1.
Choose g0 with chi(g0)!=1 mod7. If chi(g)=1 mod7, then chi(g0 g)!=1
mod7. Both eta(g0) and eta(g0 g) are1, and multiplicativity gives eta(g)=1.
This treats all g and proves the claim. No cancellation by a zero divisor
has occurred. QED.

In the retained representation, reduction in the SAME natural lattice has
actual diagonal characters chi_7 eta and eta^(-1). At a variable bad place,
exact split toric semisimplification has characters chi_7 and1. Its trace
therefore supplies (1), including products with inertia, not just one
characteristic polynomial. If Q=Nv is not1 mod7, local Frobenius supplies g0.
Thus

    q|C, q not in {2,3,5,7}, h_v=v7(Q-1)=0
                  => eta|G_v=1 mod7^n.                (3)

This is stronger than being unramified: the Frobenius value is trivial too.
It does NOT use good reduction, and does not substitute an abstract character
for the actual diagonal. The principal residual orientation is essential.

## B57.2. An order-sensitive version at h>0

Keep the global half-precision m=floor(n/2)+1 and write eta(F)=1+u,
u in7^m O, for actual local Frobenius. Its exact toric trace gives modulo7^n

    0=Q eta(F)+eta(F)^(-1)-(Q+1)
     =(Q-1)u+u^2/(1+u).

Since 2m>n, this is equivalent to (Q-1)u=0 mod7^n. Hence

    v7(eta(F)-1)>=n-h whenever n-h>0,
    ord(eta(F)) divides7^h.                            (4)

Block54 bounds inertia order by7^max(h-1,0). The local abelian image is
generated by inertia and a Frobenius lift, so its exponent divides7^h.
Consequently, if the GLOBAL exponent is7^e and lambda=eta^(7^(e-1)), then

    h_v<e => lambda|G_v=0.                             (5)

These places provide additional principal-ideal reciprocity rows for the
highest layer. The universally usable forced places in this release have h=0,
so no unknown value of e has to be chosen. Statement (3) also follows directly
from B57.1 and does not require the half-precision input.
