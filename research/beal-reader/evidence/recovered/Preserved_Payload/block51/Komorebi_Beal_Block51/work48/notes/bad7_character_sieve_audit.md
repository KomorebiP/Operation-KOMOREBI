\[
(a,b,s,t)\longmapsto(-a+b+2s\bmod4,\ -a+s+t\bmod2).

vectors, and it is onto C₄×C₂. The positive element 2 has vector

value to −1 and leaves just two choices on C₂. This proves exactly two
characters, both quadratic, with matched real signs. No evenness
assumption has been inserted.

The two fields are Q(ζ₅) and Q(ζ₁₅)⁺, viewed as quadratic extensions

## Weil-pairing and conductor consequence

The added paragraph about the reducible semisimplification is valid.
Because ν is F₇-valued, the two coefficient embeddings of the rank-two
RM factor supply ν twice in J[7] semisimplification. The Weil pairing
preserves multiplicities under η↦χ/η; since ν²=1, it forces χν twice.
These two characters differ, since χ is nontrivial. They exhaust the
four-dimensional representation, and the other RM character must be χν.
Thus the rank-two determinant is χ in this hypothetical reducible case.

For ν=ν_real, the ray table shows nontrivial quadratic tame inertia at
both (3) and (√5). Since χ is unramified there, the residual rank-two
conductor is two and its Swan conductor is zero. Preservation of Swan
conductor under reduction forces characteristic-zero Swan conductor
zero. Its tame conductor is at most two and at least the residual
conductor two. Therefore hypothetical reducibility requires

\[
a_{(3)}(\rho)=a_{(\sqrt5)}(\rho)=2.
\]

An independently established characteristic-zero conductor exponent
three at either place would consequently eliminate that specialization.
This note does not determine which parameter specializations have that
exponent; no such classification is assumed.
