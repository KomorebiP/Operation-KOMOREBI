   The exact unlabelled matrix image is not a substitute for those arithmetic data.
2. The stronger conclusion that the full first congruence subgroup is always in H
   fails when B is a single F7-line: L(7w) is absent for B=F7. Only K2 is universally
   forced under the stated depth-one assumptions.
3. The proposed extension to arbitrary positive depth is not proved; an explicit
   depth3 lower-ideal subgroup fails the K2 conclusion. No all-depth promotion.
4. A broad exploratory source query about twisted deformation obstructions returned
   several irrelevant results and no theorem resolving the fixed-local F49 problem.
   Those results were not used. The required Kummer and inflation-restriction pages
   in Milne were opened directly and inspected instead.
5. Small source cleanups removed a redundant placeholder and simplified a modular
   inverse return expression before the recorded rerun. Neither changed a scientific
   certificate. No production arithmetic failure was observed in the development
   runs. Original predecessor bytes were not changed.

The major unsuccessful inference remains explicit: a complete matrix-image theorem
and all its positive mixed-trace values do not eliminate any of the eight patterns.

6. Self-review narrowed a draft sentence about the local probe: tau_3 fixes the
   cyclotomic roots only at its stated finite precision. What is proved here is
   membership of its finite matrix in the reduction of the commutator subgroup,
   not membership of the full tau_3 image in the exact infinite cyclotomic kernel.
   The main image theorem, global seventh-root noncontainment, and all finite
   arithmetic outputs are unchanged by this wording correction.
