# B76.4. Full congruence fibres do not identify the local probe

For every admissible matrix modulo49 in the exact cyclotomic image, ALL117649
determinant-one lifts to modulo343 occur somewhere in the same image. This is
an immediate consequence of K_2 subset H and(10).

The quantifier is essential: the lifts can be images of DIFFERENT Galois elements.
They are not117649 possible values of rho(g) for one fixed labelled g in a fixed
representation. An exact image subgroup does not specify its quotient map or the
images of its local decomposition groups.

For any nonzero b in B and any a in k, choose integral lifts and put

    T_a=[[1,7a],[0,1]],          G_b=[[1,0],[7b,1]].

Both matrices are in J_B=H. Their determinants are1 and their individual traces
are2. Yet

    tr(T_a G_b)-tr(T_a)-tr(G_b)+2 =49ab mod343.             (11)

As a runs through k this realizes all49 residue values. The ordinary trace and
semilinear symmetry therefore impose no image-membership prohibition on any
required value of the fixed-seven defect. Each pair has actual preimages IF the
hypothetical representation exists, but the preimage of T_a need not belong to
G_(K_(7)), kill the correct unit periods, or be the prescribed valuation probe.

For the actual probe tau, Block74 still gives Delta(tau,g)/49=nu7 beta(g), with
nu7=c*v/(2epsilon) and c=v7(C). Nothing in(8) determines c, the local toric line,
the embedding of G_(K_(7)), or the relevant adjoint p,q solution. Those labels and
local compatibilities are the remaining arithmetic problem.

## Exact changes in the project boundary

CLOSED CONDITIONALLY: the cyclotomic determinant-one IMAGE GROUP at depth one,
including its full second congruence kernel and every higher fibre, is known.
For all eight old bounded patterns it is the same J_F7, not eight unspecified
matrix subgroups.

NOT CLOSED: an actual homomorphism from the arithmetic Galois group, its local
images in their common frame, one global adjoint deformation satisfying all local
conditions, or the numerical value of any candidate's labelled mixed trace.
This theorem does not establish arithmetic existence and does not eliminate any
of the eight patterns. It does not extend the image classification to n>=2.

## Controls against stronger statements

1. J_F7 contains K_2 but not L(7w), so K_1 subset H is false in that case.
2. A subgroup with entries only in Z7 and residual U(F7) can have a nonzero lower
   first digit but lacks U(49w). Full residual U(k) is essential.
3. The subgroup {M in SL2(O): a,d=1 mod7, lower in343O} has full residual U(k)
   but does not contain L(49). A nonzero opposite endpoint at depth3 cannot be
   substituted for the depth-one first-lower hypothesis.
4. When beta=0, an upper-triangular group supplies no lower seed, and the proof
   fails exactly where stated. A beta0 reference is not the actual object.
5. The eight symbolic mixed-trace targets all occur as matrix possibilities in
   J_F7. This neither realizes their prescribed local probe nor invalidates any
   earlier arithmetic exclusion.
