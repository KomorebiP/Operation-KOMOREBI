# Two genuine limitations of this block

## Forced13 does not supply a new independent row in the descended regime

At depths1 and2, the inherited results give full local beta-zero at13. This
is useful for general beta, but is already automatic for the descended
all-inert q=1 mod7 conductor class considered here. If gamma is the descended
unramified cocycle at13 and F is rational Frobenius, then

    gamma(F^2)=(1+chi(F)^(-1))*gamma(F)=0,

because13 is inert in K and chi(13)=-1 modulo7. Its restriction to K has
Frobenius F^2 and unramified inertia. It is locally zero. All49 possible gamma(F)
values are checked as a finite algebra control, but this is a written cocycle
identity, not a new source of independent equations. No support-uniform numerical
value of the nonzero coefficient a_phi is obtained.

new281 test and common-polynomial correlation, TWO tuples remain:
