## Conventions and hypotheses

K=Q(sqrt5), phi=(1+sqrt5)/2. The coefficient ring O is the unramified quadratic
extension of Z_7, with residue k=F49 and valuation w(7)=1. All matrices use the
SAME actual natural basis. Keep the actual depth n and m=floor(n/2)+1. The
actual diagonal correction eta_n is principal and satisfies eta_n=1 mod7^m.
Its inverse is the lower-right matrix diagonal modulo7^n. It is trivial on
full local groups at 2,3,5,7 and unramified outside the variable primes of C.

At each variable place the inherited split toric identities give

    N=mu[[-xy,x^2],[-y^2,xy]],   F=I+(x,y)^T(p,r),
    px+ry=Q-1,  t=w(mu)>=1,  (x,y) primitive,
    N21,F21 in7^n O.

The natural residual extension alpha is unramified there. Its Frobenius
upper-right entry is F12=xr, and its common global Kummer coordinates are
(a_phi,a_3,a_5), with a_3*a_5 nonzero. The same column normalization is used
for its seventh-power symbols and for the global ray-class rows.

## B55.1. Maximal inertia annihilates the natural class

Suppose eta_n is nontrivial and its complete global image has exponent 7^e.
Set L=n-e. The principal-unit order formula gives

    eta_n(g)=1 mod7^L for every g,       L>=m.

If eta_n(I_v) has the maximal order 7^e, then

    alpha|G_v=0,
    w(F12)>=t,
    w(N11)=L,  w(y)=L-t>0.

Proof. Write j=w(N11). Maximal inertia order means j=n-e=L<n. If y were a
unit, w(N21)=t>=n would imply j>=n, a contradiction. Thus x is a unit,
y is nonzero and nonunit, s=w(y)>0, and j=t+s=L. Global exponent control
applied to the SAME Frobenius gives

    yr=F22-1 in7^L O.

Consequently w(r)>=L-s=t>=1. Since x is a unit, F12=xr is divisible by7^t.
Natural inertia is already zero modulo7. Block54's capacity loss gives
h=w(Q-1)>=e+1, so the residual cyclotomic action is trivial locally. The
remaining unramified natural extension is determined by its Frobenius value,
which is now zero. This proves full local vanishing of alpha. QED.

This theorem does NOT require w(N21)=n. In particular, beta can be unramified
at a place covered by this theorem. The finite matrix checks explicitly cover
that new regime. No independent changes of basis are made.

## B55.2. The highest nonzero character layer is supported on local zeros

Define lambda=eta_n^(7^(e-1)). Its image is contained in
1+7^(n-1)O modulo7^n, identified additively with k. It is nonzero, because
7^e is the exponent of the entire image of eta_n. At a variable place,
lambda is ramified precisely when eta_n's inertia has order 7^e. Thus every
ramification place of lambda annihilates the SAME alpha by B55.1.

Put S_2={v|C: v not over2,3,5,7 and h_v=w(Nv-1)>=2}. Block54 shows that
eta, and hence lambda, is unramified at every omitted variable place. The
class-number-one ray sequence and fixed-prime splitting imply that lambda
is specified by a nonzero vector (lambda_v)_(v in S_2) in k satisfying

    sum_v ell_v(u) lambda_v=0   for u=phi,2,3,sqrt5,7.      (R)

Here ell_v is any surjective seven-primary logarithm reduced modulo7.
The vector is nonzero: a character with all inertia values zero would be an
unramified abelian seven-character, which is trivial since Cl(K)=1.
Equivalently, the ray presentation is generated by its residue factors.

Let L_v=(ell_v(phi),ell_v(3),ell_v(sqrt5)) in F7^3. All three generators are
v-units. The local Kummer condition is

    lambda_v !=0  =>  L_v dot alpha=0.                    (K)

Changing ell_v by a unit rescales the entire column of (R) and the entire
row L_v, and rescales lambda_v inversely. The equations and the zero condition
are invariant. No arbitrary comparison of separately chosen coefficients is
being used.

## B55.3. A depth-independent annihilator/reciprocity criterion

Let M be the five-row matrix of logarithms in (R). Normalize alpha by a_5=1,
so alpha=(u,r,1), u in k and r in k^*. For a candidate alpha let

    Z_alpha={v in S_2: L_v dot alpha=0}.

If every M restricted to columns Z_alpha is injective, as a map
k^(Z_alpha) -> k^5, then

    eta_n=1 mod7^n.

Proof. If eta were nontrivial, B55.2 would give a nonzero kernel vector
supported on Z_alpha for the ACTUAL alpha. This contradicts injectivity.
Nothing about n or e was assumed beyond the inherited actual hypotheses.
The conclusion therefore holds at every possible depth. QED.

The criterion is sufficient, not necessary. A nonzero kernel only survives
these necessary constraints. It does not produce the actual diagonal, a
compatible full collection of Frobenius values, beta, or a Frey Jacobian.

## B55.4. Forty-two prime-field tests suffice without coefficient descent

It is enough to test the 42 candidates

    alpha_0=(u_0,r_0,1), u_0 in F7, r_0 in F7^*.

This does NOT assert that the actual alpha has prime-field coefficients.

Proof. Fix a basis (1,omega) of k/F7 and write any normalized alpha=a+omega b,
where a,b are F7-vectors, a_5=1 and b_5=0. Since a_3+omega b_3 is nonzero,
either a_3!=0 or b_3!=0. Choose c=0 in the former case and c=1 otherwise.
Then alpha_0=a+c b has last coordinate1 and nonzero third-generator ratio.
Every F7 row annihilating alpha annihilates a and b separately, hence also
alpha_0. Thus Z_alpha is contained in Z_alpha_0. Injectivity of a matrix on
a larger column set implies injectivity on every smaller set. QED.

Conversely, a failure on one of the 42 candidates is a failure of the stated
all-k criterion, since F7 embeds in k. Hence the 42-test criterion is exactly
equivalent to the 2352-test criterion, not merely a heuristic shortcut.

## B55.5. Small dependent subsets and support-forced coefficient descent

If eta is nontrivial, there is a minimally dependent set T of logarithm
columns contained in the maximal-inertia support of lambda, and

    1 <= |T| <= 5.

All rows L_v for v in T annihilate the actual alpha. More precisely, if the
normalized actual coefficients are not all in F7, one can choose |T|<=4.

Proof. In B55.4, the space spanned by a,b has dimension d=1 or2. Each column
of M on the actual support lies in the annihilator of d independent linear
forms on F7^5, obtained by using only coordinates phi,3,sqrt5. This ambient
space has dimension5-d. A dependence over k of F7-columns is a dependence
over F7, since all minors lie in F7. A minimal dependent set has size at most
one more than its rank, hence at most6-d. QED.

The actual support need not equal T. The relation on T need not equal the
actual highest-layer character vector. This is a necessary certificate,
not a theorem that actual ramification uses at most five primes.

There is also a genuine conditional descent statement. If the natural
Kummer rows on the ACTUAL maximal-inertia support have rank2 over F7, then
the normalized actual alpha lies in F7^3. Their kernel over F7 is a line,
and extending scalars to k gives its entire k-kernel. Normalize its nonzero
last coordinate to1. Rank3 would force alpha=0 and is impossible. Rank0 or1
does not justify descent. This uses the shared coefficients directly and
does not assume the missing semilinear comparison of endpoint lattices.

## A necessary negative control

One cannot replace 'maximal inertia order' by 'any nontrivial inertia'. Set

    n=5, N=[[-2401,7],[-823543,2401]],
    F=[[148,1],[50421,344]], Q=491.

These integral matrices satisfy N^2=0, FN=491N, NF=N, det(F)=491. They have
natural content1, N21 valuation7>5 and F21 valuation5. Their lower-right
Frobenius correction has valuation3, and N11 has valuation4. Thus the local
diagonal's Frobenius image has order49 while its inertia image has order7.
The global-exponent bound e=2 is respected, but F12=1, so the natural local
class is nonzero. The prime491 splits in K, so the norm label is legitimate.

This is a local representation model, not a global Frey realization. It
shows exactly why only the highest nonzero layer may be filtered by alpha.
