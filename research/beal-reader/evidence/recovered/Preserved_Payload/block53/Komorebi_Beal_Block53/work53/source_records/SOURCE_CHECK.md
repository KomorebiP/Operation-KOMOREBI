## C1. Global unramified characters

James S. Milne, Class Field Theory, version4.03, 6 August2020.
Author-hosted source: https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf
Theorem0.4 and the following Hilbert-class-field discussion, printed p.4
(PDF index12), and modulus-one example3.9, printed p.159 (PDF index167).
The page images were inspected, not only search snippets. The Hilbert class
field is the maximal abelian extension unramified at all places and has
Galois group the ideal class group. Application: eta_m has finite abelian
7-power image and is shown to be unramified at every finite AND real place.
Thus Cl(K)=1 kills this particular character, not every ray character.

## Class number of Q(sqrt5)

James S. Milne, Algebraic Number Theory, version3.08, 19 July2020.
Author-hosted source: https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf
Theorem4.3, printed p.70 (PDF index71), Minkowski's ideal-class bound.
The page image was inspected. With n=2,r2=0,discriminant5 the bound is
sqrt5/2<2, hence every class has an integral norm 1 representative. A separate
norm-Euclidean proof is included in DIAGONAL_RIGIDITY, so this class-number
input does not rest only on a recalled table. Extracted text can drop minus
signs in unrelated quadratic-field examples; those examples were not used
as evidence for the positive-discriminant fieldK.

## C2. Finite-field order and Weil bound

James S. Milne, Abelian Varieties, version2.0, title page16 March2008 (revision
history also prints17 March2008). Author-hosted source:
https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/AV.pdf
ChapterII, Theorem1.1, printed p.75 (PDF index80), freshly inspected as a
page image. The order is the product of 1 minus the Frobenius eigenvalues;
each complex absolute value is sqrt(Q). For dimension2 this gives
#J(F_Q)<=(1+sqrt(Q))^4. Restriction of scalars from the quadratic coefficient
field supplies the norm of the rank-two determinant. The twist is unramified
at the good places used here. No new point count is required for 11 or 31.

Block52's scalar-Frobenius proof and its separate semisimplicity source
application are preserved, not replaced by this order theorem. The
mod 49 characteristic-polynomial negative control demonstrates why actual
scalar Frobenius is necessary for the new good 13 consequence.
