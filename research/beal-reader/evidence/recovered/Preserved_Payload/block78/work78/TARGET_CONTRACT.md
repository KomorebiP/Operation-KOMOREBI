# Block 78 target contract

Retain all Block77 and predecessor hypotheses and proof boundaries.

Primary target: compute enough of the Block77 affine local matching problem to
identify whether a finite-place Selmer obstruction can eliminate the eight
bounded depth-one patterns.

Exact subtargets:
1. derive the first-stage p condition at a beta-ramified toric place without an
   unknown toric-line parameter;
2. determine the finite localization maps for the two tangent modules k and
   k(chi);
3. state exactly what remains if those localization maps are surjective.

Do not claim ramification control from unrestricted H1. Do not eliminate a
pattern unless actual Frey-compatible ramification and frame conditions are
inconsistent. No new Lean or independent review is claimed.
