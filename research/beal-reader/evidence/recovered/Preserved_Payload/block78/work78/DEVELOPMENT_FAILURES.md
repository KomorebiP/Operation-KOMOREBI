# Development failures / rejected strengthenings

1. **False strengthening:** finite-local localization surjectivity was initially
   considered as a possible proof that the full Frey Selmer problem is always
   soluble. Rejected: Neukirch's theorem does not keep the correction unramified
   at every place outside the prescribed finite sets.
2. **False strengthening:** q local solutions were initially treated as exact
   framed cochains. Correct statement: H1 localization matches strict-equivalence
   classes; representative-sensitive frame conditions remain separate.
3. **Route closure:** the Block77 idea that a finite Poitou-Tate cokernel for the
   unrestricted modules might eliminate the eight patterns is closed. For these
   two modules the finite localization maps are already surjective.
