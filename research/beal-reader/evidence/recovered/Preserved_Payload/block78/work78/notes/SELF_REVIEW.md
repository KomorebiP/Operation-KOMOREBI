   radical is identified with the coefficient radical.

   unique quadratic cyclotomic subfield is imaginary. Thus G'=C6 in the first

6. **Ramification.** No theorem here controls every place outside the finite

   c p(F)-b p(T)=-hc. The opposite sign fails 691488 exhaustive packets.
8. **Frobenius lift.** F->T^mF changes b and p(F) compatibly; the affine
   functional is invariant.
9. **No pattern elimination.** The eight Block70 patterns remain. The result is
