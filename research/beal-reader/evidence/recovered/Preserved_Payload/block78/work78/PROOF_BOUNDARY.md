- At every depth-one beta-supporting toric place, the first-stage p-values obey
  the invariant affine equation c p(F)-b p(T)=-h c, and the first-order toric
  packets satisfying it are nonempty.
- For K=Q(sqrt5), finite localization is surjective for H^1(K,k) and for
  H^1(K,k(chi)). Prescribed changes can also be made while holding another
  finite set fixed. This is an application of Neukirch's exact criterion.
- Consequently Stage P has no obstruction from finitely many local cochain
  conditions alone.
- Once the Block77 H2 class is globally zero, Stage Q has no obstruction from
  finitely many STRICT-EQUIVALENCE-INVARIANT local H1 conditions alone.

  frames;
