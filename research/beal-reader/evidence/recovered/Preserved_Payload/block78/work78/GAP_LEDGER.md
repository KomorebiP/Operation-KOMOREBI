representation may ramify when beta is unramified. Unrestricted H1 can add new
ramification and therefore cannot decide the arithmetic problem.

H1(G_(K,Sigma),k) and H1(G_(K,Sigma),k(chi)) with the actual local tangent
conditions. The dual Selmer cokernel, not unrestricted localization, is the
possible global obstruction.

upper conjugacy, or use the mod343 tame-lifting equations to couple the local
representatives in one global frame.
