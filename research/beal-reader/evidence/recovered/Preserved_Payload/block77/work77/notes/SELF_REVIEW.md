   substituted for the actual twisted/local problem.
5. **Local-global H2.** Restriction to `K(zeta_7)` is injective because
   corestriction times restriction is multiplication by six, not because a

   `H^2(mu_7)=Br[7]`; `k` is two F7 copies.  Brauer-Hasse-Noether is applied only
   after this translation.

   relation; it does not make the arithmetic local embedding arbitrary.
8. **Negative control.** The Heisenberg example is not a Galois/Frey object; it
   only refutes the formal implication cup-zero => q-solvable.
