# B77.4. There is no hidden pro-7 relation at the fixed-seven place

Let

    F=Q_7(sqrt(5)).

The quadratic extension is unramified, while `Q_7(zeta_7)/Q_7` is totally
ramified of degree six.  Hence `F` does not contain a primitive seventh root of
unity.

A theorem of Shafarevich states that if a finite extension `F/Q_p` does not
contain a primitive p-th root of unity, its maximal pro-p Galois group is a free
pro-p group on

    [F:Q_p]+1

generators.  Here that rank is three.

Block 74 independently established that the local Kummer classes

    [phi], [3], [7]

form a basis of the pro-seven completion of `F^*`.  Thus the three Kummer-dual
coordinates can be taken as free pro-seven generators.

## Consequence

There is no additional Demushkin-type relation in the maximal pro-seven quotient
of `G_F` waiting to determine the uniformizer mixed trace.  Local assignments to
three pro-seven generators extend to the free group, subject of course to the
prime-to-seven cyclotomic action and the actual representation constraints.

This does **not** make the fixed-seven period condition arbitrary.  The actual
Frey representation, coefficient action, labelled uniformizer probe, and global
matching still constrain the homomorphism.  The conclusion is narrower:

> a purely local search for one more intrinsic pro-seven relation at seven cannot
> be the missing global obstruction.

The theorem was checked in the survey of Shafarevich's work by
Chebolu--Minac (Geometry & Topology Monographs 16 (2009), pp. 31--47), which
states that for a finite extension of Q_p without a primitive p-th root,
the maximal pro-p group is free on `[F:Q_p]+1` generators.
