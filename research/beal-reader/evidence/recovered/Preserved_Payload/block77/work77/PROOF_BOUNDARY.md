# Proof boundary

## Proved in this block, conditional on the retained project hypotheses

1. The second-stage `q` obstruction is cohomologous to a symmetric twisted
   triple-Massey defining-system value for `<alpha,beta,alpha>`.
2. Its ambiguity under changing the first-stage solution `p` is exactly the
   affine subgroup `2[alpha] cup H^1(K,k)`.
3. For a fixed admissible `p`, localization

       H^2(K,k(chi)) -> product_v H^2(K_v,k(chi))

   is injective.  Therefore the `q` obstruction vanishes globally iff all its
   localizations vanish.
4. Once one global `q_0` exists, imposing prescribed local deformation
   conditions is an affine H^1/Selmer matching problem, not another H^2 problem.
5. At F=Q_7(sqrt(5)), the maximal pro-7 quotient is free pro-7 on three
   generators.  Together with the already proved Kummer basis `[phi],[3],[7]`,
   this rules out an additional local pro-7 relation as the source of the missing
   fixed-seven mixed trace.

## Not proved

- Existence or nonexistence of the required global affine Selmer point for any
  of the eight bounded patterns.
- A conductor-uniform vanishing theorem.
- A numerical value of the labelled mixed trace for an actual global Frey
  representation.
- A new all-depth image theorem.
- Any elimination of the eight bounded patterns.
- Full reducible-branch closure, the full (3,5,7) signature, or Beal.

No independent mathematical reviewer or Lean compilation is claimed.
