# External-source record — Block 77

Checked 21 September 2026.

## 1. Shafarevich local pro-p freeness

Source checked: S. K. Chebolu and J. Minac, *Absolute Galois groups viewed from
small quotients and the Bloch-Kato conjecture*, Geometry & Topology Monographs
16 (2009), 31--47, MSP PDF.

The survey explicitly recalls Shafarevich's theorem: for a finite extension
`F/Q_p` not containing a primitive p-th root of unity, the Galois group of the
maximal p-extension is a free pro-p group on `[F:Q_p]+1` generators.

Application here: `F=Q_7(sqrt(5))` is the unramified quadratic extension and
does not contain `zeta_7`, so the rank is three.

Primary/survey locator:
https://msp.org/gtm/2009/16/gtm-2009-16-003p.pdf

## 2. Strong Massey vanishing over number fields

Source checked: Christian Maire, Jan Minac, Ravi Ramakrishna, Nguyen Duy Tan,
*On the strong Massey vanishing property for number fields*, Mathematische
Annalen 395 (2026), article 102, published 7 July 2026,
DOI 10.1007/s00208-026-03518-1.

The abstract states: for every `n>=3`, odd prime `p`, and number field `K` with
`zeta_p` not in `K`, the absolute and tame Galois groups satisfy the strong
n-fold Massey vanishing property relative to p.

Publisher locator:
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00208-026-03518-1

This result is contextual only.  It does not by itself imply vanishing of the
project's `F_49`-valued, cyclotomically twisted, locally constrained secondary
obstruction.

## 3. Triple Massey products

Source checked: Jan Minac and Nguyen Duy Tan, *Triple Massey products and Galois
theory*, J. Eur. Math. Soc. 19 (2017), 255--284, DOI 10.4171/JEMS/665.

The paper develops the relation between Massey products and unipotent Galois
representations.  We use it for terminology/background only; B77.1 is derived
directly from the project's adjoint cochain equations.

Locator:
https://ems.press/journals/jems/articles/14451
