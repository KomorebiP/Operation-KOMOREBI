# Gap ledger after Block61

## Closed in the stated scope

The complete ten-prime high-support class no longer has a depth-two opposite
extension. All four remaining local primes may individually pass their full
49-torsion tests, but a beta ramified only among them contradicts the same
natural Kummer coefficients and the three forced bad Frobenius values.
The new input is twisted reciprocity for beta, not more eta filtering.

3. Higher depth, arbitrary H(C), and actual full representation compatibility.
4. An independently accepted audit of the upstream geometric, monodromy,
   period, endpoint and class-number inputs. Replaying code is not that audit.
5. Separate CM nonboundary-point, higher Hilbert-level, complete signature,

## Do not reopen without new input

The four local families are valid controls, not alleged integer solutions.
The3x4 twisted matrix does have a nonzero line; it vanishes only after common
alpha compatibility. Ordinary ray rows do not apply to beta. Cohomological
descent on this inert support is not general semilinear Tate-lattice descent.
No depth-one contradiction follows from eta1 being trivial.
