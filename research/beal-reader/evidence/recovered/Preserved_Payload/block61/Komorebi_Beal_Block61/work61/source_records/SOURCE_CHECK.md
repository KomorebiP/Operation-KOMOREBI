## Freshly inspected primary exposition

`https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf`.
The title/version page and the following printed pages were inspected,
including rendered pages rather than relying on garbled parsed formulas:

- II, printed69 (PDF index77), Remark1.28(b): normal-quotient action on cohomology,
  with inner actions trivial. Used to choose different rational Frobenius lifts
  when checking conjugation invariance at the four inert support primes.
- II, printed70 (index78), Proposition1.30 and Corollary1.31: restriction followed
  by corestriction is index multiplication, and finite-group positive cohomology
  is killed by group order. Used for local/global injectivity and the vanishing
  of quotient cohomology for orders2 and6 on seven-primary coefficients.
- II, printed72 (index80), Remark1.35(a): five-term inflation–restriction sequence,
  including the invariant target and H2 obstruction. Used for existence and
  uniqueness of the descended beta class. The source labels this a remark,
  not a proof of an unrelated Tate-module descent theorem.
- V, printed150–151 (indices158–159), Theorem1.7 and proof: ray-class exact
  sequence and decomposition into residue unit factors. Used over M=Q(zeta7)
  with tame conductor at primes over S and chi^-1-equivariant target.
