# Gap ledger after Block79

PARTIALLY CLOSED G78-A: p cannot have auxiliary ramification outside fixed places
and beta support. This now supplies a justified finite Stage-P ramification set
for each frozen beta. The upper q-stage is different and remains unbounded.

NEW FINITE TARGET: choose/construct a particular global p for the restricted
problem, or compute the obstruction to its existence; determine its affine
translate in the finite character coordinates. Homogeneous difference dimensions
alone do not supply the translate. Then impose all beta-support toric hyperplanes
and fixed-seven data.

UPPER TARGET: for fixed p, q must take the two prescribed values at every beta
support. Any auxiliary upper ramification must occur where beta splits locally,
but that does not bound the set. Couple its permitted residues to actual global
periods and higher tame liftability.

Unchanged: eight residual patterns; bounded-conductor/A/C0 restrictions; positive
local Frey families; unrestricted beta supports and depths; minus component;
independent upstream mathematical review; CM/higher-Hilbert branches; full Beal.
