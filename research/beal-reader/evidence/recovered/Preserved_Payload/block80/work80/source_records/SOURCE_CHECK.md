## Dictionary and application audit

Milne's M=F7 for one ordinary coefficient coordinate. M^D=mu7.
For k use the F7 trace pairing, giving dual module k(chi). Sigma is FINITE,
contains all places above7 and infinity, and the cocycles are unramified outside
Sigma. Thus the module order is invertible in O_(K,Sigma). The two real-place
modified terms vanish at odd order. The proof of Sha1=0 is supplied in B80.1,
not cited as an unstated consequence of the source. This implies restricted
H2 injectivity; the absolute-group local-global theorem alone would not suffice.

The H1 image statement uses exactness at the local term and local Artin evaluation
against the fourteen Sigma-units. Class number, unit generator, unit independence,
all matrix coefficients and the self-conductor transfer convention are checked
in the new proofs. Principal-idele and norm functoriality conventions are the
same as the preserved Milne-CFT-based Block79 convention. The p difference has
TRIVIAL coefficients. The beta rows retain chi^-1 weights. Those two matrices
are not interchanged.
