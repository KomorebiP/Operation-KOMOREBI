closure of the actual reducible branch. The following levels are distinct.

centralizer classification, sign/gauge elimination, and the Fermat-quotient

finite code and not independently reviewed here.

three-generator natural class,7|C, all-depth coefficient theorem, and the bound's

No conductor or depth bound is needed for this necessary condition. Conversely,
its satisfaction gives only the indicated local class proportionality up to sign
and gauge, not a global representation or solution.
