## Scope and construction

The reader is one complete article with 25 content chapters, five exact Python
figures, a reading note, a table of contents, and an embedded bibliography. The
main TeX includes the prepared shared preamble. No small section is forced to a
new page. The cover and bibliography have deliberate page separation.

The source spine comes from the authenticated recovered Blocks 47–80 and the
earlier proof notes preserved in Block 47. Separate original archives for Blocks
1–46 were not present in this recovered DAG. The toolkit files are contextual
sources for the method. They are not treated as a theorem that all local
Diophantine data glue to integer solutions.

The opening chapters supply full proofs of the prime/four exponent reduction,
weighted parametrization and inverse, primitive valuation scaling, all-prime
squareclass coefficient criterion, and CRT congruence witnesses. They retain
the two everywhere locally soluble coefficient families and the distinction
between a local root and a positive primitive integer solution. The conditional
abc discussion includes the exact fixed-signature exponent and the uniform
all-Beal powered height bound. The unit-base reduction uses the accepted
classical consecutive-powers theorem with source qualifications.

The middle chapters reconstruct the actual curve/discriminant/Cartier identities,
labelled modular comparison, natural lattice segment, local depth relation,
common Kummer coefficients, period torsion criterion, twisted ray reciprocity,
bounded residual patterns, fixed-seven coordinates, nontriangular adjoint
equations, mixed trace probes, integral semilinearity and image-group boundary.

The final chapters prove the restricted Kummer kernel reduction, the conditional
finite-S bare primitive theorem, local fixed-trace primitive uniqueness, the
ordinary localization presentation, and the exact affine existence criterion
with origin independence. The arithmetic target is explicitly not supplied.
No actual pattern is eliminated by the final affine computation.

## Corrections and precision added during review

- The exact bound 2000 applies to every rational prime ramifying in beta. It is
  not a bound on the product conductor.
- Local Artin maps send the selected uniformizer to arithmetic Frobenius. This
  convention governs the principal reciprocity signs and toric affine rows.
- A global primitive is shifted by a global character. Its local image is Jz.
  The local vector itself is never added to a global cochain.
- The fixed primitive uniqueness statement is local in its fixed frame, rather
  than uniqueness of a global first-stage solution.
- The local depth lemma explicitly requires integral matrices, a primitive
  integral line, nonzero integral monodromy content, and a unit Frobenius norm.
- The odd-C irreducibility summary retains eighth powers in its reciprocity
  step and the determinant norm identity, instead of assuming an extra rank-two
  normalization.
- The actual higher global extension is restricted to its inherited triangular
  precision m=floor(n/2)+1, intersected with the period window for unit formulas.
- The mixed trace defines nu7 through local strict alignment, without erasing
  beta from the global representation. All-depth detection uses j>=n+t+1.
- The secondary obstruction is for a general fixed primitive. An explicit
  fixed-local upper primitive prevents an unsupported assertion that the actual
  bare upper H2 class is known nonzero. Prescribed geometry remains separate.
- Ordinary L is the transpose of global Kummer localization under local perfect
  pairings. Complete nullspace, not merely NA=0, gives sufficiency.
- Legacy Block71 unit-domain metadata is corrected to 2352 in the narrative.
  Original outputs remain unchanged.
- Block67 scientific PASS is distinguished from a new generic wrapper parser
  failure. Block69's archival wrapper timeout remains inconclusive, while its
  separate portable scientific run passed at unchanged bounds.
- A recent September2026 signature proof claim is reported as a separate,
  unaudited preprint. No literature-wide absence of a proof is asserted.
