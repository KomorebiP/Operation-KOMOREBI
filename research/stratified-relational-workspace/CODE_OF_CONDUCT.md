# Code of Conduct

Discussion in this repository must remain professional, specific, and evidence-oriented.

Participants are expected to:

- criticize claims, proofs, experiments, and repository practices rather than people;
- identify assumptions and evidence precisely;
- distinguish errors from disagreements about scope or terminology;
- disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect a review;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, spam, and deliberate disruption;
- respect private information and unpublished reviewer communications.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove material that violates these standards and may restrict participation when necessary to preserve a productive review environment.
