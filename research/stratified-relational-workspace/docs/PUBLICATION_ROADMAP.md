# SRWT Publication Roadmap

## Strategic decision

The 209-page monograph should now remain frozen as the master reference. The next stage is not further conceptual expansion. It is publication decomposition, external theorem review, and empirical testing.

The mathematical core is the strongest lead paper because it can be evaluated independently of the more controversial claims concerning cognition, agency, or human--AI systems. It has a single technical arc, a controlled novelty position, and a self-contained proof structure.

## Paper I — completed

**Core--Transfer Geometry of Relational Workspaces: Holonomy, Spectral Coherence, Gauge Covariance, and Faithful Coarse-Graining**

Retained material:

- core--residual systems and directed transfers
- exact global compatible sections
- holonomy obstructions and fixed cores
- the workspace operation algebra
- transfer discrepancy operators and Laplacians
- spectral extraction and perturbation stability
- approximate operation closure
- pathwise residual leakage
- gauge covariance
- faithful coarse-graining and decomposition trivializations

Excluded material:

- causal certification
- empirical relational irreducibility
- temporal identity and persistence
- formation provenance
- agency, repair, and self-maintenance
- consciousness and ethical implications

Immediate publication action: circulate this paper for specialist mathematical review before representing the synthesis as established. The highest-value reviewers would cover local systems/sheaves, spectral perturbation, and distributed or multi-agent systems.

## Paper II — next extraction

**Causal Certification of Relational Workspaces**

Proposed core:

- predictive causal states
- structural--operational alignment
- coalition risk and irreducibility
- matched predictive nulls
- feasible workspace interventions
- causal specificity and reentry
- decomposition and specification robustness
- identifiability and finite-sample certification

This paper should include a compact synthetic benchmark implementation. It should not rely only on definitions and population-level theorems.

## Paper III

**Persistence, Identity, Formation, and Workspace Agency**

Proposed core:

- moving workspace subspaces
- stable reconstruction and erasure resilience
- identity under replacement
- local-to-global gluing and hierarchy
- endogenous organization and boundary recovery
- goal persistence, counterfactual action selection, repair, and self-maintenance

This is the most conceptually ambitious paper and should follow the structural and causal papers rather than lead them.

## Empirical benchmark paper and software

A separate reproducibility package should implement:

1. exact synthetic local-system cases with known holonomy and workspace dimension;
2. approximate transfer systems with controlled spectral gaps and perturbations;
3. centralized, replicated, secret-shared, and genuinely distributed nulls;
4. participant, artifact, and channel ablations;
5. replacement and handoff tests;
6. frozen decomposition and specification sweeps;
7. finite-sample confidence envelopes for every required certification gate.

## Submission sequence

1. Freeze and externally audit Paper I.
2. Publish the benchmark code and synthetic datasets.
3. Complete Paper II with empirical results.
4. Revise the monograph in response to both mathematical and empirical criticism.
5. Extract Paper III only after the causal certification framework has survived testing.

This sequence reduces the risk that the full theory is judged primarily by its broadest claims before its structural core receives a fair mathematical evaluation.
