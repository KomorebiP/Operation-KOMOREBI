# Review guide

The monograph is broad. Reviews are most useful when scoped to one mathematical or empirical layer.

## Structural mathematics

Review Parts II-V and XVII for:

- the core-residual block calculus;
- limit-holonomy correspondence;
- transfer-Laplacian kernel and perturbation arguments;
- approximate operation closure and residual leakage;
- gauge covariance and faithful coarse-graining.

## Dynamic and causal theory

Review Parts VI-IX and XVIII for:

- canonical predictive states;
- structural-operational alignment;
- coalition risk and matched nulls;
- feasible interventions, causal specificity, reentry;
- identifiability and finite-sample certification.

## Persistence, identity, and hierarchy

Review Parts X-XIII for:

- moving-subspace stability;
- frame reconstruction and erasure resilience;
- replacement identity;
- Čech-style gluing and hierarchical collapse.

## Formation and agency

Review Parts XIV-XVI for:

- transfer identification and held-out selection;
- boundary recovery and formation transitions;
- goal retention, counterfactual policy evaluation, repair, and self-maintenance.

## Priority and interpretation

Review Parts XXIII-XXVI and `audits/priority_register.csv` for:

- whether inherited mathematics is correctly classified;
- whether synthesis claims are stated narrowly enough;
- whether philosophical conclusions follow from the formal results;
- whether the consciousness and moral-status non-implications remain explicit.

## Reporting an issue

Use the theorem-review or erratum issue form. Cite the source label from `audits/statement_index.md` whenever possible. A minimal report states the claim, the exact concern, and the evidence needed to reproduce it.
