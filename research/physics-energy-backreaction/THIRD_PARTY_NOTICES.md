# Third-party and inherited material

Python, NumPy, SciPy, SymPy, Matplotlib, TeX, and their dependencies are external software.
They are not distributed in this package and retain their own licenses. No font files are included.

The original benchmark JSON and inherited verification script are copied from the preceding
open Komorebi Physics source package. Their exact original names and SHA-256 hashes are
recorded in PROVENANCE.json. The inherited script is not presented as newly authored here.

The cited research articles are bibliographic references, not redistributed articles. Their authors
are credited in the manuscript. No claims are made over external material.
