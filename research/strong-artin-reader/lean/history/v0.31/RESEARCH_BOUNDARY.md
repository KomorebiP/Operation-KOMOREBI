# Research boundary — v0.31

## Proven at the stated scope

- One local kernel conjugator can be absorbed into the global Frobenius root.
- On an explicitly diagonalized actual kernel action, the norm image is exactly
  the set with zero coordinates at the zero geometric-sum multipliers.
- Both resonance mechanisms are computed exactly: degree torsion at lambda=1,
  and nontrivial d-th roots of unity at lambda!=1.
- In characteristic p the prime-degree norm multiplier is (lambda-1)^(p-1).
- A genuine dual-number matrix construction supplies the spectral data, rather
  than assuming that matrix entries are eigenvectors without proof.
- Defining-characteristic mismatch in the full centralizer block may obstruct;
  its total trace is not sufficient.
- A nonresonant degree supplies an actual continuous strictly prescribed
  procyclic lift, with finite discrete coefficients.
- Simultaneous restriction has a complete spectral criterion: independent
  noninvariant corrections plus one shared invariant correction.
- This computes vanishing of the actual inherited norm-localization and
  continuous local-gauge obstruction classes.
- Genuine nonzero classes and positive cases are checked.

## Non-claims

1. Diagonalizability of conjugation on the kernel is explicit. It is not a
   consequence of finite residual image in defining characteristic.
2. The automatic matrix construction is over k[epsilon]/epsilon^2. General
   mixed-characteristic coefficient rings require their own kernel coordinates.
3. One Frobenius and its procyclic degree restrictions are not a global number-
   field localization problem. Separate places need an arithmetic comparison.
4. The local-field inertia quotient and its extension-degree dictionary remain
   external. No ramified, crystalline, de Rham, polarized or fixed-type condition
   has been identified with this elementary unramified problem.
5. The single-prescription strict/gauge equivalence does not permit absorbing
   many independent conjugators into one Frobenius; the simultaneous theorem
   and counterexample retain the obstruction.
6. p-th-power prescription vanishing is not automatic even for diagonal
   Frobenius. An obstructed example is constructed in every positive rank.
7. No compatible full coefficient tower, arithmetic global Selmer vanishing,
   auxiliary-prime descent, automorphic carrier, or Strong Artin case is proved.
8. All relevant finite and continuous cohomology objects are inherited at their
   exact v0.30/v0.29 boundaries. No new equivalence to an unspecified derived API
   is asserted.
9. The geometric-sum and matrix power-map mechanisms are standard. Historical
   novelty is not claimed; this is checked integration and classification.

## Next consequential work

The nonsemisimple kernel action and actual mixed-characteristic local layers
need separate analysis: on a Jordan block the norm is a polynomial in its
nilpotent part, not just an eigenvalue multiplier. After such a block calculation,
prove compatibility through successive coefficient levels or instantiate the
actual local-field quotient. Arithmetic global localization and automorphic
occurrence remain independent tasks.
