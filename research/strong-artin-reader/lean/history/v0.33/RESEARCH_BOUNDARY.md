# Research boundary — v0.33

## Proven exactly

1. For any endomorphism N and a family of powers N^r_i, a least-exponent pivot gives a necessary-and-sufficient image criterion for one shared x with independent terms N z_i.
2. In prime characteristic p, this classifies simultaneous degree-p^a_i Frobenius norm localization for every linear T. No semisimplicity or nilpotence is needed in this case.
3. On a supplied primary block with eigenvalue lambda!=1, 1-T is invertible; simultaneous matching is equivalent to individual image membership.
4. On a nilpotent eigenvalue-one primary block, exact invertible normalization reduces every positive mixed-degree family to the power-filtration criterion.
5. These criteria compute zero of the actual inherited square-zero Frobenius matching class, and therefore classify actual simultaneous unit lifts with independent local kernel conjugators.
6. Prime-power and unipotent mixed-degree criteria compute zero of the inherited continuous local-gauge obstruction for genuine procyclic degree maps with finite discrete coefficients.
7. The matrix controls prove individual-versus-simultaneous failure and a positive shared lift on actual dual-number matrices.

## Essential limits

* For arbitrary square-zero coefficient extensions, a field-linear coordinate model of the actual kernel is an explicit hypothesis. The inherited automatic matrix model is for dual numbers, not all mixed-characteristic layers.
* Mixed-degree primary statements require the supplied nilpotent primary structure. No automatic primary/Jordan decomposition is constructed here.
* The simultaneous criterion allows independent local kernel conjugators. It does not assert all prescriptions can be matched literally or with one common conjugator.
* The pairwise/pivot sufficiency is a nested-filtration result in this structured norm problem. It does not reverse the earlier general gluing counterexamples.
* Infinite index families are supported algebraically when a pivot is provided; least natural exponent exists for a nonempty family. Positive degree is built into the prime-power/mixed-degree hypotheses. No assertion at an undefined p-adic valuation of degree zero is made.
* Only a square-zero coefficient step is proved. Vanishing must still be established compatibly at every level of a coefficient tower.
* The continuous corollaries concern one procyclic source and its degree restrictions. They are not global localization maps over the places of a number field.
* No new inertia/local-field dictionary, ramified/crystalline/de Rham/polarized condition, arithmetic Selmer vanishing, auxiliary descent, automorphic carrier, or Strong Artin case is supplied.
* No claim of historical novelty is made. These are explicit formal integrations of standard norm identities, nilpotent functional calculus, and filtered linear algebra.
