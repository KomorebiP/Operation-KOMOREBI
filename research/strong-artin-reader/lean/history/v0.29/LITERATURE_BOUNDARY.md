# Literature and conventions

Checked 2026-09-11 (America/Chicago).

Primary convention source: Stacks Project, tag 0DVG, Section 59.58,
https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0DVG . It defines continuous inhomogeneous
cochains with d0(x)(g)=g.x-x and the standard alternating differential. The current
construction follows this convention, with an explicit signed low-degree
mapping-fibre quotient for local conjugacy. No universal derived comparison is
imported from the source.

Primary formal documentation: Mathlib group cohomology LowDegree,
https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/LowDegree.html .
The build uses the PINNED local source/cache at
`aeb72f1e9981b6d35b426b16dca7759be3e7ba06`, not an unpinned live documentation API.

This is an explicit formalization/integration of standard deformation and cochain
mechanisms. No claim of historical novelty, general Selmer comparison, universal
localization surjectivity, or a new unconditional automorphic case is made.
