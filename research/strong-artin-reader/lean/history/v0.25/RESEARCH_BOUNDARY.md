# Research boundary after v0.25

## Closed as written

- Exact moving identities imply compatibility of normalized leading errors.
- The residue image of the Jacobian is enough; no integral split target is needed.
- A principal nilpotent residue kernel automatically gives the necessary leading-layer faithfulness before its first vanishing power.
- Finite coordinate polynomial systems automatically supply Taylor gain and coefficient/derivative reduction comparisons.
- Exact moving relations and residual exactness imply a root of the ORIGINAL equation system.
- Dual exactness ker(J*)=range(B*) supplies the required primal exactness.
- Associativity is an exact moving identity for actual representation errors.
- Square-zero representation lifts exist exactly when a kernel-valued linearized correction cancels their defect.

## Substantive hypotheses not discharged

The polynomial theorem assumes actual exact identities B(x)F(x)=0 and exactness
image(Jbar)=kernel(Bbar). It does not infer these from dimensions, an abstract
isomorphism to a syzygy space, or the residual Jacobian alone. Genuine higher
obstructions can persist if those hypotheses fail.

The square-zero representation theorem proves a necessary and sufficient
correction criterion. It does NOT prove every compatible two-cochain lies in
the correction image, define the complete arithmetic obstruction class, prove
continuous H^2 vanishing, or construct a Galois representation for an automorphic
packet without additional arithmetic input.

The natural general obstruction objects involve cotangent complexes and Ext
(or Galois deformation cohomology in its appropriate category). No result here
identifies the prior H1Cotangent object, by itself, with every such obstruction
space. Base change, duals, degree conventions and local conditions still need
an actual comparison theorem.

## Coefficient scope

The strongest exact-root theorem uses a principal nilpotent kernel of a
surjection to a field. It includes ordinary finite DVR coefficient levels.
It is not a theorem about all local Artin rings with arbitrary nonprincipal
maximal ideal. It does not produce an inverse-limit solution over a complete
DVR, nor formal-series evaluation, without separate compatible-system or
formal-presentation arguments.

## Crucial descent boundary

A root of F(x)+C(y) is not automatically a root of F(x). If the arithmetic
application introduces auxiliary variables, it must prove the actual local
conditions and the descent/specialization back to the desired arithmetic
problem. This checkpoint does not permit dropping that step.

## Dominant remaining goal

Construct the exact relation/correction complex for the actual continuous
fixed-restriction deformation problem, identify its obstruction and local
conditions, and show that genuine arithmetic auxiliaries give residual exactness.

Independent global existence: a nonzero patchable determinant-bearing
finite-Satake automorphic carrier remains missing. Local Tate comparison,
Chebotarev instantiation, relative patching, twist equivariance, ramified
attachment, exceptional solvable steps and unrestricted Strong Artin remain
unproved here.
