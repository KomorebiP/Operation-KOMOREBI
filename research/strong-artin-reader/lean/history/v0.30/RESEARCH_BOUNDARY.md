# v0.30 research boundary

## Proven

1. Actual Mathlib profinite completion of Z with dense integer powers; continuous maps to profinite groups are equivalent to a chosen Frobenius image.
2. Lifts across surjective profinite group maps exist without an image-order assumption.
3. For finite discrete square-zero coefficient extensions, every procyclic representation lifts, including GL_n. Its constructed continuous obstruction is zero.
4. Given an actual compact-source procyclic quotient nu, inertia-trivial representations factor through it and lift while preserving triviality on its kernel.
5. Restrictions along the constructed degree maps become exact Frobenius power equations, including constant independent local kernel conjugators.
6. The square-zero Frobenius power correction is the exact norm N_(F,d), and local gauge is the exact coboundary 1-Ad(F^d).
7. The actual v0.29 class vanishes iff the actual mismatch vanishes in the explicit norm-localization cokernel.
8. The C3 mod9 obstruction disappears after inflation to Zhat, but a trivial degree-three prescription makes the constructed local class nonzero again. Both conclusions are proved on actual obstruction classes.

## Non-claims

- No new Strong Artin case, automorphic representation, determinant-bearing carrier, Taylor-Wiles prime theorem or global localization surjectivity is obtained.
- This is an unramified local group condition. It is not a theorem about arbitrary ramified, crystalline, de Rham, polarized or prescribed-inertial-type deformation conditions.
- The theorem uses an actual profinite completion, not a discrete source or a finite cyclic surrogate. The actual local-field inertia quotient and the identification of its Frobenius/extension-degree maps are classical external input, not formalized here.
- The norm-localization cokernel governs the displayed family of degree maps between procyclic groups. It is NOT identified with an arithmetic global Selmer complex or all-place localization without a further theorem.
- Equality of vanishing is proved between the v0.29 class and the explicit norm class. A canonical isomorphism of the entire cohomology objects is not claimed.
- Unconstrained procyclic lifts need not preserve the original residual finite quotient or the whole kernel. The checked example proves that preserving it may be impossible.
- No fixed restriction is assumed liftable simply because the unramified problem is unobstructed. The norm class must vanish; the negative example shows why.
- The abstract profinite-group projectivity theorem allows profinite targets. The ring/matrix obstruction specializations in this release use finite discrete coefficient rings and one square-zero step. A compatible coefficient tower and its class comparison are separate.
- No blanket vanishing of global continuous H2 is asserted; the constructed representation-lifting class in this sector is zero.
- The norm identities are valid without group-order invertibility; this is not a proof of wild ramified obstruction vanishing.
- Historical novelty is not claimed. The universal-property argument and unramified lifting mechanism are standard. The achievement is checked integration, explicit localization and the discriminating example.

## Next consequential question

Use the explicit norm operator to treat a prescribed unramified extension restriction, with its actual conjugacy condition. Prove a substantive norm-surjectivity or distinguished-class vanishing theorem under verified spectral/order assumptions, or compute a definite obstruction. For an arithmetic local field, separately construct the inertia quotient/Frobenius dictionary.

Beyond that remain ramified local conditions, global arithmetic localization, auxiliary descent and the automorphic carrier. None is discharged by unramified local lifting.
