# v0.35 research boundary

## Closed

- Original Frobenius power equation is injective on a square-zero residue fibre when its actual kernel norm is injective.
- Bijective kernel norm gives a unique next-level root for every compatible prescribed target.
- The actual positive Z/p-power tower and reduction maps are constructed; its modulus is proved p^(r+1).
- A single bijective residual norm gives unique roots at all levels and their exact compatibility.
- Compatible entries and inverses assemble into genuine PadicInt matrix units, with exact residue formulas.
- A fixed-residue p-adic d-th root exists uniquely under residual norm nonresonance.
- Unipotent adjoint plus p not dividing d implies nonresonance; this is proved, not assumed at every layer.
- A nontrivial unipotent F_3 matrix has the resulting universal 3-adic square-root theorem.
- Simultaneous literal prescriptions have an exact shared-power criterion with a nonresonant pivot. On the identity residue fibre only that pivot degree must be prime to p.
- A solvable mod-9 cube-root branch has no mod-27 or 3-adic continuation, even though another full branch exists.

## Essential limitations

1. Nonresonance is a substantive sufficient condition. For positive matrix rank, degree divisible by p kills the scalar identity under the residual norm, so this condition cannot resolve the general prime-power-degree resonant problem.
2. Nonsemisimple residual image is permitted, but the stated unipotent sufficient condition still requires p not dividing the pivot degree.
3. A compatible target tower is data. We prove existence and compatibility of its root tower, not the existence of the target or an automorphic source for it.
4. Simultaneous matching is literal equality of matrix powers. The power identities and each stated nonresonance hypothesis are required. Arbitrary separate local roots do not imply a shared root.
5. The p-adic matrix is built in Mathlib PadicInt, not a surrogate inverse-limit type. No new general continuous representation-lifting or procyclic-to-local-field dictionary is constructed here.
6. A failed resonant branch is not a counterexample to all tower existence or Strong Artin.
7. General ramified coefficient rings, resonant prescribed towers, arithmetic Selmer/localization vanishing, auxiliary-level descent, determinant-bearing automorphic carriers and Strong Artin remain open.
8. No percentages, universal Resolution Theory theorem, or historical novelty claim.
