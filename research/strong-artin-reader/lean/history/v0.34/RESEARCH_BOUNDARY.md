# Research boundary — v0.34

## Closed at the stated scope

1. Actual additive coordinates for ker(Z/(m*p) -> Z/m), for m != 0.
2. Square-zero multiplication on that kernel when p divides m.
3. Explicit coordinate inverse by integer division by m.
4. Actual matrix-kernel coordinates and coefficient multiplication rules.
5. Automatic conversion of upper conjugation to the adjoint action of reduction modulo p.
6. Surjective matrix reduction and existence of an initial upper Frobenius unit.
7. Single prescribed-power image criteria on these mixed-characteristic layers.
8. Simultaneous criteria on the unchanged norm-localization cokernel with one shared correction.
9. Uniform specialization to every prime p and positive layer r, including defining-characteristic and nonsemisimple residual Frobenius.
10. Genuine mixed-characteristic positive and obstructed examples, including individually liftable but incompatible family prescriptions.
11. The scalar layer embedding does not have an additive retraction from the upper ring. No split-ring hypothesis is used.

## Essential limits

* The base generality is Z/(m*p) -> Z/m with p|m; the prime-power application is Z/p^(r+1) -> Z/p^r, r >= 1. Arbitrary ramified DVR layers and arbitrary finite extensions of F_p are not constructed here.
* Coordinates identify the additive kernel with matrices over F_p. They are NOT a unital ring section F_p -> Z/p^(r+1).
* The matrixDatum is a convenient datum for this actual coefficient map. The Frobenius norm and matching cokernel depend on the actual kernel, chosen upper Frobenius and target powers. No global representation or local Galois group is invented.
* We do not construct an additional homogeneous/derived continuous-cohomology equivalence or claim a new continuous local-gauge capstone beyond inherited boundaries.
* Matching permits independent local conjugators reducing to 1 modulo p^r. It does not assert literal matching of a family or one common conjugator.
* Kernel coordinates exist at every layer, but vanishing at one layer does not imply vanishing at the next. The next obstruction depends on the lifted point and the prescribed targets.
* No full inverse-limit representation, prescribed coefficient tower, arithmetic Selmer vanishing, automorphic carrier, new Strong Artin case, or unrestricted Strong Artin theorem is obtained.
* The residue-layer and norm mechanisms are standard. Historical novelty is not asserted.

## Next distinct target

Construct compatible prescribed lifts through successive coefficient levels using the actual normalized mismatch at each stage. A useful theorem must either give a substantive sufficient condition preserved by the correction choices, or record the precise first obstructed layer. Do not replace this with a statement assuming vanishing of every obstruction and call that arithmetic vanishing.

Global localization and the automorphic carrier remain independent tasks. Preserve the prior audit against confusing an auxiliary-equation solution with a solution of the original arithmetic problem.
