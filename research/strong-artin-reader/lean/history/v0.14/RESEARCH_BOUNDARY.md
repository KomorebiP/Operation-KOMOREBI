# Research boundary after Strong Artin Lean v0.14

## Closed internally

The finite algebra and degree-one cohomology now produce a genuine pre-Chebotarev element. In the clean finite residual-image regime, and under the explicit subgroup-centrality and relative spectral hypotheses, a nonzero transverse `H¹` class yields an element with:

- prescribed residual image;
- trivial deep cyclotomic label;
- nonzero actual centered-projector evaluation.

The transverse coefficient module is the literal quotient `End(W)/k·1`. Its no-fixed-vector property is proved from irreducibility, Schur’s lemma, and finite-group averaging.

## Still arithmetic/external

1. **Concrete cyclotomic centrality.** Show that the subgroup quotient used at depth `N` is central/trivial under residual conjugation. In number-field language this is the required linear-disjointness/direct-product statement.
2. **Finite quotient packaging.** Ensure residual, cyclotomic, cocycle-extension, and ramification labels all factor through one finite Galois quotient.
3. **Chebotarev.** Realize the selected conjugacy class by an unramified prime outside the prescribed finite set.
4. **Local pairing.** Identify the formal centered projector evaluation with the local Tate-duality quotient generated by the Taylor–Wiles local condition.
5. **Finite set and patching.** Iterate to kill the transverse dual Selmer group and prove sufficient support over the retained cyclic defect algebra.
6. **Packet occurrence.** Place the singular finite-Satake descent in a determinant-bearing automorphic/cohomological carrier.

## Non-claim

v0.14 does not prove a Taylor–Wiles theorem, automorphy lifting, solvable Strong Artin, or unrestricted Strong Artin. Its capstone is the exact algebraic/cohomological input immediately preceding those arithmetic steps.
