# Research boundary — v0.36

## Exactly proved

- All-depth finite solvability implies coherent p-adic solvability for genuine
  finite matrix-unit towers, including resonance and an arbitrary fixed prefix.
- The root and every member of a finite family of local conjugators are selected
  together; independently chosen finite witnesses need not initially be compatible.
- Failure has a finite empty extension level. Each fixed finite prefix level has
  a finite survivor horizon, without a computed bound.

## Crucial non-claims

1. This is a compactness EXISTENCE CRITERION, not proof that every target or every
   prefix has roots at every level. Resonant obstruction vanishing is still needed.
2. A finite tested set of levels does not establish the all-depth hypothesis.
3. Arbitrary one-step correction choices need not extend. Only persistent nodes
   are shown to have persistent children; persistence is not an effective oracle.
4. The finite horizon depends on the full fixed problem. No universal numerical
   bound, algorithm for arbitrary p-adic input, or finite-computation proof of an
   infinite statement is claimed. Adjacent equal survivor sets alone do not certify
   stabilization.
5. Uniqueness is not claimed. The two roots +1 and -1 in Z_2 give a checked control.
6. The simultaneous theorem has finitely many prescriptions. All levels must
   solve the joint tuple, not just each equation separately. Conjugators reduce
   to identity modulo p; the Frobenius prefix may be fixed at a deeper level.
7. The conclusion is an actual matrix-unit solution over PadicInt. It does not
   identify a local-field Galois group, a Selmer complex, or an automorphic object.
8. General ramified coefficient rings, effective resonant image criteria,
   arithmetic global localization, auxiliary descent, and Strong Artin remain open.
9. This is standard finite-branch compactness integrated with the actual tower.
   No historical novelty claim or progress percentage is made.

## Next distinct target

Give a genuinely effective finite-depth bound or an explicit image criterion for
one specified resonant power-map sector (not merely assume all-depth occupancy).
Alternatively, use the actual layer norm to prove a persistent family of prefixes
for a specified matrix target. Preserve distinctions between existence, branch
persistence, uniqueness, and arithmetic automorphy.
