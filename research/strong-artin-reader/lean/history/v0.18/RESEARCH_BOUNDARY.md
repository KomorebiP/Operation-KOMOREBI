# Research boundary after Strong Artin Lean v0.18

## Strongest new internal theorem

For a finite cyclic group generated by `g`, a matrix-conjugation representation `ρ`, and a vanishing norm operator, Lean proves

```text
tameInertiaRelationSpace F 1
  ≃ₗ[k]
Dual k (groupCohomology.H1 (Rep.of ρ)).
```

If a cyclic `H¹` class is represented by `X`, the pairing with a tame-relation representative `P` is exactly

```text
trace(P * X).
```

Every cyclic `H¹` functional has a unique tame-relation representative.

## Normalization reduction

Let `L` be a one-dimensional arithmetic local quotient and suppose a linear equivalence

```text
L ≃ₗ[k] groupCohomology.H1 (Rep.of ρ)
```

has been constructed. For any nonzero tame direction `P` and any nonzero arithmetic Tate functional on `L`, Lean proves that the Tate functional is one unique unit multiple of the transported trace functional. Therefore

```text
Tate(x) ≠ 0 ↔ trace(P * X) ≠ 0
```

whenever the comparison sends `x` to the cyclic class represented by `X`.

## What changed

The v0.17 quotient

```text

```

is no longer merely an algebraic surrogate: it is identified with actual first group cohomology of a finite cyclic quotient under an explicit zero-norm hypothesis.

The exact sign and unit normalization of arithmetic local Tate duality is no longer a logical prerequisite for auxiliary-prime detection. Only nonvanishing of the arithmetic pairing on the selected one-dimensional line is required.

The first arrow must prove continuity/factorization and the zero-norm finite quotient hypothesis. The second must come from local Tate duality with conventions fixed. Neither is asserted by v0.18.

## Global boundary

Even a complete local comparison does not manufacture the initial automorphic object. The dominant global gap remains:

```text
finite-Satake cyclic descent
  -> nonzero patchable residual packet in a determinant-bearing complex
  -> flat/MCM relative patched module or finite-depth carrier.
```

Strong Artin, solvable-image Strong Artin, arithmetic Taylor--Wiles patching, and universal packet occurrence remain open.
