# Research boundary after Strong Artin Lean v0.16

## Closed internally

v0.16 closes the following formal bridges.

1. **Auxiliary detection to Jacobian surjectivity.** If admissible local columns detect the dual obstruction space, the v0.15 exact finite set makes the augmented Jacobian surjective.
2. **Prime-power splitting compatibility.** A finite `p`-group label can be made trivial without losing any element of a finite prime-to-`p` residual quotient.
3. **Finite affine quotient packaging.** A normalized cocycle, residual quotient, and finite cyclotomic/splitting label define one finite quotient after restricting to the range.
4. **Conjugacy-class stability.** A commuting projector annihilates the cocycle coboundary generated by conjugation, so nonzero projector detection survives on the entire finite conjugacy class.
5. **Local cotangent algebra.** The projector trace descends through `range (1 - Ad A)` and equals the derivative of a determinant coordinate.

## First open arithmetic implication

```text
v0.16 Chebotarev-ready finite conjugacy class
  -> continuous finite Galois extension realizing the combined quotient
  -> Chebotarev prime outside the prescribed bad set
  -> q_v ≡ 1 (mod p^N), splitting in E, and the desired residual block
  -> exact local Taylor--Wiles/Tate functional.
```

Chebotarev itself is external. The exact comparison between the quotient trace functional and the normalized local Tate pairing remains unproved.

## First open deformation implication

```text
arithmetic local Taylor--Wiles functional
  -> actual auxiliary column in the global deformation/Hecke Jacobian
  -> v0.16 augmented-Jacobian surjectivity
  -> finite-depth relative lifting or patched full support.
```

v0.16 proves the middle linear algebra only. It does not prove that the arithmetic local condition supplies the modeled column, nor that first-order surjectivity persists through the nonlinear deformation problem.

## Principal global boundary

Even a complete auxiliary-prime theorem cannot patch a zero automorphic module. The dominant remaining global problem is still:

```text
finite-Satake cyclic descent
  -> nonzero residual occurrence in a determinant-bearing automorphic/cohomological complex.
```

After occurrence, one must prove relative patching/full support over the cyclic defect factor or lift through the explicit finite precision threshold.

## Non-claims

v0.16 does not prove:

- a number-field Chebotarev theorem;
- the local Tate-duality calculation;
- a Taylor--Wiles theorem or an `R = T` theorem;
- residual packet occurrence;
- a determinant-bearing carrier;
- ramified local attachment;
- solvable-image Strong Artin;
- unrestricted Strong Artin.
