# Research boundary after Strong Artin Lean v0.20

## Strongest closed theorem

Let `q : R →+* k` be a surjective homomorphism to a field with

```text
RingHom.ker q = Ideal.span {pi},
```

where `pi` is nilpotent in `R`.  Let `system` be a finite-coordinate polynomial system generated by constants, coordinates, addition, negation, and multiplication.  If `x₀` solves the system modulo `pi` and the residual Jacobian

```text
map q (jacobianMatrix system x₀)
```

is surjective, Lean proves

```text
∃ x, SameResidual pi x₀ x ∧ evalSystem system x = 0.
```

The proof constructs the residual matrix right inverse, lifts it entrywise, proves residual-fibre Jacobian stability, proves automatic polynomial Taylor gain, and invokes the v0.19 variable-Jacobian nonlinear Hensel theorem.

## What changed

The v0.19 hypotheses

```text
stable approximate right inverse throughout the residual fibre
+ one-power nonlinear Taylor gain
```

are no longer independent assumptions for finite polynomial presentations.  They follow from:

```text
surjectivity of the residual coordinate Jacobian.
```

Thus the abstract nonlinear lane is reduced to one standard arithmetic comparison:

```text
the residual augmented Jacobian produced by the Taylor--Wiles/Selmer argument
is the coordinate Jacobian of the finite deformation/Hecke presentation.
```

## Remaining arithmetic boundary

1. that the actual Strong Artin deformation/Hecke equations have been presented by the polynomial system used here;

