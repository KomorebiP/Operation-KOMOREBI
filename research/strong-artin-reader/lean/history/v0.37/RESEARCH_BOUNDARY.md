# Research boundary — v0.37

## Closed in this checkpoint

- Exact local image of a scalar integral polynomial on a ball smaller than its
  nonzero derivative norm.
- A single explicit horizon H=max(m,v_p(d)+1)+v_p(d) for d>0, m>=1, and a scalar
  unit prefix. Arbitrary resonance p|d is permitted.
- Exact deep-prefix image and uniqueness for m>v_p(d).
- A computable horizon obstruction when no scalar root exists above the prefix.
- One finite horizon implies all deeper finite solvability in this sector.
- An exact criterion within the fixed diagonal algebra and construction of
  an actual invertible p-adic matrix from its coordinate certificates.

## Boundaries retained

1. General resonant matrix power maps are not classified. A scalar derivative
   d a^(d-1) is not the general matrix derivative sum A^i X A^(d-1-i).
2. The matrix equivalence is restricted to roots in a fixed diagonal algebra.
   The one-way matrix-unit consequence produces actual matrices. It does not
   make a claim about every root of a diagonal target.
3. Nonunit prefixes and d=0 are excluded. Positive m is required to preserve
   unit status from the prescribed prefix.
4. H is a valid explicit bound, not proved optimal. Shallow prefixes may have
   multiple roots. Uniqueness applies only to the stated deep fibres.
5. Success at H certifies the original prefix, not each witness at H. The
   finite witness may change above k=max(m,v_p(d)+1).
6. These are finite tests on the required digits of a specified p-adic input.
   No access to an arbitrary uncomputable p-adic sequence is assumed.
7. No effective simultaneous gauge-conjugator criterion for general matrix
   targets, general ramified coefficient ring theorem, arithmetic localization,
   Selmer vanishing, auxiliary descent, or automorphic carrier is proved.
8. Strong Artin remains open. No historical novelty claim is made. The proof
   specializes classical Hensel lifting and supplies a formal finite horizon.
9. Fresh verification covers the four new source modules plus their axiom audit,
   using the pinned Mathlib cache. It does not rerun the preserved historical
   source closure, use a second kernel, or constitute independent specialist review.

## Next distinct target

Establish an effective local image theorem for the genuinely noncommutative
matrix power map with a derivative invertible over Q_p but singular modulo p.
An explicit bound on the denominators of the inverse derivative could control
a sufficiently small ball. Its integral remainder estimates and prefix-preserving
correction must be proved; the present scalar theorem does not supply them.
Cases with derivative singular even over Q_p require an additional argument.
