# Claim status and exact boundaries

## Unconditional results proved in the article

1. Galois extensions of one invariant irreducible representation form a quotient character torsor.
2. Characteristic polynomial coefficients rotate predictably under quotient twisting.
3. Determinant selects one cyclic torsor member when the quotient prime does not divide the rank.
4. Algebraic central characters and sufficiently ramified epsilon twists are blind when the quotient prime divides the rank.
5. The total induced object is invariant under cyclic torsor rotation and cannot select the offset.
6. Local extension spectra give an exact finite criterion for quotient twisting.
7. Global cyclic attachment is equivalent to one cyclic index lying in every local alignment set.
8. A descent of an Artin base change is Artin type at every place.
9. In the finite Galois base change setting, all normalized unramified Satake roots lie in one finite cyclotomic group.
10. The resulting Hecke polynomials form a finite integral alphabet.
11. A semisimple finite residual image with trace alphabet of size t has order at most t to the power n squared when the residual characteristic exceeds n.
12. One sufficiently large residual realization of a finite polynomial alphabet yields a characteristic zero finite image representation with the exact Frobenius polynomials.
13. One good separating residual realization gives weak cyclic attachment after one quotient twist.
14. At the cyclic quotient prime, the Galois and automorphic torsors collapse to one residual polynomial system.
15. A continuous semisimple l adic representation with a finite Frobenius trace alphabet has finite image under the stated finite ramification and density hypotheses.
16. Positive saturation by packets with entire Artin L functions proves Artin holomorphy.

## Imported theorems

The article uses established cyclic base change, automorphic induction, local Langlands for general linear groups, Chebotarev density, Brauer Nesbitt, cross characteristic representation theory, pseudocharacter realization, torsion cohomology Galois representations, and selected weight one or holomorphic limit realization theorems.

## Conditional recursive result

Strong Artin for finite solvable images follows if every invariant extension step admits at least one of the following.

1. A residual realization at a sufficiently large good separating prime
2. A continuous semisimple l adic realization
3. A quotient prime nonregular lifting theorem that realizes the canonical residual eigensystem

Finite ramified extension spectra then decide the remaining local quotient twist.

## Not proved

The article does not prove:

1. Strong Artin for all solvable finite images
2. Strong Artin for arbitrary finite images
3. existence of a residual realization at a sufficiently large separating prime for every finite Satake descent
4. existence of an l adic realization for every singular weight zero finite Satake descent
5. the quotient prime lifting theorem in unrestricted rank
6. global nonemptiness of every local extension spectrum intersection
7. unrestricted exterior power functoriality
8. Artin holomorphy for every irreducible finite image representation

## Historical boundary

The finite trace image theorem, the one large residual realization theorem, and the extension spectrum packaging require specialist comparison with existing finite group, pseudocharacter, and automorphic to Galois literature. Internal proof completion does not establish historical novelty.
