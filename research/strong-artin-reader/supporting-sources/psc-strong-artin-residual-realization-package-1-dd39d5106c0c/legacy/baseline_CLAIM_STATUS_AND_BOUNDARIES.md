# Claim status and boundaries

## Unconditional finite results proved in this article

1. Galois extensions of one invariant irreducible representation form a quotient character torsor.
2. Scalar quotient twists rotate the degree `k` characteristic polynomial coefficient by the `k`th character power.
3. When the quotient prime does not divide the rank, determinant and central character select unique members of their respective cyclic fibres.
4. In the Artin cyclic setup there is a unique automorphic descent with central character equal to the determinant of the target Galois representation whenever the quotient prime is coprime to the rank.
5. The local extension spectrum polynomial and its Fourier transform give an exact criterion for two semisimple extensions to differ by one quotient character.
6. Global cyclic attachment is equivalent to one cyclic index belonging to every local alignment set.
7. A descent whose finite extension base change has Artin type local parameters is itself Artin type at every local place.
8. If the base change has finite image exponent `M` and degree `d`, every normalized unramified Satake root of the descent lies in `mu_(dM)`.
9. The normalized Hecke polynomials are integral and lie in a fixed cyclotomic field, and only finitely many such polynomials can occur.
10. Every Galois extension torsor has a nonzero trace separator on a nontrivial quotient coset.
11. Every cuspidal automorphic descent fibre has a finite unramified coefficient separator.
12. A continuous finite dimensional complex representation of a profinite group has finite image.
13. The positive saturation criterion is sufficient for Artin holomorphy.

## Imported arithmetic theorems

The manuscript uses established external results for cyclic and solvable base change, automorphic induction, local Langlands for general linear groups, class field theory, Chebotarev density, strong multiplicity one, Brauer meromorphic continuation, and the conditional automorphic to Artin strategy of Kim and Yamauchi.

## Conditional reductions proved in this article

### Cyclic finite Satake attachment principle

If every invariant cyclic Artin step admits one descent whose local extension spectra align with the target Galois representation, then Clifford recursion gives Strong Artin for every finite solvable image representation.

### Finite Satake realization principle

If every finite Satake cusp form has a global finite image Galois realization matching its Hecke polynomials, then Strong Artin holds for every finite solvable image representation.

The second implication is proved by constructing a descent at each Clifford extension step, realizing it globally, identifying its restriction by Chebotarev, and twisting within the finite extension torsor.

## Not proved

The manuscript does not prove:

1. Strong Artin for all solvable image representations
2. Strong Artin for arbitrary finite image representations
3. global nonemptiness of the intersection of the local extension alignment sets
4. the finite Satake realization principle
5. unrestricted automorphic to Galois construction for weight zero general linear cusp forms
6. unrestricted exterior power functoriality
7. Artin holomorphy for arbitrary irreducible finite image representations

## Historical novelty boundary

The finite formulations should be compared with classical Clifford theory, cyclic base change fibre descriptions, generalized monomial groups, almost monomial groups, and automorphic to Artin criteria. Internal proof completion does not establish historical novelty.
