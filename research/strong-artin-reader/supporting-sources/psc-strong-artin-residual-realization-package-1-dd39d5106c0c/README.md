## Principal advance

The article proves two realization rigidity results that substantially sharpen the earlier finite Satake reduction.

1. A finite trace alphabet bounds the order of a semisimple residual image by a function of the rank and alphabet size.
2. One sufficiently large residual realization of a finite Satake eigensystem yields a characteristic zero finite image representation with the exact Hecke polynomials.
3. Any continuous semisimple l adic realization whose Frobenius traces lie in a finite set has finite image.

These theorems show that finiteness is automatic once one suitable residual or l adic realization exists. The unresolved gate is existence of such a realization for every singular weight zero finite Satake descent.
