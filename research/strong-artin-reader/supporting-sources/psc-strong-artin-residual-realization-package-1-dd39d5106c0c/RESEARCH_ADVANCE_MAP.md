# Research advance map

## Previous bottleneck

The earlier paper reduced solvable Strong Artin to either global alignment of cyclic extension spectra or a finite Satake realization principle.

## New reductions

### Finite trace image bound

A finite Frobenius trace alphabet now supplies a uniform numerical bound on every sufficiently large semisimple residual image.

### One large residual prime

A single residual realization at a sufficiently large good prime now produces a characteristic zero finite image representation with exact Frobenius polynomials.

### One ordinary l adic realization

Any one semisimple l adic realization with the finite Frobenius trace alphabet is automatically finite image.

### Quotient prime residual automorphy

At the cyclic quotient prime, the two extension torsors collapse residually. The unresolved step becomes a nonregular lifting theorem rather than residual automorphy.

### Exact no go results

The manuscript proves that total automorphic induction, algebraic central characters at p dividing the rank, and sufficiently ramified epsilon twists cannot determine the cyclic offset.

## Revised single core

The strongest surviving global target is:

> Every singular weight zero finite Satake descent admits one continuous semisimple residual or l adic Galois realization with the prescribed unramified Hecke polynomials.

The new theorems then force finite image and exact Artin realization.
