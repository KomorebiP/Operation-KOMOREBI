# Strong Artin reader edition 1.0: authoring and verification report

8 October 2026. Publication author: Komorebi P. Series: Operation Komorebi.

## Editorial scope

This release creates a substantive conjecture monograph and a separate visual guide. It follows the expanded Collatz and Riemann format, with the earlier Strong Artin residual realization manuscript as its mathematical spine. The new text retains the load bearing proof sequence, develops worked examples, and adds a formal appendix on later cyclic obstruction and matrix lifting results. The visual guide uses Python and Matplotlib, with exact finite data where stated and explicit labels for schematic or conditional diagrams.

The publications use the Komorebi P. pseudonym, contain no abstract or contribution disclosure, and use connected prose with minimal punctuation clutter. Historical source archives are preserved unchanged, including their original status notes and disclosures. Current corrections are recorded in the new documents and this report rather than silently rewriting historical evidence.

## Mathematical and source corrections

The review keeps the global existence and finite algebra statements separate. In particular:

- The finite trace and l-adic recovery arguments require global semisimplicity, rather than only semisimplicity of selected Frobenius operators.
- The l-adic argument can be expressed through a nondegenerate trace pairing on the generated semisimple algebra. Continuity and Frobenius density are retained explicitly.
- Cyclic cuspidal descent supplies a free quotient character fibre under the checked hypotheses. Selecting an element of that fibre does not prove attachment to a prescribed Artin representation.
- Established automorphic induction applies through valid inducing towers. It does not prove arbitrary monomial or arbitrary solvable automorphy merely by naming induction.
- Exact local alignment sets in a prime cyclic group are empty, a singleton, or the whole group. Pairwise compatible nonempty exact sets therefore have a common intersection. A generic three-set pairwise-intersection counterexample cannot represent those exact sets.
- A continuous pseudocharacter, a cohomological carrier, and an eigenvariety construction are sufficient realization routes under their own hypotheses. Unsupported equivalence claims are not retained.
- Weak unramified attachment and attachment at every place are distinct contracts. Local lifting and comparison theorems do not silently provide the latter.
- The uniform finite alphabet and good separating prime requirements in residual recovery are specified before using them. Residual label collapse at the quotient prime does not select the ordinary torsor offset.

The literature check verifies the precise primary source leaves. It corrects inherited bibliography details and distinguishes established external theorems from the newly posted Wang September 2026 preprint. That preprint's precise special sectors were read, but its entire proof was not independently audited here. No global novelty certificate is asserted.
