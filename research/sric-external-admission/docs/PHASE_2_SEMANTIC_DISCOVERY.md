# Semantic Discovery Stage

Semantic construction begins only after causal content-specific admission. Discovery and calibration semantic orbits are captured first. Final semantic orbits remain untouched until a quotient has been selected and sealed.

Construction uses no gold proof steps or explicit-CoT output. The final witness is post-seal and its role is limited to checking whether the discovered quotient corresponds to protected proof-instance distinctions. Causal patching provides the stronger mediation test.

The candidate grammar is intentionally finite and low-rank. A failure to find a valid quotient is reported as underidentification rather than repaired by opening an unrestricted neural decoder search.
