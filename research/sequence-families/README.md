# Generalized sequence families

The website separates general constructions from their finite integer instances.
The defining index is `tools/sequence_generalized.py`; its source bindings are
in `manifests/sequence-generalized-sources.json`. These bind 22 definitions to
11 maintained documents and 291 source headings. Ten constructions have exact
integer-parameter explorers. Twelve analytic constructions retain their source
domains without an analytic-continuation evaluator.

`DERIVATIONS.md` supplies the seed numerator, constant-coefficient recurrence,
complete-state modular cycle argument, parameter-polynomial recurrence and
scalar transfer composition. The earlier finite binomial and explicitly
truncated hypergeometric derivations remain in
`../sequence-connections/IDENTITIES.md`. This is a focused self-review; source
indexing and finite replay do not constitute independent whole-paper review or
establish historical novelty.

The current bounded computation receipt is
`verification/sequence-library/run-024/manifest.json`, under the predeclared
`families-contract.json`. Its independent Python Fraction constructions are
compared with actual JavaScript BigInt engines. The result covers 1,486 family
cases, 31,876 values, 19,200 finite hypergeometric values, 802 worked
alternatives, 274 modular searches and 216 scalar transfer compositions.
The modular replay checks 22,127 complete states and 218 cycle certificates.
Four transformed identification cases scan all 2,236 scalar records. Bounds
and non-claims are in the contract and result JSON.

The general algorithms are parameterized; these finite tests do not test every
permitted combination. Analytic family transcriptions and source hashes are
checked separately, with defining passages linked publicly. Arbitrary closed
form expressions and notebook data are never executed as user code.
