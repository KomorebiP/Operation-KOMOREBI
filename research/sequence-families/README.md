# Generalized sequence families

The website distinguishes family definitions from their stored integer records.
The index is generated by `tools/sequence_generalized.py`. Source passages
are recorded in `manifests/sequence-generalized-sources.json`.

The index contains 36 definitions from 12 source documents and 311 headings.
Ten families have exact coefficient calculators. Twelve are analytic families
with their stated domains. Thirteen describe discrete constructions from the
research archive, and one is a classical reference collection. The analytic
families do not have a general continuation calculator.

`DERIVATIONS.md` supplies the seed numerator, constant-coefficient recurrence,
complete-state modular cycle argument, parameter-polynomial recurrence and
scalar transfer composition. The earlier finite binomial and explicitly
truncated hypergeometric derivations remain in
`../sequence-connections/IDENTITIES.md`. These notes review the stated calculations. Source indexing and finite
tests do not replace an independent review of the papers or establish
historical novelty.

The current bounded computation receipt is
`verification/sequence-library/run-024/manifest.json`, under the predeclared
`families-contract.json`. Its independent Python Fraction constructions are
compared with actual JavaScript BigInt engines. The result covers 1,486 family
cases, 31,876 values, 19,200 finite hypergeometric values, 802 worked
alternatives, 274 modular searches and 216 scalar transfer compositions.
The modular replay checks 22,127 complete states and 218 cycle certificates.
Four transformed identification cases scan all 2,236 scalar records. Bounds
and non-claims are in the contract and result JSON.

The algorithms accept parameters, but finite tests cannot cover every allowed
combination. The analytic formulas and source hashes are checked separately,
and each family links to its defining passage. The browser does not execute
arbitrary formulas or notebook text as code.
