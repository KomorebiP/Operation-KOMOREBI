## 1. Substantive corrections

### Complete histories, rather than terminal distributions

The predecessor composition argument established the aggregate current state and action/report history. That induction alone did not demonstrate its broader claim about all intermediate labels. The revised proof sums fine path prefixes over a fixed complete coarse-state path, factors out the common history-policy probability and uses the joint row condition at each extension. It therefore proves the claimed full path law and its label-history pushforward.

The predecessor Python checker had the analogous limitation: its keys retained a terminal state and the action/report word, not every intermediate state. The replacement retains complete state paths, compares projected fine and independently specified coarse path laws, and checks posteriors and common admissible actions. A terminal-only negative control now demonstrates exactly why the previous test scope was too narrow. This is a corrected proof/test-coverage gap, not a claim that the joint-quotient criterion was false.

### Policy information and availability

The controller is explicitly a common action/report-history policy. The protected labels and mathematical coarse state are not silently made additional observations. Enabled columns alone are used in the instrument multiplication. Positive-history supports determine the common legal action intersection; an empty intersection represents a dead end, not a fabricated action.

### Safe continuation and endings

The written proof now treats a deadlock before reaching the goal as failure. It separately treats an adversarial infinite non-ending path, proves the bounded-horizon characterization of the decreasing running sequence, and handles policies depending on the entire observed history. The stop command is observed before action selection. One policy, positional in the state and command mode, supplies both running and ending behavior. The illustrative goal has a hold action in both modes.

### Core definitions and correspondence

The revised setup declares the record map's state domain, finite nonempty model/report spaces, common scalar field and linear quotient maps, finite controller catalogues and policy class, and the exact event algebra used by the occupancy count. Positive packet components are divided by their weight only when it is positive. Legal-quotient uniqueness is stated only on enabled successors.

The back matter no longer understates the existing selected Lean correspondence. It records 23 accepted mappings, four pending mappings, and the distinction between archived original declarations and edited publication prose. All 27 selected result identities remain. The source audit found 24 unchanged statement bodies and three clarified bodies. Original source files remain preserved unchanged.

## 2. Added mathematical material

### Transport of safe continuation through quotients

The new chapter proves commutation of controlled predecessors with inverse image, transport of both fixed-point sequences, equality of ending ranks, and lifting of positional witnesses. It derives the support-game relation from an exact nonnegative stochastic quotient. A one-sided enclosure theorem certifies a common policy for every model inside a declared successor envelope.

The worked counterexample shows why arbitrarily small total-variation error can still destroy a sure-ending guarantee: any positive probability of an unsafe successor leaves an adverse supported path. A high-probability statement and an all-path control statement therefore require different hypotheses.

### Representation migration and retained correctability

The new chapter gives the exact fibre criterion for migration from an old record to a specified new record, the corresponding evidence-assisted criterion, and the universal property of retaining the joint record. It proves a minimum supplementary fixed-bit bound relative to the largest old-record fibre's number of new values. The encoder's access to the missing source information is an explicit assumption, not a capability created by the theorem. Operation-intertwining and certificate-premise implications complete the revision interface.

### Proof fields and executable certificates

The new chapter admits selected Proof-Path Theory results in a self-contained notation. It proves least seeded closure, replay soundness and concatenation, exact closure from replay plus saturation, and sound/complete finite forbidden-consequence witnesses. It includes a four-node example separating a valid partial derivation from a saturated but unsupported superset. Semantic interpretation, named-source adapters, parsers, and runtime implementations retain separate assurance boundaries.
