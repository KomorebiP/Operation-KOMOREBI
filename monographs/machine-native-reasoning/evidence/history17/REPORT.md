## Mathematical outcome

Core 2.4, *A common observable quotient*, is now formally mapped to the unchanged
reviewed source. Let a finite candidate family act linearly on a finite-dimensional
space, select one complete candidate as the reference, and fix a linear protected
readout. Form the span E of all reference-word-propagated candidate differences.
The formalization proves equivalence of all-initial-vector fixed-candidate output
agreement, E being invisible to the readout, and existence of actual common linear
quotient data. The canonical carrier is the constructed quotient V/E.

The same shared quotient preserves every finite action sequence even when the
candidate changes at every operation. An explicit increasing span iteration starts
from the difference ranges and reaches E by the ambient dimension. The reference
may be changed to another member of the family without changing E. Any other common
linear projection factors uniquely and surjectively through the canonical quotient.
The canonical quotient is not asserted to be the smallest predictor for the readout.

The principal declaration is
`Komorebi.MachineTheory.CommonQuotient.source_core24`.
`source_core24_reference` separately checks the exact identity tying a supplied
raw reference family to an actual candidate. The arbitrary-function factor theorem
covers the source wording even when update/readout linearity is not restated.

The proof uses the all-vector agreement at the transformed vector T_a(v), not
only at the original v. This matters: the checked examples include systems that
agree along each fixed model from one starting state yet disagree after switching.
Another example has an invisible immediate difference that becomes visible after
one further reference operation. These are hypothesis tests, not merely illustrations.
