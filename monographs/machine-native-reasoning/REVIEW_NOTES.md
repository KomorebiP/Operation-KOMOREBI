The source-facing theorem is `CommonQuotient.source_core24`, with a second
endpoint retaining an explicitly supplied reference family and its equality to
a distinguished candidate. Every word uses the inherited left-to-right order.
The proof of invisibility uses all-vector agreement at the vector T_a^theta v;
this step would be invalid for a premise restricted to one initial trajectory.
The examples prove that weakening false.

The discrepancy is the span of all A_w (T_a^theta - A_a) v. Its reference
invariance is proved by appending an action to each generator and extending by
span induction. Candidate invariance follows by adding the one-step difference.
Actual Mathlib quotient maps define the shared updates. A readout is descended
only after the discrepancy is proved to lie in its kernel. Arbitrary supplied
common data imply agreement. The helper `agreement_of_function_factor` proves
this direction even when updates and the readout are specified only as
functions; no additional linearity hypothesis is being hidden in that reading
of the source. The canonical witness has linear updates and readout.

The quotient carrier is in the state's universe for the existential
`CommonModel`; the necessary-direction factor theorems allow arbitrary target
universes. Thus the canonical existence witness does not restrict the source's
mathematical existence assertion. No arbitrary common carrier is identified
with the canonical quotient without an explicit comparison map.

The closure starts at the sum of difference ranges. Each step contains its
predecessor and remains within the propagated span. Any plateau is invariant
and therefore already equals that span. A finite-dimensional strict increase
raises finrank, proving stabilization by the ambient dimension. This is a
universal theorem, not a finite test-horizon inference.

## Trust and delivery

The compiler and imported cache are pinned and their actual imported artifacts
are matched to the recorded inventory. Upstream dependencies are not rebuilt.
Lean's own-kernel replay is not an independent kernel implementation. Source-
correspondence review is part of this project, not external peer review.
All compilation, replay, control and finite-check outcomes are taken from
terminated receipts. The final report records any later audit or packaging
failures separately. No out-of-turn work is required by this handoff.
