# Preservation and verification

This supplement is the 12-page worked construction and proofs companion preserved from the Realization Calculus twin-volume package. Its mathematical text, TeX source, Python source, figures and original verification records are unchanged. `SOURCE_PRESERVATION.json` records SHA-256 identities for all 21 original files and their exact match to the source archive. The original `manifest.json` continues to describe the original delivery. New license and preservation files are adjacent additions.

The preserved verifier was rerun against the preserved source. It passed **12,664 exact checks**, comprising 8,969 original checks and 3,695 additional checks, including the expected negative controls. The new replay results are in `verification_closeout/`. The historical `verification.json` and `worked_steps.json` were not overwritten.

These are finite exact coefficient checks using rational arithmetic. They are not a Lean compilation or a new audit of the written infinite-dimensional proofs. The construction retains its original distinctions between signal detail, compatibility defect and extension obstruction.

The PDF was not rebuilt in this closeout. Its historical title, date and version markings are retained as provenance. The adjacent `LICENSE.md` supplies the explicit original-author CC BY 4.0 and MIT grants. Third-party font notices remain separate. Some preserved figures contain embedded Type 3 fonts without Unicode mappings.
