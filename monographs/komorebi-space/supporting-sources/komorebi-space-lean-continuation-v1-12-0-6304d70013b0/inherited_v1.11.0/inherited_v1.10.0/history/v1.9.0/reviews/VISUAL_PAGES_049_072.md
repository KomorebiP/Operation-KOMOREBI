## Findings

**V49-01 — physical page 51, printed page 45, Figure 11: curved-arrow/title
collision.** In the left “Three views” panel, the curved composite arrow from
vertex 0 to vertex 2 rises into the heading's area and crosses/touches the heading
text. The rest of the figure, its point grid, labels, and caption are readable.
Move the heading above the arc or lower/reroute the arc while preserving its
endpoints. Re-render and inspect this page after correction. This is the one
substantive diagram-layout defect found in the assigned range.

**V49-02 — physical pages 52 and 53, printed pages 46 and 47: detached source
callouts.** The pages open with “Source concordance: KS35” and “Source concordance:
KS36”, respectively, after the relevant proof ends on the preceding page. The
callouts remain legible and correctly identified. This is a minor pagination
issue, not mathematical clipping or a broken reference. Keeping each callout
with the end of its proof would improve reading continuity.

No clipped equations, overlapping body text, unresolved reference markers,
malformed displayed matrices, or isolated subsection headings were found in
this range. The table on page 58 and the other five figures inspected here are
readable. Normal proof continuations across page boundaries were not counted as
orphans.
