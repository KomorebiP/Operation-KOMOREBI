\subsection{How to read the correspondence}
The identifiers below are stable editorial identifiers, not counts of new discoveries. A row can combine several source declarations into one exposition. The named declaration, its line range, source hash, and correspondence note are recorded in \code{data/claim_ledger.json} and in the companion Lean guide. Existence of a matching source declaration is not a mechanical proof that every word of an edited statement matches its type. The displayed hypotheses and the printed proof remain part of the review.

\textbf{F} denotes the recovered foundation tree at version 0.20.0. \textbf{M} denotes the focused moving-space tree at version 0.32.0. \textbf{S} denotes the separately recovered Hilbert holonomy source. \textbf{I} denotes a new module in the integrated edition. F, M, and S retain provenance, while byte-identical copies of their referenced sources are compiled together in \path{formal/KomorebiSpace/}. All \EditionClaims\ principal items have source correspondences in that checked closure. This does not assert that every illustrative computation or explanatory sentence is independently formalized.
