# Review notes — edition 1.0.0

## Mathematical distinctions checked during editing

The exposition retains contravariance of views, the actual target of each defect map, and the difference between a global section and a collection of local appearances. The additive quotient is formed from defects of admissible corrections; existence and the invisible-kernel ambiguity criterion are separate. A general semantics map is input, not an intrinsic universal coordinate.

The tower discussion retains the strict propagation gap and target splittings. The split coordinate inverse is `Ps + Ar`, and the protected observable is splitting dependent. Finite residual telescopes do not imply norm convergence. Cofinal point equivalence does not imply invariance of arbitrary residual norms.

The nonabelian multiplication order and right torsor action are preserved. Descent glues the specified overlap gauges; uniqueness respects the chosen identifications. The composition topology and standard `Pseudofunctor.IsStack` theorem are not downgraded to a merely stack-like analogy. The theorem is also not generalized to an arbitrary site.

The analytic discussion separates existence of orthogonal projection from closed-range stability. The graph operator is orthogonal on the anchor, not a claimed global maximum-norm nearest point. Nonisometric route amplification remains explicit. The two-stage descent correction takes place inside the locally exact space. Moving-kernel bounds retain both gain certificates, or the surjective-reference and strict-radius hypotheses that derive the new one.

## Failures and corrections

1. **Retrieval:** initial semantic/title searches were incomplete or irrelevant, and one listing continuation was unavailable. These attempts did not establish absence of the sources. Filtered archive listing recovered the actual source/replay ZIPs.
2. **Concordance:** provisional references `Realization#map_comp` and `MovingKernelExamples#closed_radius_fails` did not exist. They were replaced by the actual `onLimit_comp` and `closed_radius_failure`. Final exact-name resolution passed all 112 references.
3. **Mathematical typography:** one draft line lost the backslash and initial letter of `\norm`. The intended norm factor was restored before the final build.
4. **Degenerate case:** the isometric reconstruction operator was described using `norm <= 1`, not equality, to avoid excluding the zero fiber.
5. **Build:** the first Lean-guide compilation stopped because a provisional `KomorebiRule` color was undefined. The generator now uses the actual `KomorebiMuted` color. The final build succeeds.
6. **Layout:** a long hash string overflowed. It was put on its own breakable line. Contents spacing, section breaks, source-note paragraphs, and catalogue block boundaries were adjusted to eliminate fragmented or near-empty continuation pages where possible.

Ordinary first-pass LaTeX undefined references were resolved by the subsequent passes. Final compiler output has no overfull-box or undefined-reference warnings. Historical failed development logs inside the original archives retain their original status and are not erased.

Exact finite computation checks selected models and implementations. PDF preflight checks the delivered layout.
