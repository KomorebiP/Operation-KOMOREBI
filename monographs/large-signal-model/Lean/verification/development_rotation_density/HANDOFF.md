# Rotation density dependency

The exact rational Circle element `(3/5,4/5)` has no positive finite period and its forward natural powers are dense in the whole Circle. No density or irrational-angle assumption remains in the endpoint.

`rotation_forward_dense` is the intended P46 leaf. `forward_closed_property` transports any closed property from that forward orbit to every circle point. `rotation_orbit_dense` also handles an arbitrary unit initial direction.

The source and control are frozen at the hashes in RESULTS.json. This dependency has passed compilation, module import, all11 named-declaration axiom checks, kernel replay and one semantic false-period control. Full P46 still requires the actual model/behavior law, canonical-cone equality, cone uniqueness, finite-generator obstruction and minimal rank-three claims supplied by the other work packages.
