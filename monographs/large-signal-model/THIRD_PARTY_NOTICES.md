# Licenses and attribution

The MIT license at the repository root applies to original project code, proof sources, documentation, manuscript text and original figures in this distribution.

The historical license-status files inside the immutable Lean companion and retained reference laboratory describe their earlier checkpoints. The root license is the current grant for original project material included here. Those historical notices remain intact as provenance.

## P052 fonts

The three P052 OpenType fonts under `Monograph/assets/fonts/` have their own license. They are excluded from the root MIT grant. Their full copyright, GNU Affero General Public License and font exception are preserved unchanged in `Monograph/assets/fonts/URW-COPYRIGHT-AND-LICENSE.txt`. The font exception addresses embedding in documents. Preserve that notice when redistributing the font files.

## Lean and Mathlib

Lean and Mathlib are external pinned dependencies. Their runtime binaries and upstream source trees are not bundled in this repository. Their upstream licenses remain applicable to separately obtained copies. The formal companion records exact revisions in its toolchain and Lake manifest.
