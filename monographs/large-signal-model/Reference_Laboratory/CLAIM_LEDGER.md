## Evidence levels

Written proof is distinct from kernel-checked proof. Source identity is not proof validation. A rational identity check establishes its particular instance. A numerical proposal is not a validity witness. A witness validates a submitted model, not an unknown generating process. Conditional confidence coverage requires its process, observation, predictability, and model-class assumptions. A seeded run does not validate those assumptions empirically.
