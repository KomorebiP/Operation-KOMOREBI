# Global assumptions and scenario boundaries

The proposition-level register is `PROOF_REGISTER.json` and `PROOF_REGISTER.md`. This document groups the executed sectors. A shared notation is not a shared statistical guarantee.

## S1. Exact archives and known-model closure

**Supplied:** Signal types, exact atlases/projectors, known finite linear dynamics, initial observables.

**Learned:** No unknown world model is learned in this construction.

**Certified:** Reconstruction, coherent transfer and smallest closed linear observable space under declared operators.

**Not established:** Accessibility or low dimension of the completed state.

**Principal results:** P1, P2, P3, P4, P6, P7, P8, P10, P12, P13.

## S2. Output-only autonomous delay realization

**Supplied:** An autonomous finite linear class, sufficient delay closure and span coverage.

**Learned:** Transition on the realizable observation-window space from paired outputs.

**Certified:** Exact interpolation on the observed span; full predictive identification only under coverage.

**Not established:** Unknown global dimension, arbitrary controlled dynamics or nonlinear hidden-state discovery.

**Principal results:** P14, P15, P16, P17, P25.

## S3. Finite controlled population-table realization

**Supplied:** Exact history/test probabilities, rank upper bound, complete core and finite alphabets.

**Learned:** Observable operators; one bounded interval-domain search uses only those operators.

**Certified:** A checked finite positive lift establishes validity of the fitted model at all horizons.

**Not established:** Statistical truth from exact synthetic tables or a general complete cone search.

**Principal results:** P27, P28, P29, P37, P39, P45, P49.

# Quantifier rules

Internal validity quantifies over the inputs, states and operators in the witness. Statistical coverage quantifies over data generated by the declared process. Neither statement proves its upstream observation, representation or model-class assumptions from one record.

P9 uses a nontrivial initial partition and P12 retains the one-block exception. P13 controls fixed horizons for bounded operators. P72 now explicitly assumes at least one spatial coordinate. P81 requires a strictly positive sub-Gaussian scale, a correct conditional mean, predictable regressors and a parameter bound. P82 requires an exact accessible state. P87 uses the whole returned rectangle, not just its center.

All coordinate transports must also transport the domain, mass row, initial state and uncertainty set. All density comparisons must specify the same reference measure or an explicit change-of-measure argument. All common-policy bounds actually use a common policy.
