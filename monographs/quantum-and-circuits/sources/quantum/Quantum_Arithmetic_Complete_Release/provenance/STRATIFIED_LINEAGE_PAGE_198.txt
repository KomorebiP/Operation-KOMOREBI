Boundary 21.13 (Finite twisted scope). The theorem remains finite and profiled. It assumes invertible chart
transitions, finite address sets, coherent address and splitting cocycles, and profile preservation of the operations
used. It does not prove convergence for countably infinite lane sums, descent across noninvertible bridges, higher
stack effectivity, or a canonical orbit system independent of the initially declared lane presentation. Orbit count is
not identified with dimension, rank, entropy, information, or complexity. The permutation-module and cyclotomic
formulas have classical algebraic antecedents; their role here is to provide exact native realizations of the twisted
lane doctrine.
