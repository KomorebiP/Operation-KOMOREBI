# Release contract: version 1.0.0

## Deliverable

A self-contained research repository on classical restored circuits, complete discrepancy covers, and exact-projection limitations. The title is **Komorebi Circuit Obstructions**, not “a proof of P versus NP.” The source baseline is the Complete Packets and Projection checkpoint dated September 21, 2026.

## Acceptance criteria

The source checkpoint is hashed and replayable. Active checkers work without importing a nested predecessor. Their full scientific results match the frozen baseline. The core proofs have definitions and stated hypotheses. Every central claim has a proof location, a finite evidence description, and an explicit limit. Counterexamples and failed routes are retained. A fresh extraction can execute the quick and full verification commands. Generated results cannot overwrite the frozen input evidence. No network or external service is needed for local scientific verification.

Release completion means these artifact requirements are satisfied. It does not mean that every historical conjecture has been proved.

## Scope lock

The maintained results are C01–C08 in the claim register. No superpolynomial general-circuit lower bound, polynomial-time SAT evaluator, quantum lower bound, or formal-verification claim is part of version 1.0.0.

No additional mathematical discovery is a prerequisite for tagging this scope. New theory belongs in a separately scoped branch or later release, not an ever-growing completion checklist.

## Maintenance

Corrections to a retained proof, broken reproduction commands, wrongly reported finite ranges, and provenance defects remain valid maintenance work. Version 1.0.0 is a fixed snapshot. Subsequent corrections receive a new version and an explicit correction note; historical bytes are not silently rewritten.

## Separate publication decisions

A remote destination, visibility, and a distribution license were not specified. They remain owner-controlled publication choices. This local release neither grants a new distribution license nor makes material public. They are not unresolved mathematical obligations.
