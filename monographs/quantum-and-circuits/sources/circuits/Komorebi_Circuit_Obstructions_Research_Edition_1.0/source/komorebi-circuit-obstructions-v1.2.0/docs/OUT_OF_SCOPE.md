# Archived research directions

These are not release blockers, promised features, or claims of success. They are retained so that scope closure does not erase uncertainty.

## Unrestricted shared-generation lower bounds

A sufficient route to P != NP would force superpolynomial total cost for every complete cut-discrepancy cover of an explicit NP decision family. This is not established. Degree parameters, packet counts, restriction growth, and ambient algebra dimensions do not supply the missing theorem.

## Efficient exact projection

The witness-fingerprint expression is succinct before coefficient extraction. No polynomial-time evaluator has been supplied. The naive multiplicative compiler fails on overlaps. A context-dependent alternative remains unproved.

## Formalization and independent review

Lean verification, independent mathematical review, and an exhaustive novelty audit are useful separate projects. They are not represented as already completed, and are not prerequisites of this bounded source-and-proof release.

## Earlier research lanes

Fusion and semifilter measures, monotone lifts, clique boundaries, code-based quotient transport, restoration grades, and proposed curvature measures remain preserved in the original checkpoint archives. Only statements explicitly admitted to `CLAIMS.md` are maintained as version 1.0.0 claims. New work should begin from a new scope contract rather than reopening every historical “continue” prompt.
