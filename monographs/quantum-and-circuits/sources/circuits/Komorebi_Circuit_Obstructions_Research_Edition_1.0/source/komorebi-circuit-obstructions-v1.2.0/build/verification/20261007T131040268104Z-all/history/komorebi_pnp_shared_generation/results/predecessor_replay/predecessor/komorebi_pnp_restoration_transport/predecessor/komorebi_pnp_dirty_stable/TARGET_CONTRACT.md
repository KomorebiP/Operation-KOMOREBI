# Target contract

Original target: resolve P versus NP.

Direct sufficient target retained from the predecessor: prove a superpolynomial
unrestricted circuit lower bound for an NP-complete decision family, equivalently a
superpolynomial lower bound for stable clean-ancilla reversible oracle length.

Dirty/ambient paths, arbitrary auxiliary wires, fanout/reuse, compute-copy-uncompute,
negative information and commutator shortcuts are all permitted.  A lower bound inside
a clean subgroup or preferred representation does not count as the target theorem.
