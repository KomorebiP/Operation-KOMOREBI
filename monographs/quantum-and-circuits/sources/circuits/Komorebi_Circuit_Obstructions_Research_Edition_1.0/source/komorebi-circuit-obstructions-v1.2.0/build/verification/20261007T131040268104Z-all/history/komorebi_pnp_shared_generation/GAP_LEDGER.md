# Current gap ledger

Original goal: P versus NP. NOT REACHED.

## Closed in this block (written proof, not Lean)

C1. Every positive point, not only one nonzero safe function, contributes a cut-degree obligation.
C2. Exact dual moment-span certificates for the pointwise obstruction.
C3. All-size unbalanced-OR family separates complete coverage from even affine-optimized one-packet immunity.
C4. Exact restored Toffoli calibration for all Boolean functions on at most three bits.
C5. Explicit affine-factor family with much harder quadratic extraction in nonlinear-gate count, and failure of safe-support preservation.
C6. Exponential dimension for a fixed linear multiplicative witness summary retaining all point idempotents.
C7. Exact succinct weighted-witness trace formula and conditional one-sided randomized evaluator reduction.

## Failed routes

F1. Equality of affine-optimized alpha and b. It held on all functions through four bits but fails for the explicit five-bit unbalanced-OR function. This is a mathematical counterexample, not a numerical failure.
F2. Charge isolated homogeneous nonlinear ranks to the full circuit with a polynomial overhead in Toffoli count alone. The projection family disproves this. An overhead depending polynomially on input length is not excluded.
F3. Replace an overlapping product's weighted trace by the product of its traces. One shared Boolean variable already disproves this.

## Still open

O1. Superpolynomial shared total generation lower bound for all complete cut-discrepancy histories of an explicit NP family.
O2. A universal circuit simulation into a model constrained by the exponential algebra-dimension theorem. No such simulation has been shown and it must not be assumed.
O3. A uniform polynomial-time evaluator of the weighted trace from succinct verifier circuits. The implemented evaluator remains exponential in witness length.
O4. A metric on succinct overlapping compatibility defects that tolerates affine changes, grade mixing, arbitrary dirty workspace, and sharing with an audited per-gate cost.

## Next discriminating direction

A useful new lemma must control synthesis/evaluation cost of overlapping defects, not only dimension, degree, rank of extracted packets, or nonzero-polynomial existence. Preserve the projection and overlap counterexamples as required controls. Another linear bound alone does not close O1.
