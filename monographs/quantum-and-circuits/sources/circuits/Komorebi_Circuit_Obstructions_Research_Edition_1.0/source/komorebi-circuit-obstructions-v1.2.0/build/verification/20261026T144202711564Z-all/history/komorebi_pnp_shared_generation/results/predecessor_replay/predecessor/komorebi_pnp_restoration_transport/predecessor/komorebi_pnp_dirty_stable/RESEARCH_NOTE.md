# Komorebi P vs NP: stable dirty-circuit normalization

Date: 2026-09-21

## Status

The conjecture is not resolved in this block.  The purpose of this continuation is to
put the dirty-circuit analogy into a circuit-faithful form and to remove two false
shortcuts: semantic resolution curvature and exponentially weighted interaction grade.
The surviving sufficient endpoint remains a superpolynomial lower bound on an
ambient-safe obstruction such as the existing fusion cover number `tau` for SAT,
CLIQUE, or another NP-complete family.

## 1. Stable dirty length

For a Boolean function `f:{0,1}^n->{0,1}`, define `ell_st(f)` to be the least number
of gates in a reversible circuit over NOT, CNOT and Toffoli, with any number of clean
ancillas, such that on the clean subspace

    (x, 0^a, b) -> (x, 0^a, b XOR f(x)).

Ancillas must be restored at the endpoint but may be used arbitrarily in the interior.
This is the stable ambient word metric relevant to the earlier dirty-workspace theory.

### Theorem 1: linear equivalence with ordinary circuit size

Let `C(f)` be fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT circuit size, with free constants and reuse.
Then

    C(f)/5 <= ell_st(f) <= 6 C(f) + 1.

Proof of the upper bound.  Compute every irreversible gate into a fresh clean wire.
An AND needs one Toffoli.  A NOT into a fresh zero target needs NOT followed by CNOT.
An OR into a fresh zero target uses

    t <- t XOR a XOR b XOR (a AND b),

hence two CNOTs and one Toffoli.  Thus the forward computation costs at most `3s`
reversible gates for `s=C(f)`.  Copy the final signal to the designated output by one
CNOT and reverse the complete forward computation.  The work wires return to zero.
The total is at most `6s+1`.

Proof of the lower bound.  Simulate a reversible circuit gate-by-gate by an ordinary
Boolean DAG, retaining the previous expression for every wire.  NOT costs one gate.
CNOT updates `t` to `t XOR c`; XOR costs four AND/OR/NOT gates via

    (t OR c) AND NOT(t AND c).

Toffoli updates `t` to `t XOR (ab)`, costing one AND plus four gates for XOR.  Thus a
`t`-gate reversible circuit gives an ordinary circuit of at most `5t` gates for the
clean output bit.  Therefore `C(f)<=5 ell_st(f)`.

Consequently a superpolynomial lower bound on `ell_st(SAT_n)` is already an
unrestricted circuit lower bound.  Conversely, dirty detours are fully admitted in
`ell_st`; a lower bound proved only inside a preferred clean subgroup is insufficient.

## 2. Exact semantic elimination has no curvature

For a Boolean function `F`, let

    E_i F = F|_{x_i=0} OR F|_{x_i=1}.

Then for distinct coordinates `i,j`,

    E_i E_j F = E_j E_i F

identically, because both sides are the OR of the four restrictions obtained by fixing
`(x_i,x_j)` to the four Boolean pairs.

Therefore a proposed exact semantic curvature

    Omega_{ij}(F) = E_j E_i F - E_i E_j F

is identically zero.  Nonzero elimination curvature can only refer to a chosen
representation, truncation, normal form, or cost filtration.  It is not an intrinsic
semantic obstruction of SAT and cannot by itself constrain arbitrary algorithms.

The checker exhaustively confirms the commutation identity for every truth table on
2, 3 and 4 variables (65,808 truth tables total).

## 3. Algebraic closure is already maximal at time zero

Let

    B_n = F_2[x_1,...,x_n]/(x_i^2-x_i).

Every Boolean function has a unique algebraic normal form, so the input variables and
constants already generate all of `B_n` under XOR and multiplication.  Equivalently,
`B_n` is the ring of all F_2-valued functions on the Boolean cube.

Thus an obstruction defined only by membership in the Boolean algebra generated by
currently available signals is vacuous: every target function belongs to that algebra
before any gate is executed.  More generally, semantic ideals in a finite Boolean ring
are principal.  Pure algebraic generation therefore forgets the resource that matters.
A successful Komorebi invariant must be FILTERED by construction cost or carry an
independent lower-bounding norm on the ambient word metric.

This is the same clean/ambient lesson as the prior dirty-circuit work: closure is not
complexity.

## 4. Interaction grade cannot carry exponential debt by itself

Let

    m_k(x_1,...,x_k) = product_i x_i.

The clean oracle `b -> b XOR m_k(x)` can be implemented with `k-2` clean ancillas by
forming a chain of partial ANDs, toggling the output, and uncomputing the chain.  The
number of Toffoli gates is

    2k - 3.

Hence any stable gate-Lipschitz obstruction norm `D` satisfying `D(local gate)<=1`
must obey

    D(U_{m_k}) <= 2k-3.

So higher interaction order cannot legitimately be assigned exponential weight when
that interaction factorizes through a chain of lower-order operations.  This directly
rules out the naive proposal

    grade r obstruction -> weight exponential in r.

What could remain expensive is not grade but STABLE PRIMITIVE GENERATION: many
incompatible classes which cannot be generated by reusing a small collection of
lower-grade factors, even after adding and restoring ancillas.

The checker verifies the `2k-3` clean construction exhaustively for every input for
`2<=k<=8`.

## 5. Relation to the existing fusion obstruction

The predecessor block uses the two-sided semifilter cover number `tau(f)` and proves

    tau(f) <= C(f).

Combining with Theorem 1 gives

    tau(f) <= C(f) <= 5 ell_st(f),

or

    ell_st(f) >= tau(f)/5.

Therefore `tau` is already safe against dirty reversible shortcuts: a dirty path cannot
silently evade a `tau` lower bound.  The previous linear CLIQUE bound is an ambient
lower bound; it is simply too small.

This clarifies the role of the new graded language.  A second obstruction calculus is
useful only if it proves a stronger endpoint lower bound than current fusion methods,
or if it yields a lower bound on `tau` itself.  Merely assigning extra debt to clean
representations is not enough.

## 6. The surviving Komorebi target

The most precise surviving version of the earlier intuition is the following.

A family of obstruction classes should be called *stably primitive* only after
quotienting all classes obtainable by:

1. products/tensors of already generated classes,
2. coordinate changes of polynomial construction cost,
3. introduction of clean ancillas,
4. arbitrary dirty reversible excursions,
5. uncomputation/restoration,
6. commutator synthesis and reuse.

A useful norm `D_st` would need a theorem of the form

    D_st(f) <= poly(ell_st(f))

proved for EVERY reversible word, not only a selected normal form, and an explicit
NP-complete family satisfying

    D_st(F_n) = n^{omega(1)}.

The first requirement is an ambient conservation theorem.  The second is the endpoint
lower bound.  Either would be major progress; both would settle the desired
nonuniform separation and hence P!=NP.

The current block does not prove either statement.

## 7. Why the previous 'resolution curvature' idea must be replaced

Exact eliminations commute, while high-order monomials are cheap after stabilization.
So the viable object is not semantic curvature and not commutator DEPTH.  If a
curvature language is retained, it has to mean metric holonomy: the extra stable word
length forced by transporting the same information through mutually incompatible
coordinate systems.

That brings the problem back to the strongest part of the earlier dirty-circuit work:
intrinsic-versus-ambient distortion.  For P versus NP, however, the quantity to bound
is the ambient distance itself, not the clean/ambient gap.

## 8. Exact computational checks

`checks/verify_dirty_stable.py` performs three independent finite controls:

- 5,880 clean-input evaluations of randomly generated 2--4 variable Boolean circuits
  after compute-copy-uncompute reversible compilation;
- all 65,808 Boolean truth tables on 2--4 variables for commutation of two existential
  eliminations;
- exhaustive truth-table verification of the clean `k`-controlled toggle construction
  for every `2<=k<=8`.

These checks support the finite constructions only.  They are not evidence for a
circuit lower bound beyond the written theorems above.

## 9. Current proof boundary

Established here:

- clean restored reversible word length and ordinary Boolean circuit size are linearly
  equivalent up to explicit constants 5 and 6;
- exact existential elimination curvature is identically zero;
- unfiltered Boolean algebra generation is semantically maximal at the input layer;
- high interaction grade alone is cheap after stabilization (`2k-3` Toffolis);
- existing fusion `tau` lower bounds automatically survive dirty reversible detours.

Not established:

- a superlinear or superpolynomial endpoint bound for SAT/CLIQUE;
- a nontrivial stable primitive generation norm with a universal gate-Lipschitz theorem;
- P!=NP or P=NP.

## 10. Attempted shared-negative threshold obstruction for CLIQUE

A further endpoint attempt was made using overlapping negative instances rather than
unique near-clique witnesses.

Fix a k-set K of vertices.  For each pair e={a,b} subset K let

    B = K^c union {a,b}

and let G_B be the graph containing every edge except edges internal to B.  Its maximum
clique has size k-1, so it is a negative CLIQUE_k instance.  Let mu_K be uniform on the
q=binom(k,2) such graphs.

For k>=5 the coordinatewise majority graph v_K has all edges incident to K present and
all edges inside K^c absent, hence is positive.  The matching-coordinate probabilities
under mu_K are:

- an edge internal to K is present with probability 1-1/q;
- an edge from K to K^c is present with probability 1-2/k;
- an edge internal to K^c is absent with probability 1.

Thus

    Phi_K(S)=1 iff mu_K(S)>1/2

is a consistent semifilter at v_K.

This construction has substantial transport overlap: one negative graph G_B is used by
binom(|B|,2) different positive K's.  However the threshold family is still too weak.
Independently color every negative G_B into categories

    I=A intersect B with probability 1/2,
    X=A\B          with probability 1/4,
    Y=B\A          with probability 1/4.

For a fixed K the q support points receive independent categories.  With counts i,x,y,
the pair covers Phi_K whenever

    i+x > q/2,   i+y > q/2,   i <= q/2.

As q grows, the first two inequalities hold with probability tending to one while the
third has probability tending to 1/2.  Hence the expected fraction of these canonical
semifilters covered by one arbitrary semantic pair stays bounded away from zero; some
pair achieves that constant fraction.

So this natural shared-negative / majority-measure realization cannot yield a
superpolynomial cover lower bound.  The failure is informative: large support overlap
alone does not force enough coupling when the semifilter is determined by one scalar
measure threshold.  A successful endpoint construction would need genuinely
higher-order, non-measure obstruction data or another invariant altogether.
