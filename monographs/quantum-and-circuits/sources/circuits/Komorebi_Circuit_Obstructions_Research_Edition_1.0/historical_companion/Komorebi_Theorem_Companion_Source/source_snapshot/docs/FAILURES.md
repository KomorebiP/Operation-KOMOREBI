# Counterexamples, corrections, and execution failures

## Mathematical failures retained as results

1. **Semantic elimination curvature:** exact existential eliminations commute. Nonzero semantic commutators cannot be claimed for those exact operators.
2. **Raw obstruction and residual counts:** small circuits can have exponentially many residuals. Counting expanded states does not charge their shared construction.
3. **Affine one-packet equality extrapolation:** agreement through four inputs fails in the stated unbalanced-OR family from five inputs onward.
4. **Cost-free homogeneous extraction:** C05 shows that a projected grade can have greater nonlinear cost and can lose safe support. Input length and total affine cost must remain visible.
5. **Multiplicative weighted trace on overlaps:** C08 provides a one-bit exact counterexample. The disjoint-variable rule does not extend to shared variables.
6. **Dimension-to-circuit inference:** C06 excludes a specified fixed faithful algebra summary. It does not exclude succinct representations or nonlinear, context-dependent computation.

The original sources and more extensive route records are preserved under `archive/checkpoints/`. This file summarizes boundaries, not every historical calculation.
