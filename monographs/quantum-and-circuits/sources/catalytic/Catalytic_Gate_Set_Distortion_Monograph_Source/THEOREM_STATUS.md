# Theorem and evidence status

This ledger is a compact guide to the logical status of the monograph. It does not replace the hypotheses and proofs in the text.

## Proved symbolically

- General nested-model definitions for restricted and ambient cost, additive defect, multiplicative distortion, and vector-valued resources.
- Schreier-holonomy interpretation of ambient shortcuts for subgroup gate models.
- Endpoint Green-kernel first-arrival identity for finite generator systems.
- Algebraic word-map dimension bounds, generic rigidity, defect-codimension lower bounds, and finite-field sparsity for the unitriangular model.
- Universal six-row transvection atom with intrinsic length seven and ambient length six over every nonzero commutative unital ring under the stated transvection cost convention.
- Three-cross, one-phase catalytic mechanism and analytic count of the twenty minimal binary six-dimensional motifs.
- Interval-tree lower bound and exact intrinsic length of the connected snapshot families.
- Two-upper-gate, weighted-catalyst, and rank-one/bandwidth-one excursion bounds.
- Direct-sum, recursive dyadic, and tree-tight tensor amplification.
- Existence of the stable tensor-normalized ambient rate by subadditivity.
- Qudit modular-SUM realization of the universal atom.
- Analytic restricted cost three and ambient cost two for the four two-qubit Clifford exceptional matrices, forming one orbit under free final phase gates. Completeness of this four-element classification uses the independently replayed finite census.

## Complete finite computations

- No binary unitriangular defect through dimension five.
- Exactly twenty defective targets in `U_6(F_2)`, all with distances `7 -> 6`.
- No ternary defect through dimension five; the universal atom gives the dimension-six ternary defect.
- Selected exact ambient distances for connected targets in dimensions seven, eight, and nine over `F_2`.
- Binary symplectic Clifford quotient censuses:
  - one qubit: no defects in total or entangling cost;
  - two qubits: no total-count defect and four entangling-count defects of type `3 -> 2`;
  - three qubits, swap-free total count: twenty-four defects, maximum one;
  - three qubits, total count with unit-cost SWAP: no defects;
  - three qubits, entangling count: 1,624 defects, maximum two.
- Complete three-bit affine-versus-Toffoli enumeration: no affine target is shortened in either total-count or entangling-count cost under the declared no-ancilla model.

## Conjectural or open

- Universal minimality of dimension six over every field or commutative ring.
- Exact geometry and irreducible components of defect loci.
- Border catalysis for the canonical atom and higher families.
- The true asymptotic order of the maximum unitriangular defect between the current `Omega(n log n)` lower bound and quadratic upper bound.
- Whether multiplicative distortion is universally bounded and whether interacting catalysts exceed the clean-carrier `3/2` regime.
- Tensor activation: zero one-copy defect but positive regularized tensor capacity.
- The first Toffoli-assisted affine defect beyond three bits, if one exists.
- Root-system thresholds outside type A.

## Contextual source, not a proof input

The quantum-Latin-square discussion is used as an example of an existence defect: an ambient entangled realization can exist while a restricted non-entangled realization does not. No circuit theorem in the monograph is inferred from that result.
