\section*{How to use this atlas}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How to use this atlas}
This atlas develops three quantitative examples of finite resolution and a family
of two interacting entire functions. Fourteen computed plates connect the definitions,
parameter geometry, growth laws, precision certificates and exact recovery.
Complete proofs appear in the accompanying \emph{Special Functions} monograph.

An exact rational inequality is a certificate. A numerical curve illustrates its
scale within its stated truncation and precision. Neither replaces the analytic
proof of a statement about an infinite object. The limits of each construction
are recorded alongside its results.
