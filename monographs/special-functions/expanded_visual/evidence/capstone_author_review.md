## Conventions and continuity

Every capstone starts from the same finite record x=(0,1,1,2,3,5,8,13), with original indices 0 through 7 and zero extension elsewhere. A(z)=1-z-z^2 is a tap polynomial; filtering a record P means multiplication A(z)P. A(C3) is the separate operation of polynomial evaluation at the three-phase carry matrix. The full filtered support has indices 0 through 9.

C02 uses all three phases, with u=z^3 under synthesis. C04 explicitly switches to observing only the even channel at modulus two, with u=z^2 under synthesis. C05 retains that observation and adds a stated exact recurrence model. C06 explicitly returns to modulus-three output routing. Matrices have output-phase rows and input-phase columns. The causal direct-form history holds preceding input samples oldest first. Its phase clock is the next global output index modulo three.

## Direct derivations, separate from finite computation

1. **Finite boundary.** For P_N=sum(n=0,...,N-1) F_n z^n with N>=2, the coefficient of A(z)P_N at index 0 is zero and at index 1 is one. Each coefficient at 2<=n<=N-1 is F_n-F_(n-1)-F_(n-2)=0. At index N it is -F_(N-1)-F_(N-2)=-F_N. At index N+1 it is -F_(N-1). All later coefficients vanish. This proves the displayed general finite-prefix identity, including N=2 where the interior range is empty.

2. **Three-phase transfer.** For f=(f0,f1,f2), C3 f=(u f2,f0,f1), and scalar synthesis S3(f)=f0(z^3)+z f1(z^3)+z^2 f2(z^3) satisfies S3(C3 f)=z S3(f). Applying this twice and subtracting gives S3((I-C3-C3^2)f)=A(z)S3(f). Direct row multiplication at f=(2u+8u^2,1+3u+13u^2,1+5u) yields (-13u^3,1,-21u^2). Synthesis restores the complete ten-position residual.

3. **Interior perturbation.** Q=P8+z^5. Polynomial distributivity gives A(z)Q-A(z)P8=A(z)z^5=z^5-z^6-z^7. This is the stated experiment, not a universal rule for interpreting unknown errors or choosing a recurrence model.

4. **Projection obstruction.** The difference Q-P8=z^5 has no even coefficient, so the two observed inputs agree. After multiplication by A(z), the difference has the even coefficient -1 at original index 6, giving -u^3. If any map U on even observations satisfied R20(A(z)P)=U(R20 P) for every finite integer polynomial, those equal arguments would have to give equal results. They do not. No linearity assumption on U is needed for this contradiction.

5. **Declared-model completion.** A length-eight record with seeds a,b and recurrence x[n+2]=x[n+1]+x[n] for n=0,...,5 is exactly (a,b,a+b,a+2b,2a+3b,3a+5b,5a+8b,8a+13b). Its even observations are (a,a+b,2a+3b,5a+8b). Consequently E0 fixes a and E1 fixes b=E1-E0. The last two observations equal those generated by these seeds precisely when E2=3E1-E0 and E3=3E2-E1. These checks are therefore necessary and sufficient for a unique completion in this exact finite recurrence family. They do not identify arbitrary records outside the family, including Q.

6. **Streaming execution.** Before fine index n, maintain (x[n-2],x[n-1]; n mod 3). Compute y[n]=x[n]-x[n-1]-x[n-2], append it to the globally addressed phase, shift the two input values, and increment n. Induction on n gives the same arithmetic and phase address as whole-record convolution, regardless of where chunks end. Two additional zero inputs give the entire degree-two filter tail. The record has seven possible interior cut positions, hence 2^7=128 positive chunk compositions; the generator checks each one.
