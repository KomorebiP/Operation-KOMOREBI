# Bounded primary-source checks

Checked 2026-10-07. This is formula/hypothesis verification for the consolidation, not a global novelty survey. No licensed source PDFs are republished in this package.

## Báez-Duarte criterion

Luis Báez-Duarte, *A sequential Riesz-like criterion for the Riemann hypothesis*, International Journal of Mathematics and Mathematical Sciences 2005:21, 3527–3537, DOI 10.1155/IJMMS.2005.3527. Published PDF: https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/IJMMS/Volume2005_21/141565.pdf.

Theorem 1.1, page 3527, uses the same finite reciprocal-zeta binomial sum as the principal packet amplitude and states the bound with every positive epsilon. The exact finite-difference identity in the manuscript supplies the notation match. Dividing its squared bound by the positive energy asymptotic gives the coherence form; the exponent can be renamed. Classification: classical theorem, same form after this explicit normalization. The present check authenticates and extracts the theorem, not a new audit of its full published proof.

## Askey–Wilson scalar equation

NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, §18.28, equations 18.28.1_5 and 18.28.6_1–6_2: https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.28. The normalized terminating scalar expression, operator, and eigenvalue match Paper VIII. Parameters must avoid undefined denominator factors, with removable cases handled separately. Positive orthogonality has additional parameter restrictions in 18.28.2/6. The eigenfunction law alone establishes neither that orthogonality nor equality with the constructed Casimir-overlap matrices. Classification: classical formula after direct notation translation; matrix identification remains conditional.

## Gaussian support interval

Walter Gautschi, *Orthogonal polynomials: applications and computation*, Acta Numerica 5 (1996), 45–119. Author copy: https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/wxg/selected_works/section_04/146.pdf. Opening discussion, pp. 48–49, gives Gaussian exactness through degree 2n−1 and places nodes in the smallest interval containing the measure's support. This supports interval containment, not membership in the original discrete spectrum. The packet-specific one-node counterexample is derived directly in the manuscript. Classification: established Gaussian-quadrature input.
