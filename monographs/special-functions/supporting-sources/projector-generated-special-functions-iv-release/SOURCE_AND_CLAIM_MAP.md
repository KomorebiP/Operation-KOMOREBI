## Computational certificates

The Python program checks finite Gram positivity, direct and finite-difference agreement, finite-window convergence toward the time-average kernel, Gamma asymptotics, the principal closed form, log-cumulant convergence, the signed-resolvent equation, moment-exact Pade reduction, the Borel-Riesz identity, and figure inventory.

## Open boundary

The paper does not prove the Riemann hypothesis. For the principal parameters, RH remains equivalent to subpolynomial growth of the normalized time-zero Mobius coherence. Gamma dephasing, positive Gram structure, character decoherence, and finite signed reductions do not prove that bound.
