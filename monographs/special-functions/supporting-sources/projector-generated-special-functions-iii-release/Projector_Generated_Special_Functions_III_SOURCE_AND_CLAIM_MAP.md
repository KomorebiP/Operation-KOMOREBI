# Source and claim map

## New proved statements in this paper

### Positive packet measure

The squarefree packet energy is represented as the moment sequence of a positive discrete measure on `[0,1)`.

### Strict generalized Hankel positivity

Every minor of the kernel `E_{r+s}` with strictly increasing nonnegative row and column exponents is strictly positive. The proof uses the discrete Andréief identity and positivity of generalized Vandermonde determinants.

### Zero-free packet-energy resolvent

The Stieltjes function

\[
\mathcal E(z)=\int\frac{d\nu(x)}{1-zx}
\]

is analytic and zero-free on `C \ [1, infinity)`.

### Moment-exact finite reduction

An `m`-step Lanczos reduction preserves the moments of degrees `0` through `2m-1` exactly. The resulting nodes lie in the packet spectrum and the quadrature weights are positive.

### Gamma and Laguerre limits

Under the tilted measure `x^k dν/E_k`, the scaled endpoint coordinate `k(1-x)` converges to `Gamma(alpha,1)`, where

\[
\alpha=\frac{2a-1}{\beta}.
\]

Fixed-degree orthogonal packet polynomials, Jacobi coefficients, kernels, nodes, and weights converge to the corresponding generalized Laguerre objects.

### Shifted Hankel determinants

For fixed degree `d`, the paper proves the full leading constant and exponent for

\[
\det[E_{k+i+j}]_{i,j=0}^{d}.
\]

### Principal edge cusp

For `a=beta=2` and conductor one, the resolvent has the square-root endpoint law stated in the paper.

## Classical inputs

The paper uses established results on:

- squarefree counting
- Hausdorff and Stieltjes moment theory
- generalized Vandermonde positivity
- orthogonal polynomial recurrences
- Gaussian quadrature
- Lanczos tridiagonalization
- generalized Laguerre polynomials
- the Báez-Duarte criterion

## Computational evidence only

The finite plots and tables illustrate convergence rates at declared truncations. They do not replace the asymptotic proofs.

## Open boundary

The positive energy denominator is completely structured in this paper. The signed numerator

\[
c_k=\sum_n\mu(n)n^{-2}(1-n^{-2})^k
\]

still requires an arithmetic coherence estimate. The paper does not prove RH or GRH.

## Attached quantum source

The number-basis Hamiltonian `H|n> = log(n)|n>` is connected to the 2026 quantum-dynamics construction of Wei and collaborators. This paper uses that source only as a quantum realization context. No figure from the source article is copied or adapted.
