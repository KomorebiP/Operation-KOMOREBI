# Projector-Generated Special Functions III

## Squarefree Stieltjes-Jacobi Functions, Laguerre Packet Universality, Hankel Determinants, and Moment-Exact Reduction

**Author:** Komorebi P.

This release develops the positive moment and orthogonal-polynomial theory generated by the squarefree packet energy

\[
E_{a,\beta,q}(k)
=
\sum_{\substack{n\ge1\\(n,q)=1}}
\frac{\mu(n)^2}{n^{2a}}
\left(1-\frac1{n^\beta}\right)^{2k}.
\]

The paper proves:

- a compact positive packet-measure representation
- strict generalized Hankel total positivity
- a zero-free Stieltjes resolvent on the slit plane
- a Jacobi continued fraction
- moment-exact Lanczos and Gaussian reduction
- Gamma endpoint scaling
- fixed-degree generalized Laguerre universality
- convergence of Jacobi coefficients and Christoffel-Darboux kernels
- convergence of Gaussian packet quadrature
- complete leading asymptotics for shifted Hankel determinants
- a square-root spectral-edge cusp in the principal case

The positive theory does not prove the Riemann hypothesis. The signed principal Möbius coherence bound remains open.
