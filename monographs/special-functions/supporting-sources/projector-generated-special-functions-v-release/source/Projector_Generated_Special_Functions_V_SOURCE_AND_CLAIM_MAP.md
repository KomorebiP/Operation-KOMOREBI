## RH boundary

For the principal parameters, define

\[
c_k
=
\sum_n\frac{\mu(n)}{n^2}(1-n^{-2})^k
\]

and

\[
E_k
=
\sum_n\frac{\mu(n)^2}{n^4}(1-n^{-2})^{2k}.
\]

The paper records the exact equivalence

\[
\mathrm{RH}
\iff
\frac{|c_k|^2}{E_k}
=O_\varepsilon(k^\varepsilon)
\quad\text{for every }\varepsilon>0.
\]

This is a theorem target and not a proof of RH.
