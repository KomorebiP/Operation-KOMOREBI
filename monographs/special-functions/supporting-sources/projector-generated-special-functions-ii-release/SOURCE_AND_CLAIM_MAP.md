# Source and claim map

## Proved in this paper

The paper proves the following statements under the hypotheses written in the manuscript.

1. The Taylor germ of each cyclotomic Bernoulli–Lerch detail is a triangular transform of cyclotomic packets of generalized Stieltjes constants.
2. Nonprincipal character transforms of generalized Stieltjes constants recover the complete derivative sequence \(L^{(m)}(1,\chi)\).
3. The holomorphic detail variance equals both the sum of non-DC channel products and the mean pairwise squared difference of the child packets.
4. The primitive packet resultant is entire and vanishes exactly when a primitive detail channel vanishes.
5. The squarefree packet-energy sequence is a Hausdorff moment sequence and is completely monotone.
6. Every finite packet-energy Hankel matrix is positive definite.
7. The packet-energy generating resolvent is analytic and zero-free on the slit plane \(\mathbb C\setminus[1,\infty)\).
8. Packet energy is an exact finite difference of the squarefree zeta ratio with conductor Euler factors removed.
9. Packet energy has the stated power-law asymptotic.
10. The normalized inverse-scale packet coordinate converges to a Gamma distribution.
11. Distinct Dirichlet-character packets become asymptotically orthogonal at the stated rate.
12. The quantum/logarithmic transfer representation and its long-time diagonal-power identity hold in the declared Hilbert-space range.

## Classical inputs

The paper uses established facts about:

- the Laurent expansion of the Hurwitz zeta function
- generalized Stieltjes constants
- Bernoulli multiplication
- Dirichlet characters and Dirichlet \(L\)-functions
- squarefree counting
- partial summation
- beta and gamma asymptotics
- Hausdorff moment sequences
- the Báez-Duarte criterion

The logarithmic quantum Hamiltonian and direct zeta-linked dynamical construction are taken from the 2026 work of Wei, Zhai, Lu, and collaborators. No figure from that source is reproduced or adapted.

## Open statements

The paper does not prove the Riemann hypothesis. The open principal coherence statement is equivalent to RH after the proved energy normalization. The paper also leaves open the global zero theory of the primitive resultants, stronger total-positivity laws for Stieltjes packet matrices, and extensions to automorphic coefficient systems.

## Numerical role

The Python calculations verify displayed identities, asymptotic convergence, finite positivity, and finite character decorrelation. They do not replace the analytic proofs or establish historical priority.
