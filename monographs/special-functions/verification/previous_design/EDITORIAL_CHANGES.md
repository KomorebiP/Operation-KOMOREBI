# Editorial and design changes

The cover now follows the recent core-volume typography: a dark plum field,
large unembellished serif title, restrained gold rule, and the Realization Calculus
identity. No logo, development date or release number appears on the cover.

The interior uses seven genuine chapters rather than independently headed sheets.
Part openings, section numbers, appendix letters, running heads, folios and captions
have a shared hierarchy. The front matter has Roman folios and the main sequence
has Arabic folios. Contents and PDF outlines provide navigation.

Figures retain their original vector artwork and computational data. Captions are
numbered by chapter and remain with the relevant figure. Narrow table columns are
ragged-right to avoid stretched spacing. Long headings do not hyphenate. The worked
code example remains together, and isolated continuation paragraphs were reflowed.

The proof appendices retain their mathematical statements. Computational scope and
provenance discussions were moved into the evidence register. Glossary and references
remain at the back. Limited prose edits remove redundant development language.

A rendered-page check exposed a bold-capital-A font-mapping defect when the font was
selected by its PostScript name. Explicit OpenType file selection corrected the defect.
The final build has been checked for that defect across all text spans.
