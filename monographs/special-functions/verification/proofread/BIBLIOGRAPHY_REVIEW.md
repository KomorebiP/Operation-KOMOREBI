# Bibliographic review scope

The production bibliography has 15 numbered entries. Duplicate references were consolidated and citations in the main text and appendices were linked to that bibliography.

Targeted primary-source metadata checks covered Méndez, Dascaliuc and collaborators, Blasiak and Flajolet, Byrnes and collaborators, Alrabaiah and collaborators, Uvarov, Voros and the published erratum, Coffey, Elkies, and Schilling–Song–Vondraček. DLMF definitions were checked at their official source. The source URLs are preserved in `monograph/references.tex`.

Corrections include the plural title *Hypergeometric Zeta Functions*, complete Uvarov journal details, a single Méndez entry with the exact Example 2.4 locator, and the Voros erratum. The erratum's publication record was verified, not independently audited for all implications. Some inherited bibliography metadata was retained without a new exhaustive review.

Two web screenshot attempts for the Méndez PDF failed at the retrieval layer. Its parsed primary text remained accessible. A direct Schilling DOI open also failed, while official publisher metadata was accessible. No claim of successful visual retrieval is made for those failed requests.

This is a targeted citation and attribution check, not a systematic historical-priority survey or an independent proof audit of every cited source.
