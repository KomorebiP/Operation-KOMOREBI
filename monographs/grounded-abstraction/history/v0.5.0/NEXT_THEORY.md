# Next theoretical work

**Current base: Grounded Abstraction v0.5.0.** Output-relative prediction and exact noisy correction are no longer wholly open headings: this edition closes several restricted questions within them.

## Closed within this edition's hypotheses

The unrestricted calibration class admits no larger all-word domain for a nonzero smaller output. For a finite family of fixed linear systems, paired observability computes the exact agreeing initial subspace. Whole-space agreement is equivalent to a common observable quotient. Evidence initialization and minimum linear predictor dimension have an exact criterion. Fixed-world likelihood-profile masses give a minimal equivalence of priors for joint evidence prediction and target correction, with an exact migration criterion under changed partitions.

## First-priority extension

Develop an approximate common-quotient contract. Specify the uncertainty family, the output norm, the horizon, leakage into observable directions, and the growth bound before deriving an error radius. Small one-step discrepancy or a nearly invisible uncertainty range alone cannot justify arbitrary continuation. The exact hidden-return example is a required negative control.

## Further bounded questions

Economical noisy memory should charge finite-precision storage and accumulated update error, especially after low-probability reports. Restricted experiment grammars may admit smaller memory than the all-word likelihood interpolation proof requires. Model admission needs a declared rule for assigning or recovering mass when new worlds or likelihood profiles appear; an old partition record does not do this automatically.

These are theoretical research questions. None makes a broad trained-agent implementation a prerequisite for publication. Reuse Sufficient Resolution and Machine Understanding where their existing assumptions match, rather than duplicating their results under a new title.
