# First-edition closeout

**Version 1.0.0 · 9 October 2026**

The first edition is closed at a bounded theoretical scope, not at the construction of a general learned agent.

## Completed scope

1. Exact evidence-relative identification, correction memory, calibrated continuation, fixed-model output comparison, a common observable quotient, and evidence-initialized linear realization.
2. A finite retained probability-field construction with explicit likelihood floor, within-cell model error, repeated quantization and horizon dependence.
3. A visible/hidden norm comparison with an exact no-return limit, a finite-horizon leakage bound and a separately conditioned infinite summed bound.
4. One posterior decision certificate combining memory and response error under matching histories, cells and loss semantics.
5. A complete eight-world worked example, a revision-dependency table, and a first-edition reading guide.
6. Review of all twenty statements' assumptions, verbatim preservation of the sixteen predecessors, targeted review of fourteen reference entries, three small exact suites, rendered page inspection and clean rebuilding.

The supplied review records give the actual depth of these activities. Completion does not imply that every external paper was read in full or every theorem was independently re-proved by a second researcher.

## Not required to freeze this edition

Broad training, construction of an end-to-end agent, empirical cognitive attribution, a fully formalized proof kernel, or unrestricted model discovery. These are not retroactively claimed by the version number.

## Next research

State-changing noisy evidence, economical adaptive precision, stable long-horizon filtering under additional structure, restricted action languages and model admission are separate extensions. Read NEXT_THEORY.md. New results should preserve the original edition and state which assumptions change.
