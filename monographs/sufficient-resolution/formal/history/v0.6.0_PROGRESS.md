## Next selected target

Quantitative Hilbert-space globalization, beginning with finite-dimensional real
inner-product spaces. Given A:G→V and delta:V→W with delta A=0, separate a local
vector into realizable data, compatible-but-obstructed data, and inconsistency.
Prove the exact squared-distance decomposition, then a residual-to-distance bound
with an explicit coercivity/smallest-positive-singular-value assumption. Do not
infer global admissible realization merely from pairwise compatibility, and do
not silently discard the obstruction component or conditioning constant.
