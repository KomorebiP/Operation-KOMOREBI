## New principal contract

Let X and Z be real Banach spaces. Let L, A and q be bounded real linear maps of
appropriate types. Define delta = qL-Aq and E(t)=q exp(tL)-exp(tA)q. Differentiation
of the two-sided propagator and the fundamental theorem of calculus establish

    E(t) = integral_0^t exp((t-s)A) delta exp(sL) ds.

This is an equality of continuous linear maps for every real t, using oriented
integrals. It is not assumed, and is not a finite Taylor approximation.

For t>=0, suppose the actual flows obey

    ||exp(sL)|| <= ML exp(a s),   ||exp(sA)|| <= MA exp(b s)    (s>=0),
    ||delta|| <= epsilon,        ML, MA, epsilon >= 0.

Then

    ||E(t)|| <= ML MA epsilon R(a,b,t),
    R(a,b,t) = integral_0^t exp(b(t-s)) exp(a s) ds.

The exact scalar response is (exp(a t)-exp(b t))/(a-b) for a!=b, and
 t exp(a t) for a=b. Rates may be negative, zero or positive.
The more general envelope theorem only needs bounds on the interval [0,t].

A readout O and reduced readout V with ||O-Vq||<=eta give

    ||O exp(tL) - V exp(tA)q||
       <= eta ML exp(a t) + ||V|| ML MA epsilon R(a,b,t).

If the true flow is bounded by ML and the reduced flow by MA exp(-rate s),
rate>0, the uniform allowance is

    eta ML + ||V|| ML MA epsilon / rate.

Arbitrary initialization z instead of qx adds its own term

    ||V|| MA exp(-rate t) ||qx-z||.

The model allowance is multiplied by ||x|| for state-level readout estimates.
Exact composition transports both stages' defects. Scalar systems attain the
two-rate estimate. For every epsilon>0, the scalar pair L=epsilon and A=0 has
exact generator defect epsilon but flow error exp(epsilon t)-1 unbounded in t.

orders 0–4, and 25 scalar rate pairs. It rejects 15 corrupted/invalid inputs.

## Proof boundaries

The new theorems concern bounded, time-independent real Banach-space generators.
No finite-dimensionality, injectivity, surjectivity or invertibility of q is needed.
Numerical residuals on a finite sample do not certify the required whole-map bounds.
Flow-growth constants are hypotheses; they are not inferred from eigenvalues.
No claim about unbounded generators, time-varying/nonlinear dynamics, stochastic
forcing, data-driven identification, approximate minimum dimension, constrained
positivity/integrality, or arithmetic globalization follows from this block.
This formalizes and connects mathematical results; it is not a novelty/priority audit.
