## Block 12

Completed mathematical contracts: actual residual monotonicity/continuity,
strictness and uniqueness, existence from a feasible residual or actual infimum
distance, positive root construction, direct strong discrepancy consistency,
small-data handling, approximate discrepancy bands, source-condition squared-error
rate, kernel ambiguity limit, and exact endpoint/scalar boundaries.

The construction no longer requires a supplied asymptotic lambda/noise schedule.
Instead it requires a valid noise allowance and the proved discrepancy rule.
No closed-range or finite-dimensional hypothesis is imposed in these results.
