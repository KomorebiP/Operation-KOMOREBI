## New contracts

The actual normal residual controls source correction at scale 1/λ and fitting
correction at the sharp scale 1/(2√λ). Measurement and solver margins transfer an
observed inexact discrepancy into the exact accepted band. Actual squared records
imply near-accepted sources. Valid noise allowances and vanishing source-correction
budgets give strong minimum-norm consistency without closed range or a separately
prescribed parameter schedule. Small-data outputs may also be inexact. Numerical
kernel leakage tends to zero; true source-kernel ambiguity remains in the limit.

The finite checker does not solve an inverse problem or assume its computed source
is exact: it verifies normal residuals and squared measurement/budget inequalities.
It is tested Python, not formally verified code.

No overall completion percentage is assigned. The infinite research program does
not have a frozen inventory of commensurable final goals. The selected fixed-operator
inexact-discrepancy contract is distinct from these wider obligations.
