## New finite controls

`scripts/check_continuous_approximation.py` uses only `fractions.Fraction`, with
seed 20260913, source/reduced dimensions 0–3, Taylor degree eight, and mixed-time
degrees 0–4. It checks generator defects, power-series coefficients, an independent
polynomial-antiderivative expansion of the Duhamel integrand, coefficient norm
bounds, time composition, cascaded models, readouts, initialization, distinct/equal
scalar rates, and growth-free counterexamples. Invalid sizes, short/corrupted
coefficient tables, and invalid orders are rejected.

These are finite rational-coefficient controls, not a numerical evaluation of an
entire exponential. They do not infer flow growth bounds, replace the universal
Lean proofs, or formally verify Python/JSON parsing.

## Mathematical boundaries

The new exact Duhamel identity uses bounded time-independent generators on real
Banach spaces and holds at all real times with oriented integrals. Quantitative
norm bounds require nonnegative time and explicit whole-flow growth envelopes.
Those envelopes are not inferred from eigenvalues or sampled data. No unbounded,
time-varying, nonlinear/stochastically forced, constrained-realization, approximate
minimum-dimension, or arithmetic-existence result is asserted. An arbitrary
Volterra-solution reconstruction and Hilbert-space globalization remain separate.
