## Next selected target

Canonical minimum-norm global reconstruction. For a bounded A:G->V between Hilbert
spaces with closed range, construct a bounded reconstruction on range(A) using
A restricted to ker(A)^perp, compose with P_range(A), and prove the inverse/projection
identities. Derive source-error bounds from an explicit lower norm bound for A on
ker(A)^perp. Keep that source-conditioning constant distinct from sigma for delta.
