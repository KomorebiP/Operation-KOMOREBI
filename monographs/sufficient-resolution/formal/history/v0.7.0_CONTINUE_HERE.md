    r=P_R v, o=(P_K-P_R)v, i=(I-P_K)v,
    v=r+o+i uniquely and orthogonally,
    dist(v,R)^2=||o||^2+||i||^2.

linearly equivalent to K intersect R^perp. Do not call this a quotient-norm isometry.

    dist(v,R)^2<=||o||^2+(||delta v||/sigma)^2.
