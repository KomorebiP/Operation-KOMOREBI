## Finite rational checks

`scripts/check_tikhonov.py` uses `fractions.Fraction`, seed 20260913, source/target
dimensions 0–5 and lambda values 1/4, 1, 4. It checks actual normal inverses,
quadratic objective gaps, whole-map sharp PSD bounds, solver certificates,
source-kernel ambiguity, source-condition bias, operator changes including rank
changes, parameter identities, and scalar sharpness. Bias/noise cancellation is
included to reject an incorrect claim that these two terms are orthogonal.

The augmented least-squares comparison shares rational matrix helpers with the
main construction. Nine SymPy inverse comparisons use a separate exact library
implementation. Neither route is a formally verified algorithm. Finite successful
checks do not prove the generic theorems; those have separate Lean proofs.

The CLI caps dimensions at 16, JSON at 1 MB, rational text at 256 characters and
coefficient numerator/denominator bit lengths at 2048. It rejects floats, bools,
nonpositive lambda, malformed dimensions and unsupported fields. It assumes
known rational matrices and standard Euclidean metrics.

The example returns source (1,1), fit error squared 42, source norm squared 2 and
penalized objective 44 at lambda=1. Its unregularized minimum-norm source is
(3/2,3/2), with fit error 41 and penalized objective 91/2. The new fitting error
splits as 42=16+25+1. These are tested rational values, not an additional concrete
matrix Lean instance. The universal Lean results and actual scalar Lean examples
are separate.

## Mathematical boundaries

The core constructs the actual Tikhonov inverse for bounded REAL Hilbert operators
and lambda>0, WITHOUT closed range or finite dimension. Closed range is reinstated
only when comparing to the old pseudoinverse or its quotient geometry. The selected
source lies in ker(A)^perp; invisible source content is not recovered. Bias and
propagated noise need not be orthogonal. Fixed-lambda rank-change bounds retain
operator norms and inverse-lambda factors; they do not certify the lambda=0 inverse
or a bound uniform as lambda vanishes. Alpha for A differs from sigma for delta.

Strong convergence as lambda tends to zero, general noisy parameter selection,
nonlinear/constrained penalties, positivity/integrality, unknown-operator learning
and arithmetic existence remain separate. Tikhonov and Lax–Milgram are classical;
this is a verified integration, not a historical novelty certificate.
