## Main boundaries

Projectors/closedness are explicit. Compatible obstruction and local inconsistency
are separate. In a nonclosed quotient, the native seminorm can vanish on a nonzero
class. Quantitative reverse transport needs a bounded reverse certificate. Refinement
fixes the metric and compatibility space. No positivity, integral or arithmetic
realization, unknown-operator learning, or nonlinear global theorem is inferred.
