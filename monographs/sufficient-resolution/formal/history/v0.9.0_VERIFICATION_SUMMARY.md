0–5, nonorthogonal compatible bases, redundant global columns, realization shifts,
refinement, perturbations, and norm identities. The ambient-projector route is
compared against Gram-metric constrained minimization in compatible coordinates;
the two routes share rational matrix helpers. Input dimensions in the evaluator
are capped at 16 and its JSON input at 1 MB. The example's squared intrinsic norm

separate generic Lean theorems give the universal claims.

operator, positivity, or arbitrary arithmetic realizability.

## Mathematical scope

The old quotient and harmonic equivalence are unchanged. The new isometry uses
Mathlib's actual quotient norm, under explicit projection hypotheses. The native
quotient inner product is preserved with its projection instance in scope. Norm
definiteness fails for nonclosed subspaces, as separately proved without the positive
hypotheses. The isometric class section is not the global source inverse. Arbitrary
local data still require zero inconsistency in addition to zero obstruction.
Quantitative reverse transport requires a supplied bounded left inverse. Refinement
fixes the compatibility space and ambient metric. No general regularization,
changing-rank stability, nonlinear, positive, integral or arithmetic existence result
is inferred. The central Hilbert quotient geometry is classical; this is a verified
implementation/integration, not a historical novelty certificate.
