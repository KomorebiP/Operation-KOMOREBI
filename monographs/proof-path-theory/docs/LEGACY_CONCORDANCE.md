## Moved out of the canonical theorem layer

Earlier documents contained toy simulations of idea growth, meaning lag, social interpretation, and observer noise. Their conceptual motivation remains relevant, but they are not presented as empirical laws or core theorems in 1.0. The canonical release retains only the finite mathematical statements and declared models needed to support reproducible analysis.

Historical files should be preserved in release archives or a dedicated `legacy/` branch rather than copied into the canonical package namespace.
