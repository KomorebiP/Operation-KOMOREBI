# Monographs and companion volumes

**Komorebi P.**

Read Realization Calculus and Komorebi Space together. Realization Boundaries develops questions about finite representation and its limits. The remaining volumes extend the framework into analysis, physical models, signals, computation, and mathematical method.

## Foundations and semantics

| Work | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Realization Calculus](realization-calculus/) | The collected core development, from packets and transfer to refinement and applications. |
| [Komorebi Space](komorebi-space/) | The ambient framework for realizations, projections, compatibility, residuals, and obstructions. |
| [Realization Boundaries](realization-boundaries/) | Representability, finite descriptions, and explicit limits of realization. |
| [Sufficient Resolution](sufficient-resolution/) | Resolution conditions and the information retained by a representation. |

## Analysis, signals, and physical models

| Work | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Large Signal Model](large-signal-model/) | Algebraic signal models and their mathematical foundations. |
| [Special Functions](special-functions/) | Cyclic families, sequence constructions, reciprocal structure, and exact methods. |
| [Quantum and Circuits](quantum-and-circuits/) | Quantum models, channel interfaces, and circuit constructions. |
| [Physics](physics/) | Discrete and continuous model structures and local constructions. |
| [COS Method](cos-method/) | Quadratic-form probability calculations, tail accuracy, and numerical evidence. |
| [COS Method: formal continuation](cos-method/formal-continuation/) | A separate continuation of the COS formal development. |
