# After the first-edition freeze

Ordinary maintenance should prioritize substantive corrections, independently reproduced finite results, and clearer presentation over adding more unrelated theorem labels.

Research directions beyond the frozen scope include learning new experiment semantics under calibration constraints; generating and validating stronger proof-search strategies; extending grammars while preserving applicable prior scope; improving the equation-acquisition algorithm rather than only reusing its output; and measuring transferable gains under a prospectively fixed task law with full development costs.

A separate applied study would require genuine learner/environment identification and held-out evidence. It must not treat the present analytical constructions as experiments establishing AGI or indefinitely recursive improvement.
