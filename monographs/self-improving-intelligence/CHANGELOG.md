# Revision history

## 0.6.0 — 8 October 2026

Expanded the 47-page discovery-synthesis edition to a 55-page, seven-part recursive-acquisition edition. Added five statement/proof pairs, three counterexamples, two analytical figures, and three primary references. All 23 preceding statement/proof pairs and nine core reference programs remain unchanged.

The new construction discovers candidate arithmetic equations from probes, admits only replayable equations, and uses them to quotient the ordered synthesis grammar. The exact teacher-proposal and counterexample trace is preserved. A stronger supplied normalizer, setup costs, semantic invalidation, and an out-of-grammar target remain explicit controls.

A second development replaces singleton-state counting with checked repair-cover weights. It preserves the exact first returned tree in an ordered finite search while allowing one repair to succeed in several cases. Changing the repair language can invalidate the weight witness. The greedy witness constructor is supplied and is not asserted optimal or learned.

New exact checks comprise 14 groups and 111,024 assertions. Together with the unchanged prior suites, the final total is 62 groups and 318,167 assertions. Introduced `meta_synthesis.py`, `weighted_repair_pruning.py`, `demo_meta.py`, `check_meta_models.py`, and two regenerated analytical vector figures. Added a first-edition scope plan, clarified construction costs, and consolidated the open recursive boundary.

A preliminary parity negative control used a domain with only one even value and was therefore expressible. Its domain was corrected before the final checks. A combined tool call was interrupted before producing a final execution receipt; the final stable source was rerun to confirmed completion. Removing one redundant related-work paragraph eliminated a nearly empty continuation page. The review record preserves these events.
