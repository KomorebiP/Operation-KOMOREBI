## Scope beyond the freeze

Learned physical experiment semantics, learned primitive proof search, autonomous extension of the synthesis language, a broad task-distribution advantage, and repeated improvement of the final equation learner are separate research goals. A first edition neither solves them nor postpones publication until they are solved.

Version 1.0 is the first consolidated theoretical edition. It is not a claim of external review, general intelligence, indefinite acceleration, journal acceptance, or Lean verification.
