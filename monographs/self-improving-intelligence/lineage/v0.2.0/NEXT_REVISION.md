# Next revision: scope rather than version inflation

The current draft now contains a real change to a discovery algorithm within a supplied finite family. The final missing recursive step is not another table of faster returned controllers. It is a demonstrated improvement to the procedure that proposes or selects those discovery algorithms, evaluated from comparable fresh starting conditions.

The next theoretical targets are:

1. **A costed language of compiler transformations.** Permit more than a fixed first/balanced choice while retaining a decidable semantics or checkable witness for each candidate. The cost of constructing and checking a candidate must remain in the comparison.
2. **Non-oracular model acquisition.** Replace the complete supplied experiment table with an acquired, explicitly uncertain model. Preserve model-class uncertainty and representation scope, and distinguish a physically informative observation from a renamed internal field.
3. **Joint resource robustness.** Extend the chosen lookup metric to a declared vector of compilation work, retained source/controller storage, observation cost, and verification work. A decrease in one coordinate is not a universal speedup.
4. **An executable fair race.** The current bounded-slowdown proposition requires resumability and is not implemented by the sequential fallback. A future implementation must account for stored states, dispatch cost, and candidate-checking work.
5. **Preserved evaluation through further generations.** Separate fresh paired comparison evidence, candidate selection, model validity, and changes to the score itself. A new candidate or audit contract requires a new justified comparison, not reuse of favorable old evidence.

A 1.0 edition should consolidate the finite scope, review the bibliography and proofs, and give one complete, inspectable account of what is supplied, selected, discovered, checked, and charged. It need not claim a general neural AGI or settle unrestricted recursive self-improvement to be a complete theoretical edition.
