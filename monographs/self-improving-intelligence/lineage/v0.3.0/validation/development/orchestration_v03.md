# Orchestration failures during v0.3 preparation

An interactive session attempt returned StreamingExecNotEnabledContainerError. A short asynchronous-shell tool call returned a timeout, but its subprocess subsequently completed with exit zero and a complete receipt. A later 45-second combined rendering/validation command timed out before final receipt completion. Its old receipt was not accepted as evidence for the modified source. The final source was rerun through detached local standard streams and explicitly polled to termination. The final driver recorded zero for both run_validation.py and check_computation_record.py. Fresh rebuilding then reran the unchanged final source successfully. No mathematical assertion or LaTeX compilation failed. These transport/time-limit failures are distinct from deliberately rejected invalid mathematical inputs.
