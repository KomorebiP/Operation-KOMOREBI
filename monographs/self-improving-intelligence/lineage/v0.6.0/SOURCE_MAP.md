## Added primary references

### [21] Max Willsey and colleagues — egg

*egg: Fast and Extensible Equality Saturation*. Proceedings of the ACM on Programming Languages 5 (POPL), 2021. DOI: 10.1145/3434304. Primary preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03082.

The primary author/title/abstract/venue/DOI record was checked, with primary publication context. Use is limited to the established role of e-graphs and equality saturation in representing equivalent expressions and supporting optimization and synthesis. No benchmark number or external correctness theorem from egg is used as a premise. The new reference implementation is not an e-graph implementation and is not compared as a competitor to egg.

### [22] Chandrakana Nandi and colleagues — Ruler

*Rewrite Rule Inference Using Equality Saturation*. Proceedings of the ACM on Programming Languages 5 (OOPSLA), 2021. DOI: 10.1145/3485496. Primary preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10436. Readable author copy: https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~djg/papers/oopsla2021.pdf.

The primary metadata and abstract were checked, and selected introduction and rule-inference passages were inspected in the author-hosted full text. This is not an independent audit of the paper's implementation or complete proofs. Use: automatic rule inference has direct precedents, and equality saturation can reduce the expression/rule search. The present manuscript makes no priority claim for that general idea and imports no Ruler performance statistic. Its own ordered-trace preservation theorem has a self-contained proof for the more restricted model.

### [23] David P. Williamson and David B. Shmoys

*The Design of Approximation Algorithms*. Cambridge University Press, 2011. Author companion site: https://www.designofapproxalgs.com/. Author-hosted full text: https://www.designofapproxalgs.com/book.pdf.

The author/book record and introductory set-cover/linear-programming material were inspected. Use: repair-cover weights instantiate the familiar feasible-dual lower-bound argument. The manuscript gives its own short summation proof and does not rely on an unstated approximation ratio or optimal dual solver. The implemented greedy weight constructor is deliberately compared with a stronger feasible fractional assignment.

## Review boundary

This is targeted primary-source positioning, not an exhaustive novelty search. Readable full text being available does not imply that every page or proof was audited. Earlier failed and irrelevant retrievals were not counted as successful evidence. The new results are proved explicitly, so an inaccessible external proof is not silently required for correctness.

The source audit checks all 23 bibliography keys and their use. That is a syntax and integrity check, not a scholarly priority audit. No third-party full paper or standalone font file is distributed in this archive.
