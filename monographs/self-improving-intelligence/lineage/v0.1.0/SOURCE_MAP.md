**Review date: 8 October 2026.** This is a targeted primary-source review, not a comprehensive priority audit. The manuscript derives its conditional mathematical statements in place. Public agent results supply context, not evidence that the proposed Komorebi architecture is realized.

## Bibliographic and factual boundary

No internal experimental score, speculative extrapolation, or unverified third-party performance total is included as evidence for the integrated system. The AIDE and Hyperagents passages were checked specifically because their relation to recursive *process* improvement matters. The rest of the public literature review is mainly abstract/metadata-level and supports only the narrow contextual claims made. It is not a full-text audit of every cited proof.

External sources were searched during drafting. Their full texts are not redistributed. The references are identified in the manuscript and here so that a reader can inspect the original arguments independently.
