# Source map and first-edition literature review

**Reference date: 8 October 2026.** This is an itemized primary-source review at the depth stated below, not a comprehensive novelty survey or independent audit of all external proofs. The manuscript's thirty conditional statement/proof pairs are stated in full. Public agent reports supply context, not evidence that this package implements their systems or that the proposed Komorebi architecture achieves general intelligence.
