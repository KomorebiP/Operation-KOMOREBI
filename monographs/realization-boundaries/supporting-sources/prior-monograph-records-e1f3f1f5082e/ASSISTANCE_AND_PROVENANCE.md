# Assistance and provenance

The author credit in the reading edition is Komorebi P., following the project's established publication identity.

This monograph was prepared with AI assistance in the current collaboration. The work included organization and expansion of the supplied research notes, writing and reviewing ordinary proofs, exact-arithmetic implementations and tests, reference checking, figure production, and LaTeX layout. This record does not assert that an independent human referee reviewed the text or that the author separately approved every proof.

Two supplied archives are retained in `provenance/`. Their research statements were used as source material, then developed into the continuous monograph. The new text includes a general pure-preimage theorem, a complete diagonal homomorphism description, endomorphism and automorphism consequences, root-fiber counting, and an explicit categorical distinction between faithful observation and full reconstruction. These are presented with their ordinary proofs. Their historical priority is not claimed.

The inherited prime-spectrum coding overlaps explicitly with standard computable-structure theory. That attribution appears in the text and bibliography. Literature verification was targeted, not an exhaustive novelty search.

No Lean verification or independent external certification was performed. The exact finite tests and visual typesetting review are recorded separately. No infinite noncomputable selector was evaluated by the Python programs.

The main book intentionally omits development versions and operational logs from its narrative. Those records remain in this package for inspection.
