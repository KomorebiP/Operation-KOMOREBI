## Exact mathematical coverage

### The actual generated subgroup and its closure

`endomorphismHull S` is the additive subgroup generated by all `f s`, where `f` is an arbitrary actual additive endomorphism and `s` belongs to the supplied seed set. The identity and compositions are available because all endomorphisms are admitted. The universal invariant-subgroup property and invariance of the hull are proved.

`topologicalEndomorphismHull S` takes the topological closure inside the original group with its supplied topology. `ordinaryTopologicalHull S` instead closes the subgroup generated using group operations alone. These are distinct definitions, not interchangeable labels.

The reusable certificate uses an actual prime-coordinate kernel. It proves containment of the entire operator hull and its topological closure in that closed kernel. An actual nonzero prime seed proves the kernel proper. No bound on the number of admitted endomorphisms is imposed.

### D and every paired group need infinitely many seeds

For D, a finite seed family has finite total support. For G_A, each seed can have infinite support, but its support outside the selected primes is finite. The new proof retains this distinction and chooses an omitted unselected coordinate for any finite family. The previously checked arbitrary-homomorphism diagonality preserves that zero coordinate.

Thus both the algebraic and topological operator hulls are proper for every finite seed set. The exact ranks are defined as infima of the cardinals of genuinely generating subsets. A checked `IsLeast` theorem supplies the countable upper witness and excludes every finite lower cardinal. Countability and infinitude refer to the original infinite groups.

For D and every G_A, the algebraic full-endomorphism rank, topological full-endomorphism rank, and ordinary topological rank are each aleph_0. The ordinary topological result follows from the stronger operator obstruction and the genuine countable upper bound.

### The completion has ordinary topological rank one

`finite_coordinate_interpolation` applies the finite Chinese remainder theorem to distinct actual prime coordinates, producing one natural number with any prescribed finite coordinate pattern. This proves that the ordinary cyclic subgroup of the all-one point is dense in P under the product topology. Nontriviality and the Hausdorff property rule out zero generators, so the exact ordinary topological rank is one.

The proof transports a specific generator through the actual continuous additive equivalence with Mathlib's canonical profinite completion. `completionGenerator W` is a point of that canonical completion, not a redefinition of P. Its generation property and the rank-one theorem are proved for every rational preimage H_W.

`paired_seed_rank_separation` bundles the three original-group rank statements with rank one of the actual completion for every binary selector. This closes the complete statement of monograph Theorem 7.9, not merely its non-generation half.

### Where the new generator comes from

The all-one point is proved absent from D and from every paired G_A. The named generator in the canonical completion is therefore not in the range of the original eta map. Its infinite order is proved, even though each coordinate has finite order.

For every prime p, the nonzero prime seed e_p is outside the ordinary cyclic subgroup generated by the all-one point. Consequently this subgroup is proper even though it is dense. This is a positive counterexample to conflating topological cyclicity with algebraic cyclicity.

Under the separate contract admitting all endomorphisms of P, the all-one point does generate P algebraically: multiplication by an arbitrary ambient coordinate family sends one to that family. The corresponding operator seed rank is exactly one. Such multipliers may contain infinitely specified data. This result is not a finite-description or computable-realization theorem.
