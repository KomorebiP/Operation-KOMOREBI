## Retrieval limits and corrections

A HAL link for the earlier CRDT report returned an access challenge; it was not treated as a retrieved source. The identified 2018 author manuscript was used instead. Several broad searches returned irrelevant material and were refined. The original Jackson–Wolinsky ScienceDirect page returned a 403/internal fetch failure, and an attempted author PDF was inaccessible through the web tool. The publisher reprint abstract and bibliographic records support the limited contextual citation, not an assertion that the original full text was audited. No missing source was reconstructed from an old citation handle.

The Holmström scan required one visual page inspection because parsed text was absent. The Bikhchandani model page was visually inspected to resolve its tie-breaking convention. Source figures and tables are not reproduced in the manuscript. All four added figures are generated from the manuscript's own analytical constructions.

## Attribution boundary

Semilattice identities, data processing, total-variation duality, coupling, Chebyshev bounds, matching, and elementary Bayesian renewal arguments are established mathematical ingredients. The contribution claimed here is their explicit integration into an institutional realization contract and the separation of guarantees they support. The nine additions are not nine claims of mathematical priority.
