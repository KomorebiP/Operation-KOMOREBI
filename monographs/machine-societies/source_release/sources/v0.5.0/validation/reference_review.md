## Retrieval limitations and corrections

Initial broad/multi-query searches returned some irrelevant matches. They were not treated as evidence; exact titles and primary URLs were then used. Several direct DOI fetches failed, and an attempted FIPA specification returned an access error. Those failures are not counted as source verification. Primary author, publisher, and repository alternatives supplied the records used above. The proposed 2026 cultural-evolution citation was not independently identified and was excluded rather than reconstructed from an earlier unsupported citation marker.

The retained normative-agent references, social-choice references, and other established sources remain as in v0.4.0. Their historical audit is preserved under `sources/v0.4.0/reference_review.md` and must not be mistaken for a newly executed review of every bibliography entry.
