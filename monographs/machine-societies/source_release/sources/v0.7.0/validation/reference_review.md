## New reference: Boyd and Vandenberghe
**Convex Optimization**, Cambridge University Press, 2004. Author-hosted book page: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/ . The full-book PDF timed out on attempted fetches. The accessible primary lecture slides at https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxslides.pdf were inspected instead, including the linear-programming duality material in Chapter 5, slide 5.14 (PDF page index 167). The article cites the author-hosted slides explicitly. The full book was not read in this revision.

Use in manuscript: finite convex minimax/LP duality for the common-monitor problem. The paper supplies the reduction to a finite matrix game and its institutional interpretation. The citation does not establish a new minimax result or empirical monitoring law.

## Existing reference rechecked: Chatterjee, Doyen, Henzinger, and Raskin
**Algorithms for Omega-Regular Games with Imperfect Information**, Logical Methods in Computer Science 3(3:4), 2007. Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/0706.2619v3 . Section 3.1 gives the subset construction for sure winning, including the observation-filtered successor construction and strategy correspondences. The paper separates sure and almost-sure objectives and makes the observation-based strategy restriction explicit.

Use in manuscript: foundation for finite sure-game synthesis. Our catalogue and common-observation assumptions are stated directly. The result is not presented as unrestricted decentralized synthesis. The existing bibliography key `chatterjee` was reused. An attempted guessed publisher page did not resolve and was not used as evidence.

## New reference: Chatterjee and Doyen
**Partial-Observation Stochastic Games: How to Win when Belief Fails**, author preprint, 2011. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.2141 . Full text checked at https://arxiv.org/html/1107.2141v1 , particularly the distinctions between sure, positive, and almost-sure objectives and the limits of belief-based strategies. A version-2 HTML request failed; the available version-1 text was used.

Use in manuscript: boundary warning that our support-based stationary strategy result is not universal for broader strategic stochastic games. The preprint date/version is stated without inventing later publication metadata.

No new cultural-evolution or empirical-agent claims were introduced. The 50 inherited bibliography entries were checked for key integrity and use, but their full texts were not all independently re-audited. General arguments in the new section remain conditional on the definitions and stated mathematical assumptions.
