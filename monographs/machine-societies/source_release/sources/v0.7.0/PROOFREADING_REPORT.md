## Mathematical checks and qualifications

**One common monitor.** The finite minimax argument has compact nonempty polytopes and an affine objective. It uses one sanction policy for all listed deviations, not a separate policy chosen with hidden knowledge of the deviator. A mixture obstruction can invalidate simultaneous deterrence despite successful separate tests. The false-positive bound and all error margins constrain that same monitor. Penalties must be authorized, committed, collectible, and interpreted with the stipulated utility model. Auditor incentives and unlisted/adaptive deviations are not solved.

**One common information boundary.** The finite continuation construction selects from a catalogue of already implemented local protocol profiles. It does not grant access to hidden states or create a public record by definition. Admissibility is intersected over all states still possible under the common history. The successor relation includes all outcomes covered by the sure guarantee. Incentive inequalities are not permission to remove physically possible noncompliant paths from that relation.

**Safety versus service.** The greatest fixed point can include an idle loop. A separate rank certificate proves bounded, publicly certifiable completion while retaining a safe continuation. The argument for eventual sure completion uses finite branching to obtain a finite bound. It does not apply that claim to almost-sure completion, and it does not equate an unobserved physical event with certified completion. After service, a safe suffix can leave the progress attractor. The independent checks retain this distinction.

**Exact transport.** The abstraction theorem preserves and reflects observations, admissible profiles, safety and goal labels, and successor images. These obligations include amendments and handovers when they belong to the catalogue. Exact support equality, not a small average prediction error, supports the sure-strategy claim. The proof uses the commuting successor-set update and induction through both fixed-point constructions.

**Shared-budget example.** The richer audit laws and two-slot cost are stipulated. Its monitor `(0,1,1)` has false-positive rate `1/4` and incentive margin `1/2` at penalty `2` and gains `1`. Three due services consume three additional slots. The common contract is feasible at five slots, not four. A rescheduling agreement changes the original deadline contract rather than retroactively fulfilling it. The executable demo reproduces both cases and checks the feasible certificate.

## Finite implementation coverage

The new suite includes 864 exact common-monitor LP comparisons against a separately coded two-deviation dual, mixture and false-positive boundary cases, observation-error controls, all 10,368 two-state/two-profile safety contracts in the declared catalogue, their four completion masks, and 3,888 exact concrete/reduced model refinements. The fixed-point results are compared with direct stationary-policy enumeration and reachable-cycle checks. Deliberately invalid certificates and transport assumptions are rejected. The commons is checked for capacities 0 through 8 and due work 0 through 5.

Integer masks and `fractions.Fraction` supply exact arithmetic. The reference LP routine is bounded vertex enumeration, not a scalable optimizer. The possibility-set construction can be exponential. Separately coded comparisons remain authored within this project and are not independent external replication.

## Corrections, failures, and recovery

The first integration script used an introduction sentence that no longer occurred in v0.6. It raised `ValueError: substring not found` before writing. The shell then built the unchanged staged predecessor. That preliminary build was not treated as the integrated revision. The anchor was corrected, the additions were applied, and the 90-page manuscript rebuilt successfully.

Visual inspection found that the initial legends in the two new plots encroached on the data region. Both legends were placed below the axes, the plots regenerated, and the affected pages inspected again. A grammatical article was corrected and the nonempty-profile-catalogue convention made explicit. Input handling was strengthened for invalid ranks, out-of-range goal masks, and negative state masks. All mathematical assertion runs completed without an assertion failure.

Several broad source searches were irrelevant. The full Boyd–Vandenberghe book PDF timed out, so the accessible author-hosted lecture slides were used and cited explicitly. An unavailable version-2 preprint HTML URL was replaced by the available version-1 full text. These retrieval failures were not counted as successful source review. The exact reviewed sources and limits are in `validation/reference_review.md`.
