# Claim and dependency map — first edition

The arrows below express logical prerequisites, not a claim that all outcomes follow from one master theorem. The theorem index supplies printed locations and exact titles.

| Core question | Relevant foundation | Added requirement | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can an organization be summarized? | Exact quotient, lumpability and governance abstraction | Preserve the action family, labels and specified dynamics | Authority, knowledge or consent from prediction alone |
| Can representations be translated? | Institutional message semantics, epistemic back-and-forth and typed residuals | Match the relevant operations and observation relations | All meanings or rights from matching strings |
| Can institutions federate? | Cycle constraints and noncommutative holonomy | Distinguish frame synchronization from compatible invariant states | A common reference frame from bilateral compatibility alone |
| Can a reporting rule deter deviations? | Bounded-sanction testing and simultaneous minimax | One admissible monitor, every listed deviation, false-positive cap and model-error margin | Auditor effort, legitimate penalty or complete deviation coverage |
| Can a strategic auditor perform that work? | The specified inspection game | A credible, funded and verifiable service contract | Collusion resistance or an honest verifier from the equilibrium equations |
| Can confidentiality and enforcement coexist? | Conditional likelihood-ratio bounds and finite testing | The full available transcript and the selected neighbor relation | Protection of side information omitted from the transcript |
| Can all promised services be performed? | Joint continuation and certified progress | A common budget, supported transition model and implemented profile catalogue | Service for an unstated workload or inaccessible evidence |
| Can appeals be performed? | Hall matching and interval windows | Reserved capacity, evidence, reviewer qualification and executable disposition | A favorable decision or reversal of an irreversible act |
| Can running preserve the ability to end? | Ending attractor followed by a running invariant kernel | Authenticated observable command, a funded target and appropriate holds | An incentive to obey shutdown or an unmodeled delivery bound |
| Can certificates remain applicable? | Guard soundness, invariant domains and authorized handover | Valid premises throughout the action interval and a common progress rank | Soundness of the guard from its own label or an unsupported assurance cycle |

## Kinds of claims

Definitions fix a mathematical or operational convention. Proved statements are conditional arguments in those conventions. Analytical examples demonstrate possibilities and obstructions within supplied models. Tests validate finite implementations. Design proposals specify useful objects but are not empirical findings. Open problems identify extensions that are not resolved by this edition.

## What first edition means

The finite results, their exposition, source, examples and documented checks are packaged as a stable reference edition. A future erratum or extension can update it. Independent review, full proof-assistant formalization, comprehensive literature priority review and any specific deployment authorization remain separate activities.
