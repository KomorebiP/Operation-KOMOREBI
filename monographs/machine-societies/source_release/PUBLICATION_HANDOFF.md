## Suggested public description

Machine Societies develops a mathematical account of persistent plural computational organization. It studies when rules, authority, obligations, shared records and methods admit a common realization, and when the proposed guarantees conflict. The first edition also analyzes strategic auditing, confidentiality, effective appeals, affected outsiders and authorized endings. Its results are conditional theoretical statements and analytical constructions, not a demonstration of an autonomous machine society or an authorization to deploy one.
