# Targeted reference review — first edition

**Review date: 8 October 2026.** This record concerns the six references added after v0.7. The 52 retained bibliography entries were preserved and checked for source consistency. This is not a new full-text or priority audit of every retained entry. No public source is used as evidence that the author's own artificial population realizes the theory.

## Sources actually consulted

1. **Santoni de Sio and van den Hoven (2018), Meaningful Human Control over Autonomous Systems: A Philosophical Account.** Original Frontiers full text, DOI 10.3389/frobt.2018.00015. The tracking/tracing distinction supports the brief contextual paragraph about relevant human reasons and responsible actors. It is not cited as a proof of the proposed external-effects contract.
   https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2018.00015/full

2. **von Stengel (2016), Recursive Inspection Games.** Author preprint full text and author publication listing consulted. Mathematics of Operations Research **41(3), 935–952**, DOI 10.1287/moor.2015.0762. The volume was checked against the author's publication list rather than copied from inconsistent indexed metadata. The manuscript explicitly uses its own small simultaneous game, not the paper's recursive-game formula.
   https://arxiv.org/html/1412.0129v2

3. **Dwork and Roth (2014), The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy.** Author book page and full author-hosted PDF consulted. Definition 2.4, postprocessing, composition discussion and the conditional two-stage formulation in Appendix B supply the relevant foundation. The sharp finite testing envelope in the manuscript is proved directly and is not labeled a new general DP theorem. The publication metadata is Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science 9(3–4), 211–407, DOI 10.1561/0400000042.
   https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/privacybook.html
   https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/Papers/privacybook.pdf

4. **Hadfield-Menell, Dragan, Abbeel and Russell, The Off-Switch Game.** Author preprint full text consulted. The citation motivates keeping shutdown incentives separate from the existence of a settlement procedure. Its assumptions about utility uncertainty and behavior are not imported as an unconditional shutdown guarantee. The bibliography identifies the author-preprint lineage rather than claiming a new result about all agents.
   https://arxiv.org/html/1611.08219v3

5. **Mehmood, Sheikhi, Bak, Smolka and Stoller (2022), The Black-Box Simplex Architecture for Runtime Assurance of Autonomous CPS.** Full author preprint consulted, including the safety argument and decision-module assumptions. NASA Formal Methods 2022, pp. 231–250, DOI 10.1007/978-3-031-06773-0_12. It supports the runtime-assurance precedent, not the truth of an institutional guard's external premises.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2102.12981v3

6. **Phan, Yang, Clark, Grosu, Schierman, Smolka and Stoller (2017), A Component-Based Simplex Architecture for High-Assurance Cyber-Physical Systems.** Extended author preprint full text consulted, especially Sections III-C and III-D on contracts and the asymmetric assume-guarantee rule. The account distinguishes sound temporal/circular reasoning from unsupported mutual assertions. Conference DOI 10.1109/ACSD.2017.23 is recorded alongside the consulted extended preprint.
   https://arxiv.org/html/1704.04759v1

## Retrieval and scope limits

An attempted publisher-link opening for the differential-privacy book returned an internal retrieval error. It was not treated as successful review. The author-hosted full PDF was available and supplied the definition and composition material. Broad search results were used only for discovery and not as substitutes for the cited primary texts. Reference metadata and the paragraphs actually supported were checked. No exhaustive citation-impact, priority or current-policy survey was performed.

The publication URLs are navigation aids, not execution inputs. External articles and font files are not bundled. Newly executed exact checks depend on the delivered mathematical code and manuscript, not on network access.
