# Synopsis

*Projector-Refinement Physics, Volume II* develops a unified cosmological application of projector and refinement methods. The first part establishes curved multicomponent FLRW backgrounds, gauge-invariant scalar, vector, and tensor perturbations, linear growth, primordial power-law dynamics, recombination benchmarks, and finite-scale propagation laws. The second part separates a visible average field from hidden refinement-detail modes, proves a no-go result for an earlier additive dominant-shell mechanism, and formulates a scale-covariant replacement in which the same detail density clusters as dark matter and stabilizes a vacuum-like average mode.

The third part propagates the resulting momentum distribution through collisionless Boltzmann transport, a coupled photon-baryon-neutrino-refinement system, effective recombination and reionization, late-time line-of-sight sources, CMB lensing remapping, and scalar lensing B modes. The fourth part follows the same frozen transfer function into nonlinear halo formation, paired particle-mesh evolution, explicit halo catalogues, baryonic response envelopes, one-zone cooling and feedback, and a controlled audit of self-bound subhalo remnants. The final part states linked observable signatures, erasure thresholds, decisive rejection patterns, and the remaining microscopic, numerical, statistical, and external validation requirements.

The monograph is not a claim of observational establishment. Its purpose is to place the proposed dark-sector mechanism, its exact model identities, its controlled calculations, its corrected branches, and its falsifiability conditions into one coherent publication-style source.
