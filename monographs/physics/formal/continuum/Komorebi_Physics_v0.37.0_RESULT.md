# Komorebi Physics v0.37.0 — result

Block 37 is a checked Cartesian coding layer between the symbolic octree continuum and the closed Euclidean unit cube.

## Proved

- `Fin 8` is exactly equivalent to three binary coordinate digits.
- The digit equivalence preserves the uniform probability measure.
- The induced infinite-address equivalence preserves the full product measure.
- The product-space equivalence induces an exact `L²` linear isometry.
- Positional base-two decoding is continuous, measurable, and surjective onto `[0,1]^3`.
- The cube is a topological quotient of both the three-bit address space and the original octree address space.
- Agreement through depth `n` forces coordinate error at most `2⁻ⁿ`.
- Binary decoding is not injective; `0.1000…` and `0.0111…` are distinct streams representing `1/2`.

## Not proved

The cube decoder has not yet been proved measure-preserving for Euclidean Lebesgue volume. Consequently, the Block 36 octree `L²` density theorem has not yet been transported to Euclidean-volume `L²`.
