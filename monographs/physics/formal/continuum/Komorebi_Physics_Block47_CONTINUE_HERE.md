## Next target: unbounded continuum Dirac Hamiltonian

Define a weighted Fourier domain, for example

\[
\mathcal D(H_D)=\left\{f:\sum_n (1+|n|^2)\|\hat f(n)\|^2<\infty\right\}.
\]

Construct the modewise multiplier

\[
(\widehat{H_D f})(n)=H_n\hat f(n).
\]

Then prove:

1. density of the weighted domain;
2. closedness and symmetry;
3. self-adjointness using modewise finite-dimensional self-adjointness and direct-sum criteria;
4. identification of `continuumTorusUnitary` with `exp(-it H_D)`;
5. graph or strong-resolvent convergence of the central-difference generators.

Do not claim strong-resolvent convergence until the full operator domains and resolvent bounds are formalized.
