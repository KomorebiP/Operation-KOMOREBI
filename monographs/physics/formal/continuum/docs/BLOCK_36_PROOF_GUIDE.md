# Block 36 proof guide — weighted octree reconstruction and L² density

## Purpose

Block 35 constructed an exact finite-cell interpolation into genuine `L²`, but only for unit-volume cells and without proving density of a named multiresolution family. Block 36 closes both gaps on the octree address continuum.

The checked continuum is

```text
Address := ℕ → Fin 8
```

with the uniform infinite-product probability measure. A prefix of length `n` labels one of `8^n` octree cells.

## Equal-volume interpolation

For a finite measurable partition whose cells all have volume `v > 0`, the interpolation uses the factor `v⁻¹ᐟ²`. If `ψ i` is the normalized finite coefficient, the continuum value on cell `i` is

```text
v⁻¹ᐟ² • ψ i.
```

The theorem `EqualVolumePartition.step_inner` proves

```text
⟪step ψ, step φ⟫ = ⟪ψ, φ⟫.
```

`EqualVolumePartition.stepIsometry` packages this as a linear isometry into genuine `Lp E 2 μ`.

## Octree cells

At level `n`:

- side length is `2⁻ⁿ`;
- cell volume is `8⁻ⁿ`;
- number of cells is `8ⁿ`;
- volume equals side length cubed;
- prefix cells are measurable;
- distinct prefix cells are disjoint;
- their union is the whole address continuum;
- every prefix cell has exactly the declared product measure.

These statements produce `Octree.prefixPartition` and the isometry `Octree.prefixStepIsometry`.

## Nested finite-prefix subspaces

`Octree.initialLevelSubmodule a` is generated by constant-vector indicators of measurable cylinders depending on the first `a + 1` digits. The theorem

```text
initialLevelSubmodule_mono
```

shows that these subspaces are nested with increasing prefix depth.

Their algebraic directed union is `Octree.finitePrefixSubmodule`.

## Density argument

The density proof has four steps.

1. Finite-coordinate measurable cylinders generate the product σ-algebra.
2. Those cylinders form a finite set algebra and are measure-dense for the product probability measure.
3. Constant-vector indicators of a measure-dense family generate a dense set in `L²` by `Lp.induction`.
4. Every finite-coordinate cylinder indicator belongs to one finite-prefix submodule.

The load-bearing theorem is

```text
Octree.finitePrefixSubmodule_dense
```

and the approximation corollary is

```text
Octree.exists_initialLevel_approx
```

which states that every continuum `L²` state is approximated to arbitrary positive tolerance by a state in one finite-depth prefix-step subspace.

## Interpretation

The finite coefficients are normalized cell amplitudes. The corresponding physical cell value is obtained by multiplying by the inverse square root of cell volume. This is the exact normalization making the finite counting-space norm agree with the continuum `L²` norm.

## Boundary

The address continuum has not yet been formally identified with `[0,1]^3` or `ℝ³`. A measure-theoretic binary-coordinate realization is the next bridge. No continuum Dirac generator or propagator convergence is claimed in this block.
