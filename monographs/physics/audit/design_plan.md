# Publication design pass

The reading material is rebuilt with a shared typography and page-layout layer.
The mathematical body text, equations, proof environments, scientific figures, and
formal source archives are not intentionally modified. The two older model books
use 11-point text to match the rest of the volume.

Design direction: a restrained navy, dark-teal, and copper identity, white reading
pages, unboxed theorem statements, consistent opening hierarchy, generous leading,
mirrored page furniture, roman preliminary folios, continuous Arabic body folios,
and re-created navigation. Scientific figure data and geometry are preserved.

The execution audit and repair notes are recorded separately.
