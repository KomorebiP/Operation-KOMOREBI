# Third-party rights and dependencies

The open grants in `LICENSE` apply only to project-original material and
rights held by Komorebi P. Bibliographic citations and quoted third-party
passages do not transfer their ownership. Any external datasets retain
their source terms. Historical computational packages preserve their
source-specific attribution and limitations.

Lean, Mathlib, Python and TeX are external software. Their licenses and
notices remain their own. The portable Lean runtime used for verification
is not redistributed in this publication package. The original formal
source archive contains its retained third-party notices.

Font programs are not included in this package. The PDF embeds font subsets
for document rendering; installed fonts are required to rebuild it.

Source provenance is recorded in the source concordance and archived
verification records. Neither a successful build nor the open grant
substitutes for verification of rights in separately sourced material.
