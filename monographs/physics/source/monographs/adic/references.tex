\subsection*{Project sources}
\begin{enumerate}[label={[S\arabic*]},leftmargin=2.5em]
\item Komorebi P. \emph{Projector-Refinement Physics, Volume I}. The arithmetic source studies cover unit registers, Haar refinement, finite-adelic dynamics, product walks, radial self-energy, polyphase structure, infinite Haar limits, spectral renormalization, and hypergeometric companion functions.
\item Komorebi P. \emph{Volume I Technical Supplement and Independent Review Dossier}. The arithmetic claim dossiers and formula registries specify the scope of the corresponding derivations and computational checks.
\item Komorebi P. \emph{Volume I Second Implementation Reproduction}. Finite Haar tests provide internal reproduction evidence for their stated inputs, not for the entire infinite theory.
\item Komorebi P. \emph{Komorebi Physics formal source}. The verification appendix identifies related tower and concrete reconstruction results. Shared interfaces are not a certificate for every arithmetic theorem.
\end{enumerate}
