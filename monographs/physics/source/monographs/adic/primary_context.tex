\subsection*{Scope of the literature check}
The hypergeometric bottom parameters used here are positive, so the nonpositive-integer exclusions in \S16.2 do not arise. The Chebyshev formulas use the standard first- and second-kind definitions. These scalar conventions do not supply an unbounded-operator domain.

The cited Teschl, Kozyrev, and Aharonov--Ambainis--Kempe--Vazirani passages are used only for the contextual comparisons stated above. Their settings differ from the finite tree, compact adic hierarchy, and irregular arc graph of this volume. The comparison is limited to the explicit uses recorded for each reference. The Vaidyanathan PDF could not be retrieved; its metadata and author abstract establish only the stated historical context. All exact model propositions in this volume carry internal derivations. No exhaustive novelty search or literature-priority claim is made.
