\chapter*{Result Status and Reading Conventions}\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Result Status and Reading Conventions}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.8cm,style=nextline]\item[Theorem] Proved from stated mathematical assumptions.\item[Conditional theorem] Proved under explicit regularity, branch, truncation, or model hypotheses.\item[Controlled computation] Numerical result with declared residual or convergence controls.\item[Model proposition] Consequence of a proposed action or state-selection rule not uniquely derived from the foundational calculus.\item[Open problem] Required step not completed.\end{description}
An internal audit is evidence about the implementation or derivation tested. It is not a substitute for literature comparison, independent reproduction, or observational confirmation.
