\chapter*{Abstract}\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This volume develops the cosmological and phenomenological branch of projector-refinement physics. It begins with relativistic FLRW backgrounds and perturbations, constructs a scale-covariant refinement dark sector, evolves its produced phase-space distribution through collisionless and Einstein--Boltzmann hierarchies, and follows the resulting cutoff through CMB lensing, nonlinear matter evolution, halo catalogues, baryonic response models, ionizing-source histories, and subhalo-core survival tests. Exact theorems, conditional model results, controlled computations, and unresolved physical claims are labeled separately. The current candidate picture identifies hidden refinement-detail modes with dark matter and a density-locked average mode with a transient vacuum-like component. The book also records the analytic obstruction that rejected an earlier additive mechanism and the nonlinear calculations that weakened several initially attractive small-scale lensing predictions. The result is a falsifiable research program rather than an established replacement for general relativity and $\Lambda$CDM.
