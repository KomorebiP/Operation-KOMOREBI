\chapter*{Preface}\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
Volume I developed the mathematical, relativistic, gauge, gravitational, strong-field, and adic foundations. This volume asks what cosmology follows when a refinement fiber is treated as a physical internal sector and one fixed dark-state construction is propagated across observable scales.

The manuscript is edited as a research monograph rather than a development log. Superseded additive branches are not presented as current theory. Their decisive no-go result is retained because it explains why the scale-covariant branch was introduced. Completion percentages and workflow language have been removed. Numerical results are stated with their domains, approximations, and negative controls.

This edition does not claim literature priority, precision agreement with cosmological data, or external validation. It is intended to make the current model, its deductions, and its rejection criteria inspectable in one place.
