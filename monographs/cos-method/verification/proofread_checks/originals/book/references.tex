\chapter{Sources and further reading}
The mathematical development brings together the quadratic-form engine,
the query-adaptive K-COS packet theory, and the streaming polyphase
factorization analysis. The visual studies and finite-algebra formalization
supply complementary reading routes. These author sources are preserved in
the accompanying provenance records, together with the executable inputs and
source hashes needed to distinguish proofs, numerical runs, and compiler
records. They are not separate repeated chapters in the volume.

The external references below supply the classical inversion and COS
foundations used in the text. The integration of these methods with
query-specific spectral packets and separately certified operator
representations is discussed in Section~\ref{sec:prior-work}.
