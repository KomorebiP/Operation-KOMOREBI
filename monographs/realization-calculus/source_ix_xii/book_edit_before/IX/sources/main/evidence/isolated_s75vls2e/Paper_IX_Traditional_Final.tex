\begin{abstract}
Arithmetic packet coordinates connect congruences among Euler coefficients
with reconstruction at finite and $p$-adic precision. We develop this
connection, prove the stated Nishibuchi congruence conjecture in its full
parameter range, and extend the framework to local polynomial deformation. The arithmetic part distinguishes normalized and unnormalized
Euler packets, proves amplified difference bounds, gives exact dyadic
displacements and least packet periods for four residue addresses, and records
an optimal classical dyadic reconstruction tower. The lifting part identifies
finite obstruction classes, shared corrections and correction fibers, constructs
compatible polynomial towers, and proves quantitative persistence under
simultaneous coefficient and parameter deformation. An analytic implicit
function theorem supplies local solution graphs and computes their strict
derivatives from the polynomial Jacobian. Every continuous local root
selection through the same nonsingular point agrees with this graph near
the base parameter. Source references identify the corresponding Lean proofs;
classical derivations without a dedicated formal bridge are marked explicitly. General singular
persistence, unrestricted presentation invariance, global descent and further
exact valuation questions remain open within this program.
\end{abstract}
