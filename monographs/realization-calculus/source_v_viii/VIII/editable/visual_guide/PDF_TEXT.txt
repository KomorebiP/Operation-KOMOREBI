Three models to carry through the paper
The first is a six-dimensional operator 𝐴= 𝐷+ 𝑢𝑣𝑇. Every entry of 𝐴is nonzero, yet three 2 × 2 diagonal

subproblem.

Conventions used in the examples
Matrices act on column vectors. A block 𝐴𝛽𝛼goes from source 𝛼to target 𝛽. Products act from right to
left. Real examples use transpose; complex examples use adjoint unless a vectorization identity explicitly
requires transpose. Column-major vectorization stacks the columns of a matrix.
An arithmetic count follows the paper’s modeled multiply/add convention. It is not a count of executed
machine instructions. The tiny scheduling and amortization examples use declared synthetic units, not
measured GPU times. The original benchmark corpus remains historical data in the preserved source tree.
Evidence levels
The original theorem ledger records its inherited finite formal scope. The new exact checks validate these
particular examples. A plot samples a formula. A historical timing describes its recorded case and machine.
None of these evidence types can silently substitute for another.
Read this as: Before interpreting a number, identify its object, norm, output contract, and timing
scope. Most apparent contradictions disappear once those four fields are explicit.
