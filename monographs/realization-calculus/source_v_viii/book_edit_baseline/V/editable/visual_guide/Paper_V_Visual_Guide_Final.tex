\head{Three reading routes}
\textbf{Signal coordinates:} V3 to V11, then V20 and V21.
\textbf{Selection and error:} V12 to V19.
\textbf{Nonlinear geometry:} V22 to V25.
\head{One model per question}
The sixteen-sample signal gives exact rational values. The depth-three tree
has twenty-six complete prunings. The reflection orbit has four exact
quarter-turn points. These bounded examples expose the paper's mechanisms
without replacing its general statements.
\head{Read the hypotheses with the picture}
Zero extension, periodic indexing, and infinite sequences describe different
objects. Known jumps differ from estimated jumps. A finite optimum depends
on a specified cost, and a coordinate transformation does not establish an
optimal sampling rule. V26 records the evidence behind this guide.

\head{A connected route}
The lattice example extends exact addressing. Frame bounds then quantify the
stability of analysis and synthesis. Trace packets remove specified singularities,
while overlap and location formulas measure errors in their estimates. Three
local detectors address separated jumps under contrast hypotheses. The final
page combines the recovered packet with a chosen approximation operator.
\head{Keep the evidence attached to its claim}
The twenty-result core carries its preserved Lean map. Part II carries
traditional proofs and the current mathematical review, with a separate exact
worked-example checker. The rerun symbolic visual suite, current arithmetic
runs, saved compiler transcripts, and rendered figures each retain their own
scope. Neither a figure nor a finite check expands the formal theorem map.
\head{What the example numbers mean}
The additional checker uses exact rational arithmetic. Its logarithmic sampling
budget is enclosed using a positive Taylor partial sum. Curve coordinates are
converted to decimal positions for drawing. Displayed formulas retain exact
values. All source-drawn plates are editable TikZ, and the final comparison
figure is preserved from the expanded manuscript's vector source.
