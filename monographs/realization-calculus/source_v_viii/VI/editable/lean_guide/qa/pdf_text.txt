1.4 Common mistakes that the interfaces prevent
Column-stochastic transition matrices act on probability columns. Observable propagation uses the
transpose. Probability aggregation is a rectangular mass-summing map. A weighted conditional ex-
pectation is a square projector on observables. Their identities are related but their types differ. Simi-
larly, 𝑒𝑡𝐴describes a general generator, while a positive fractional operator produces 𝑒−𝑡𝐴𝛼. The minus
sign changes the Duhamel defect in the fractional semigroup theorem.
Zero evidence, one coarse packet, a zero-dimensional space, a zero spectral value, and a nonnormal
hidden generator are not interchangeable edge cases. Each belongs to a particular interface. This guide
identifies the relevant hypothesis at the point where it is used.

4 Bayesian normalization, sufficiency, and flow
4.1 An equation is not a probability claim
Lean defines the posterior by a total real division. This makes the expression meaningful as a term even
when evidence is zero. Theorems asserting unit mass or probability preservation require nonzero or
positive evidence explicitly. The basic definition is:

noncomputable def update (p l : I →ℝ) (i : I) : ℝ:= l i*p i / evidence p l
def l1 (p : I →ℝ) : ℝ:= ∑i, |p i|
Core 7.1 proves composition of two likelihood updates with both the first evidence and the combined

Source: KomorebiPaperVI/FiniteBayes.lean.

4.4 The coarsest meaningful partition
BayesianPartitions forms a quotient by equality of the whole likelihood profile. Its canonical coarse

map:
Source: KomorebiPaperVI/BayesianPartitions.lean.
theorem canonical_coarsest_nontrivial (l : Y →I →ℝ) (hl : ∀y i, 0 < l y i)
(f : I →A) (hf : Function.Surjective f) [Nontrivial A] (h : FamilyCloses l f) :

The qualifier “nontrivial” excludes the automatic one-packet case. The theorem does not claim that
