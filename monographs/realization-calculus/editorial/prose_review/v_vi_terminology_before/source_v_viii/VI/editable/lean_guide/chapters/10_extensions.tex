\subsection{The operator substitution migrated from Chapter IV}
The migrated reciprocal-family proposition concerns bounded Banach operators and holomorphic functional calculus, together with an explicit classical functional-calculus input. It belongs with Chapter VI's operator language and is retained with its written proof. The core 15.1 result instead constructs and proves naturality of canonical finite-dimensional jets. Their relationship is conceptual. The migrated proposition is outside the 33-result formal contract.

\subsection{The three original continuum conjectures}
The latest core retains three open conjectures: transfer-semigroup realization on completed state spaces; uniform refinement control for meaningful discretization families; and latent-packet closure for stable infinite packet systems. Finite or bounded special cases proved elsewhere in the edition do not settle these universal formulations. In particular, summable local defects alone do not guarantee a semigroup limit without the appropriate completion, compatibility, and closure conditions.
