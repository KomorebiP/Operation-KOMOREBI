\KomorebiOpeningNote{This guide records the formal coverage of the final Euler paper and teaches the retained arithmetic, finite obstruction, polynomial tower, quantitative deformation, and analytic regularity proofs. The opening chapter maps the strengthened congruences to their existing Lean endpoints and identifies the new proofs that remain to be formalized. The complete original teaching chapters, exact source excerpts, and named-source catalog follow.

The Lean compilation evidence is inherited. Fresh work in this edition reconciles source hashes, retained logs, declaration inventories, source syntax, and strict axiom records. Lean and Lake are unavailable in the present environment; no new compiler run is claimed. The new factorial and mixed-difference theorems are proved in the main paper, with their proof and computation evidence supplied separately, but have no compiled Lean endpoint in this release.}
