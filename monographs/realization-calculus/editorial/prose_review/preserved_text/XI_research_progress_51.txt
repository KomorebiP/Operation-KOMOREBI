22.1 Four numbers, not one unnamed conductor

Let F be a nonarchimedean local field with residue cardinality q, and let V be a complex Weil represen-

                             a(V) = dim V −dim VIF + Sw(V).                               (22.1)

We use a for the ordinary conductor throughout this part. The Swan conductor, an epsilon exponent
for a specified additive character, and the self-pair conductor of a representation of GLn(F) are named
separately.
Take ψ0 trivial on OF and nontrivial on p−1F  . Set ψ1(x) = ψ0(ϖ−1x), so ψ1 is trivial on pF and nontrivial

                            fψ0(V) = a(V),     fψ1(V) = a(V) −dim V.                          (22.2)

The determinant factor in the change of additive character is independent of s and does not alter this
