Part V Scope and formal evidence
15 Scope boundaries and further questions
The large-zero theorem settles eventual simplicity but does not classify the finite exceptional set. A
remaining question is whether every zero of D𝑁is simple and every reduced zero of Δ𝑁is positive.
Both statements hold for 𝑁= 2 and 𝑁= 4. The argument here proves them only outside a compact
set for general 𝑁.
The leading correction in theorem 11.8 may admit iteration to a full exponential expansion for 𝜏𝑚.
Such an expansion is an open problem, not a result of this chapter. It could refine the entire correction
in the zero-zeta comparison and lead to explicit special values.
The symmetry-orbit zero zeta has a meromorphic continuation with one pole. No functional
equation is obtained. A branch dependent sum over the 𝑁roots of each reduced zero must specify
logarithms on the rays.
The coefficient families in sections 7, 9 and 10 have exact formal parameter-addition laws. Their arith-
metic behavior, including odd-prime congruences, dyadic weighting, and 𝑝-adic or Amice–Mahler
interpolation, belongs to Chapter IX. General bounded-operator semigroup, resolvent, and Feshbach
theory belongs to Chapter VI. Chapter III covers general nonlinear operation trees, while abstract
completion and occupancy questions belong to Chapter X. None of those theories is inferred from
the scalar complex-analytic results proved here.
