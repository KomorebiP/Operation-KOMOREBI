files including the map, manifest, gate, pins, and documentation. The guide stores 44 exact excerpts.

the 143 saved transcript reports. generated/static_audit.json separates current checks from inherited
counts and records the checksums used. The original report and transcript remain in the preserved
finite release. Verification sources and receipts are under formal/ in the formal source distribution.
