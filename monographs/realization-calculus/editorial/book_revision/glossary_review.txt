REALIZATION CALCULUS: GLOSSARY REVIEW

Scope

The glossary contains 73 alphabetized entries and 74 references to the twelve
mathematical texts. Its definitions are concise reading aids, each grounded in
the cited local definition, theorem, or section. They are not replacements for
the hypotheses in those sources, a global unification of every local notation,
or an independent proof audit.

The selected entries cover seed generation and rank, exact packet coordinates,
refinement and transfer, analytic sections and zeros, signal reconstruction,
evolution and hidden memory, finite quantum constructions, packet algorithms,
Euler congruences, residual forests, restricted arithmetic bridges, and patching
support. Komorebi Space is not assigned a new definition from these twelve
papers: its separate foundational treatment is outside the source set used here.

Editorial review

Every definition was reread against its cited source context. The mathematical
manuscripts were not edited. No mathematical-token changes were made to them.
The new glossary preserves the distinction between algebraic and dense
generation, idempotent and orthogonal projectors, exact regrouping and
approximation, candidate obstruction packets and cokernel obstruction classes,
projector denominator weights and coordinate visibility depths, module support
and automorphic realization, and supplied bridge data and a universal bridge.

The connection entry distinguishes the minimal and reversible interfaces. The
flatness entry is explicitly about connections, not the unrelated translated
differential flatness in the Euler chapter. Residual test values and arithmetic
residual roots have separate entries. Packet support and module support also
have separate entries. The arithmetic entries preserve all indicated existence
and realization conditions. In particular, no entry implies Conjecture 66 or
unrestricted arithmetic realization is proved.

Reference validation

All 74 anchor quotations match their stated original local PDF pages after
Unicode NFKC normalization and removal of non-alphanumeric characters. Each
anchor is unique within its cited mathematical text. All see-also targets exist.
There are no duplicate term names. The glossary source metadata records the
component paths, exact hashes and page counts used for this review.

The source copies are undergoing an authorized editorial rebuild. Their future
pagination must be resolved from the anchors before final hyperlinks are added.
The old local_page value is a provenance location, not a promised final book
folio. The section labels and anchor phrases provide the intended destination.
A failed anchor match must be reviewed against the edited source, not silently
redirected to an arbitrary nearby page.

Display and coverage

Definitions are at most 45 words each. Body prose is ASCII apart from the
WinAnsi-supported accent in the name Frechet as spelled in the glossary.
No inline TeX, Greek letters, proof formulas, release identifiers, version
references, or dates are needed in the rendered entries. Section labels in
reference metadata retain their exact local form. A standalone notation index
was not added because the same symbols have different local meanings.
