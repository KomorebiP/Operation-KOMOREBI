FRONT MATTER AND BOOK ASSEMBLY — INDEPENDENT READ-ONLY QA

Scope
Reviewed book_component_adapter.py and book_layout.py before their first full run, the final agent front-matter reports, and all 41 entries in structure_inventory.json. Simulated the adapter’s cover/TOC decisions against every current raw component. Read every mismatch directly. Checked the ending text of all 15 mixed prose/Contents pages and all retained margin lines in the three preserved readers. No root modules or PDFs were edited.

Definitive selection
frontmatter_keep_overrides.json gives one-based physical keep/exclude pages and inclusive ranges for every component. Its selection is for front matter BEFORE the separately audited canonical-plate deduplication. Raw component paths, hashes, page totals, explicit reasons, and mixed-TOC rectangles accompany every entry. Replace heuristic frontmatter selection with these sets; do not accidentally undo canonical duplicate-plate redirections after that later step.

Detected discrepancies
1. XI_research_progress p. 2 was unconditionally excluded as an edition map. It contains unique mathematical scope: separate open realization, H5, and corrected-character occurrence obligations; no native marking inferred from host dimension; required supplied actions and permitted changes for the finite corrections; and the selected candidate’s native marking/phases task. Parent confirmed the page is now kept. Eight exact prose proposals turn it into a timeless reading map while retaining those limits.
2. Six contents-only pages escaped the heuristic: II_traditional pp. 3–4, III_traditional p. 4, VI_traditional pp. 5 and 7, XI_visual p. 6. Some pages contain a later List of Figures/Tables heading preceded by TOC entries, which the heuristic misclassifies as introductory prose. Others have sparse entries without dotted leaders. Direct text inspection confirms these contain navigation only. Explicit exclusion fixes the incomplete cleanup.
3. No additional over-exclusion of unique prose was found in the simulated selection.

Mixed pages
The fifteen genuine prose/Contents pages end in local navigation, with no unique prose after the Contents suffix. The map supplies the top boundary and exact preserved prefix for each. Visual reading-route pages without a literal Contents suffix are retained whole, including II/III/V/VI/VII/VIII route explanations and XI atlas p. 2. They must not be deleted just because their directories overlap the book’s navigation.

Protected content and integration gates
• III_analytic_supplement p. 1 contains title and mathematical material on the same page; retain it.
• IV_visual p. 14 contains the branch-sensitive reciprocal-power clarification absent from the overlapping main plate. IV visual deduplication is not authorized.
• XI_research_progress pp. 37 and 56 retain all introduction and evidence prose above their local Contents tables.
• XI_research_atlas pp. 35–36 stay, per the parent’s conservative retention choice. Qualify the 32 routes through the book page map; do not delete their unique evidence-scope paragraphs.
• III_release_record and IV_verification_closeout may be omitted only with the rebuilt formal companions containing their documented integration. The final I–IV report records those integrations and successful builds; original reports remain attachments.

Book-layout observations
The book map uses zero-based keep_pages/source_page/book_index values; glossary local_page and this override file use one-based physical source pages. The conversion is deliberate and must occur once. Canonical plate deduplication remains a separate stage after front matter. Main-text canonical plate targets must be retained before redirects are accepted. The root confirmed corrected destination families: II/III plateN.1, V vpageN, VI vvpageN, VII/VIII atlasvpageN. Existing main/visual sorting places unique visual-reading routes before the canonical embedded atlas. X’s unmatched pages are conservatively retained; its deduplication relies on the documented original append-copy provenance together with exact normalized text identity. The code must not generalize that text-only criterion to unrelated graphical pages.

Preserved-PDF furniture
Across every selected page of IV visual, XI progress, and XI atlas, the adapter’s upper/lower margin thresholds encountered only running document/series labels, production headers, and folios. No scientific prose or formulas were found in those margin bands. Mathematical formula spans inside prose-edit blocks require separate span-preserving handling; that task belongs to preserved_pdf_proofread.py.

Limits
This is selection and assembly-risk QA. It does not prove scientific claims, revalidate historical formal or numerical evidence, certify final page-coordinate links, or inspect the as-yet unassembled book visually. Final retained-page accounting, redirects, glossaries, and annotation coordinates still require the parent assembly check. No glossary content was changed during this assignment.
