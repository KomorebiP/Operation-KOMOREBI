THE EDITION

The book presents the twelve core papers of Realization Calculus with
a deep plum, mulberry and muted gold identity. It has 3,068 pages: twelve
preliminary pages, twelve part openings, and 3,044 component pages. A single
set of global folios, linked contents, part routes and PDF bookmarks connects
the twelve papers in chronological order.

Thirty-six of the forty-one selected treatments were rebuilt from editable
sources. Five supporting treatments retain their original page layouts and scientific contents:
Paper III's two-page release record; Paper IV's later visual guide and
verification closeout; and Paper XI's two Conjecture 66 research companions.
These pages are fitted as complete vector pages, without cropping their
original scientific content. Displayed project branding is updated to Realization
Calculus. Exact original PDFs are also retained separately for reference. The
layout exceptions are identified in the book and its source map.

The source-built material uses coordinated serif text and sans-serif
navigation. Visual studies retain compact plate geometry where that is needed
to keep a worked example together. Paper XII's visual study also retains its
compact sans-serif body. This is a coordinated publication design, not a claim
that all forty-one treatments have identical source geometry.

READING AND REFERENCES

Large outer folios navigate the complete book. A small component label in the
footer retains the local PDF page label. Theorem, equation, section and figure
numbers belong to their named paper and treatment. Internal named destinations
and current companion routes are rebuilt into the global page map.

References explicitly labeled frozen refer to the exact original reader PDFs.
All forty-one selected originals are attached to the complete PDF and supplied
under frozen_readers/. Attachment support varies by PDF viewer. Use the folder
when a viewer does not expose attachments. The final audit checks attachment
bytes and any embedded-reference link destinations.

The frozen readers contain 3,047 selected component pages. Paper III's duplicate
186-page complete reader is not appended a second time. Paper X's intentional
thirty-page atlas overlap remains part of its selected reading material. This
page accounting is not a claim that every repeated mathematical idea is unique.

MATHEMATICAL AND EVIDENCE SCOPE

The selected scientific bodies, hypotheses, proof status and local claim
numbering are retained. Source comparisons, page checks and visual inspection
support the publication work. They are not an independent proof audit, a new
run of the twelve Lean projects, or a new scientific computation.

Expanded prose and formal companions can have different coverage. Paper III's
expanded ordinary proofs remain distinct from its bounded Lean core. Paper X's
target-family and carrier-recovery witnesses remain independent unless the
additional comparison premises are supplied. Paper XI's Conjecture 66 remains
open under its four stated contracts. Paper XII still requires the actual
arithmetic, patching, support, compatible Hecke, classicality and representation
inputs specified in its conditional statements.

Earlier compiler, numerical and source-recovery records keep their original
dates and limitations. The complete frozen research distributions remain the
authoritative formal/evidence archives. This publication package contains the
reading sources and their publication records; it does not duplicate every
historical formal archive. The wider Komorebi program is outside this edition.

REBUILD

Install Python dependencies from requirements.txt and a suitable TeX environment
providing XeLaTeX, pdfLaTeX, BibTeX, kpsewhich and the packages used by the sources.
Install the separately supplied URW Base35, Latin Modern and DejaVu fonts. The outer
book uses explicit P052 and Nimbus Sans Type 1 files at the documented system
paths; it does not silently substitute a missing typeface. Some component
builders require the recorded exact font hashes. Font programs are not bundled.

From the extracted package directory:

  python3 rebuild_monograph.py

This assembles the supplied components, audits the result and creates the preview.
The branding audit compares the selected raw builds with their display copies.
To compile the available editable sources first:

  python3 rebuild_monograph.py --from-sources

The five supporting PDFs retain their original layouts, with an explicit display
branding adapter. Their exact original input files are retained. A source
rebuild can change pagination or PDF bytes with another TeX/font environment.
Cross-platform byte identity is not promised. The build does not run Lean,
mathematical experiments or figure generators. Existing historical reports are
not retroactively turned into evidence for a later build.
The raw-build preservation audit applies to the supplied builds and is skipped
after fresh source compilation, whose PDF hashes may legitimately differ.

INTEGRITY AND RIGHTS

Before editing or rebuilding, verify the pristine extracted package:

  python3 -B scripts/verify_integrity.py --strict

The exact manifest verifies regular-file membership, sizes and SHA-256 hashes.
It does not certify mathematical truth. Rebuilding intentionally changes files
and therefore invalidates that delivered manifest until a new one is made.

Original writing, figures and editable writing sources use CC BY 4.0. Original
software and machine-readable material use MIT. Third-party and font terms
remain separate. Earlier unassigned-license statements in preserved records
are historical and superseded for original project material by the current
grants. Inherited font-provenance limits are retained in the notices.
