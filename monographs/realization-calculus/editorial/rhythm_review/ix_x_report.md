The candidate contexts contain 58,758 whitespace tokens after masking mathematics and literal material; these include TeX commands and are not a count of ordinary prose words. No eligible candidate contexts remain unread.

The journal records 322 changed paragraphs across 57 files. Most changes turn independent prose clauses into sentences. Other changes simplify optional phrases such as “manuscript-proved items,” “named-source catalog” and “square-root-in-area bound,” and remove unnecessary emphasis. Technical terminology, exact historical source records, mathematical lists and scope qualifications remain.
