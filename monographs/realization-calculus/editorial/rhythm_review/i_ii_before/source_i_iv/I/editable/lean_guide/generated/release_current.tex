\subsection{Primary and semisimple constructions}

The verification receipt for the primary and semisimple constructions (archival identifier Block 08) covers 54 production modules. It freshly compiled eight additions, authenticated 46 predecessor source/object pairs, and replayed the whole logical closure. Its 1,634 source-owned entries comprise 1,627 logical declarations and seven separately inventoried generated partial runtime helpers. The latter were not proof-replayed. The recorded transitive axiom set is limited to \code{propext}, \code{Classical.choice}, and \code{Quot.sound}. These quantities describe that receipt; it does not claim that all 54 source modules were freshly compiled.

The continuation proves the intrinsic primary quotient and generator count, the geometric formula, scalar extension, and the six-way cyclic criterion. It also proves noncommutative module-top reduction and the nonsplit semisimple formula, including actual compatible decompositions from the Wedderburn--Artin existence theorem. The final existence theorems do not take a desired rank equality or a compatible decomposition as an input. They are noncomputable mathematical constructions, not executable decomposition algorithms.

These are source-navigation entries. The correspondence map supplies the exact declaration names and hypotheses; its verification receipt determines the accepted scope.

For the typed-grammar correspondence, the finite-vertex path algebra uses its standard vertex-and-arrow presentation: vertex idempotents are pairwise annihilating and sum to one, and each arrow lies in its target/source corner. The formal \code{PathAlgebra} names that presentation. \code{GrammarIdeal} takes an arbitrary two-sided ideal and constructs the comparison between the iterated ideal quotient and the combined presentation; its two comparison maps are proved inverse. Thus the presentation convention is explicit when reading the manuscript notation $kQ/I$. It is not an assumed module-category equivalence or a supplied conclusion about generators.



\subsection{Verification scope}

The verification receipt reports complete correspondences for all 69 proof-level records in the 108-record inventory. Each conclusion retains the hypotheses and presentation conventions recorded in the correspondence map. Definitions, boundaries, and classical inputs are classified separately. The record includes targeted semantic reviews for 47 proof-level records and accepted predecessor correspondences for 22 others. The total of 69 accepted correspondences does not mean that all records received independent reviews in the same verification event.

The recorded audit checks 116 formal source modules plus the retained boundary suite. Including that suite, it inventories 3513 source-owned entries and checks 3500 logical declarations. Of these, 3499 are checked by logical replay and one declaration sharing an upstream name receives a fresh-name kernel body check after exact type/value comparison. It inventories 13 runtime helpers separately and records 12 rejected negative controls. The allowed transitive axiom set is \code{Classical.choice}, \code{Quot.sound}, \code{propext}. The receipt is \code{formal/evidence/RELEASE_VERIFICATION.json}; its detailed compiler and logical-replay evidence is under \code{formal/evidence/whole_closure/}.

The recorded computational checks pass for 96 exact finite or symbolic visual assertions, 33 companion tests, and 10 regenerated PDF/PNG figure pairs. These bounded computations have their own scope and do not replace the formal declarations.

The pinned upstream dependency cache is reused. Logical replay uses Lean's official kernel; it is not an independently implemented checker. The source-to-manuscript correspondence is a separate reviewed mathematical artifact.
