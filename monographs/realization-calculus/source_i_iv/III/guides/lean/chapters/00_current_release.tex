\subsection*{The mathematical objects are the actual ranges}

These submodules carry their inherited norms. The continuous jet uses the actual inclusions and output restriction. Its underlying multilinear map is the previously defined algebraic range jet.
\[
 \|J_{\gamma\mid\alpha}B\|\leq \|Q_\gamma\|\,\|B\|.
\]
The product of norms of the input projectors belongs to the estimate for the ambient extension. It is unnecessary in the sharper bound for the literal restricted jet. Closed ranges and the continuous coordinate equivalence are proved for continuous projectors.

\subsection*{What the derivative notation commits to}
Mathlib defines \code{iteratedFDeriv} as a total function. Reconstructing its chosen value is unconditional. That identity alone does not assert differentiability of an arbitrary map. The composition theorem supplies local differentiability hypotheses explicitly. Its \code{OrderedFinpartition n} uses the canonical order of the blocks by their greatest elements. Each original slot is selected by the partition embedding, and every intermediate label assignment is summed. In the real or complex smooth setting, symmetry identifies this formula with the manuscript's unordered-partition notation.

The support theorem says that a nonzero composite jet has at least one decorated partition with a nonzero outer jet and all inner jets nonzero. It gives no converse. Constituent maps can compose to zero, and distinct terms can cancel. The formal composition identity also covers order zero through the unique empty partition. The positive-order manuscript formula requires no new nullary convention.
