REALIZATION CALCULUS — EDITORIAL LEDGER
Revision 1.1.0 | 21 October 2026

REQUEST

Rename the collected monograph Realization Calculus in place of Operation
Komorebi, remove version/date and production-history discussion from the scope,
and make the presentation more professional.

READER-FACING CHANGES

The cover, title page, running book title, metadata and output filenames use
Realization Calculus. The subtitle remains Projectors, Transfer and Arithmetic,
with authorship credited to Komorebi P. The restrained plum palette is retained.
The cover now has a clear two-line title hierarchy and no printed year.

The scope describes seeds, packet resolutions, projectors, transfer, analytic
control and arithmetic realization. The introduction supplies a mathematical
reading route. Reading conventions and rights pages use concise publication
prose. Dates, version identifiers and release procedures are omitted from the
new scope and editorial opening pages. License names retain their proper titles.
Part openings describe the results and mathematical boundaries without compiler
run dates or archival-repair history. The technical records retain provenance.

DISPLAY BRANDING

Only publication labels reading Operation Komorebi are changed in component
PDFs. The original scientific text, figures, page numbering and reference routes
remain the inputs to the collection. Exact original PDFs remain attached and
available under frozen_readers. The display adapter and its comparison report
record every changed label, including cases where the old project name appeared
beside Realization Calculus and the combined label is shortened to one name.

VERIFICATION AND BOUNDARIES

The previous publication review is retained under
editorial/revision_history/initial_collected_design. Source bodies and research
claim status are not revised by this editorial change. Paper XI Conjecture 66
remains open. Paper XII retains its stated arithmetic, patching, support,
same-field Hecke, classicality and representation hypotheses.

The rebuilt book is checked against every displayed component, including text,
vector geometry, page bounds, bookmarks, internal destinations and exact original
attachments. Branding edits are checked separately against original components
for nonbranding text, page counts, navigation and pixels outside edited areas.
Final results and exact output hashes are recorded in the companion audit files.
No new Lean replay or mathematical verification is claimed by this revision.

DELIVERY

The three deliverables replace the existing collected-book Library items, retaining
their identities and version histories. The user-facing scope carries no build or
version narrative. This ledger and the package manifest provide that information
for reproducibility.

CHECKED PUBLICATION RESULT

The complete book contains 3,068 pages and the design preview contains 33 pages.
The final structural audit reports no errors or warnings: all 3,044 component
pages preserve their normalized text and vector geometry, all 41 exact original
attachments match their source hashes, and the book retains 2,471 bookmarks,
8,008 internal links, 46 URI links and 32 valid attachment links.

The display adapter removes 150 old labels on 149 pages in six components:
144 replacement lines and six redundant old labels removed where the desired
series title already exists. All 35 unaffected components remain byte-identical
to their raw builds. All 154 pages of adapted components preserve nonbranding
glyphs and positions. The changed areas passed independent visual inspection.

The independent pixel comparison retains one explicit diagnostic: on Paper IV's
visual guide page 12, a small circle's x coordinates reserialize by approximately
0.0000153 point. At 72 dpi this changes two antialias edge pixels by at most 3/255.
The surrounding text and figure structure are unchanged. The same region is
pixel-identical at 144 and 288 dpi. No comparison mask was expanded to conceal
this difference, and no mathematical or visual content was removed. Detailed
measurements are retained with the branding audit.
