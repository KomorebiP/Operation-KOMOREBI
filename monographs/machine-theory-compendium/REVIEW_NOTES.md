# Assembly, editorial and mathematical review

**Machine-Native Reasoning · Collection edition 1.0.0 · 9 October 2026 · Komorebi P.**

## What was created

A 27-page connected synthesis, four analytical figures, a sixteen-interface bridge register, a small exact connecting example, and navigable readers preserving twenty-four selected PDFs. The complete reader has 1,919 pages: 1,859 original pages, 27 synthesis pages, six front-navigation pages, three division pages and twenty-four source cards. The three smaller readers have 511, 697 and 714 pages.

This is an editorial integration with precise mathematical examples. It is not a full rewriting or theorem-level deduplication of every source, not a newly integrated runtime, and not an exhaustive machine-archive census.

## Source preservation

All twenty-four retrieved original PDF hashes agree with SOURCE_CATALOG.json. All 1,859 unique source pages preserve their extracted text, dimensions and rotation in the master. The same checks passed again in the split readers, for 3,718 source-page comparisons. First, middle and last pages from each original were also rendered and compared with their master counterparts: all 72 pixel comparisons passed at the declared rendering scale.

The full reader carries 2,202 bookmarks. All bookmark destinations and the checked internal/generated cross-volume link destinations are in range. Original web links, historical sandbox references and external-document links inside the source works are retained, not authenticated or guaranteed reachable. The source PDFs are provided separately when exact original document behavior is needed.

Six prior complete source ZIPs were preserved. Their CRC checks passed, and recursive inspection found no standalone font files. The source works retain independent versions, evidence boundaries and rights. No blanket relicensing or hosted publication was performed.

## New connecting mathematics

Four elementary results state evidence initialization of a predictive quotient, composition of two operation intertwiners, posterior/response error in a bounded decision, and price-relative event savings. They are restatements or direct compositions of the source machinery, not claims of new priority.

The new example uses eight worlds (target bit, sensor convention, irrelevant nuisance). It checks exact display continuation, calibration, a coordinate translator, sixteen correction-memory occupancy states, loss of target–sensor association in separate marginals, a jointly feasible protocol and a universal-repair scope change. The linear example uses two three-dimensional operators with one common two-dimensional quotient. It therefore permits arbitrary switching in this example; this is not inferred from agreement of fixed-model trajectories alone.

The small exact suite passed eight groups and 18,316 assertions using integers, finite sets and fractions.Fraction. Scope and per-group domains appear in validation/connection_checks.json. These are bounded checks authored in the same project. The general elementary arguments are the prose proofs, not the enumeration totals.

## Editorial inspection

All twenty-seven new synthesis pages were rendered and inspected in contact sheets. All thirty-three master navigation, part and source-card pages were also rendered and inspected. Selected pages received full-scale inspection for the cover, contents, mathematical claims, diagrams and long titles. Mechanical tests found no text outside the new page boundaries, unresolved markers or final LaTeX warnings.

The imported 1,859 pages were not independently proofread line by line in this assembly. Their source identities, text and geometry were checked, and sampled renders were compared. Source-level empirical, mathematical and formal assertions remain attributed to their original editions.

## Actual failures and corrections

1. The first editorial LaTeX build failed because a right-aligned tabular cell changed the meaning of the row terminator. The column definition was corrected to include the proper array-backslash handling. The failed log is retained as validation/synthesis_build_1.log. Subsequent builds succeeded.
2. Early layouts had a contents spill, an isolated figure page, and short continuation pages. Figure placement and repeated prose were revised, and contents spacing was adjusted without removing a connecting proof. A cover-paragraph line-breaking diagnostic was corrected. The final synthesis is twenty-seven pages and its final build log has no warnings or box diagnostics.
3. Full-scale inspection found the word “decision” crossing its arrow in the new sensor diagram. The label was moved beside the arrow, and the message-provenance caption was moved away from the action box. The figures, PDFs and final receipts were regenerated after that correction.
4. The first combined rebuild call exceeded the outer tool execution window after completing five commands. It wrote no completion receipt and left an empty sixth-command log. The partial attempt was not accepted. A supervised rerun was then launched with detached standard streams and polled to completion within the same interaction. The command-level limits remained unchanged.
5. No unexpected mathematical assertion failed in the new example suite. No source-materialization failure occurred for the selected twenty-four PDFs. These statements concern this assembly only, not the historical failures documented inside the source works.

## What was not rerun

No inherited full scientific suite, Lean project, training run, hardware benchmark or external empirical evaluation was executed. Archived formal and experimental evidence remains archived evidence. No claim of independent peer review, new theorem novelty, unrestricted recursive improvement or a completed learned Neuralese–OSL interface is made.

The complete source archives for all older PDFs were not recovered. Typed Proof Learner/Resolution Completion are represented by a handoff note only. The full source queue is in UNASSEMBLED_BRANCHES.md.

## Reproduction boundary

The fresh rebuild uses the shipped source PDF files and vector figures. It recompiles the synthesis, reruns the new finite checks, reassembles all readers and reruns their page-preservation/navigation checks. The actual clean-rebuild comparisons and input hashes are in validation/fresh_rebuild.json and validation/build_receipt.json. This is same-environment reproducibility, not independent or cross-platform replication.

PDF byte identity is not required. Windows execution, PDF/A, tagged accessibility, every historical external link and all underlying scientific results are not certified. A checksum proves agreement with this snapshot inventory, not the truth or independent origin of its claims.
