## The two added mathematical obligations

### Dependency-complete reuse

Theorem 24.1 requires a finite acyclic graph, semantically complete direct dependencies, sound local verification rules, and applicable external premises. Its induction reuses unchanged witnesses outside stale descendants and restores affected claims only after their actual premises are re-established.

The theorem does not infer a complete dependency graph from arbitrary code. The negative control intentionally declares only u for a claim about u=v. Changing unrecorded v can leave that incomplete registry satisfied while the claim becomes false. This is an explicit failure of the hypothesis, not a claim of universal fault detection.

The demonstration binds actual program text and canonical artifact values, then replays the admitted equation library, grammar quotient, capacity rule, repair-weight witness, and controller. Its dependency graph and primitive replayers are supplied. Parent content identifiers aid consistency; they do not authenticate an external source or establish its authorization.

Adding a universal repair invalidates the old weights and controller support. Recomputed weights give a valid one-step, one-record controller. The old performance assessment remains blocked because it refers to the preceding controller and comparison. It is not silently reissued as evidence that the replacement is faster.

Changing only resource prices leaves the semantic certificates valid but requires a new assessment. That assessment can validly report a loss. Validity of a cost calculation is not the same claim as positivity of its margin.

### Common resource prices and workloads

Proposition 25.1 is a statement about a fixed complete additive event ledger, not a hardware performance law. Componentwise nonnegative savings are necessary and sufficient for nonnegative savings at every nonnegative price vector. A specified finite convex price family can be checked at its listed vertices.

The actual weighted-search difference is (1552, 1552, -1868) in model reads, rational additions, and capacity comparisons. Equal prices give +1236; prices (1,0,1) give -316; prices (0,0,1) give -1868. Scope and witness reads are common in this isolated comparison. The extra preparation and lifecycle work is not thereby erased from the total project cost.

The setup example of 2000 comparison units is explicitly hypothetical. It illustrates amortization, not a measured charge for the complete equational learner. Peak memory, retained storage, bytes, and elapsed time require their own models. The manuscript does not sum incompatible units and call the result a universal speedup.

## Actual failures and presentation corrections

1. The initial expanded layout produced 63 pages, with a contents spill and short continuation pages. A revised layout still left only the reference entry on a second contents page. Inter-entry spacing was reduced while keeping the contents and body font sizes unchanged. Repeated summary prose was removed, and the final length is sixty pages. No formal statement or proof was deleted to achieve that length.
2. The new resource table initially produced underfull-box diagnostics. Ragged-right paragraph columns corrected those warnings while preserving the full content. The final build has no LaTeX warnings or box diagnostics.
3. The first combined full-validation container call timed out at its outer allowance before a completion receipt was written. Its partial log was preserved as `validation/development/first_edition_outer_timeout.log`. An old receipt was not accepted as evidence of the new source. A supervised rerun completed with exit status zero, matching before/after input hashes, and all five exact suites passing. The inherited first suite retained its 90-second per-process wall limit, and the remaining commands retained their 45-second limits. This changed orchestration, not the mathematical domains or assertion tolerances.
4. Several public DOI/source fetches did not return readable full text. Primary alternatives and the depth actually reached are listed in `SOURCE_MAP.md`. Those failures are not counted as full-text reviews. Existing meta-level improvement claims in Hyperagents were made more explicit, and a sketching bibliography entry uses an abbreviated author list instead of pretending to resolve a discrepancy in coauthor order among records.

No mathematical assertion or LaTeX compilation failed in the completed first-edition runs. The layout diagnostics, access failures, and incomplete outer execution remain documented failures and are not erased by successful reruns. Deliberate rejection of bad witnesses, scopes, and prices is a negative control, not an unexpected test failure.
