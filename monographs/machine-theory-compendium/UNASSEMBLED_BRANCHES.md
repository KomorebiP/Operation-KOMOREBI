# Coverage beyond the selected collection

The twenty-four full PDFs in SOURCE_CATALOG.json are the material actually assembled. The collection does not claim that a search of all historical machine material was exhaustive.

| Branch | What this collection includes | What it does not claim |
|---|---|---|
| Typed Proof Learner v0.3.0 and Resolution Completion v0.12.0 | The recovered cross-chat handoff note, an editorial connection entry and the stated integration boundary | Complete source archives, a newly verified integration into the world-action dispatcher, or a current blanket formal status |
| RRCT / J-space / causal self-model and workspace experimental lineages | Their conceptual relationship to Neuralese, SRWT and the institution theory | A full deduplicated empirical archive or a fresh scientific evaluation |
| ARC / task-specific PR-LASM software | Large Signal Model foundations 0.4.0, which explicitly preserves software version 2.3.0 as a separate source | Every solver version, every benchmark record or a newly measured generalization score |
| Model-Theory Normalization and additional proof/formal projects | Their place in the continuing retrieval queue | Their standalone sources or formal counts folded into the collection |
| Wider Resolution Theory, projector calculus and mathematical practice | Sufficient Resolution, Stratified Universality, PRIQ and Proof Path Theory supply substantial selected foundations | Every philosophical, numerical, arithmetic or earlier mathematical edition |
| Older complete source packages and additional visual/Lean guides | All selected original PDFs, plus six named complete source ZIPs | Complete editable source or formal-runtime coverage for all twenty-four PDFs |

These are assembly boundaries, not findings that missing work does not exist. A future extension should retrieve the named artifacts, preserve exact source identity and choose a source-specific authority order before adding them. Higher filenames or larger version numbers are not, by themselves, evidence of supersession.

The selected long SRIC monograph and Neuralese edition include historical experiments. They remain separate source records. The new synthesis uses their theoretical distinctions without importing an empirical claim of an integrated agent.
