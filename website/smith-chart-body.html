Periodic over λ/2

Analytic coordinates

Impedance and admittance, resolved by residue

The principal example is now the actual normalized impedance function, not a synthetic polynomial. Packet formulas are analytic identities in the open reflection disk |Γ| < 1. The selected packet is j and the order N comes from the controls above.

z(t) = (1+t)/(1−t),   y(t) = z(−t) = 1/z(t)
PN,0z = (1+tN)/(1−tN)
PN,jz = 2tj/(1−tN), 1 ≤ j < N

The packets sum exactly to the normalized impedance. For even N, the corresponding admittance packet equals (−1)j times the impedance packet. For odd N, the dyadic refinement to 2N separates the parity contributions.

What the decomposition does not preserve

Passivity: PN,0z(t) maps the open disk into the positive-real half-plane. A nonzero j packet generally does not. These packets are analytic algebraic terms, not independent passive RF loads.

Pole cancellation: the individual packets have denominators 1−tN and poles at roots of unity, even though their complete sum only has the original pole at t=1. Dropping packets can create apparent boundary singularities.

Coprime projector structure

Dyadic–odd projector factorization

For N = d × o with d a power of two and o odd, gcd(d,o) = 1. The direct packet is the intersection of its two residue conditions. When o = 1, the odd projector is the identity.

Poisson geometry of the sensitivity weight

w(ρeiθ) = 2 Pρ²(2θ)/(1−ρ²)
pρ(θ) = (1−ρ⁴) / [2π(1−2ρ² cos(2θ)+ρ⁴)]
|dz/dθ| + |dy/dθ| = 2ρ w(ρeiθ)

The phase-sampling overlay uses closed-form inverse Poisson quantiles, not quadrature. Its equal-mass intervals minimize the largest integrated sum-of-coordinate-speeds bound; this is not a universal reconstruction optimum.

A reproducible scan uses 32 uniform nodes 2πk/32 or equal-mass boundaries Q(k/32), where Q is the inverse CDF, with errors sampled at 65,536 angles. The overlay instead shows mass-midpoint nodes. In this noiseless scan the impedance error is about 7.65× lower with the equal-mass boundaries. For h(t) = t, uniform nodes are better (0.00433 vs 0.12927). These sampled maxima are not bounds for every angle.

Boundary sampling diagnostics

The optional overlay distributes points around |Γ| = ρ in proportion to the symmetric sensitivity weight w(Γ) = |1 − Γ|−2 + |1 + Γ|−2. This weight is half the sum of the absolute complex derivative magnitudes of normalized impedance and admittance. On a fixed-radius phase orbit it is proportional to the total absolute angular speed |dz/dθ|+|dy/dθ|. It is not a dimensionless relative condition number.
