Lean 4, the cauli compiler fork, Mathlib and supporting Lean packages
retain their Apache 2.0 licenses and author attributions.

Komorebi modifications: separate worker source; packed standard libraries;
Emscripten runtime keep-alive; workspace cache reset; fatal exit handling;
checksum checks; syntax-probe completion checks. These are marked in source.

The isolation service worker follows the MIT coi-serviceworker approach:
Guido Zuidhof and contributors, https://github.com/gzuidhof/coi-serviceworker.
It is limited to the browser Lean page, with checksum-verified compiler caching.

The compiler's exact upstream revisions and packaged file checksums appear in
lean/browser/engine/SOURCE.json. Source proof checksums are in examples.json.
