## This companion's new analytical construction

The three-bit source, separate A/B observation access, exact eighteen-event schedule, communication receipts, viewer-only environment display, entropy histories, 3D probability projection, and controlled access comparisons are newly authored illustrative material in `code/model.py` and `code/visuals.py`.

The source paper does not claim that this exact two-agent schedule was executed previously. The illustration does not inherit a theorem of safe operation under arbitrary environmental change. In particular, the sensor changes between investigations and the reset rule is supplied. Before an agent receives the contradiction or reset notice, its old certainty can be stale.
