# Claim Boundary

## Established by this release

This release establishes that:

1. a replay-capable intervention interface has been implemented for the reviewed multimode Coconut recurrence;
2. the interface can capture and replace the exact hidden vectors reinjected at latent-token positions;
3. donor, semantic-twin, corruption, truncation, sealing, receipt, and grouped-bootstrap mechanics are executable;
4. an engineered procedure-specific fixture passes every declared gate;
5. a toy causal language model verifies exact replay and sensitivity to altered latent states;
6. the package can freeze a real checkpoint, dataset, donor plan, config, and maximum claim before execution.

## Not established in this environment

The external GPT-2 PrOntoQA checkpoint was not downloaded or executed here. Therefore this release does not establish:

- checkpoint accuracy;
- latent necessity;
- temporal order sensitivity;
- proof-specific latent content;
- semantic-twin invariance;
- endogenous semantic discovery;
- causal semantic editing;
- cross-model transport.

## Maximum real Pilot 0.1 claim

Even after a successful real run, the maximum claim is `PROCEDURE_SPECIFIC`.

This means that a same-answer, different-proof trace is not interchangeable with the task's own trace under the declared support and that a premise-order twin remains compatible. It does not identify a unique semantic interpretation of the trace.

## Synthetic fixture boundary

The included `PROCEDURE_SPECIFIC` result is an engineered positive control. It validates the audit's ability to recognize a known procedure-specific trace. It is not evidence about a trained language model.

## Publication boundary

Pilot 0.1 should be reported as:

- an infrastructure and preregistration artifact before the external run;
- a checkpoint-specific causal audit after the external run;
- not a general theorem about Coconut, latent reasoning, or Neuralese.
