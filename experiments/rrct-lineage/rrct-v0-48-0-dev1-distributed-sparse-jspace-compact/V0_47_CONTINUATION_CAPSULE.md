## Completed result

Two independently parameterized neutral self-indexed agents were given complementary information in a bounded synthetic task. A rank-four common manifold was discovered from synchronized local workspace trajectories without evaluator factor labels.

The frozen four-seed holdout contained 384 unique dyadic cases and 3,456 matched architecture decisions. All 28 frozen endpoints passed.
