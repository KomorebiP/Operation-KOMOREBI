# RRCT v0.49 Continuation Capsule

## Current strongest result

Two independently initialized transformer agents trained from scratch developed an aligned late residual-access state.

The state was:

- exact for shared factor reports;
- reusable across five shared outputs;
- selectively ablatable;
- causally patchable;
- transmissible from either agent to the other;
- largely free of private-role information.

## Critical negative results

- The analogous early average-Jacobian state was not semantic, aligned, or causally necessary.
- The late state explained about 79% of residual variance and was not a tiny sparse workspace.
- Same-rank PCA performed comparably to the Jacobian-informed basis.
- A foreign patch was corrected by persistent local evidence after another round.
- Full four-seed replay was not completed; one sealed seed replayed exactly.

## Correct current claim

A trained-transformer shared late-access manifold is established in the synthetic dyad.

A persistent distributed J-space, Jacobian-specific workspace, pretrained-language-model result, or subjective shared space is not established.

## Next experiment

Use two instrumentable pretrained open-weight language models. Estimate moving token-indexed J-lenses from residual streams during repeated interaction. Compare Jacobian, PCA, random, message-only, one-way, and recurrence controls. Require bidirectional causal patching, flexible reuse, private-state preservation, and partner generalization.
