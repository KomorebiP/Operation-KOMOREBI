# RRCT v0.30 handoff

## Bottom line

RRCT v0.30 now treats `(active, handle, source, rank)` as the minimum persistent
structural self-boundary signature.  Structural changes require prospectively
new evidence; same-signature geometry can still refresh immediately.

The frozen official outcome is **integrity PASS, 10/10 scientific gates PASS,
overall PASS** across eight untouched seeds and 21,920 retained rows.

This is a bounded functional self-model result.  Do not summarize it as
phenomenal self-awareness or consciousness, and do not describe its higher-mode
admission as controlled causal-rank inference.

## What was closed

- One same-pair rank dropout no longer overwrites the accepted structure.
- A matching candidate can advance only after 120 new completed rows, despite
  fits every 20 rows.
- Sustained pair/rank changes produce explicit candidate, revision, epoch, and
  latency receipts.
- Null revocation uses the same fresh-row eligibility clock.
- Fit confidence now pairs each full/half statistic with its own threshold.
- Learner RNG is fixed independently of the evaluator/world seed.
- The official harness pins runtime, freezes 105 files, journals each seed
  exactly once, and reconstructs all 31 seed metrics from exact raw-row
  schedules before aggregation.

## Official result

- Stable exact structural accuracy: 1.0 in every seed.
- Transient exact structural accuracy: 1.0 in every seed.
- Transient adaptive rank revisions: zero in every seed.
- Transient pressure: 78 correct-pair rank-one proposals; all eight seeds
  potent.
- Weak overlap control: false-rank revisions in all eight seeds.
- Sustained contraction/restoration: exactly one revision each in every seed;
  terminal-tail accuracy 1.0.
- Slowest restoration: 460 rows against the 480-row ceiling.

## Next implementation target

Implement a conditional reduced-rank null for modes above one.  The present
rerandomization is calibrated for the global sharp null of no handle-mediated
interaction; it destroys lower modes and therefore cannot provide a formal
test of `rank < k`.

Then address, in order:

1. non-overlapping structural confirmations or explicit online error control;
2. learned persistence/changepoint timescales rather than a supplied 120-row
   interval;
3. confirmed projector/effect geometry and formal revocation equivalence;
4. action-conditional causal credit; and
5. end-to-end use of the revised boundary in workspace selection and behavior.

Use new pilot and official cohorts.  Seeds 5701, 5707, 5713, 5719 and 5801,
5807, 5813, 5819, 5821, 5827, 5839, 5843 are now exposed development/official
seeds and cannot serve as prospective confirmation again.
