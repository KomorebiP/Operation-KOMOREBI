# RRCT v0.31 handoff

## Bottom line

RRCT v0.31 adds a lower-mode-preserving, cross-fitted reduced-rank
randomization bootstrap and integrates its inferred rank into the persistent
online causal boundary.

The frozen official outcome is **integrity PASS, scientific FAIL (7/10 primary
gate booleans)** across eight untouched seeds and 30,720 retained rows.  One
underlying estimator miss—seed 6001, `rank_one_strong`—flips three coupled
gates.  Preserve that result without replay, exclusion, threshold relaxation,
or post-outcome tuning.

This is incomplete progress in bounded functional causal self-modeling.  It is
not evidence of phenomenal self-awareness or consciousness.

## What was closed

- Pair discovery occurs on a proposal fold before either confirmation fold is
  inspected.
- Each higher-mode null preserves the proposal-fitted lower-rank response under
  handle reassignment.
- Both disjoint confirmation folds must pass a strict finite-Monte-Carlo
  threshold, and rank testing stops at the first failure.
- The squared next singular value is computed in the exact Cholesky ridge
  metric and equals the next penalized-objective improvement.
- A second nonoverlapping 480-row fit is required before the inferred pair/rank
  enters persistent boundary state.
- The official harness freezes 946 files and eight immutable evidence-tree
  roots, journals every seed once, and independently reconstructs all retained
  summaries and aggregate gates.

The test remains an approximate one-step composite-null bootstrap with observed
history held fixed.  It is not an exact Fisher test, recursively regenerated
dynamic null, or sequence-wide error-control method.

## Failure diagnosis

At seed 6001's strong rank-one fit, the conditional path returns no pair, rank
zero, confidence zero, and `null_fit`.  On the same realized commitments,
sources, observations, and outcomes, the global comparator finds the correct
pair `(3, 3)` at rank one with support `0.5788106389700153`.

The v0.31 telemetry lacks proposal survivor count/ambiguity and per-fold
statistics, thresholds, add-one values, and reason codes.  It cannot identify
whether the conditional null arose during proposal selection or confirmation.
The official world was not replayed.  Because `rank_one_strong` is a separate
random episode, the miss alone is not a monotonicity result.

## Next implementation target

Freeze a fresh, stage-resolved operating-characteristic assay before changing
the decision rule:

1. Retain proposal multiplicity, pair, statistic/threshold, every confirmation
   statistic/threshold/add-one value, and an explicit null-reason enum.
2. Pair fresh worlds across rank zero and a predeclared rank-one strength grid
   using common structure/noise; approximately 200 worlds per cell is a useful
   starting point.
3. Estimate selection and confirmation power separately, alongside false-
   positive behavior.  Do not replay or tune against seed 6001.
4. Develop a better-powered valid held-out-fold combination only on development
   worlds, then freeze new untouched official seeds.
5. Make discovery sensitivity, literal one-window nonadmission, two-window
   admission, and integrity separate primary endpoints.  Candidate correctness
   and fitted margin should not duplicate one miss across primary gates.

After that, address a dynamic null or explicit online error control under
changing histories, then reconnect the learned boundary to action credit and
downstream behavioral use.
