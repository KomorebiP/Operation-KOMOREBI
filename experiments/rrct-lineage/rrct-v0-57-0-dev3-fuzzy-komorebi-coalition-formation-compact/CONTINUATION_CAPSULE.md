# RRCT v0.57 Continuation Capsule

## Established

Fuzzy coalition matrices can be learned from terminal behavior and used as positive, non-idempotent Komorebi packet operators.

Across eight fresh seeds:

- all 22 frozen endpoints passed
- fuzzy packet mean MSE was 0.000511
- held-out blend MSE was 0.001130
- oracle hard blend MSE was 0.054644
- hardening the learned fuzzy state increased blend error 74.4 times
- membership strength correlated 0.5475 with causal ablation impact
- idempotence defect correlated 0.9171 with true task fuzziness
- packet partition, reconstruction, and transport certificates all passed

## Critical negative result

Direct unconstrained fuzzy edges achieved lower MSE than the packet factorization:

```text
direct edge MSE = 0.000263
fuzzy packet MSE = 0.000511
```

The evidence supports graded relational structure and the usefulness of the packet calculus. It does not establish unique predictive superiority.
