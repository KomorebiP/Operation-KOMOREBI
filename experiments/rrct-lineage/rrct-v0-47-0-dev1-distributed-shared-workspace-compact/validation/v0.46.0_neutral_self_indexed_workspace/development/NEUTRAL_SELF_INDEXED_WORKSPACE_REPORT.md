## Interpretation

In this synthetic residual-stream model, a Jacobian-discovered, Komorebi-certified sparse workspace supports report, directed modulation, flexible reuse, and internal reasoning. Binding that workspace to RRCT lineage and provenance is separately required for autobiographical ownership, planner use, and recurrent consequence-driven evidence allocation.

The main dissociation is that workspace-only systems can retain flexible task use while failing autobiographical ownership, and self-model-only systems can retain lineage information while lacking flexible global access. The unbound combination does not repair this: access and self-modeling must be linked. Planner binding and recurrence are then separately necessary for action control and consequence-driven reallocation of evidence.

## Boundary

This is a controlled functional test of a J-space-like workspace analogue, not an experiment on Claude and not evidence of phenomenal consciousness, sentience, valence, moral status, or subjective experience.
