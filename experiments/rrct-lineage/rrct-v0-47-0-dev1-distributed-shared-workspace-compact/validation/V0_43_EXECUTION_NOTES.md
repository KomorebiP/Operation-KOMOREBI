## Evidence retained

The release keeps source, model definitions, exact configurations, seed and freeze records, 3,072 condition-level compact decision rows, all aggregate summaries, receipt digests, endpoint adjudication, and conclusions.

It does not keep raw per-probe vectors or repeated matrix snapshots. Those are deterministically regenerated by the sealed source, seeds, and configuration. The release verifier performs that regeneration and requires exact normalized equality.

## Fresh-boundary rescue interpretation

The `memory_cut` arm withholds persistent boundary memory but preserves the already grounded temporal identity authorization. After the pre-outcome state requests additional evidence, the harness supplies a newly instantiated current certificate produced by the same learner-defined boundary construction.

The reported 20 versus 16 evidence comparison counts candidate preview pairs only. It does not include the full computational or sample cost of rebuilding the boundary certificate, so it is a conservative and incomplete estimate of the benefit of persistent memory.

## Scientific boundary

The holdout is fresh with respect to seeds, but its world generator and four task scenarios are inherited from v0.32 and were known during design. Results are descriptive across four worlds and should not be treated as broad statistical proof or independent replication.
