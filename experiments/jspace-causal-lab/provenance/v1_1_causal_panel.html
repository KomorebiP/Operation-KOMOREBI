Experiment boundary and measured validation

The default J frame is formed from centered, averaged gradients of four exact-report logits through the two silent updates. It is output-informed. Three dimensions are the rank limit of four centered rows, not an emergence result.

Projected edits are artificial. Earlier workspace studies failed stronger natural-state audits. No new support certificate is claimed here. A whole paired donor is a native-state control, not a localized write. Same-rank controls can also affect output.

These are small saved GRU models, not the hidden states of this chat assistant. No language-model J-space, general intelligence or consciousness claim follows.

Source weights and candidates are frozen. All edits are inference-time interventions. The answer network is never changed, bypassed or replaced by the exact evaluator.
