What this comparison can and cannot establish

The recipient sees the partner only through the message interface. At the first exchange its state is identical in the original, edited, and replay branches. Equal incoming packets must therefore produce equal recipient updates. This equality checks the causal wiring and implementation. Whether the packet induces the intended answer and whether it stays effective are separate empirical questions.

Only the first packet is intervened upon. Later feedback is unrestricted, so restoration may cease to block the effect and a replayed effect may disappear. Reference errors remain visible. A whole donor replaces both states and is a competence reference, not proof of a native local edit.

Projected states remain artificial. No new training, natural-support certification, general reciprocity, or chat-assistant hidden-state access is claimed.
