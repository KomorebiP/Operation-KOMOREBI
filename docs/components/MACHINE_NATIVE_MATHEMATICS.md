# The Possibility for a Machine-Native Mathematics

**Komorebi P. · Revised reading edition · 29 October 2026**

[Read the revised edition](../essays/MACHINE_NATIVE_MATHEMATICS.md) · [Download the 25-page monograph PDF](../../website/assets/publications/machine-native-mathematics.pdf).

This essay introduces the possibility of mathematical representations, abstractions, and languages shaped for machine reasoning. Its opening separates mathematical truth from the representational systems used to approach it. Page 2 identifies its purpose as explaining the conceptual architecture of Komorebi. The wider papers, formal developments, code, and experiments carry their own technical claims.

The author note explains the use of **Komorebi P.** as a pseudonym and the role of AI assistance. The [public project and authorship statement](../PROJECT_STATEMENT.md) sets out the author's responsibilities and the publication circumstances.

## What was revised

The complete essay has been revised for clarity and a more natural voice.
Repeated pull quotes, unnecessary fragments, semicolons, and dash clauses have
been removed or rewritten. The monograph has a dedicated cover, linked contents,
numbered chapters and sections, running headers, and Palatino-family text and
mathematics. Its Markdown manuscript and generated TeX are editable.

Local mathematical clarifications state the linear hypotheses of the residual
law, take joint abstractions onto their image, define equivalence pushforwards,
and separate value sufficiency from decision sufficiency. Conditions for the
cost and expectation formulas are explicit. The [editorial review](../../publication/machine-native-mathematics/EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md) records these changes.
