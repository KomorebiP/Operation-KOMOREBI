Active development with a separately compiled Lean subset: the two-valued scaffold and the two-world additive/Boolean symmetry obstruction. The wider structural and forcing claims need their own formalization.

## Before you start

**Suggested background:** Read the problem statement, recorded scope and open questions first. The program's definitions determine its subject prerequisites.

**Recorded results and limits:** The separate Lean 4.24.0 companion compiles 15 unchanged scaffold statements and 19 two-world declarations, including both assertions of Theorem 7.3. Both obstruction theorems have empty axiom inventories. General Boolean-algebra-valued transport, graph and forcing results remain outside this formal scope.

**Remaining work:** Formalize a precise next contract for the Boolean-algebra-valued transport differential or finite graph integration. The original 0.1.0 source edition and its uncompiled label are preserved.

The background advice is an editorial starting point. The work itself states its prerequisites and hypotheses.
