# Future Developments

My future research will focus on Hilbert's 12th and 24th problems, the Strong
Artin conjecture, and the Langlands program. I will also continue refining my
work on machine learning and reasoning, improving the existing mathematics and
software, and making the website and its GitHub repositories easier to access
and use.

Updated 3 November 2026. The linked records describe progress, remaining
requirements, and the evidence for each work.

These plans and the existing work will likely evolve as I review research and
tools released by OpenAI and others.

## Main research directions

### Hilbert's 12th problem

I will continue developing the explicit arithmetic constructions and
investigating the remaining questions about analytic comparison and realization.
The existing Hilbert 12 studies provide starting points. Their finite results
and open analytic obligations are recorded separately.

[Ambient dual slice](works/hilbert12-ambient-dual-slice.md) ·
[Analytic Fourier branch](works/hilbert12-analytic-fourier.md) ·
[Cubic divisor development](works/hilbert12-cubic-divisor.md)

### Hilbert's 24th problem

I will investigate the simplicity and comparison of proofs, building on the
current work on proof paths, validity, interpretation, and review. This includes
exploring how these ideas can make proofs easier to understand, compare, and
check.

[Proof Path Theory](works/proof-path-theory.md)

### Strong Artin

I will continue my research on the Strong Artin conjecture, with particular
attention to the remaining global automorphic attachment and to reproducible
checks of the existing formal developments. The general conjecture remains open.

[Strong Artin: current work and evidence](works/strong-artin-reader.md)

### Langlands program

I will investigate connections between arithmetic realizations, Hecke data, and
automorphic constructions, building on Papers XI and XII. As this work advances,
I will continue to state the outstanding arithmetic inputs and conditional
comparisons explicitly.

[Paper XI: arithmetic realization](works/paper-xi.md) ·
[Paper XII: conditional automorphic completion](works/paper-xii.md)

### Machine learning and reasoning

I will refine the current work on machine learning and reasoning, including the
Large Signal Model, Machine Native Reasoning, and Machine Understanding.
Priorities include sharper mathematical models, clearer links between theory and
implementation, and reproducible evaluation of practical claims.

[Large Signal Model](works/monographs-large-signal-model.md) ·
[Machine Native Reasoning](works/monographs-machine-native-reasoning.md) ·
[Machine Understanding](works/research-machine-understanding.md)

## Reading editions and visual guides

The twelve core papers already have online reading editions and visual guides.
The [Large Signal Model](works/monographs-large-signal-model.md) also has an
online edition with its statements, written proofs, and formal correspondence
linked together.

I plan to extend guided reading paths across the collection, bring more books
into the reading library, and connect explanations more closely to worked
examples. The expanded Machine Native Reasoning book still needs its own source
and edition checks before publication.

## Lean and reproducible checks

I will continue improving the browser Lean workspaces, especially library
loading, compiler compatibility, and reliable checking of larger dependency
chains. This includes expanding access to retained formal sources, particularly
the newer Special Functions developments, and keeping reproduction instructions
aligned with published editions.

Further formalization will follow explicit maps between manuscript claims and
Lean declarations. Editions that rely on inherited compiler evidence still need
fresh checks against their pinned sources and dependencies.

[Try browser Lean](https://KomorebiP.github.io/Operation-KOMOREBI/lean/browser/index.html) ·

## Interactive mathematics

I will continue developing the Signal, Recovery, and J-Space laboratories, with
clearer links between each experiment and the mathematics it illustrates.
Priorities include guided experiments, useful comparisons, and results that
readers can inspect and reproduce.

New examples will state their assumptions and the scope of their exact or
numerical results. They will also link to the relevant sources. The evidence
for the Large Signal Model's mathematical edition is recorded separately from
the evidence for its software and empirical development.

[Open interactive tools](https://KomorebiP.github.io/Operation-KOMOREBI/interactive.html) ·
[Current laboratory features](READER_SITE.md) ·
[Large Signal Model software and limits](works/large-signal-model.md)

## Website and GitHub accessibility and use

I will make the website and GitHub repositories easier to navigate and use.
Planned improvements include clearer starting points, better search and
cross-links, simpler setup instructions, useful downloads, and straightforward
ways to report corrections.

I will continue reviewing keyboard and screen-reader access, mobile reading,
mathematical reading order, diagram descriptions, and the original PDFs. I will
also work to recover or reconstruct the exact editable source corresponding to
Paper IV's visual PDF, with a documented edition record and comparison.
