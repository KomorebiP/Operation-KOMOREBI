# Future Developments

The main future research priorities are Hilbert's 12th and 24th problems,
Strong Artin, the Langlands program, and refining the current work on machine
learning and reasoning. Alongside these directions, work will continue on the
existing mathematics, software, and the accessibility and usability of this
website and its GitHub repositories.

Updated 3 November 2026. These are current directions; their linked records
describe progress, remaining requirements, and the evidence for each work.

These directions and the existing work will likely be updated as I work through
research and tools released by OpenAI and others, and consider what they add to
the questions being developed here.

## Main research directions

### Hilbert's 12th problem

Continue the explicit arithmetic constructions and investigate the remaining
analytic comparison and realization questions. The existing Hilbert 12 studies
provide starting points, with their finite results and open analytic obligations
recorded separately.

[Ambient dual slice](works/hilbert12-ambient-dual-slice.md) ·
[Analytic Fourier branch](works/hilbert12-analytic-fourier.md) ·
[Cubic divisor development](works/hilbert12-cubic-divisor.md)

### Hilbert's 24th problem

Develop the direction concerning simplicity and comparison of proofs, building
on the current work on proof paths, validity, interpretation, and review.
Further work will examine how these ideas can make proofs easier to understand,
compare, and check.

[Proof Path Theory](works/proof-path-theory.md)

### Strong Artin

Continue the Strong Artin research, focusing on the remaining global automorphic
attachment and on reproducible checking of the existing formal developments.
The general conjecture remains an open research goal.

[Strong Artin: current work and evidence](works/strong-artin-reader.md)

### Langlands program

Investigate connections between arithmetic realizations, Hecke data, and
automorphic constructions, developing the directions in Papers XI and XII.
The outstanding arithmetic inputs and conditional comparisons will remain
explicit as this work advances.

[Paper XI: arithmetic realization](works/paper-xi.md) ·
[Paper XII: conditional automorphic completion](works/paper-xii.md)

### Machine learning and reasoning

Refine the current machine learning and reasoning work, including the Large
Signal Model, Machine Native Reasoning, and Machine Understanding. Priorities
include sharper mathematical models, clearer links between theory and
implementation, and reproducible evaluation of practical claims.

[Large Signal Model](works/monographs-large-signal-model.md) ·
[Machine Native Reasoning](works/monographs-machine-native-reasoning.md) ·
[Machine Understanding](works/research-machine-understanding.md)

## Reading editions and visual guides

The twelve core papers already have online reading editions and visual guides.
The [Large Signal Model](works/monographs-large-signal-model.md) now also has an
online edition with its statements, written proofs, and formal correspondence
linked together.

Next steps include extending guided reading paths across the collection,
bringing further books into the reading library, and connecting explanations
more closely to worked examples. The expanded Machine-Native Reasoning book
needs its own edition reconciliation before publication.

## Lean and reproducible checks

Continue improving the browser Lean workspaces, especially library loading,
compiler compatibility, and reliable checking of larger dependency chains.
Extend access to retained formal sources, including the newer Special Functions
developments, and keep reproduction instructions aligned with published editions.

Further formalization will follow explicit maps between manuscript claims and
Lean declarations. Editions that rely on inherited compiler evidence still need
fresh checks against their pinned sources and dependencies.

## Interactive mathematics

Develop the Signal, Recovery, and J-Space laboratories further, with clearer
routes from an experiment to the mathematics it illustrates. Priorities include
guided experiments, useful comparisons, and results readers can inspect and
reproduce.

Broader examples should retain their assumptions, exact or numerical scope,
and links to the relevant source. The Large Signal Model's mathematical edition
and its software and empirical development have separate evidence records.

[Open interactive tools](https://KomorebiP.github.io/Operation-KOMOREBI/interactive.html) ·

starting points, better search and cross-links, simpler setup instructions,

reading order, diagram descriptions, and the original PDFs. Recover or
reconstruct the exact editable source corresponding to Paper IV's visual PDF,
