# New to Komorebi

Begin with the Euler number calculation, then compare the signal and quantum case studies. Each can also be read independently.

1. Read the [project introduction](../START_HERE.md) and [authorship statement](../PROJECT_STATEMENT.md).
2. Use the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) for the terms **packet**, **transfer**, **residual**, and **realization**.
3. Follow [Euler numbers through packets, polynomials, and residuals](../demonstrations/EULER_PACKETS.md). Work through the reciprocal series, coordinate changes, and exact residual calculations, then reproduce the results with its standalone checker.
4. Continue with [factorial lanes](../demonstrations/FACTORIAL_LANES.md) for an arithmetic companion, [wavelets and polyphase transfer](../demonstrations/WAVELET_POLYPHASE.md) for a complete signal calculation, and [quantum phase and measurement](../demonstrations/QUANTUM_PHASE_MEASUREMENT.md) for information-preservation questions. The [case-study index](../DEMONSTRATIONS.md#extended-case-studies) offers further counting and polynomial routes.
5. Use the [project map](../PROJECT_MAP.md) to choose another subject, or the [first software examples](../FIRST_RUN.md) to try a library.
6. Read the [common computational language](https://github.com/KomorebiP/komorebi-math/blob/main/docs/UNIFIED_LANGUAGE.md).
7. Choose one [work](../BROWSE.md). Read its introduction and recorded limits before following technical references.

Power series and polynomial calculations help with the Euler and factorial studies; the wavelet study uses finite vectors and matrices. The [three elementary demonstrations](../DEMONSTRATIONS.md#three-elementary-demonstrations) offer shorter introductions. You can read the calculations and work introductions without installing software.
