## Scope and evidence

Publication preserves each component's stated mathematical and experimental boundaries. Paper XI retains open Conjecture 66 obligations. Conditional statements retain their hypotheses. Finite formal developments do not certify every analytic, physical, or implementation claim in a surrounding monograph.

At the initial publication check, the latest inspected hosted Math qualification and Polyphase fuzz runs failed before any job steps executed. Those failures supply no fresh test results.
