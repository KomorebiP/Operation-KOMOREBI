# Public project and authorship statement

**Komorebi P. · First published 27 October 2026**

Komorebi is my independent mathematical research project, published under the name **Komorebi P.** It brings together manuscripts, formal developments, software, calculations, experiments, and guides for readers.

At its center is Realization Calculus, which studies how mathematical objects are represented, decomposed, transferred, and reconstructed, and what information or obstructions remain after those operations. Komorebi Space provides a framework for comparing compatible realizations. The individual works develop different parts of this program, each with its own assumptions, claims, and supporting evidence.

## Why the work uses a pseudonym

I use a pseudonym to give readers room to encounter the work on its own terms: through its definitions, arguments, constructions, and results. Biography, credentials, and institutional affiliation can provide context, but they cannot settle the mathematical questions.

For me, the pseudonym is a working arrangement rather than a commitment to permanent anonymity.

[Read the author's note in the current edition of *The Possibility for a Machine-Native Mathematics*](essays/MACHINE_NATIVE_MATHEMATICS.md#authors-note).

## Authorship and AI assistance

This project has developed with substantial AI assistance in mathematical formulation, exploration of possible proof approaches, drafting, programming, exact calculations, testing, review, references, figures, and document production. The extent of that assistance and the review history vary across the collection and are described in the disclosures accompanying individual works.

I am the named author and take responsibility for what I publish and for correcting it. Crediting AI assistance is part of giving an accurate account of how the work was produced. That assistance does not, by itself, establish the correctness of a result.

Review must be documented separately. Publication under my name does not imply that every proof has received my personal approval, independent human review, or external certification.

## Current state and publication circumstances

This work remains in progress and has, in many respects, been rushed because of circumstances shaped by the AI industry. I am continuing to correct, verify, and organize it.

The works are at different stages of development and review. The [finalization queue](FINALIZATION_QUEUE.md) records remaining editorial work, source checks, proof replays, software tests, and reviews. Completion and confidence labels reflect the evidence recorded for each work. All executable software remains **beta and a work in progress**.

## How to evaluate the collection

Each result should be read alongside its exact statement, hypotheses, argument, source revision, and supporting evidence. Written proofs, accepted Lean declarations, exact calculations, numerical experiments, and software tests establish different things. A formal proof also requires a faithful correspondence between the formal statement and the mathematical claim it is intended to support.

The [completion guide](COMPLETION_STATUS.md) distinguishes publication readiness, Lean coverage, and the basis for confidence in a result. A finished reading edition may contain open questions. A completed formal contract covers its registered claims, and a conditional result retains its assumptions. Originality and broader scientific applicability require their own assessment.

I have made the collection public so that others can inspect, question, reproduce, and build on it under the [applicable terms](../LICENSE.md). Corrections and precise objections are welcome. Identifying the work, revision, and statement or function at issue, together with the discrepancy and a reproducible example where possible, helps me investigate and make a useful correction.
