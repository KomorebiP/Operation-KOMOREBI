PAPER IX EXTERNAL FONT DEPENDENCIES

No standalone font binaries are bundled. Rebuilding requires an installed TeX
distribution, its declared font packages, and the external OpenType/TrueType
fonts below. The scripts install no software and download no dependencies.

The traditional and visual readers use pdfLaTeX with Latin Modern, mathpazo,
Helvetica-compatible sans text and the Computer Modern mathematical families
selected by the packaged TeX style. The PDF font inventory records embedded
URW Palladio L, Nimbus Sans L, Pazo Math, Latin Modern Mono, and Computer Modern
subsets. Figure PDFs have their own embedded font subsets, including DejaVu.

The Lean guide uses XeLaTeX. Its preamble and shared publication style require
URW Base35 P052 and Nimbus Sans, Latin Modern Math, DejaVu Sans and DejaVu Sans
Mono. In the tested Linux environment the shared style's URW selectors point
to /usr/share/fonts/opentype/urw-base35/. The bare OpenType filenames in the
preamble are resolved by the installed TeX/font configuration. A different
installation must provide those declared fonts and satisfy the selectors.
This is a tested reproduction dependency, not a claim of a Windows-native
font installer or a fallback-font substitution system.

Use the root rebuild_pdfs.py entry point for a copied-source build of the whole
reading set. It stages the packaged house style, sources and figures in an
empty external directory. The direct sources/lean/build.py entry point also
stages that house style for its isolated guide build.

PDF byte identity, trailer-ID-normalized identity, and extracted-text/page-count
comparisons are separate recorded checks. Different font/TeX installations can
affect layout and bytes. Current release identities are the hashes in RELEASE.json.
