                                                 Abstract
      This paper develops the arithmetic realization of the projector-resolved seed calculus. Integer
  and rational sequences are treated as coefficient objects acted on by residue projectors, shifts, finite
  differences, exponential-generating-function convolution, triangular coordinate changes, and p-adic
  completion. The analytic families introduced earlier are reorganized in a single proof-normalized
  arithmetic notation. Congruential Euler packets are shown to satisfy exact lower-triangular inverse
  systems with explicit denominator clearing. For every odd prime p, the alternating odd-power
  coordinates
                                                p−1
                                                X
                                       Sn (p) =     (−1)a (2a + 1)n
                                                 a=0

  recover the even Euler numbers through an explicit unit-triangular transform whose mth off-diagonal
  carries p2m . Truncation at depth M therefore yields a uniform congruence modulo p2M +2 . The
  dyadic sector is developed separately. A new exact coordinate system

                                                    3n+2 − 1
                                          Rn =
                                                 4(n + 1)(n + 2)

  for even n is inverted by the coefficients of z 2 / sinh2 z after the rescaling z = 2t. Both coordinate
  and inverse coefficients are 2-adically integral, and the depth-M reconstruction is uniform modulo
  22M +2 . The global odd-prime finite-difference theorem is integrated in the normalization used
  here: the unnormalized packets j!Cmp,j (n) satisfy the all-class bound, the normalized packets
  satisfy the corresponding factorial-loss bound, and the full bound is recovered without loss in the
  standard window 0 ≤ j < p. This proves the uniform standard-window period and also identifies
  a concrete counterexample to the earlier unrestricted normalized all-residue formulation. Mahler
  coordinates convert finite differences into coefficient shifts, and companion-matrix jumps produce
  exact arithmetic subsequence recurrences. Sixteen code-generated figures, an exact formula registry,
  expanded exact arithmetic checks, and the accompanying formal proof supplement complete the
  release. Only the canonical arithmetic obstruction packet and the prime-independent arithmetic
  spine remain conjectural in this paper.

Scope and claim boundary. The paper proves the exact packet identities, triangular inverse
laws, odd-prime Euler coordinate theorem, odd-prime supercongruence ladder, weighted dyadic
Euler reconstruction, dyadic tail bound, the global odd-prime finite-difference theorem in both
unnormalized and normalized packet coordinates, the uniform standard-window period, Mahler
difference identities, and recurrence-packet formulas stated below. The unrestricted normalized
all-residue bound is not retained: it is false once j! carries positive p-adic valuation, and the
correct factorial-loss estimate is stated explicitly. Classical ingredients such as Lucas’ theorem,
Mahler expansion, Bernoulli numbers, the Chinese remainder theorem, and companion-matrix
recurrence theory are identified as inputs. The complete proof of the global odd-prime theorem
is included in the release as a formal supplement.

15     Conclusion
The arithmetic realization is not a list of unrelated congruences. It is one operator system:

                     coefficient seed −→ residue packet −→ triangular coordinates
                   −→ weighted inverse −→ difference packet −→ p-adic completion
                                       −→ exact reconstruction.

    For odd primes, alternating odd-power coordinates give an explicit Euler transform with a two-unit
valuation gain at every transfer level. At the dyadic prime, a different coordinate system and inverse
are required; their exact generating-function product recovers the same Euler seed, while the natural
visible weight is again two per transfer level. Congruential Euler packets place both constructions inside
a common triangular family. Lucas reduction explains the first standard window, Mahler coordinates
expose finite-difference weights, companion jumps control recurrence packets, and CRT addresses
recombine dyadic and odd phases without pretending that their integral denominators are identical.
    The result is a proof-normalized arithmetic packet calculus. The global odd-prime finite-difference
theorem now separates three distinct facts: the unnormalized all-class bound is exact, the normalized
standard-window bound is exact, and the unrestricted normalized all-residue bound fails by a precisely
measured factorial loss. The remaining native conjectures concern canonical obstruction packets and
prime-independent gluing rather than the finite-difference theorem itself.
