Section 9 states the Frobenius valuation for ordinary Euler numbers defined by \(\operatorname{sech}t\): at every even starting index, the order-\(k\) difference along even Euler indices has valuation \(k+v_2(k!)\). The derivation follows Theorem 9.2, on printed pages 29–30. Thus the integral divided-power Euler moments used in the address-zero proof are odd, not merely integral.
