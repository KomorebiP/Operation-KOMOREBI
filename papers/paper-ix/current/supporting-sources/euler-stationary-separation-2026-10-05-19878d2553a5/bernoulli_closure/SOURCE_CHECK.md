## Bernoulli height identity

NIST DLMF 25.6.2: https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.6.E2 . The displayed formula
is zeta(2n)=(2*pi)^(2n)|B_(2n)|/[2(2n)!], for ordinary n>=1. It is used
only for the elementary real-height lower bound in the conditioning note.
