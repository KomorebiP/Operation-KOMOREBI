# External source check and transfer boundary

No new external theorem is used in the proofs of this checkpoint. The
quadratic preparation is proved directly; Jacobi, packet, and chart inputs
are linked inherited project theorems with their recorded scopes.

The bounded search considered p-adic common-zero and logarithmic-form
methods. The primary source actually opened for the common-zero route was
Bacik, Ouaknine, Purser, and Worrell, *On the p-adic Skolem Problem*,
[author PDF](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/p-adic-skolem25.pdf),
arXiv2504.14413v4, 11 May2026, accessed2026-10-05, pages10-12.

Version matters: its footnote explicitly retracts the proceedings' Lemma10
statement. The current Theorem11 assumes p-adic Schanuel and finite coprime
polynomials over a number field, evaluated at finitely many algebraic-base
exponentials and logarithms. Its derivative rationality conclusion also
requires the specified irreducible polynomial and derivative representation.

The actual source-free U,V here are convergent infinite Newton tails. No
finite exponential-polynomial representation, coprimality certificate, or
required algebraic-coefficient dictionary has been proved. The theorem is
therefore not imported. It supplies neither the requested unconditional
nonvanishing nor a height bound for the prepared quadratic coefficients.
This is a transfer failure for the representation currently available;
it is not a claim that the Euler target is equivalent to the Skolem problem
or that its proof requires Schanuel.

The later [finite-model dictionary](ordinary/exponential_model/FINITE_EXPONENTIAL_ACTUAL_MODELS.md)
does give finite exponential representations of the approximants after
polynomial multiplication. It supplies no irreducibility or common-zero
certificate. More fundamentally, nonvanishing of each finite model would
still not prove that its stationary valuation is below the error. A
quantitative separation bound with the actual growing order and heights
remains necessary. This observation is an inference from the project
comparison theorem, not an additional conclusion of the cited paper.

Searches for p-adic logarithmic-form estimates produced results for algebraic
bases with integer exponents. No such expression with independently certified
nonzero difference was established at the actual dyadic critical point.
Those estimates were not used, and no global novelty claim is made.

The PDF was read through retrieval; no local authenticated source cache or
PDF byte hash is claimed. All internal computations have separate manifests.
