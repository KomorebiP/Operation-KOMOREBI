This guide teaches the formal constructions and proof mechanisms behind the arithmetic, finite obstruction, polynomial tower, quantitative deformation, and analytic regularity results. It includes exact source excerpts, hypotheses and boundary cases, a complete named-source catalog, and manuscript-to-guide navigation.

The Lean compilation evidence is inherited. Fresh work here reconciles source hashes, retained logs, declaration inventories, source syntax and strict axiom records. Lean and Lake are unavailable in the present environment; no new compiler run is claimed.
