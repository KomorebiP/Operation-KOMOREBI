1 How to read the formal development
The development uses two complementary mathematical languages. Arithmetic packets begin
with sequences and formal power series, then transport divisibility through exact coordinate
changes. Polynomial lifting begins with a finite residual equation, passes to compatible digit
towers, and finally enters complete p-adic and analytic spaces. The transition between these
languages is explicit: a rational coefficient has to be shown integral before it becomes a p-adic
integer; an integer congruence tower has to be shown Cauchy before it becomes a root; a formal
polynomial derivative has to be identified with a strict Fréchet derivative before it supports an
analytic inverse theorem.
This guide follows those transitions. Read the contract before the excerpt: it states the coefficient
domain, ambient dimensions, and hypotheses that may be declared earlier in the source. The ex-
cerpt is an exact contiguous selection from a preserved Lean file. Its line range and source hash are
supplied in the accompanying excerpt inventory. The proof reading then explains what the dis-
played tactics accomplish mathematically. A theorem imported from another module is a depen-
dency, not a hidden new assumption; a hypothesis supplied to a theorem remains a mathematical
assumption even when its name does not appear in the theorem’s axiom closure.

1.2 Reading the Lean notation
Fin d →R means a vector with exactly 𝑑coordinates over 𝑅. In rectangular lifting, 𝑑counts un-
knowns and 𝑒counts equations. In a parameter split, Fin (e+k) →R separates 𝑒solved coordinates
and 𝑘free parameters. A theorem using Fin (m+k) may instead have 𝑚solved coordinates and 𝑒
equations; the distinction matters when a fiber has 𝑝𝑚−𝑒elements.
A →l[R] B is a linear map, while A →L[R] B is a continuous linear map. An arrow decorated ≃L[R] is
a continuous linear equivalence. The analytic derivative is a continuous linear map; a raw matrix
becomes one through the explicitly defined matrixCLM. IsUnit J for a square matrix means it is
invertible in the matrix ring. It does not mean each matrix entry is a scalar unit.

tities before reduction modulo a prime. The power-series versions use coefficient extensionality,
so the same proof mechanism acts on PowerSeries R.
