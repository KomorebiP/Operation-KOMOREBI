# Code of Conduct

Discussion should be precise, evidence-based, and focused on the mathematics,
software, or documentation. Criticism of claims is expected; personal attacks,
harassment, intimidation, and deliberate misrepresentation are not.

Reviewers should distinguish a proof objection from a novelty objection, a
build failure from a mathematical failure, and disagreement over exposition
from an error in the theorem. Maintainers may remove content or participation
that prevents constructive technical review.
