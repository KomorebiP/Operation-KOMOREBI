\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:publication-conclusion}

The paper establishes a finite, typed, and auditable form of stratified universality.  Its common object is not one formula and not one global coordinate system.  It is a resolved syntax together with profile-preserving realizations, normalized local charts, explicitly typed bridges, and a descent doctrine.

The technical results follow a single proof architecture.  Packet and refinement data are made literal in the additive-Karoubi envelope.  Representation groupoids are separated from information-losing transformations.  Bridge passports identify what is preserved, reflected, or forgotten.  Holonomy, cokernel, Brauer, and higher-coherence defects record different failures of global trivialization.  Effective descent turns compatible local presentations into global semantic objects.  Compatible native invariants then descend and become complete on the declared normalized components.

The finite profiled Universal Lane Transport Theorem isolates the algebraic mechanism inside this architecture.  Complete finite packet systems give exact analysis and synthesis.  Every morphism becomes a unique lane-to-lane block matrix, composition becomes contraction over intermediate addresses, and certified coarsening aggregates fine blocks without losing the underlying morphism.  A selector separates retained transport from its residual.  Additional additive, multilinear, abelian, or fold structure contributes only the corresponding printed clause.  The holonomy-twisted extension shows that chart loops may permute lane labels without destroying this calculus: matrices then form a local system, descended morphisms are exactly its compatible sections, object-lane orbit sums give the finest ordinary global coarsening, and pair orbits carry exact path-count composition.  Factorial residue lanes provide a concrete arithmetic realization, while the other specializations retain their native hypotheses rather than being identified indiscriminately.

The resulting theory is neither storage universality nor a claim that all mathematical theories are categorically equivalent.  It combines an exact finite lane calculus, including its finite holonomy-twisted form, with a relative classification theorem for a finite certified atlas.  This is the stable foundation for extension: new strata or lane doctrines can be added only by supplying their normalizations, transport laws, effectivity proofs, invariant comparisons, and exact claim boundaries.  Historical novelty remains a separate literature and peer-review question.
