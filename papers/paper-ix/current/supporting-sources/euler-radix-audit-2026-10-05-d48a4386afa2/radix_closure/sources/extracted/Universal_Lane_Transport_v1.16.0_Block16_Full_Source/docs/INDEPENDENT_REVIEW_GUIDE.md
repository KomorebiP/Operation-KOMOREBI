# Independent mathematical review guide

A useful review separates formal correctness, application hypotheses,
classical antecedents, novelty, and exposition.

## Finite-profile theorem audit

1. Verify that every lane system supplies orthogonal split idempotents summing
   to the identity.
2. Check both analysis-synthesis composites and their domains.
3. Verify uniqueness of matrix entries using the chosen projections and
   inclusions.
4. Confirm that composition uses the same intermediate resolution.
5. Confirm that refinement is a sum over all descendant endpoint pairs, not an
   endpoint sample.
6. Check the Boolean selector identities and the three-term residual formula.
7. Verify every functorial, multilinear, kernel, and fold conclusion against
   its explicit preservation hypotheses.

## Holonomy-twisted theorem audit

1. Check the identity and cocycle laws for chart functors, descent
   isomorphisms, address bijections, and splitting comparisons.
2. Verify the two displayed splitting-intertwining equations.
3. Derive the matrix transition map and check its source and target types.
4. Verify that analysis of a descended morphism is a compatible section.
5. Verify that chartwise synthesis of a compatible section satisfies descent.
6. Check that chartwise matrix contraction commutes with transition.
7. For the base-fiber normal form, prove independence of the chosen path using
   loop invariance and prove surjectivity by transport from the base chart.
8. For object-orbit descent, prove path independence of each orbit subset.
9. Prove the finest-coarsening statement only within the Boolean algebra of
   sums of the declared projectors.
10. Use the simultaneous loop image on source-target pairs; do not replace it
    by an unjustified product of separate monodromy groups.
11. Verify that orbital structure constants are independent of the selected
    representative in the output pair orbit.
12. In the cyclotomic example, distinguish Galois character orbits from
    coefficient-residue packets.

## Computational audit

The verifier is deterministic. Review the frozen action families, exact orbit
partition routines, pair-orbit basis tests, product counts, selector negative
controls, refinement diagrams, Ramanujan-sum formulas, and source-contract
checks. Confirm that all arithmetic is exact and that the total is reproduced
without changing the test schedule.

## Reporting a concern

Use the mathematical-review issue template. Include the exact label, the
smallest disputed statement, the relevant hypothesis, and a derivation,
counterexample, or explicit correction. State separately whether the concern
is about proof, typing, application, attribution, novelty, or exposition.
