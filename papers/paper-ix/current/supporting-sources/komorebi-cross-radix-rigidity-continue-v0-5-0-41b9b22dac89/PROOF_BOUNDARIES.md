# Proof Boundaries — v0.5.0

## Certified

A claim is certified only when its exact statement occurs in the saved `src/` snapshot and its required new module passes the pinned strict Lean gate. `AUDIT.json` is the machine-readable ledger.

## Bell remains external

Bell's generalized Cobham theorem is not formalized here. It may be represented only by an explicit downstream hypothesis/interface. No theorem in v0.5.0 upgrades Bell to a Lean-certified result.

## Integral normal form remains rejected

The v0.4.0 square-sequence obstruction is retained byte-for-byte. The DFT/Reynolds theorems live over finite complex residue profiles and do not imply a radix-independent integral lattice. Any future integral theorem must retain localization and explicit defect data.

## Affine decoder boundary

The equations `AB=P`, `ABA=A`, and `BAB=B` are proved for the explicitly defined projector-factorized affine section normal form in `CrossRadixAffineDecoder.lean`. They do not certify equality with a stronger unsaved root-aggregation operator from exploratory work.

## Positive-radix boundary

The raw-radix lcm theorems in `CrossRadixSectionReynolds.lean` assume positive radices. Zero-radix behavior is not folded into the semigroup by convention.

## Still open after this checkpoint

- the exact characteristic-zero rank/nullity formula for degree-`d` profiles;
- the explicit Bell-conclusion interface and downstream uniform all-radix hull theorem;
- active-shell/minimal-hull sharpening;
- localized integral common-lattice theory with retained Smith/Fitting/radix-local defects.

## Novelty boundary

Lean verification establishes the formal statements relative to their imports and hypotheses. It does not establish literature novelty, publication priority, or peer review.
