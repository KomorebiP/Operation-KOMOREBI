# Proof Boundaries — v0.7.0

## Certified meaning
A v0.7 claim is certified only when its exact statement occurs in saved `src/` and its new module passes the pinned strict Lean gate.

## Bell boundary
Bell's generalized Cobham theorem is external. The saved Lean structure `BellDownstreamInterface` is a normalized downstream contract, not a proof or verbatim restatement of Bell. The checked Bell root-of-unity rationality theorem used for the intended external route is Theorem 1.5 and is integer-valued. The rational-root-of-unity to quasi-polynomial normalization is also external in v0.7.

## Common-hull scope
The common-hull theorem is over complex-valued sequences satisfying the explicit downstream interface. It proves one fixed finite-dimensional linear hull contains every positive affine/radix section and that this hull is positive-section invariant. It does not assert that every complex sequence or every two-base regular sequence satisfies the interface.

## Rank/nullity scope
v0.6's rank/nullity theorem remains restricted to the saved finite complex degree-bounded projector-factorized affine section normal form; it is not identified with an unsaved stronger root-aggregation operator.

## Integral boundary
The naive base-independent integral normal form remains false and rejected. Future integral work must retain localized Smith/Fitting/radix-local defects.

## Novelty boundary
Lean certification establishes the formal statements relative to imports and explicit hypotheses. It does not establish literature novelty, publication priority, or peer review.
