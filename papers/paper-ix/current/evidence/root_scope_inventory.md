## Publication claim boundary

- Nishibuchi Conjecture3.9 is solved by the main congruence development; it is not universal root simplicity.
- Native13.2 concerns presentation invariance of an intrinsic obstruction object; Native13.3 concerns compatibility, completion, and global descent. Neither is claimed completed in general.
- Exact classical tails at every depth have a finite prepared polynomial. Explicit classification of every such polynomial and universal simplicity remain open.
- Regular walls and the two stated infinite multiplier families have proofs/certificates within their hypotheses. Necessary frontier residues do not imply existence of an exact singular ordinary index.
- Actual stationary approximation reaches unbounded precision. It does not prove a passing strict test at some depth for every index.
- Preparation coefficients and the actual critical point are not assumed rational/algebraic.
- Historical v26 cubic formalization receipts are retained as prior evidence. They do not formalize the latest all-depth root continuation or constitute a new build here.
