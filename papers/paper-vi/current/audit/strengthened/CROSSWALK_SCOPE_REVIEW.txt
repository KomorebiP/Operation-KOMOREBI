FOUR ADDITIONAL RECORDS REQUIRED BY SCOPE COMPARISON

1. thm:packet_convolution. Earlier hypothesis is an arbitrary strongly continuous semigroup; a bounded generator is not assumed. Part II had stated its theorem for a bounded generator. The earlier proof works without generator differentiation: insert the finite resolution between T(s) and T(t), then on both sides to reconstruct T(t). Retain the earlier record and its strongly continuous setup.

2. prop:packet_resolvent. Earlier scope again permits an unbounded generator of a strongly continuous semigroup, with Re z above a semigroup growth bound. The integral should be interpreted after applying the operator to x in X: an X-valued Bochner integral, not automatically a B(X)-norm Bochner integral. Bounded packet projections commute with this integral. The general Laplace resolvent theorem was authenticated in Engel–Nagel, One-Parameter Semigroups for Linear Evolution Equations, Chapter II Theorem1.10(ii)–(iii), printed pp55–56 (PDF71–72), at the author-hosted URL already recorded in INITIAL_SOURCE_AUDIT.txt. The theorem and proof were read. The source uses improper Riemann integration on each vector; under its exponential bound, continuous vector integrands are also absolutely Bochner integrable. This is an exact translation, not an extra regularity assumption on operator-norm continuity.

3. cor:fractional_low_mode_dispersion. The earlier statement supplies an explicit all-discrete-mode coefficient:
   0 <= (k*pi)^(2*alpha) - lambda_(k,h)^alpha
     <= alpha*(4*k^2)^(alpha-1)*(k*pi)^4*h^2/12,
   0<alpha<=1, 1<=k<=n, h=1/(n+1).
   The corresponding exponential discrepancy is at most t times this bound. Part II's fixed-k O(h^2) statement did not preserve this coefficient. Proof checked: sin x >=2x/pi on [0,pi/2] gives lambda_(k,h)>=4k^2; the decreasing derivative alpha*x^(alpha-1) and the existing ordinary eigenvalue bound yield the coefficient. The exponential map is t-Lipschitz on the nonnegative line. Retain this complete corollary and proof with the grid setup.

4. thm:memory_compression_stability. Earlier bounded-A estimate is
   sup_[0,T] ||x-xtilde|| <= M*T*delta*exp((||A||+k)*T).
   Substituting exp(tA) into Part II's general mild-semigroup estimate gives a different and usually looser constant. Thus the earlier quantitative result is not literally preserved by that crosswalk destination. Proof checked: integrate the difference equation; Fubini bounds the memory difference by k times the integral of the solution discrepancy plus M*t*delta. Gronwall gives the stated estimate. Retain the full theorem/proof with bounded A, continuous solutions, integrable kernels and common forcing/initial value.
