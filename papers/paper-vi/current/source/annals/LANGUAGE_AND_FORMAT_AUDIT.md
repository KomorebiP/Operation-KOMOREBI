# Paper VI Language and Format Audit

## Language pass

The article was rewritten as a continuous mathematical paper rather than a project report. Section openings explain the purpose of each construction before introducing notation. Five short examples were retained because they clarify transfer order, lumpability, likelihood loss, hidden memory, and nonconstant hazard.

The prose avoids repeated summary boxes, promotional language, internal completion terminology, and claims of novelty inside theorem statements.

## Punctuation checks

- Semicolons in the LaTeX source: 0
- Semicolons in extracted PDF text: 0
- Visible ASCII hyphens in extracted PDF text: 0
- Forced page breaks: 0
- Forced prose line breaks: 0

Mathematical minus signs and en dashes in page ranges are preserved as mathematical or typographic symbols.

## Journal structure

The manuscript contains a self contained abstract, introduction, main result summary, theorem sections, limitations, conclusion, disclosure, and a complete cited bibliography.

## Remaining typesetting messages

There are no overfull boxes, undefined references, undefined citations, or duplicate labels. Six minor underfull line warnings remain because automatic word hyphenation is suppressed. They do not produce visible clipping or overlap. The journal class also emits its standard internal `cmtiup` package warning.
