# Annals Submission Checklist for Paper VI

## Present in the package

- English manuscript
- PDF submission file
- Complete LaTeX source
- BibTeX bibliography
- Self contained abstract of 180 words
- Complete references with article titles and page ranges
- Every bibliography entry cited in the manuscript
- Twelve numbered and captioned vector PDF figures
- No forced page breaks
- No forced prose line breaks
- Location specific language model disclosure
- Verification code and machine readable results
- Proof, language, build, and mathematical audits

## Author actions before submission

- Independently verify every theorem and proof
- Complete a specialist novelty and priority review
- Check every bibliographic record against the final source
- Add a correspondence address and email if desired
- Decide whether the figures should remain in the initial submission
- Confirm that the disclosure satisfies the journal policy current on the submission date
- Submit the PDF or an arXiv identifier through the journal procedure

## Boundary

Formatting and internal verification do not establish correctness, novelty, or suitability for acceptance.
