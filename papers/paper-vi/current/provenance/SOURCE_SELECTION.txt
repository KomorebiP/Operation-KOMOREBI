PAPER VI — SOURCE SELECTION FOR THE OCTOBER 5, 2026 EDITION

The recovered file search returned the complete v0.5.0 release with its visual
appendix, the fifth-block formal release and standalone PDFs, the strengthened
Annals edition and its package, and the earlier Annals edition and its package.
The search result had no continuation cursor. No later Paper VI revision was
identified in that search.

   Extracted source/complete: 609 files, including the 534-file snapshot.

2. PSC_Paper_VI_Strengthened_Annals_Edition_Package.zip
   Earlier broader mathematical branch, August 2026.

   Extracted source/strengthened: 40 files.

3. PSC_Paper_VI_Annals_Human_Edition_Package.zip
   Earlier Annals branch, August 2026.
   Compared at statement/proof level because the strengthened branch did not
   retain every earlier result. Needed results are explicitly reconciled in
   the final traditional edition rather than assumed to be superseded.
   Extracted source/annals: 90 files.

All 739 extracted files are retained byte-for-byte, together with the three
archives. Checksums and exact inventories are in RECOVERED_ARCHIVES.json and
RECOVERED_SOURCE_MANIFEST.json. Standalone duplicate PDFs and the contained
fifth-block release were not downloaded again merely to duplicate their files.

The holomorphic operator-substitution material deliberately transferred out
of Paper IV is also included, with its original excerpt and source hash in
editable/traditional/source/PAPER_IV_MIGRATION_PROVENANCE.json. Paper IV's

This is a reconciliation of the recovered project sources. It is not a claim
to have searched every external repository or independently established all
