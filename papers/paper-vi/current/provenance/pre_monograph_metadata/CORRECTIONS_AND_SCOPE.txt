PAPER VI — FINAL EDITION CORRECTIONS AND SCOPE

SOURCE RECONCILIATION
The later v0.5.0 manuscript and the broader strengthened manuscript have
different scopes. Both are retained. Every earlier Annals result is accounted
for in a semantic crosswalk; material not fully subsumed is retained with its
proof and required setup. The crosswalk distinguishes actual hypotheses and
quantitative constants rather than treating a matching topic title as proof
that one result contains another.

The resulting Part III preserves general C0-semigroup statements, reversibility
and Dirichlet descent, exact coarse filtering, potential perturbations, explicit
fractional low-mode coefficients, sharper bounded-generator memory estimates,
and other earlier-only results. Five earlier examples are also retained.
The holomorphic operator-substitution section transferred from Paper IV is
received here with provenance. Paper IV's closed files are not changed.

The exact result, proof, example and other statement counts are recorded in
EDITION.json and editable/traditional/PROOF_PRESERVATION.json. These are record
counts with conceptual overlap, not a count of independent new results.
All 739 recovered source files and all three source archives remain unchanged.
Corrections apply only to final editable copies.

MATHEMATICAL CLARIFICATIONS AND REPAIRS
- Varying-space fractional defects have summable operator norms. This does
  not assert a sum of operators living in unrelated Hom spaces; the inherited
  formal appendix already made the norm interpretation explicit.
- Holomorphic contour formulas specify admissibility, common resolvent set
  and unit winding about the relevant spectra.
- The finite Cayley–Hamilton moment criterion states finite hidden dimension.
- Memory L1 estimates require a positive decay rate.
- The bounded Banach-generator memory transform receives a full spectral
  growth argument, with the zero hidden space handled separately.
- Nonnegative killing alone gives a sub-Markov chain and need not imply
  transience. Eventual-cause inverse formulas explicitly assume stability.
- Inverse limits used as vector spaces have bounded linear bonding maps.
- Auxiliary-state realization preserves intermediate rectangular dimensions.
- Volterra estimates specify their positive finite horizon, strongly
  continuous semigroup, Bochner forcing and continuous mild solutions.
- Rational Laplace realizations state the ordinary kernel class and distinguish
  pointwise assertions for continuous representatives from almost-everywhere
  uniqueness for general integrable kernels.
- In autonomous retained closure, the candidate generator is forced to equal
  the retained block by differentiation at time zero.
- The retained survival transform keeps its complex half-plane scope.

The exact old/new text and reasons are in
editable/traditional/EDITORIAL_CHANGES.json. Separate raw-source audits and
final correction validations are in audit/. Final validations identify the
actual source revisions reviewed; original audit findings are not erased.

EXTERNAL INPUTS AND FORMAL BOUNDARY
The migrated bounded Banach holomorphic calculus is broader than the finite
matrix jet construction of core 15.1. Its contour, algebra-homomorphism and
composition inputs were checked against Michael Taylor's author-hosted
Lectures on Banach Algebras, Appendix A. The bounded-generator growth input
was checked against Engel–Nagel, Chapter IV, Corollary 3.12(iv), in the
author-hosted book. The audit records give exact locators and limitations.
No full external copyrighted book is redistributed in this package.

The formal denominator remains 33 scoped core results supported by 341 explicit
Lean theorem declarations in 47 production proof modules. The inherited
September 6 compiler and axiom evidence is preserved; it was not rerun here.
The current source and numerical checks do not substitute for a kernel run.
The scope of manual formal-interface review is identified in the audit reports,
and is not represented as an exhaustive semantic audit of every declaration.

The three broad core conjectures remain open. Finite, bounded, common-gap,
fixed-partition and finite-state results retain their stated restrictions.
This finalization makes no claim of novelty, priority, unrestricted continuum
generation, unbounded-generator convergence or globally optimal nonnormal
model reduction. Primary-block optimization concerns the stated certificate.
