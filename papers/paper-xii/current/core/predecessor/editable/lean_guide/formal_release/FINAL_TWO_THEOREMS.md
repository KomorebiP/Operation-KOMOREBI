## 3.1. Completed tensor quotient

Use the paper's finite family of complete Noetherian local O-algebras R_i,
with the declared common residue field, and closed local-condition ideals I_i.
Write

    Rloc = completed tensor over O of the R_i,
    J = closure of the ideal generated by all canonical images of the I_i.

There is a canonical O-algebra equivalence

    completed tensor over O of (R_i / I_i)  ≃  Rloc / J.

Both maps are continuous for the actual completed/quotient topologies, and the
equivalence sends the class of every factor element to its canonical quotient
image. The completed algebras are constructed, not supplied as interface data.
The raw tensor ring need not be assumed local or Noetherian for the general
comparison. The manuscript hypotheses supply the finite-generation and local
structure used in its specialization.

The reusable topological result is

    completion(S) / closure(I * completion(S))  ≃  completion(S / I),

for the countably based additive uniform setting used by adic rings. The
quotient S/I has the quotient topology; its identification with the image-ideal
adic topology is proved. The quotient of the completion is proved complete.
The two comparison maps are constructed and their inverse laws proved by
continuity and density. The supplementary theorem `exact_completed_kernel`
proves equality of the canonical completed-map kernel with the stated closed
extended ideal.

## 3.3. Completed global base change for simultaneous local conditions

Let B = R-square be the supplied complete global ring, equipped with the
continuous local structural map Rloc -> B. Construct the actual condition ring
C = completed tensor of (R_i / I_i), and then the actual completed tensor

    Rtau = B completed-tensor-over-Rloc C.

The identification C ≃ Rloc/J is proved by Claim 3.1, not assumed anew.
For every continuous local O-algebra point p : B -> P into a complete Hausdorff
target, the following are equivalent:

    There is a unique continuous local O-algebra point g : Rtau -> P
    whose composite with the canonical global map equals p.

    For every i, the original local restriction of p kills I_i.

The result is natural in coefficient-preserving target maps. The actual global
ring is bicontinuously identified with

    B / closure(J * B).

This proves surjectivity of the canonical global map. A factor of a local point
is consequently local. The Noetherian/common-residue/local-map specialization
derives the finite-generation conditions and structural ideal bound internally.

## Edge cases and retained assumptions

An empty tensor family retains the base ring's adic topology. A unit-ideal
condition gives the zero ring; the quotient comparison still holds, but no
nonzero local-ring-with-residue-field claim is made for that case. Point targets
retain their completeness and Hausdorff assumptions. Existence and
representability of the arithmetic deformation problem remain supplied input.
The results do not create residual automorphic occupancy, classicality, attached
Galois representations, or universal companions.
