# Paper XII: literal-original, revised, and final claim crosswalk

The preserved release's eight amended claim numbers are **6.12, 9.4, 9.5, 9.6, 10.5, 12.2, 12.4, and 12.6**. These are theorem-number locators, not a claim that the original wordings were retrospectively proved. The table records independently checked mathematical reasons for keeping their contracts separate.

| Claim | Inherited original compiler label | Original issue | Revised/final contract | Independent primary verdict for original literal wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.12, componentwise occupancy | Unchecked (the single original not-started claim) | Says each component contains the support; full support on a reducible spectrum has the opposite containment. | Each component is contained in the support. | Refuted, with the fully supported regular module of the node ring as counterexample. |
| 9.4, pointwise realization | Partial | Topology and continuity necessary to extend Frobenius agreement are implicit. | Hausdorff characteristic-zero coefficient field, continuous compared representations, same polynomials on a dense conjugacy union, semisimplification/Brauer–Nesbitt input. | Correct only after a stated restriction. |
| 9.5, locus realization | Partial | Inherits the implicit topology/continuity interface of original 9.4. | Locus restricted to points satisfying the explicit realization and topology hypotheses. | Correct only after a stated restriction. |
| 9.6, derived realization | Partial | Full support supplies a surviving degree, but no inclusion of that degree in a specified classicality range. | An actual finite-projective model interval is contained in the declared classicality range; comparison inputs explicit. | Incomplete, with the smallest missing implication that realization applies to the surviving eigensystem. |
| 10.5, simultaneous completion | Partial | A branch in abstract finite forests does not by itself give all coefficient, continuity, integral-polynomial, and local-condition effectivity conclusions. | Actual continuous quotient representations/classes over complete separated coefficients; finite frame selection; specified integral Frobenius lifts; every-frame polynomial ideal conditions. | Incomplete, with the smallest missing implication from compatible labels/classes to the claimed effective complete realization. |

| 12.6, five gates | Partial | A support comparison alone does not give a compatible Hecke point over the fixed coefficient field; arithmetic family and representation interfaces are implicit. | Actual support inclusion and supplied `y∘f=x`, actual nonzero fiber vector, pointwise realization, two existing common-spine families. | Incomplete, with the smallest missing implication that the original coefficient point admits the required compatible Hecke point. |

The standard counterexamples are exact scope tests, not new proof routes. For 9.6/12.4 a perfect complex concentrated in degree one over a field has full support while a classicality assertion confined to degree zero provides no relevant realization. For 12.6, scalar extension `Q → Q(i)` preserves full support but does not extend the identity `Q → Q` to `Q(i) → Q`.

The tensor theorem 3.1 was not changed in statement between the original and raw revised versions. Its proof was expanded from an abstract functorial assertion to actual tensor, completion, closure, inverse-map, locality, and boundary constructions. Global base change 3.3 likewise has a substantially expanded proof; the final statement clarifies its originally intended deformation-category context.
