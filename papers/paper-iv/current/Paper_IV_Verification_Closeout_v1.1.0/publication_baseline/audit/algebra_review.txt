PAPER IV: INDEPENDENT BOUNDED ALGEBRA REVIEW

Normalized claim and primary verdict
------------------------------------
For every positive integer N, the formal unit series Delta_N(w) = sum
(-1)^n w^n/(Nn)!, Phi_(N,j)(w) = j! sum w^n/(Nn+j)!, with 0 <= j < N,
generate the displayed reciprocal parameter, Bell/Newton, tangent,
residue-section, Appell and theta identities after w=z^N. For complex
analytic powers a fixed logarithm is retained. Under the stated simple-
nearest-zero and positive-integral-order hypotheses, subtraction of the
complete principal parts yields the displayed coefficient expansion.

PRIMARY VERDICT: proved as written, for these reviewed algebraic and
conditional nearest-pole claims. No defect was found in this bounded
review. This is not a verdict on all 68 Paper IV results, their formal
encodings, global zero geometry, or the full final edition.

Domain and conventions
----------------------
N is a positive integer. Formal identities lie in C[[w]] and polynomial
parameter extensions. All denominators have constant term one. Their
formal logarithms have zero constant term. Block coefficients use (Nn)!,
not n!. The reduced variable is w=z^N. A holomorphic power uses one fixed
logarithm on a zero-free domain, normalized at zero when it contains zero.
For the zero-product branch, the standing N>=2 from sec:zero_geometry,
source line 1642, remains in force. Multiplicities are retained in zero
multisets. There is no source claim that all small zeros are simple.

Dependency graph
----------------
1. Defining entire series -> unit formal Delta and Phi series.
2. Formal log/exp coefficient rules -> reciprocal Bell polynomials,
   exact parameter degree, additive parameter convolution.
3. Delta*S'+r*Delta'*S=0 (in z), or B'/B = r sum kappa_k w^(k-1)
   (in w) -> the two reciprocal recurrences. Hessenberg determinant
   expansion gives the same recurrence and constant term.
4. D(z)=Delta(z^N) -> tangent normalization by N and factorial
   (Nn+N-1)!, and differential Bell/Riccati identity using D^(N)=-D.
5. C(z)*E_j(z)=z^j/j! -> triangular reciprocal system. Multiplication
   by exp(xz) -> Appell derivative, translation, convolution, explicit
   coefficients, symmetry and inverse constant-coefficient operator.
6. H(z)=Delta(-z^N) -> theta reconstruction; its logarithm has power
   sums (-1)^m kappa_m (u^(Nm)+u) -> theta Newton recurrence.
7. CONDITIONAL LEAF: genus-zero product for N>=2 -> zero power sums,
   partial fractions, symmetric-function and theta zero-data formulas.
   The algebraic consequences were reconstructed. The product theorem
   itself, its exact external source, and large-zero proof are outside
   this subreview, to be covered by the parent analytic audit.
8. Positive integral reciprocal order + simple nearest zeros + next
   zero radius -> meromorphic principal parts -> Cauchy coefficient
   bound -> nearest-pole and orbit-reduced coefficient formulas.

Write B_r=Delta^-r. The coefficient of w^n in exp(r sum kappa_m w^m/m)
is B_n(r*kappa_1,...,r*(n-1)!*kappa_n)/n!. Multiplication by (Nn)!
gives P. Its top r-degree is n and top coefficient
(Nn)!/[n! (N!)^n], because kappa_1=1/N!. In D*S'+r*D'*S, the
coefficient at z^(Nn-1) gives respectively choose(Nn-1,Nk) and
choose(Nn-1,Nk-1). Both endpoint ranges are correct. In the Newton
matrix, last-row cofactors contribute (n-1)!/(n-k)! times p_k D_(n-k),
with the sign canceled by the negative superdiagonal. Dividing by n!
gives the same recurrence.

give (-1)^n on the right. D=e^g locally and D^(N)=-D give the displayed

C*E_j=z^j/j! gives sum_q choose(Nn+j,Nq) C(q)=delta_(n0), including
the nonunit diagonal choose(Nn+j,Nn). The leading logarithmic
coefficient of Phi^-1 is -j!/(N+j)!, giving the claimed exact degree
in r. Appell coefficients arise by finite convolution. The power r is
applied to the formal symbol before substitution of the derivative,
which removes an otherwise possible fractional-operator ambiguity.
Only finitely many derivatives act on any polynomial. The leading
Appell term is x^m and all remaining degrees differ by multiples of N.

PASSED: thm:theta_reconstruction, prop:theta_factors,
prop:theta_newton_recurrence (lines 1559-1624).
H(z)=D(rho*z) for rho^N=-1, giving (-1)^n for block coefficient n.
The degree of the qth product in reconstruction is at most
Nq+(n-q)<=Nn. At u=0 the product is one. At u=-1 and even N, H(-z)=H(z)
so the product is again one. Writing the log of the theta product in
w gives exactly (-1)^m*kappa_m*(u^(Nm)+u)/m. There is no missing N in
the theta Newton recurrence because its differentiation is in w.

PASSED: thm:nearest_circle_expansion, cor:leading_dominant_zero,
cor:orbit_reduced_asymptotic, cor:secant_asymptotic (lines 2510-2629).
Subtracting every principal-part term removes each nearest singularity.
The remainder is holomorphic throughout |z|<R2. Cauchy's estimate at
any R<R*<R2 gives O(R*^-m); converting to the exponential normalization
multiplies it by m!. Near rho, D(z)=-rho*D'(rho)*(1-z/rho)+O((1-z/rho)^2),
so the leading coefficient is (-rho*D'(rho))^-r, including the sign.
Since D'(omega^k*tau)=omega^-k D'(tau) and m=Nn, every orbit contributes
N identical terms. For r=1 no lower principal part exists, and the
source correctly removes the m^(r-2) error. For cosine the two nearest
zeros give 2*(2n)!*(2/pi)^(2n+1).

P_(1,n)(r)=r^n. T_1=1, hence T_1(0)=1 and T_1(n)=0 for n>0.

PASSED: n=0, r=0. All reciprocal and theta constant terms are one.
At r=0, reciprocal positive block coefficients vanish. At u=0,
positive theta coefficients vanish. These cases do not create a
positive-degree assertion about the specialized value at r=0: the
exact-degree statement concerns the polynomial in r.

PASSED: orbit multiplicity. For rho!=0, z->z^N is locally invertible;
therefore ord_rho D = ord_(rho^N) Delta. An orbit has exactly N points.
The least moduli satisfy R_w=R_z^N. Small multiple zeros are retained
in the product multiset; the nearest-pole theorem expressly assumes
simple nearest zeros, so it does not apply silently to a multiple zero.

Restriction controls (not source counterexamples)
-------------------------------------------------
If the standing N>=2 were removed from the zero-product consequences,
N=1 would refute them: Delta_1=e^-w has no zeros, so its zero product
is 1 and all zero sums are 0, whereas kappa_(1,1)=1. The source retains
N>=2 and is not refuted by this example.
If r>0 integral were removed from the pole expansion, r=0 gives the
constant one and negative integral r gives an entire function instead
of the stated poles. The source retains the required restriction.
A zero of multiplicity d would yield pole order r*d. The source's
simple-nearest-zero hypothesis is therefore essential and sufficient.
