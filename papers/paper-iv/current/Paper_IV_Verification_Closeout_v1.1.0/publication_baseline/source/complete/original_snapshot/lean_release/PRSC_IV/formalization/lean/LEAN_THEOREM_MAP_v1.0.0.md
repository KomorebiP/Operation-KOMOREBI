## Scope retained in the final release

Imported standard fact 11.1, the general coefficient-order criterion, remains explicitly imported. It is not one of the 68 theorem-like results and is not used by the checked order proofs. Open problems, motivation, novelty and priority are outside the formalization contract.

Complex powers use the stated formal series, normalized analytic germ, or a fixed holomorphic logarithm on the declared zero-free domain. The determinant is the paper's specialized diagonal trace-class construction and limit of actual finite-compression determinants. The nearest-pole theorems retain their stated simple-zero and positive-integral-order hypotheses. These are the manuscript's original scopes.
