# Mathematical Summary

The paper begins with the cyclic projectors

\[
\mathcal B_{N,j}F(z)
=
\frac1N\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1}\omega^{-j\ell}F(\omega^\ell z).
\]

Applied to the exponential, these projectors produce a basis for \(y^{(N)}=\lambda^Ny\). A signed cycle produces a basis for \(y^{(N)}=-y\) and the denominator

\[
D_N(z)
=
\sum_{n\geq0}(-1)^n\frac{z^{Nn}}{(Nn)!}.
\]

The finite algebra \(\mathbb C[\xi]/(\xi^N+1)\) and its signed cyclic shift give a complete matrix realization of the signed sections.

The reciprocal powers

\[
D_N(z)^{-r}
=
\sum_{n\geq0}P_{N,n}(r)\frac{z^{Nn}}{(Nn)!}
\]

have coefficients that are polynomials of exact degree \(n\) in \(r\). The paper derives Bell polynomial formulas, two exact recurrences, a determinant formula, and a parameter semigroup.

Normalized exponential sections give congruential reciprocal functions and Appell systems. Their space and order parameters satisfy a two parameter convolution law.

The zero theory uses

\[
D_N(z)
=
\frac1N\sum_{\eta^N=-1}e^{\eta z}.
\]

Two adjacent exponentials govern every large zero. The positive representatives satisfy

\[
\tau_m
=
\frac{\pi}{\sin(\pi/N)}\left(m+\frac12\right)
+O(e^{-\gamma m}).
\]

The article gives the first displacement term and proves that all sufficiently large zeros are simple and lie on the exact symmetry rays.

Writing \(D_N(z)=\Delta_N(z^N)\) gives an entire function of order \(1/N\). It has a genus zero product and a trace class determinant model. The logarithmic coefficients equal the power sums of the reduced zeros.

The exponentially accurate zero locations compare the reduced zero zeta with

\[
A_N^{-Ns}\zeta\left(Ns,\frac12\right).
\]

The difference is entire, so the zero zeta has one simple pole with an explicit residue.
