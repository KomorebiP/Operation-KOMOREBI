# Build Audit

## 1. Clean build

The final source and bibliography were copied into an empty directory and compiled with `aomart`. The build produced a 39 page PDF.

The only compiler warning comes from the installed `cmtiup` package requested through the journal class. There are no undefined references, undefined citations, overfull boxes, or underfull boxes.

## 2. Render comparison

The working PDF and the clean build PDF were rendered at 150 dots per inch.

| Item | Result |
|---|---:|
| Pages compared | 39 |
| Visually changed pages | 0 |

## 3. PDF preflight

The PDF is openable, searchable, unencrypted, and not scanned. All listed fonts are embedded. It contains no forms, scripts, or XFA content.

## 4. Visual inspection

All 39 pages were rendered at 180 dots per inch and reviewed through contact sheets. Dense theorem pages and the zeta continuation pages were also inspected individually. No clipped text, overlapping formulas, broken glyphs, black boxes, or margin intrusions were found.
