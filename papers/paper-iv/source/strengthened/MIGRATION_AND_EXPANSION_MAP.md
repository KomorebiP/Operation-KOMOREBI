## 3. Material added

### 3.1 Finite algebra and matrix realization

The transfer and signed systems are now realized in quotient algebras. The signed cyclic shift gives a matrix model, a determinant identity, a group law, and an exact formula for every matrix entry.

### 3.2 Reciprocal coefficient structure

The article now includes the logarithmic Newton recurrence, a lower Hessenberg determinant, and exact formulas for the first three reciprocal polynomials.

### 3.3 Appell structure

The revised paper adds the two parameter convolution law and the differential operator representation. The classical Euler relation is stated with its correct scaling and translation.

### 3.4 Zero geometry

The article now gives the indicator polygon, zero free dominant sectors, a stronger two term remainder estimate, a first zero displacement, a derivative sign formula, and an explicit first correction for order three.

### 3.5 Spectral product structure

The reduced denominator now has a trace class determinant model. The logarithmic coefficients are traces of powers of the zero operator. Reciprocal coefficients become complete symmetric functions of the inverse zeros.

### 3.6 Zeta consequences

The article now gives exact models for orders two and four in addition to the general Hurwitz zeta comparison.

## 4. Material corrected

1. The root filter domain is now a disk centered at the origin.
2. Complex congruential powers are taken only on a domain where the reciprocal is holomorphic and nonvanishing.
3. The Euler polynomial relation includes its required scaling and translation.
4. The literature comparison no longer makes an unverified claim of being sharper than the general theory.

## 5. Material deliberately excluded

1. Arithmetic congruences and local prime analysis remain assigned to Paper IX.
2. General nonlinear tree expansions remain assigned to Paper III.
3. General operator resolvent theory remains assigned to Paper VI.
4. Large coefficient registries remain in the verification supplement.

This keeps Paper IV centered on one analytic mechanism rather than reopening the full application catalog.
