## Reading rule

This is the normative map for the frozen 25-item Paper II Lean contract: the
21 numbered definitions, propositions, theorems, and corollaries in the main
text; the Smith phase identity; and the three algorithm specifications. The
table below contains exactly 25 numbered rows.

The classifications are part of the contract:

- **Exact** proves the item at the typed scope stated in the row.
- **Stronger** proves that scope and also removes a hypothesis, includes an
  endpoint case, or permits a more general typed setting.
- **Automatic** constructs the stated conclusion from the structural data in
  the row, without taking the corresponding conclusion as a certificate.
- **Certificate-explicit** checks the conclusion from displayed finite,
  algebraic, filtration, inverse, or coefficient certificates; it does not
  construct those certificates.
- **Scoped realization** checks the displayed realization or a deliberately
  narrower algebraic form, not every conventional analytic or arithmetic
  interpretation of the prose.
- **Specification** proves a mathematical algorithm contract. It does not by
  itself provide extracted code, a zero-test, a symbolic solver, or a runtime
  bound.

## Scope boundary

The denominator is **25 mapped contract items**, not 25 claims of automatic
construction. Rows 12, 13, and 25 now derive finite-graph rationality and its
recurrence automatically; rows 7, 10, 11, 14–16, 21, and 24 retain explicit
certificate inputs. Row 18 proves the concrete `ZMod` arithmetic directly from
the diagonal data and also retains a certificate route for abstract finite
fibres; row 17 is a scoped rational-entry realization. The map does not cover
historical priority, novelty, external review, numerical residuals, Python test
coverage, benchmark performance, nonlinear Paper III constructions, or any
claim beyond the declarations and scopes listed above.
