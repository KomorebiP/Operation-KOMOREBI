# Annals Submission Checklist

## Included requirements

* English manuscript
* Submission PDF
* Self contained abstract of 182 words
* Complete bibliography
* Every bibliography entry cited in the article
* BibTeX database included
* Annals `aomart` class used
* No forced page or line breaks
* No figures requiring vector source files
* Language model contribution disclosure included before the references
* Searchable PDF with embedded fonts

## Final author checks

1. Verify every theorem and proof independently
2. Complete a specialist novelty and priority review
3. Confirm the author name and any correspondence address
4. Decide whether the submission will use the PDF attachment or an arXiv identifier
5. Review the language model disclosure against the policy in force on the submission date
6. Send the submission to the Annals editorial office using the journal instructions

## Scope

This checklist records formatting and package readiness. It does not predict editorial suitability or acceptance.
