Three different operations. On the left, all coefficients survive a regrouping. In the middle, an integer weight controls which
operator words can matter modulo a chosen power. On the right, a rational projector needs a denominator cleared before
integral precision can be recovered.
Address resolution
A coefficient such as 𝑥6 can sit in the even packet, then the residue-2 packet modulo 4, then the residue-6 packet
modulo 8. Its value does not become more accurate. Its address becomes more specific, while the full object remains
exactly reconstructible.
Arithmetic precision
An operator word with total filtration weight at least 𝐿vanishes in the specified depth-𝐿quotient. Increasing 𝐿
can make additional words relevant. This is a change in the precision of the ring in which the answer is required,
not a lossless rearrangement of one fixed list.
Separation precision
For the integral involution used later, 𝐸+ = (𝐼+ 𝐴)/2 is an exact rational projector but not an integral one at 2.
One extra input digit suffices to recover the projected difference to the requested precision. The packet is present
over the fraction field even when its integral separation is delayed.
Read this as: Keep three quantities distinct: the packet address, the precision cutoff, and the denominator
weight. Finer addresses do not automatically improve arithmetic precision or remove a spectral denomi-
nator.
