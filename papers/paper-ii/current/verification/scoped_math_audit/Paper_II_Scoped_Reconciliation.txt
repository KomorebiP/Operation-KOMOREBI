DECISIVE FINDINGS AND MINIMAL REPAIRS

F01 — Block-path filtered inverse (substantive hypothesis gap).
Locator: expanded thm:block_path_formula, source lines 685-718.
It says D is invertible on every packet, then invokes thm:weighted_inverse,
which requires D^{-1} in the complete filtered algebra B.
Counterexample: B=Z_p acting by scalar multiplication on X=Q_p,
F^L B=p^L Z_p, one packet I, D=pI, and one weight-one transfer N=-pI.
D is an invertible operator on the packet, but D+N=0 has no inverse.
Minimal repair: explicitly require the assembled packet inverse
sum_alpha D_alpha^{-1}P_alpha to belong to B=F^0 B, with D also in B; declare finitely many
edges with positive integer weights. No proof route changes are necessary.

F02 — Zero coefficient ring versus nonzero packet definition.
Locators: def:packet_system and prop:residue_algebra.
At R=0 and N=1 the only selector is zero, contradicting the required nonzero
idempotent condition. All displayed residue identities are nevertheless true.
Minimal repair: retain the identities over all commutative rings; qualify
the assertion that the entire listed family is a packet system by R!=0, and
carry that condition into the nested-refinement packet-system conclusion.

F03 — Empty gluing and zero-dimensional lattice.
Locators: def:lattice_packet, thm:gluing_weight,
cor:length_and_weight and prop:weight_from_inclusion.
For G=0 one may use an empty Smith list, so a_r and max_j a_j are undefined.
For V=0 the packet family and inclusion matrix may likewise be empty.
Minimal repair: use max({0} union {a_j}) and the corresponding nonnegative
maximum for packet weights, or explicitly state the separate zero convention.
The gluing exponent of the zero module is zero. The existing proof works.

F04 — Precision-depth domain.
Locator: thm:precision_delay.
Minimal repair: m,d are integers with m>=0 and 0<=d<s in the necessity
branch. If negative d were admitted, E=I, s=0, d=-1, m=0 would violate the
claimed necessity: any x,y in Lambda have Ex-Ey in Lambda.
This makes explicit the intended meaning of an extra precision digit.

F05 — Holomorphic defect domain and contour.
Locator: thm:holomorphic_defect.
Require bounded complex-linear C in addition to bounded T_f,T_c. Require a
finite rectifiable admissible Cauchy cycle in the common resolvent set,
inside the holomorphy domain, with winding one around the union of spectra
and zero outside that domain. These conditions justify both integral exchange
and the length estimate. If arbitrary nested positive contours are allowed,
C=1, T_f=1, T_c=0, h(z)=z with circles of radii 2 and 3 gives RHS=2 but LHS=1.

F06 — Linear maps, bounded maps and complex time.
Locators: sec:defects setup, thm:exponential_defect, multilevel setup.
Explicitly state C and C_ell are linear. For norm and integral results,
all participating maps are bounded. Complex t requires complex spaces;
real Banach spaces support the real-time statement. The displayed proofs
then apply without changing their identities.

F07 — Resultant unit/sign normalization (local wording).
Locators: ex:loose_resultant and the paragraph after ex:lattice_dependence.
Under the standard ordered resultant convention,
Res(x,x-pi^s)=-pi^s. Say 'up to a unit' or 'resultant valuation s'.
The denominator bound and every computed separation weight are unchanged.

F08 — Defined-domain notation.
Locators: thm:residue_refinement, def:filtered_algebra,
cor:minimum_path_weight.
State N,M>=1 and 0<=j<N; call filtration pieces additive subgroups/two-sided
ideals so the quotients are typed; define F^infinity B=intersection_L F^L B=0,
or restrict the displayed minimum-path inclusion to finite distance and give
zero separately. These are explicit conventions already used by the proofs.

F09 — One-coordinate Smith existential (uniquely forced display repair).
Locator: canonical thm:smith-solve proof.
The displayed equivalence must begin '(there exists x, p^s x=b mod p^L)'
rather than a free x equation. For example p=2,L=1,s=0,b=1,x=0 makes the
free-x equivalence false although the solvability statement is true.
The theorem statement and intended fibre-count argument concern existence.
