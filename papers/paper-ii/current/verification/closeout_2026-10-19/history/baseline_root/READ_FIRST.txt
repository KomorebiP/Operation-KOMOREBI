FORMAL COVERAGE LIMIT
The general rectifiable holomorphic-functional-calculus theorem and the
holomorphic part of exact compatibility retain open Lean bridges. The checked
construction of actual integral cycles, chosen-cycle defect identities and
bounds does not establish classical integral/length identification or canonical
contour independence. The manuscript theorem is retained at its full stated
scope, with the external mathematical source and exact formal boundary given
in the paper and guide. This package must not be described as fully formalized.

The included kernel audit checks the current local source closure against the
pinned official Lean kernel and authenticated upstream cache. It is not an
independent kernel implementation or a fresh build of all upstream Mathlib.

Directories source/ and earlier verification/ folders preserve predecessor
material and its original dates/statuses. They are not the current release
status. Failed development checks are retained in the evidence history; only
successful exact-source receipts can authorize a current compiled endpoint.
