# Source and Claim Map

## Projector-Resolved Seed Calculus X
### Universal Residual Seeds: Deformation Forests, Transport Categories, and System Completion

**Author:** Komorebi P.

## Internal mathematical results

The paper proves the following within its stated finite, formal, and completeness hypotheses:

- the residual partition of a moduli problem and no-crossing under positive-depth perturbations;
- inverse-limit reconstruction for represented complete deformation problems;
- finite-branch compactness by a finitely branching inverse-tree argument;
- exact counts for smooth formal charts and delayed weighted charts;
- exact chain-projector decomposition of tangent and obstruction cohomology;
- rational seed-zeta formulas for smooth, weighted, and singular power seeds;
- the exact singular count
  \[
  \#\{x\bmod\varpi^m:x^e=0\}=q^{m-\lceil m/e\rceil}
  \]
  and branching dimension \(1-1/e\);
- the directed source-SCC minimum-seed theorem for finite transport graphs;
- finite-depth compatible-system completion under a common algebraic spine, nonempty finite companion levels, finite branching, and closed compatibility conditions;
- the residual companion obstruction caused by an empty finite companion level;
- finite Frobenius separation of finitely many semisimple carrier constituents;
- compatible-carrier summand recovery under integral nonnegative multiplicity conditions;
- exact joint spectral occupancy projectors in a fixed finite-dimensional semisimple carrier, with trace equal to rank;
- a conditional system-completion criterion assembling the proved layers.

## Classical inputs

The paper treats the following as established background:

- representability of framed lifting functors and, under the Schur hypothesis, deformation functors;
- identification of tangent directions with cocycles or first cohomology;
- control of presentation relations by second cohomology;
- Hensel lifting, completeness, compact inverse limits, and finite-field arithmetic;
- semisimple representation theory, Brauer--Nesbitt, and Chebotarev separation;
- the definitions of weakly and strictly compatible systems;
- spectral projectors for commuting semisimple operators.

## Finite numerical certificates

The code reruns exact finite certificates for:

- a three-root residual partition and stored no-crossing edges;
- reconstruction of a compatible base-five digit branch;
- delayed dyadic separation of the sign packets at depth two;
- smooth, weighted, and singular seed-zeta coefficients;
- exact modular cohomology dimensions before and after packet decomposition;
- source-SCC seed rank, independently checked by exhaustive subset search;
- companion forests for \(x^2+1\) at primes \(2,3,5,7,13\);
- finite Frobenius multiplicity recovery;
- exact joint occupancy projector ranks \(3,2,1,0\).

These checks support the implementation and displayed examples. They do not replace proof review and do not establish universal arithmetic or automorphic existence.

## Conjectural statements

The following remain conjectural or externally reviewable:

- universal residual companion occupancy at every coefficient prime;
- universal construction of an algebraic strictly compatible spine;
- universal automorphic occupancy of every finite Hecke packet;
- transitivity or finite directed seed rank for the complete admissible residual transport category;
- unrestricted global reciprocity outside the explicitly conditional criterion;
- historical novelty and priority of the broader seed formalism.

## Claim boundary

The paper completes the formal vertical theory and several finite horizontal completion mechanisms. It does not prove that every geometric representation enters a compatible system, that every compatible system has an automorphic member, or that unrestricted Langlands reciprocity follows without additional existence theorems.
