# Migration and expansion map

## Original article

The original Paper X was titled

> Projector Resolved Seed Calculus X: Universal Residual Seeds, Deformation Forests, Transport Categories, and System Completion

It contained residual partition and no crossing statements, inverse limit reconstruction, finite lift forests, smooth and weighted counts, cohomology packets, source component generation, finite companion completion, Frobenius trace separation, compatible carrier recovery, finite spectral occupancy, numerical certificates, and broad conjectural extensions.

## Final article

The revised article is titled

> Residual Deformation Forests, Companion Systems, and Module Support

The new title describes the proved mathematical content without suggesting universal companion existence.

## Material retained and strengthened

### Residual fibers

The residual partition and positive depth no crossing principle were retained. Formal reconstruction was separated into represented inverse limits, finite branching, and the Mittag Leffler stable core. This permits dead finite nodes while identifying the part of the forest that survives to arbitrary depth.

### Level counting

The smooth and delayed weighted charts were retained. The counting theory was expanded by

1. visibility partitions
2. a nonarchimedean separation depth
3. rational weighted level series
4. exact power nilpotent counts
5. diagonal nilpotent counts
6. exact normal crossing counts
7. branching exponents

### Obstruction theory

The tangent and obstruction packet decomposition was retained. It was placed inside a general cochain complex and supplemented by

1. a universal cokernel obstruction class
2. exact finite child counts
3. packetwise correction criteria
4. complete small extension obstruction torsors
5. an explicit warning that a group called second cohomology is useful only when it is part of a complete obstruction theory

### Residual transport

The source strongly connected component theorem was retained and separated from arithmetic existence. Branch transport and naturality of obstruction vanishing were added.

### Companion systems

The former prime independent seed language was replaced by a common algebraic Frobenius spine. Companion levels, one place completion, simultaneous completion, empty level obstructions, closed local conditions, and exact spine recovery are now stated separately.

### Frobenius separation

The finite trace separator was retained and its hypotheses were made explicit. The carrier theorem now requires a known strictly compatible decomposition, local conditions stable under the relevant sums and summands, and multiplicities within the available carrier capacity.

### Module support

This is the largest new block. Finite spectral occupancy was generalized to finite modules over commutative rings through

1. support and annihilators
2. the zeroth Fitting ideal
3. higher Fitting generator strata
4. central idempotent factors
5. full support versus faithfulness
6. arbitrary base change
7. flat and faithfully flat base change
8. specialization
9. adic completion

### Finite occupancy

The finite projector results were retained, but the trace statement was corrected to characteristic zero. Artinian central idempotents work without semisimplicity. Commuting split semisimple operators give exact joint projectors.

### Infinite occupancy

The revised article adds the tail projection counterexample and proves positive results under

1. finite dimension
2. finite module length
3. compact nested intersections
4. the Mittag Leffler condition
5. adic module completeness

### Conditional completion

The former global assembly statement was rebuilt. Vertical completion and the common spine give a simultaneous compatible family. Compatible carrier multiplicity and module support are then treated as two different occupancy mechanisms. Neither is allowed to stand in for the other.

## Material moved out of the article body

1. Package maps
2. PowerShell instructions
3. release metadata
4. repeated finite result tables
5. internal project status language
6. universal automorphy or reciprocity claims

The original source and records remain under `legacy`.

## Statements left outside the proved region

1. Universal residual companion existence at every coefficient place
2. Universal construction of a common algebraic spine
3. Universal nonvanishing on a prescribed global component
4. A comparison theorem identifying compatible carrier multiplicity with module support in complete generality
5. Automorphy and unrestricted global reciprocity
6. Historical priority for the article's combined formalism
