## Human prose pass

The introduction, section openings, examples, remarks, theorem transitions, boundaries, and conclusion were rewritten as ordinary mathematical prose. Long noun chains were reduced. Vague expressions such as “otherwise complete” were replaced by explicit hypotheses. The paper distinguishes formal reconstruction, arithmetic existence, and module nonvanishing rather than presenting them as one automatic consequence.
