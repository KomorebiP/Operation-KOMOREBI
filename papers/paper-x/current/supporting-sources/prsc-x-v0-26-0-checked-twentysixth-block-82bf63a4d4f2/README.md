**Block 26 proves arithmetic Frobenius separation for representations of the actual absolute Galois group of the rationals that factor through one fixed cyclotomic field.** The common finite cyclotomic factor remains an explicit hypothesis. Full Claim 9.1 remains partial, and the source ledger stays **17 checked / 3 partial**.

Fix a number field $L/\mathbb Q$ generated by a primitive $n$-th root of unity, with $n\ne0$. The actual restriction map

$$
r_L:G_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q)
$$

is constructed through an embedding into the fixed algebraic closure and proved surjective and continuous. Pullback along a surjection preserves and reflects equivariant linear isomorphisms and irreducibility, and identifies the lattices of invariant subspaces. Thus the finite-group separator applies to actual continuous representations $\rho_{\lambda,i}\circ r_L$.

For compatible, pairwise nonisomorphic irreducible systems factoring through this field, the separator chooses distinct permitted rational primes outside any finite exclusion set. Its common algebraic trace matrix has nonzero determinant after every coefficient embedding. Ordinary member-trace comparisons retain the corresponding good-prime condition.

The valuation bridge uses Mathlib's actual decomposition subgroup and its residue-action kernel. For a valuation subring of $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ with residue characteristic $p$ and $p\nmid n$, inertia acts trivially on the cyclotomic field and every factored representation. A supplied decomposition element acting by $x\mapsto x^p$ on residues restricts to the finite cyclotomic arithmetic Frobenius. This proves equality of the full linear operators and hence their traces.

The ideal model supplies the missing absolute Frobenius existence. For each rational prime $p$, Lean constructs a maximal ideal $Q$ of the integral closure of $\mathbb Z$ in $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ above $(p)$ and an actual absolute-Galois element in its stabilizer with $p$-power action on the residue field. The proof derives the continuous integral action and perfection of the residue field, then applies the proved profinite stabilizer-to-residue surjectivity theorem. No Frobenius witness, residue-surjectivity hypothesis, or perfection premise is supplied. Its restriction is proved to equal the finite cyclotomic Frobenius when $p\nmid n$. Actual ideal inertia also acts trivially on the factored representations. The final separator therefore has actual arithmetic Frobenius witnesses.

The ideal and valuation constructions use their respective actual definitions. This block does not identify those two local models.

Chosen global lifts exist by restriction surjectivity and have the same values in representations factoring through $L$. Those arbitrary choices are not asserted to belong to a specified absolute decomposition subgroup. No density of those choices in the whole absolute Galois group is asserted.

The boundaries are explicit. A non-surjective restriction can identify inequivalent rational irreducible representations. Samples can cover one quotient while missing another factor. Distinct square roots $1,-1\in\mathbb Z_2$ have equal residues, so the unit condition on the root order cannot be omitted from the local reduction argument.
