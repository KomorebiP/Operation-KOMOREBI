## Exact source implication

Mathlib `Nat.forall_exists_prime_gt_and_eq_mod` requires a nonzero natural modulus n and a unit residue u in ZMod n. It proves a rational prime p above any prescribed natural bound with p mod n = u. The new proof sets the bound to the maximum of the desired bound and the supremum of a finite forbidden set. The unit condition proves p is coprime to n. No prime-distribution assumption is left as a hypothesis.

Mathlib `IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.galEquivZMod` supplies the actual multiplicative equivalence between a cyclotomic number field's Galois group over Q and the unit residue group. Transporting Dirichlet's theorem through the inverse equivalence therefore yields actual automorphisms at actual rational prime indices. This statement allows n=1; no nontriviality is assumed.

The residue attachment is also compiled. `galEquivZMod_stabilizer` proves the chosen automorphism fixes each actual prime ideal above p. `adjoin_singleton_eq_top` proves the full ring of integers is generated by the integral primitive root. Equality of the reduction-after-action map and the p-power Frobenius-after-reduction map is proved on that generator, hence on every integer. The residue characteristic p is derived using injectivity of the map from Z/(p). Finally `ramificationIdx_eq_of_not_dvd` proves the actual ramification index is one. Thus the theorem `primePowerAutomorphism_unramified_arithmeticFrobenius` combines unramifiedness, decomposition-group membership, and the arithmetic p-power residue condition without assuming an attachment map.

## Classification and scope

Formal corollary of checked primary Mathlib results, with a newly assembled Lean proof. No claim of mathematical novelty. Arithmetic Frobenius uses the p-power convention; an inverse would be the other convention. There is no global Chebotarev theorem here and no general compatible-system separation theorem. Coverage is for one specified cyclotomic extension of Q and its quotient classes, after every fixed finite excluded set. General finite nonabelian extensions and arbitrary absolute-Galois quotients remain outside this module.
