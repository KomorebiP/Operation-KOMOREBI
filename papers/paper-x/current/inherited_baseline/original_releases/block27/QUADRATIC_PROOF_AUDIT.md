# Independent raw-source audit: Paper X Block 21

Normalized claim. Let `q : G_Q → C₂` be the actual quotient obtained by the action on a chosen square root of −1 in the algebraic closure of ℚ. For every commutative coefficient ring B, let `Packet B` consist of group homomorphisms `G_Q → Bˣ` whose kernels contain `ker q`. For every commutative base ring R and R-algebra B, the actual quotient-ring points

\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{R\text{-alg}}(R[X]/(X^2-1),B)
\]

are naturally equivalent to roots of `x²=1`, to unit-valued characters of C₂, and to `Packet B`. For an I-adically complete ring R, compatible packet families over all `R/Iⁿ` reconstruct uniquely. If the positive quotient rings are finite and specified allowed packet sets are downward closed and occupied at every positive precision, there exists an actual continuous packet member satisfying all of them. Over ℤ₂, this specified packet consists exactly of its distinct trivial and sign members. They agree modulo 2 and differ modulo 4. The actual modulo-8 character with generator value 3 has no lift within this packet modulo 16.

Primary verdict: **proved as written** for the seven formal modules under their explicit hypotheses. The final isolated seven-source replay and all independent edge applications passed, with source hashes unchanged.

## Exact scope and hypotheses

- `G_Q` is `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup ℚ`, definitionally the ℚ-algebra automorphisms of `AlgebraicClosure ℚ`, with its Krull topology. It is not a finite group renamed as a Galois group.
- `C₂` is `Multiplicative (ZMod 2)` with its discrete topology and specified nonidentity generator.
- The arithmetic kernel is characterized by fixing the chosen square root of −1. Surjectivity is derived using the nonrationality of that square root and the infinite Galois fixed-field theorem.
- The point and character equivalences require only commutative coefficient rings. They allow the zero ring, characteristic two, and nonreduced coefficient rings. They do not require 2 to be invertible.
- Packet continuity follows from factoring through the actual continuous map to the finite discrete quotient. It holds for the units topology induced by any coefficient topology. No adic topological compatibility is secretly used for that step.
- Completion uses `IsAdicComplete I R`, which includes the separation needed for uniqueness. The separate extensionality theorem assumes only `IsHausdorff I R`.
- Conditions begin at precision `k+1`. The artificial precision-zero quotient is the zero ring and is handled explicitly. Downward closure, finite levels or finite quotient rings, and occupancy at every required precision are hypotheses. Surjectivity of transitions and a preselected compatible branch are not hypotheses.
- The two-packet classification uses the integral-domain property of ℤ₂. It is not asserted over nonreduced finite quotients or over arbitrary coefficient rings.
