The nonisomorphism premise uses `MemberwiseEquiv`, meaning existence of an actual equivariant linear isomorphism at every coefficient index, not equality of labels. A factorization through this one L is part of the supplied data; this is not a theorem for arbitrary absolute Galois representations.
- `PermittedPrime n ∅` is the subtype of natural primes p coprime to n. The additional excluded finite set is a freely quantified `Finset ℕ`. For every λ, the complement of `good λ` is finite. Compatibility asserts that the actual trace at the chosen global lift equals the embedding of the common E-valued trace only when `good λ p` holds. - The matrix has row j and column i equal to `t i (v j)`. The conclusion gives injectivity of the underlying natural primes, goodness at λ₀, avoidance, a nonzero determinant over E, and a nonzero determinant after every coefficient embedding.
- For each selected j it constructs a maximal ideal Qj of `integralClosure ℤ (AlgebraicClosure ℚ)` lying over the integer ideal (p_j), and γj in `Gal((AlgebraicClosure ℚ)/ℚ)` stabilizing Qj. Every δ in the actual `Qj.inertia GQ` acts as the identity in every member. Each final ordinary trace equality still has `good λ (v j)` as a premise.
- The finite family I may be empty. Its empty matrix has determinant one; the witness families and avoidance clauses are then empty. The root-order-one case is admitted. There is no effective bound on the selected primes and no assertion that arbitrary chosen global lifts are dense.

## Dependency graph and reconstructed proof

1. **Actual restriction.** `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup ℚ` is, by the pinned definition, the algebra automorphism group of Mathlib's fixed algebraic closure. `embedding L` is an actual ℚ-algebra embedding. `restriction L` uses the `Algebra L Qbar` induced by that same embedding. `restriction_commutes` therefore states the defining action identity on every embedded element. Mathlib's `AlgEquiv.liftNormal` extends a finite-field automorphism to the normal algebraic closure; `restrict_liftNormal` proves that extension is a right inverse to restriction. This supplies surjectivity, rather than assuming a quotient map is onto. The finite embedded field's fixing subgroup is the kernel and is open in the actual Krull topology, proving continuity and open kernel.

2. **Representation transport.** A `Subrepresentation` is exactly a submodule stable under the specified action. Given a surjection f:G→H, an H-action and its pullback have exactly the same stable submodules: choose a preimage of each h for the nontrivial direction. The order isomorphism retains each underlying submodule, so it preserves and reflects the simple-order condition defining irreducibility, including nontriviality. The identical preimage argument reflects equivariance of every linear map. Pullback and descent of an actual representation equivalence retain its underlying linear isomorphism and work on different coefficient modules. `character` is the actual linear trace, so its pullback formula is equality of the same endomorphism before taking the trace.

3. **Finite arithmetic and system separation.** The finite sample is the inverse cyclotomic Galois/units isomorphism of the actual residue p mod n. The predecessor prime theorem uses the proved Dirichlet theorem to find arbitrarily large primes in each unit residue class while avoiding any finite set. It proves stabilizer membership, ramification index one and arithmetic p-power residue action in the finite cyclotomic field, with prime existence supplied by lying-over. The predecessor separator derives nonisomorphism of members using actual isomorphisms and the appropriate pairwise cofinite good-set overlaps. Character independence is for the actual monoid algebra and simple modules, and finite evaluation spanning supplies the matrix. These are internal proved leaves; no coverage premise is silently inserted into the cyclotomic specialization.

4. **Every global lift.** `liftSample` chooses an absolute Galois preimage of the finite sample under the proved surjection. The all-lift action theorem evaluates ρ at equal restrictions, so it gives equality of the full linear actions for every possible lift. The finite-prime witness theorem is explicitly a statement about the restriction in L. No membership of an arbitrarily chosen lift in an absolute decomposition subgroup follows from this construction, and no such claim is used. `finite_absolute_system_separator` first derives the finite irreducibility/nonisomorphism/compatibility premises and then applies the predecessor separator. Its last clause is universally quantified over γ, with the exact restriction equality and that coefficient index's good-prime condition retained.

5. **Actual valuation inertia.** The pinned `ValuationSubring.decompositionSubgroup` is the stabilizer of the valuation subring; `inertiaSubgroup` is the kernel of its residue-field action. Every n-th root belongs to every valuation subring: if only its inverse is known to belong, use x=(x⁻¹)^(n−1). For roots x,y with equal residues, the geometric sum s satisfies s(x−y)=0 and reduces to n·ȳ^(n−1). When n is a unit its reduction is nonzero, so the domain property forces x=y. Thus actual valuation inertia fixes every relevant root, and a supplied decomposition element with p-power residue action acts by p-power on those roots. The embedded primitive cyclotomic root generates L, giving the restriction identities and full representation-action consequences. Residue characteristic p and p coprime to n imply the required unit condition. This route proves recognition and inertia triviality; it does not construct a valuation Frobenius element.

6. **Actual absolute integral Frobenius existence.** Put B=`integralClosure ℤ Qbar` and G=`Gal(Qbar/ℚ)`. B is the actual subtype of algebraic integers, with its action inherited from the actual field action. Qbar is the fraction field of B. The infinite Galois fixed-field theorem and `IsGaloisGroup.of_isFractionRing` prove that the fixed ring is ℤ; neither result requires G finite. Each element stabilizer is open because that element generates a finite extension, proving continuity of the action on discrete B. The profinite residue-stabilizer theorem therefore applies to the actual ideal action, invariant algebra and reduction map.

   For a maximal Q over (p), the quotient B/Q has characteristic p. Every residue class has a p-th root: choose a representative b in B, choose y in algebraically closed Qbar with y^p=b, and prove y integral because its positive power is integral. Frobenius is injective on the residue field and is now proved surjective. The resulting residue automorphism is exactly the forward p-power map and fixes the image of ℤ/(p). Profinite stabilizer surjectivity lifts that exact automorphism to an element of the actual G-stabilizer of Q. Lying-over supplies a maximal Q above every rational prime p, so neither the ideal nor its Frobenius lift is an existence premise.

   An additional independent exact-source review checked the profinite theorem and its infinite-group instance chain. Its inverse-limit fibers consist of finite-quotient stabilizers satisfying the precise residue compatibility equation. Nonempty finite fibers and the cofiltered compactness theorem give compatible lifts; the actual profinite group is recovered from its finite quotients. Every algebraic integer belongs to a finite fixed subring because its stabilizer is open, so the resulting group element has the desired action on every residue class. This is genuine simultaneous lifting, not a choice of unrelated finite lifts. Details and exact source signatures are in `privatechecks/block26_independent/stabilizer_source/EXTERNAL_THEOREM_AUDIT.md`.

7. **Integral Frobenius recognition and unramifiedness.** `map_injective_on_roots` generalizes the same geometric-sum argument to any homomorphism of integral domains for which n remains nonzero in the target. This does not assume the ambient homomorphism injective. Apply it to B→B/Q, after deriving n≠0 from p coprime to n. Every n-th root in Qbar is an actual element of B. Hence a p-power residue action has p-power action on embedded cyclotomic roots, proving that its restriction is exactly the finite sample. The actual ideal inertia is defined by γ·x−x∈Q for all x; the pinned source proves it is contained in the ideal stabilizer and is the image of the kernel of the residue-stabilizer map. Applying the root argument with exponent one proves restriction kills this actual inertia, then proves every factored representation kills it.

8. **Assembly.** The separator first chooses the rational primes using the finite cyclotomic result. For each chosen prime it independently constructs an actual ideal and Frobenius element by the absolute integral existence theorem. Recognition proves its restriction equals the finite sample. The all-lift trace identity and the ideal-inertia theorem give the remaining clauses. The construction is attached to ideals above the selected rational primes. It does not require identifying these ideals with a separately chosen valuation subring or with the separately constructed predecessor finite-prime witness.

## Remaining implication and strongest safe conclusion

The restricted main theorem constructs actual arithmetic Frobenius witnesses and an unramified separator for systems with the supplied common cyclotomic factorization. The earlier missing global-lift existence is resolved in the actual integral-ideal model. Only its separately defined valuation model lacks a proved comparison/existence bridge; this does not obstruct the final ideal-model theorem.

For full source Claim 9.1, the unproved distribution step is arithmetic coverage, surviving every finite deletion, for the finite quotients needed by general compatible irreducible systems. Constructing one absolute Frobenius above every rational prime does not prove those primes realize every required quotient conjugacy class. The C₂×C₂ boundary verifies why coverage of the present cyclotomic quotient cannot supply that step. The cheapest decisive next research obligation is an exact arithmetic coverage theorem for the next desired non-cyclotomic finite quotient, attached to the already constructed absolute ideal Frobenius elements; there is no need to repeat this block's restriction, root-reduction or separator arguments.
