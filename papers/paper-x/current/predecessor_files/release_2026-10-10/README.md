## What this edition finishes

The edition reconciles historical and current claim status, corrects a missing
floor in the displayed digit-extraction algorithm, adds an integrated atlas,
and expands the formal guide from eight to thirty-five pages. The latter gives
a twenty-record concordance, explicit hypothesis explanations, countermodels,
and four complete source listings. The terminal production source is unchanged.

The publication pass is complete. Its inherited **20/20** record means completion
of the **amended conditional internal claim contract**. It is not a claim of
universal companion existence, carrier existence, or nonzero occupancy. The
transported target family and the recovered carrier summand are independent
outputs. An identification requires an additional comparison. The printed
arithmetic terminal source is over the rational absolute Galois group.

This edition performs **no fresh Lean/Lake compilation** and has no external
peer-review certification. Saved kernel evidence, current executable finite
checks, source preservation, and publication tests are separately identified.
