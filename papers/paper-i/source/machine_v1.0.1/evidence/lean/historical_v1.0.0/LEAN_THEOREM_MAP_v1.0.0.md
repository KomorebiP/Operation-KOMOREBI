Author: Komorebi P.

Lean component: v1.0.0

## Reading rule

This is the normative declaration map for the 35 rows in the Paper I Lean
completion contract. `Exact` means that the declaration proves the revised
manuscript statement at the scope recorded here. `Stronger` means that it also
removes a manuscript hypothesis, covers a more general typed situation, or
proves an additional endpoint case. A row is not permission to import any
claim listed under **Exclusions**.

Rows 13, 14, 32, and 35 are deliberately certificate-explicit. Lean verifies
the conclusion from the displayed primary, eigenbasis, polynomial, quotient,
or spectral certificates. It does not construct those certificates from a
bare invocation of Jordan form, Wedderburn--Artin, or the finite-dimensional
spectral theorem.

## Scope boundary

The map certifies all 35 completion-contract rows only at the scopes above.
Classical existence/classification results, historical-priority claims,
numerical sample coverage, executable extraction, and benchmark performance
are outside the theorem-completion denominator. The algebraic elliptic model
is checked elsewhere in the same Lean component, but it is not one of these
35 contract rows and therefore is not used to inflate this coverage count.
