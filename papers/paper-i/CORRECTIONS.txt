   Add explicitly: X_alpha is nonzero for every alpha in I.
   Without this, an asymmetric relation may involve only a zero block:
   take X=C, P_1=I, P_2=0 and R={(1,1),(2,1)}. The supported bounded
   operators are all B(C), hence adjoint closed, although R is asymmetric.
   The printed proof works with the restored nonzero-packet assumption.

   Packet-propagation alone permits a zero cyclic packet. With X=k,
   A=kI, P_1=I, P_2=0 and an empty generating family, the graph has two
   source components but packet-pure seed rank is one. The preceding
   weighted theorem already includes the required nonzero assumption.
