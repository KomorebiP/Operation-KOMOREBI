KOMOREBI PAPERS I–XII
Three-companion publication series
Edition standard, 5 October 2026

Each paper receives three separately readable, jointly indexed documents.
There is no page ceiling. Completeness, readable proofs and usable examples
determine length. Visual explanations support the argument and retain exact
mathematical or computational provenance.

1. TRADITIONAL PAPER
The full mathematical exposition: motivation, definitions, hypotheses,
theorems, proofs, worked examples, figures, references and stated open questions.
Preserve substantive expanded branches rather than shrinking the manuscript to
match the subset represented in one formalization. Fix identified errors with
explicit change records. Stable labels connect statements to both companions.

2. LEAN GUIDE
An explanatory guide to the actual formal project: type and module architecture,
statement-to-declaration correspondence, proof walkthroughs, supplied inputs,
toolchain and dependency pins, build instructions, verification records and
negative controls. Distinguish a proved formal implication from construction
of the hypotheses used by that implication. Scope and evidence date are explicit.

3. MACHINE-NATIVE PAPER
A readable specification of the structured mathematical object and its usable
interfaces, delivered with the records and runtime it documents. Include stable
IDs, typed domains, hypotheses, dependencies, transformation/action contracts,
proof and evidence references, exact worked requests and responses, and complete
record catalogues when useful. Match claims to the implemented operational
behavior. A source record, a parser check and a Lean theorem are distinct.

COMMON RELEASE REQUIREMENTS
- Komorebi P. attribution, consistent notation and navigable PDFs.
- Editable LaTeX, exact figure sources/assets and build instructions.
- Recorded source versions and a crosswalk among the three companions.
- All final PDFs rendered and visually inspected.
- Every new mathematical correction listed with its affected statement IDs.
- Fresh checks distinguished from preserved historical checks.
- No arbitrary length cap or compression of proofs to meet a page target.
- Original sources retained when an editorial correction creates a new revision.

SERIES STATUS
Paper I is closed as the first completed three-companion set. Papers II–XII
are frozen at their collected source state by user request. Their new
companion editions are not complete, and no further authoring is queued.

Final edition denotes a settled, reviewed publication text at its declared scope.
It does not change the status of an explicitly conditional result or open problem.
