## What changed mathematically

### Cyclicity and the two polynomials

For every finite-dimensional endomorphism T over an arbitrary field, Lean now proves

    HasCyclicVector T ↔ minpoly k T = T.charpoly.

The desired equivalence is not an input. The proof works on the actual polynomial module of T. It relates a vector's orbit to a polynomial-module quotient, uses the proved PID annihilator-attainment theorem, identifies orbit dimension with minimal-polynomial degree, and obtains the two implications. On a nonzero space, it also proves the canonical operator-algebra seed rank is one exactly when the two polynomials agree.

The zero space is handled separately and tested. It has an at-most-one-seed presentation but rank zero. The polynomial equivalence still holds there. This is a classical theorem newly integrated into the formalization, not a claim to have newly discovered that theorem.

The old four-way cyclic assembly no longer needs its supplied polynomial bridge. Its other seed-number and geometric/Jordan-multiplicity inputs remain explicit. The expanded six-way statement is therefore still only partly mapped.

### Spectral inputs built from self-adjointness

For a finite-dimensional real or complex inner-product space, self-adjointness now constructs the eigenbasis, distinct spectral labels, grouped multiplicities, polynomial selectors, maximizing eigenspace and matching lower/upper seed bounds. Multiplicity is proved equal to the intrinsic eigenspace dimension. The conclusion is the exact canonical seed rank over the actual algebra generated by T.

Real symmetric and complex Hermitian matrices are exposed as public corollaries. Ordinary complex symmetry is not substituted for Hermitian symmetry. The zero-dimensional case yields rank zero.

These are noncomputable mathematical constructions. A verified executable eigensolver has not been produced. The row-32 mathematical certificate gap is closed at the self-adjoint scope, while the row-35 executable-algorithm obligation remains.

### Conditioned coordinates

The actual continuous-linear equivalence e has condition number ‖e‖‖e⁻¹‖. The block proves both norm bounds relating T and eTe⁻¹, proves the condition number is at least one on a nonzero space, and specializes the statements to the existing literal packet analysis and synthesis maps. This maps the current condition-number definition and conditioned-coordinate proposition.

The current traditional paper already carries the necessary nonzero guard. The older machine mirror does not, so it is retained as historical lineage and is not used to override the current statement. No false "new correction" to the current paper is claimed.
