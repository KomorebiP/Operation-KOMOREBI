PATH ALGEBRA TRANSLATION
Use the standard generators-and-relations definition of kQ: mutually orthogonal vertex idempotents summing to one, and each arrow in its target/source corner. Multiplying the corner relations gives the usual zero product for noncomposable paths. Every nonempty generator word reduces to a composable arrow path with endpoint vertices or zero; the empty word is the finite sum of vertex paths. Every typed path defines such a word. Equivalently, unital algebra maps from this presentation are exactly assignments of complete orthogonal idempotents and corner-compatible arrows, which is the universal property of the path algebra. The source proves the typed path action and actual subsequent two-sided-ideal quotient comparison. It does not claim an independently implemented convolution basis theorem; that extra representation is not required for the stated module-equivalence theorem.
