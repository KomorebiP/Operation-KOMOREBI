CATEGORY AND TYPED GRAMMAR RELEASE CONTRIBUTION

Production modules (dependency order)
CategoryExport, CategoryPackets, GrammarAlgebra, GrammarTyped, GrammarModules,
GrammarEquivalence, GrammarIdeal, GrammarPaths, GrammarFactorization,
CategoryBoundaries.

PSC-I-D-TYPED-GRAMMAR
  GrammarAlgebra.Grammar, TypedData, TypedRealization, TypedHom,
  TypedPath, PathCombination, grammarFromPathRelations, idealGrammar.
  Finite vertex set, arbitrary arrow set, typed arrow maps, actual homogeneous
  finite linear path relations. The result also handles arbitrary two-sided ideals.
PSC-I-D-COLLAPSED-REALIZATION
  TypedData.total, TypedData.vertexOperator, TypedData.arrowOperator,
  TypedRealization.collapse, TypedData.path_action,
  TypedData.pathCombination_action.
  The finite product represents the finite direct sum. Every actual path acts
  as its source/target block, with the path map composed from the arrow maps.
PSC-I-T-TYPED-MODULE-EQUIVALENCE
  typedModuleEquivalence, typedIdealModuleEquivalence,
  typedPathRelationModuleEquivalence, idealPresentationEquiv.
  These are genuine mathlib CategoryTheory.Equivalence objects with ordinary
  ModuleCat as target. Collapse is proved full, faithful and essentially
  surjective. The comparison isomorphism is explicit vertex-range analysis
  and finite-sum synthesis, not an assumed equivalence.
  The path algebra is defined by its standard vertex/arrow presentation:
  complete orthogonal vertex idempotents and source/target arrow corners.
  GrammarIdeal proves the equivalence with the literal quotient of this path
  algebra by TwoSidedIdeal. GrammarPaths ties the presentation to typed paths
  and their finite homogeneous linear combinations. No independent convolution
  path-algebra implementation or path-basis theorem is claimed.
PSC-I-P-ACTION-FACTORIZATION
  CollapsedRealization.span_imageAlgebra_eq,
  CollapsedRealization.seedRank_imageAlgebra_eq.
  Literal equality of the submodule spans as sets and equality of actual
  cardinal-valued seed ranks under the constructed grammar action and its image.
PSC-I-D-PACKET-MORPHISM
  CategoryPackets.PacketObject, PacketMorphism, fiberProjector.
  Object packets are nonzero. Label maps may merge labels or omit target labels.
PSC-I-D-SEED-MORPHISM
  CategoryPackets.SeedObject, SeedMorphism, hull_eq_span.
  Algebraic part complete. The continuous/topological variant is owned by the
  topology worker; this file does not silently treat it as algebraic.
PSC-I-T-NATURALITY
  CategoryPackets.hull_natural, imageHull_le, imageHull_eq_top,
  transferBlock_naturality, seeded_surjection_generates.
  Exact image-algebra hull, full noninjective double-fibre block sum and seeded
  generating corollary. No injectivity/surjectivity label restriction.
PSC-I-P-SEED-SYSTEM-CATEGORY
  CategoryPackets.packetCategory, seedCategory.
  Actual mathlib Category instances with proved closure and laws.
PSC-I-T-REALIZATION-EXPORT / PSC-I-P-REALIZATION-EXPORT
  CategoryExport.Expression, evaluate_transport, equation_transport,
  additive_equation_transport, transportLimit, transportColimit.
  Structural induction over primitive morphisms, identities, zero maps, sums,
  negatives, scalar multiplication and composition. Limit/colimit transport
  uses the actual PreservesLimit/PreservesColimit classes. Analytic, tensor,
  completion or domain-specific hypotheses are not manufactured by this module;
  any construction-specific interpretation retains its declared preservation
  assumptions as the manuscript requires.

VALIDATION
Fresh compilation with the common pinned-runtime wrapper, warningAsError,
autoImplicit=false and relaxedAutoImplicit=false. Exact final source/object
hashes and exit codes are in final_compile.json. All key declarations report
only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound (equation_transport uses propext).
No sorry/admit, new axioms, native_decide or unsafe declarations in production.

Positive boundaries: an actual noninjective two-to-one packet merge, full block
naturality for that merge, empty-vertex path algebra forced to the zero ring.
Negative controls: claiming the two merged labels differ is rejected; omitting
the identity-sum law for a zero projector on Q is rejected.
