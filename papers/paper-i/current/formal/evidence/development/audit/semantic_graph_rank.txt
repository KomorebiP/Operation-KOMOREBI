84 PSC-I-P-PACKET-PURE-RANK-BOUNDS: proved as written
For finite packets in an acting operator algebra A, unrestricted seed rank is at most packet-pure rank, which is at most the cardinal sum of actual diagonal-corner seed ranks. The corner unit acts as the projector.
The left inequality is direct inclusion of admissible generating sets. The upper inequality chooses attained minimal generating sets under each genuine corner algebra, embeds them into X, and proves their union generates by projector completeness and corner_action_ambient. The union cardinal is bounded by the cardinal sum. This works for arbitrary infinite dimensions and arbitrary cardinal ranks, including zero packets.

86 PSC-I-T-SOURCE-COMPONENT-SEED-RANK: proved as written
