Finite source components and actual surjective block propagation

GraphSources defines SCC as Antisymmetrization of ReflTransGen of the supplied
edge relation, hence identifies exactly mutually reachable vertices. The
condensation order is the descended reachability order. CondensationEdge is
explicitly witnessed by an original edge between distinct classes. Source is
proved equivalent to having no incoming CondensationEdge. Finiteness is used
to choose a minimal predecessor; every vertex is consequently reached by an
actual vertex of a source SCC. Source coverage is derived, never assumed.

For Paper I the edge relation is TransferEdge P ops from the declared generator
family, and the ambient algebra is packetGeneratedAlgebra P ops, the actual
Algebra.adjoin of projectors and generators. EdgeSurjective is the manuscript
condition: each actual graph edge admits T in that generated algebra whose
actual range-restricted block restrictBlocks P T b a is surjective.

GraphPropagation proves whole-range containment propagates along every finite
path by choosing preimages under these surjective blocks and invariance of the
submodule. The local variant only requires invariance under pure blocks with
both endpoints in the same SCC; intermediate vertices are proved to remain
in that SCC. Containment of all source component packets then implies the
whole ambient submodule is top, using automatic finite source reachability
and the original complete projector decomposition.

GraphSourceProjection uses zero unreachable blocks of the actual generated
algebra to prove the exact source compression identity Q_C T = Q_C T Q_C.
Q_C is the literal sum of original projectors in the SCC fibre, is proved to
belong to the generated algebra, fixes original packets in C and annihilates
original packets outside C. No supplied compression certificate is assumed.

Six strict-compiled graph boundaries cover empty-edge sourcehood, one-arrow
orientation/no reverse path/source-tail/non-source-head/distinct classes, and
contraction of a two-vertex directed cycle. All four modules freshly compile
with warningAsError=true, autoImplicit=false, relaxedAutoImplicit=false. No
sorry, axioms or proof escape introduced. Cardinal minima and their equality
with local source ranks are intentionally proved in the separate PacketSource
modules; this receipt does not overclaim their completion.
