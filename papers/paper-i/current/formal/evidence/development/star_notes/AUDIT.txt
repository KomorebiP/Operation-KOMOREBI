PSC-I-C-SUPPORT-PATTERN-STAR — full bounded Hilbert statement

Source: formal/PaperIRelease/BoundedStar.lean

The support space is the set of continuous linear maps whose actual ambient
P_b T P_a transfer blocks vanish outside a source-first relation R a b.
The equivalence with the existing algebraic support predicate is proved via
the underlying linear maps; it is used only for sufficient closure laws.
The converse never supposes arbitrary algebraic maps are bounded.

Hypotheses: real/complex scalar field (RCLike), complete inner product space,
finite bounded projector family, self-adjoint projectors, nontrivial actual
range of every packet projector. The nontriviality is exactly the manuscript's
nonzero-packet assumption. No finite-dimensionality hypothesis is added.

Adjoint necessity constructs rankOne x y from nonzero x in packet b and y in
packet a. Its only possible block is (b,a). Its adjoint rankOne y x is nonzero
in (a,b), which forces symmetry. Sufficiency uses adjoint block reversal.

Unitality necessity evaluates a forbidden diagonal identity block at a nonzero
actual packet vector. Multiplication necessity composes rankOne z y and
rankOne y x. The surviving block is inner(y,y) times rankOne z x, nonzero by
positive definiteness and the chosen nonzero vectors. This forces transitivity.
The sufficient construction is a mathlib Subalgebra and then StarSubalgebra;
the final iff asserts equality of its carrier with the literal bounded support
space. The conclusion is not restated as a premise or a supplied certificate.

Boundaries: equality support gives a star-subalgebra; universal support contains
all bounded operators; a single arrow on two nonzero packets is not closed
under adjoints; empty support cannot contain the identity with a nonzero packet.
The main theorem allows empty I, hence the forced zero ambient space. No
unwarranted nonempty-index hypothesis is added.
