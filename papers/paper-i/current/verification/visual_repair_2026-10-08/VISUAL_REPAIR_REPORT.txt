Paper I visual repair
8 October 2026

All ten main text figures have been redrawn to match the clarity of the
visual atlas. The previous chart headings overlapped their subtitles.
Several schematic labels were too small or too close to arrows and formulas.
The earlier monograph review did not catch those defects.

The corrected figures use separate title and subtitle bands, larger labels,
fixed page boxes, readable contrast and the monograph's teal palette.
All ten are included at 96 percent of the paper's text width. The packet
matrix charts identify inputs and outputs explicitly. The shift arrows and
elliptic weights now have clear space around them. The elliptic figure shows
the weighted matrix and states that two applications preserve each packet.
The proof family diagram keeps every formula inside its frame.

The paper now has 106 pages. Its 22 atlas plates occupy consecutive physical
PDF pages 84 through 105. The chapter text and atlas source files are identical
to the preceding edition after excluding the figure width settings.
The mathematical statements, proof text and record inventories are preserved.

Verification
The three pass XeLaTeX build finishes without overfull boxes, unresolved
references, multiply defined labels or missing characters. Every main figure
was inspected as a PDF render and again on its final manuscript page. A second
reader checked all ten embedded figures. Neighboring pages, atlas boundaries
and the references page were checked after the changed pagination. Figure
text bounds fit their PDF page boxes. Embedded labels remain readable at
about 9 to 10 points. No overlap, clipping or malformed glyphs was observed.

Figure data
All original sampled data and finite ranges are retained. The eight step
Krylov ranks were checked by exact rational elimination. The full 8 by 24
semisimple grid uses exact integer arithmetic. The six packet support matrix
was checked from the 24 coordinate periodic shift. Path sizes 2 through 20
and cycle sizes 3 through 20 were checked from numerical adjacency spectra.
The sampled transfer and coordinate matrices keep random seeds 23 and 91.
Their small residuals and displayed norms are numerical illustrations.
These checks do not replace the proofs or extend the finite plotted ranges.
No new theorem audit or Lean compilation was needed or claimed for this pass.

The prior monograph report and its mathematical corrections remain available
in MONOGRAPH_REVISION_REPORT.txt. The 96 exact atlas checks recorded there
belong to that earlier pass and have not been represented as a fresh run here.

Editable drawing sources are in editable/traditional/figure_sources.
The source package retains the original figures under source/provenance.
Current page indexes and the companion crosswalk have been updated.
