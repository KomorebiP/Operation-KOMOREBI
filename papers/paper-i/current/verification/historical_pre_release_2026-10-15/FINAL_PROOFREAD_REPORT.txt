RESOLVED MANUSCRIPT ISSUES: BOTH CLOSED

A. Algebraically closed multiplicity corollary.
   Source: 02_foundations.tex, cor:algebraically-closed-formula.
   Current display number 5.7, printed page 18, PDF page 26.
   The corrected statement explicitly declares:
   - an algebraically closed coefficient field k;
   - a finite-dimensional unital k-algebra A;
   - J=Jac(A) and a finitely generated left A-module M;
   - a nonnegative integer q and compatible decompositions
       A/J ~= product_(i=1)^q M_(d_i)(k),
       M/JM ~= direct_sum_(i=1)^q (k^(d_i))^(m_i);
   - positive integer d_i and nonnegative integer m_i;
   - the actual A/J-action through the chosen algebra isomorphism, with
     natural columns for one factor and zero action from other factors.
   The formula is srank_A(M)=max_i ceil(m_i/d_i), with max(empty)=0.
   The proof first applies thm:module-top, then the existing semisimple
   theorem to A/J and M/JM. The q=0 case is explicit.
   Compatible split decompositions are a printed hypothesis here. This
   local repair does not add a theorem that constructs that classification
   from algebraic closure alone. The dependency list now includes both
   module-top and semisimple rank. The chosen scope is the radical-quotient
   specialization, rather than the narrower semisimple A-only reading.

B. Relation module and free presentation in the summary.
   Source: 05_worked_models.tex, section 18, "The common operator language".
   The section begins on printed page 62 (PDF page 70); the corrected
   paragraph and display are together on printed page 64 (PDF page 72).
   The undefined expression "relations: F^m/N_S" is removed. A finite
   ordered family S=(s_1,...,s_r) and its evaluation map are introduced.
   Two separate, fully typed entries now read:
       relation module: N_S := Ker(epsilon_S) <= Acal^(oplus r);
       free presentation: Acal^(oplus r)/N_S ~= Hull_Acal(S).
   These are exactly the objects in def:seed-evaluation and
   thm:free-presentation, with the acting algebra consistently Acal.
   The empty-family case r=0 gives the zero relation module, zero quotient,
   and zero hull. No claim of finite presentation is introduced: the
   relation submodule need not itself be finitely generated.
