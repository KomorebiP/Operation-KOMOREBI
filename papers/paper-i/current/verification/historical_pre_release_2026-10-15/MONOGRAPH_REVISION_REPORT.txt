Paper I: Modern Monograph revision
8 October 2026 | Komorebi P.

Result
The traditional Paper I is now a 102 page Modern Monograph, including all 22
visual atlas plates. The design uses P052 serif text, quiet sans serif
navigation, teal section headings, pale statement panels, and a linked contents
page. Every final page was rendered and visually reviewed. The atlas plates
occupy consecutive physical PDF pages 80 through 101.

Editorial coverage
The entire traditional manuscript and visual atlas were read. Prose semicolons
and rhetorical or compound hyphens were replaced with natural phrasing.
Mathematical minus signs, identifiers, filenames, URLs, and published reference
titles were retained. The opening overview was rewritten, and explanations
were added where generation, coordinates, cancellation, quotient maps, or
completion needed more context. No new theorem was introduced.

The supplied current manuscript already omitted an authoring AI disclosure.
This edition contains none. The separate project authoring note remains outside
the paper. Scientific scope and formal verification boundaries remain in place.

Mathematical corrections and clarifications
1. cor:cyclic-matrix now assumes X is nonzero. The criterion and its proof refer
   to nonzero primary components, since absent components have multiplicity zero.
2. prop:conditioned-coordinates now assumes X is nonzero for the stated bound
   kappa >= 1. The zero space has identity norm zero and does not satisfy that
   bound. The norm contract now explicitly requires a finite product norm
   equivalent to the maximum norm, matching the preceding Banach theorem.
3. The exact sequence, independent product action, and tensor product proofs
   now use indexed generating families. Each vector still uses a finite sum.
   This makes the written proofs cover the cardinal-valued rank already defined
   by the manuscript. Their theorem formulas are unchanged. The shared action
   proof and glossary were clarified in the same way.
4. A recap now describes the maximum for independent actions, agreeing with
   its theorem. An unnumbered exact sequence guide uses the actual submodule L
   in place of the previously undefined K and explains the lifting argument.
5. The quotient example now distinguishes inherited block indexing from
   support. A nonzero block can become zero in the quotient.
6. The opening directed example explicitly requires cyclicity of the source
   corner before concluding that one seed suffices. The generator summary
   states the finite generation and semisimple quotient assumptions where used.

All 108 stable statement identifiers, 129 main-text labels, 72 proof
environments, and 22 plate numbers remain in their original sequence. Prose
inside two displayed diagrams was rephrased without changing the relations.
Braces were added around macro subscripts for Unicode math compatibility.

Validation
The final PDF was built with three XeLaTeX passes. There are no overfull boxes,
undefined references, duplicate labels, or missing character diagnostics.
All page text lies within page bounds and no page is empty. An independent
visual check also reviewed the atlas and its compact quotient and shift figures.
Fresh exact example checks passed 96 out of 96. A clean rebuild from the
distributed source also produced 102 pages with identical extracted page text.

The bounded independent mathematical audit rechecked the named corrections
and their dependencies in the manuscript. Its corrected targets pass. This is
not a new audit of every proof, an external novelty check, or a new Lean run.
Existing classical inputs and formal coverage retain their own scopes.

Resolved build and layout failures
The first typesetting attempts stopped because the Unicode math setup requires
braces around certain macro subscripts. Grouping the affected subscripts fixed
the build without changing their mathematical meaning. The first rendered
atlas allowed several concluding callouts to spill onto extra pages. Adjusted
diagram sizes, line spacing, and callout padding now keep each plate on one
page while retaining its complete text. Earlier review files record findings
before these final corrections. The final mathematical and visual rechecks
supersede those outstanding editorial notes.

Reproducibility
Editable text, the monograph style, fonts and licenses, exact figure sources,
build instructions, checks, and this review are included. Historical proof and
verification artifacts retain their original contents and dates. The updated
current manifests identify this edition and its exact PDF.
