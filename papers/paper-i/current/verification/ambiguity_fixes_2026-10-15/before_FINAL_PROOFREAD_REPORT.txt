UNRESOLVED MANUSCRIPT ISSUES: TWO

A. Algebraically closed multiplicity corollary.
   Source: 02_foundations.tex, cor:algebraically-closed-formula.
   Current display number 5.7, printed page 17, PDF page 25.
   The first decomposition prints A as a product of split matrix algebras,
   while the module decomposition uses M/JM. J is not rebound in this
   corollary and finite generation is not restated. Read as the preceding
   finitely generated specialization with J=Jac(A), the printed algebra is
   semisimple, so J=0 and the narrower formula is valid. A broader radical-
   quotient specialization is also plausible, but replacing A by A/J would
   change the displayed scope. The source was left unchanged. A subsequent
   mathematical edit should explicitly choose and state the intended scope.

B. Undefined free-presentation notation in the summary.
   Source: 05_worked_models.tex, section 18, "The common operator language".
   Printed page 62, PDF page 70.
   The summary displays "relations: F^m/N_S", but F and m are not defined
   there. The earlier free-presentation theorem uses A^{oplus r}/N_S as
   the presented hull, and N_S itself as its relation module. Either
   "free presentation: A^{oplus r}/N_S" or "relations: N_S" would be
   coherent, but these are different objects. Neither was silently chosen.
