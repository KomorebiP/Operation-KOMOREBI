# Paper VII visual guide: final prose edition

The 42-page guide contains a cover, one route page and all 40 worked visual pages. Their narrative bodies agree with the integrated traditional atlas. All 38 paired editable vector plates agree, and the supplied generators carry the six revised human-readable captions. Drawing geometry and the mathematical labels of the preceding proofread edition are preserved.

All prose, routes and captions were reviewed. Explanations use complete sentences and fewer unnecessary hyphens and semicolons. The final review includes the original-system qualification for complete positivity, the controlled mode-regime explanation, the trace norm hypotheses for spectral dimension, and the V27 title and V28 table repairs. Earlier notation and label-placement corrections remain in place.

`Paper_VII_Visual_Guide_Final.tex` is the entry point. `source/front.tex` supplies routes, and `source/pages.tex` plus `source/extended_pages.tex` supply the worked pages. Run `python3 build.py` with the root dependencies. The isolated builder performs four XeLaTeX passes and emits one PDF through xdvipdfmx. It accepts `--output-directory DIR` and `--check-only`. Font resolution uses supplied relative files or the documented installed OpenType faces.

After rebuilding the traditional volume and its map, run `python3 make_crosswalk.py --refresh-source-locators`, rebuild this guide, and run `python3 make_crosswalk.py` again to pin its final PDF. The crosswalk retains all 80 traditional reading targets for the 40 V-pages and checks their actual printed labels and physical positions. The integrated atlas omits standalone locator arguments.

The twelve extended plate sources were regenerated byte-identically. The original 26-plate generator requires unavailable SymPy and was not rerun. Its changed caption strings were checked against the paired sources. The earlier exact symbolic/rational computations retain their October 9 dates and finite scope. Regenerating a plate is distinct from proving its illustrated mathematics.

Current source, navigation and rendered acceptance reports are under `../../verification/final_prose_2026-10-18/reviews/visual/`. Earlier candidate and edition reports retain their exact hashes and stage labels. The inherited formal evidence and five open physical continuum targets are unchanged.
