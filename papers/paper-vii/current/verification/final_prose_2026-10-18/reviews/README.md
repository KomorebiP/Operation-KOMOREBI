# Source and rendered review evidence

The role folders retain source edits, independent cross-review, mathematical-span comparisons, exact excerpt checks, and rendered candidate reviews. Candidate findings are not silently rewritten as final results. The accepted PDF hashes appear in the final role reports and in `../FINAL_PDF_REVIEW.json`.

Prose-stage file hashes may precede later documented spacing or generated locator updates. `../SOURCE_CHANGESET.json`, the closed contracts and the final acceptance source pins identify the final source files. A report with a pending candidate step describes its original stage. Historical authoring helpers preserve the commands used at that stage and may contain original scratch paths. They are not the current portable verification interface. Use the root read-only commands for the shipped package.

Rendered review includes direct inspection and exact raster reuse. Each final report states its coverage and the comparison regions. Rendering alone is not counted as manual inspection. The source and PDF checks are editorial evidence, not a new Lean kernel run or a universal proof-validity certificate.
