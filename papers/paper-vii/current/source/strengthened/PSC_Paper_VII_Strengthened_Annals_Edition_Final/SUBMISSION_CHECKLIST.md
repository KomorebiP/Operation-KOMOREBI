# Annals submission checklist

## Required manuscript items

1. English manuscript: complete
2. PDF submission file: complete
3. Self contained abstract below approximately 200 words: complete at 193 words
4. Bibliography: complete
5. Every bibliography entry cited: complete
6. LaTeX source: complete
7. Vector figure sources: complete
8. Numbered and captioned figures: complete
9. No forced line or page breaks: complete
10. Language model contribution disclosure: included before the bibliography

## Author actions before submission

1. Independently check every theorem and proof.
2. Verify the historical position of the principal results.
3. Verify every bibliographic record against the published source.
4. Decide whether to add a correspondence address and email.
5. Decide whether the article should be submitted alone or coordinated with the preceding foundational papers.
6. Prepare the submission email and attach the PDF or supply an arXiv identifier.

## Scope statement

The paper proves finite and bounded operator results. It does not claim the continuum field theoretic or gravitational milestones listed in Section 14.
