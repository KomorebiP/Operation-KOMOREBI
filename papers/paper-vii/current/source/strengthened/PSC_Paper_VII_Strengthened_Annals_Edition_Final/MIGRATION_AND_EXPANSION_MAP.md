# Migration and expansion map

## Overall change

The original finite quantum manuscript was reorganized into a standalone article with a clear separation between proved finite results, bounded tower results, imported standard facts, and continuum requirements.

| Original topic | Final location | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Quantum seed generation | Section 2 | Expanded to the full multiplicity formula and Schmidt rank interpretation |
| Cyclic projectors | Section 3 | Placed inside finite group isotypic theory and tensor refinement |
| Circuit packets | Section 4 | Expanded to unitarity identities, interference, and charge pruning |
| Symmetry dynamics | Section 5 | Strengthened with an exact commutator norm and leakage probability |
| Moving frames | Section 6 | Rebuilt around the Kato connection, direct packet rotations, and curvature |
| Quantum channels | Section 7 | Expanded to two sided packets, Choi symmetry, coherence orders, and Lindblad evolution |
| Error correction | Section 8 | Recast as a compression quotient obstruction with explicit syndrome recovery |

| Euclidean transfer | Section 10 | Separated from continuum claims and expressed through finite reflection positivity |
| Spectral dimension | Section 11 | Strengthened with fixed time torus convergence and an order of limits theorem |
| Constraint algebra | Section 12 | Replaced physical compression by the correct quotient closure obstruction |
| Quantum spacetime discussion | Sections 13 and 14 | Replaced broad claims with bounded tower theorems and explicit continuum milestones |

## Material moved out of the main theorem chain

1. Long numerical tables are retained in the legacy and verification directories.
2. Package instructions and internal status notes are excluded from the article.
3. Toy computations that did not support a general theorem are retained only as verification data.
4. Claims about anomaly freedom, Lorentzian reconstruction, the Einstein limit, and a spin two sector are stated only as definitions or missing milestones.
