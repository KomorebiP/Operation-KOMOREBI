ADDITIONAL ANNALS FINDINGS
A-TRANSFORM Annals Section 4, The one dimensional symbol — correct only after a stated restriction
h in ell1 does not make H(z) a numerical transform at every nonzero z, nor does x in ell2 make X(z) absolutely convergent.
Witness: At N=1 set h(n)=1/(n+1)^2 for n>=0 and 0 otherwise. This is ell1 but H(1/2) contains 2^n/(n+1)^2 and diverges. Separately x(n)=1/(n+1) for n>=0 is ell2 but X(1) diverges.
Repair: For h in ell1 and |z|=1 the kernel symbols converge absolutely. For x in ell1 the numerical channel identity holds absolutely. For x in ell2 use L2 Fourier transforms and equality almost everywhere. For finite kernels, evaluation at any nonzero z is valid when weighted input channel series converge absolutely.
Proof after repair: Unit-circle absolute filter summability and finite channel partition justify transform; Fourier multiplier interpretation via Plancherel. FIR extension follows latest result 05.

A-ORDER Annals thm:cyclic_mobius proof — incomplete, with the smallest missing implication
Original proof establishes Phi^N=id, proving order divides N; exact order N additionally needs no smaller positive power.
Witness: General implication g^N=1 does not show order N (identity is a counterexample when N>1). The concrete rotation does have order N.
Repair: Declare integerN>=1. If kth power is identity, its conjugate rotation fixes w=1/2, hence exp(2*pi*i*k/N)=1, soN dividesk. Combined withNth power identity this proves exact order.
Proof after repair: Faithful conjugacy with actual primitive rotation supplies necessity.

A-DIMENSION Annals thm:impulse_generation proof — correct only after a stated restriction
The proof calls c0(Z^d) a proper subspace of ell-infinity without explicitly assuming d>=1.
Witness: For d=0 the lattice is one point and c0=ell-infinity.
Repair: Explicitly declared positive spatial dimensiond>=1, or remove proper. The closure theorem itself remains valid atd=0.
Proof after repair: Z^d infinite for d>=1; constant sequence 1 is bounded and not c0.
