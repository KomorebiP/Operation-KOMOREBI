Required/suggested wording: State that every Q_m used in estimated-packet reconstruction is a bounded linear operator. Add linear to notation and relevant theorem hypotheses.
Repaired argument: Linearity gives Q_m(s+j-jhat)=Q_ms+Q_m(j-jhat). Triangle inequality and the linear operator norm give the advertised bound.
Dependency impact: estimated packet identity; estimated packet norm bound; post-detection reconstruction; estimated Gibbs bound

B | actual false claim if constants unconstrained
Locators: prop:nonunitary_tree_bounds
Existing context: Local displayed inequalities impose no nonnegativity on A_v, B_v. The induction multiplies by child lower constants.
Decisive witness: Two successive zero maps on R along a root to child to terminal unary tree, both with A_v=-1 and B_v=0. Each local inequality -|x|^2<=0<=0 holds. Recursive lower constant at root is (-1)(-1)=1, but leaf output is zero for x=1.
Required/suggested wording: Require 0<=A_v<=B_v<infinity. Strict A_v>0 is needed only when asserting a positive frame lower bound.
Repaired argument: All child a(w) are nonnegative by induction, so multiplying local lower bounds preserves inequalities. Upper constants are nonnegative as well.
Dependency impact: nonunitary recursive bounds only; unitary pruning and additive optimization unaffected

CRITICAL DERIVATIONS

higher_order_trace: For ell<m, truncated-power ell derivative is continuous at tau; for ell=m it has unit jump; for ell>m its side derivatives vanish. Packet derivative-jump matrix is diagonal in (m,tau). Subtracting coefficients cancels every trace through r - 1; piecewise integration by parts cancels interface terms, giving global W^{r,p}.

higher_order_location: Integral in location of phi_{m-1,u}; its Lp norm on (0,1) is <=1/((m-1)!((m-1)p+1)^(1/p)), so Minkowski gives the displayed Lipschitz constant.

geometry: Region intersections partition the domain exactly. Equal-amplitude error is |a|mu(E triangle F)^(1/p). A local tube estimate can only be applied inside its radius range.

deterministic: D_k=a_q+(s(t_{k+1})-s(t_k))+(eta_{k+1}-eta_k) on a one-jump cell. Residual<=Mh+2eps. Strict threshold interval exists iff a_min>2b. Midpoint error<=h/2 and disjoint detected cells let the location Lp errors add in pth powers.

probability: Independent sigma-subGaussian differences have mgf<=exp(sigma^2lambda^2). Chernoff gives two-tail<=2exp(-t^2/(4sigma^2)); n-cell union bound with t =2sigma sqrt(log(2n/delta)) gives delta. Adjacent contrasts need not be independent for the union bound. Sigma=0 is handled as deterministic zero noise, avoiding division by zero.

haar: Smooth coefficient<=ML^(3/2)/4 by pairing half-cell points. Half-line step coefficient=|a|sqrtL min(u,1-u). Bounded noise coefficient<=eps sqrtL. Compare upper and lower for threshold. Modulo L, either grid places tau at distance >= L/4 from endpoints.

best_basis: Finite prunings give an attained minimum; each subtree pruning stops or splits. Uniform pruning-cost perturbation<= epsilon_star implies optimum difference<= epsilon_star. Strict competitor gaps remain positive if 2 epsilon_star < g. Ties/singletons need explicit total selection conventions.

difference: Binomial coefficients have sum of absolute values 2^m; (d + 1)st difference vanishes on a degree<=d polynomial stencil. The breakpoint-band theorem requires a nonzero response assumption and does not prove every breakpoint has one.
