PAPER V — FINAL EDITION CORRECTIONS AND SCOPE

Both source branches are retained. The later v1.1.0 manuscript descends from
the recovered adaptive-tree edition and contains 20 scoped proof records.
The earlier Annals branch develops a broader analytic theory with 63 proof
records. The later source does not withdraw those developments. The final
traditional volume therefore includes the full corrected formal core as Part I
and the complete corrected mathematical developments as Part II.

All 119 numbered records and all 83 proof environments are retained. There is
conceptual overlap between the parts; these are not 83 independent new results.
The original 598 source files and both source archives remain byte-for-byte
unchanged. Corrections apply only to the final editable copies.

CORE CORRECTION
The probabilistic local-error certificate now explicitly assumes measurable
random node costs and a measurable joint good event. Its proof explains why
finite deterministic comparisons make the selector-preservation event
measurable. This specializes the existing outer-measure monotonicity interface
to ordinary probability. No formal source or theorem target was added.
An isolated capitalization error in another proof was also corrected.

DEVELOPMENT-PART REPAIRS
The source review identified real missing hypotheses, boundary cases and
proof omissions. The final copies make the following explicit:

- Bounded linear approximation operators in estimated-packet identities and
  their reconstruction/Gibbs consequences. Boundedness alone is insufficient.
- Nonnegative local frame bounds in recursive nonunitary trees.
- Half-line step conventions and valid smooth extensions on Haar supports.
- The declared radius range for local tube estimates.
- Convergence in probability, fixed finite jump models and admissible grids.
- Nonempty jump lists when taking the minimum nonzero jump amplitude.
- Stop-on-ties selection and a separate singleton-pruning case.
- Independence across sample sites and repeated observations.
- Channel-delay versus original-sample-delay units.
- Sufficient regularity for the imported contextual Gibbs fact.
- Zero-noise treatment before dividing by the sub-Gaussian variance proxy.
- The distinction between a growing upper-tail certificate and actual noise
  maxima, which need not grow for every sub-Gaussian distribution.
- A consistent complex inner-product convention in the smooth-Haar proof.
- Unit-circle/L2-a.e. transform domains and weighted absolute convergence for
  off-circle numerical evaluation.
- The converse needed to prove exact cyclic order, not only divisibility.
- Positive spatial dimension where the bounded-sequence closure is proper.

Exact old/new text, reasons and occurrence counts are recorded in

FORMAL BOUNDARY
The inherited Lean release still covers the explicitly scoped 20 core results
through 225 theorem targets in 23 proof modules. The 63 development records
do not enlarge that coverage. The complete Lean guide explains both the
positive coverage and the remaining scope differences. No fresh compilation
or axiom audit is claimed for this edition.

The two broad core edge-recovery conjectures remain open. Separated-jump
recovery under explicit finite-model assumptions is a valid narrower result.
It does not establish universal recovery, general image topology, minimax
optimality, infinite-tree consistency or a historical-priority claim.
