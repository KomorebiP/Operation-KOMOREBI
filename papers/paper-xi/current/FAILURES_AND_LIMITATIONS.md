# Paper XI — failures, corrections and remaining limits

18 October 2026 · Revised edition 1.1.0

This record preserves the distinction between a corrected defect, a failed execution attempt and an unresolved mathematical problem. Current outcomes belong to the final dated receipts; historical records are retained under `historical/`.

## Mathematical corrections completed in this edition

The earlier breakwise phase display used a full-space expression where the hypotheses allowed fixed vectors. The revised statement uses the actual moving spaces, explicitly assumes the symplectic action, and gives the corresponding nondegeneracy argument. The odd-characteristic formal alignment names the odd-characteristic theorem and retains its `p ≠ 2` hypothesis. It adds no order-prime-to-p condition.

The twisted-algebra proof no longer asserts that every literal complex cocycle comes from a finite central extension. Direct averaging supplies the required semisimplicity. Other corrections specify the `F_p` pairing convention, separate induced carrier dimension from irreducible orbit length, make scalar loop holonomies explicit, and handle rank-one tame conductor and trivial-character Gauss-sum cases. The complete before/after source diff and focused review identify the exact changes.

The malformed Appendix B approximation index was restored from its raw note. Transition indices were made explicit, one broken word was repaired, and the opening preservation sentence was updated to acknowledge this edition's actual proof changes. Bibliographic spelling, epsilon notation, case protection and Tate's part locator were corrected. These are closed local defects, not claims of resolving the general arithmetic endpoint.

## Four open mathematical tasks

The universal H1–H4 arithmetic realization remains open. Full same-candidate H5 remains open. The corrected-character occurrence problem in the fixed Continuation113 candidate remains open. The actual native square/section and honest-phase certificate remains open wherever the stronger fixed-native contract is prescribed. Exact contracts and continuation directions are in `PROGRESS_LEDGER.md` and `CONTINUE_HERE.md`.

The characteristic-zero rank-three Continuation113 occurrence branch is distinct from the characteristic-p rank-nine construction with two cubic stages. The excluded original character must not be restored merely because a corrected character passes a scalar test. The three fixed-center extension possibilities require an actual occurrence argument.
