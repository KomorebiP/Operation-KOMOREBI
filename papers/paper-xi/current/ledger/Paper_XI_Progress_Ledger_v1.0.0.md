**retained_checked:** Prior material retained with its stated scope.
**completed_selected_scope:** The explicitly named subset was reviewed; not an entire source corpus.
**partial:** Some material present, named integration or checking remains.
**queued:** Not completed in this publication block.
**not_run:** No execution occurred.
**open:** Mathematical endpoint remains unresolved by this package.

**reconciled_selected_scope:** Named inherited branches read and reconciled in ordinary prose with listed new bounded checks; not blanket proof or compiler certification.
**blocked_source_recovery:** Required earlier editable source archive was not recovered. Alternate preserved-reader continuation documented.
**receipt_not_recovered:** Production source present but successful terminal compilation receipt not present among recovered evidence. Not evidence that compilation fails.
**synchronized_candidate:** Original readers aligned and rebuilt; final release sign-off not implied.
**in_progress:** Named execution/checkpoint work is underway, not yet complete.
**synchronized_reviewed_candidate:** Original readers aligned, rebuilt, source-compared and visually reviewed. Final five-reader release sign-off remains separate.
**released_documented_scope:** Reader publication signed off after named internal checks. Open research, unavailable sources, inherited external inputs and unexecuted formal checks remain separate.
