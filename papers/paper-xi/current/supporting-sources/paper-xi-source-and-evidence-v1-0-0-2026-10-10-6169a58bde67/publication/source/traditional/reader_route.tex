\subsection*{The endpoint is a quantified construction problem}
Conjecture 66 asks for an actual arithmetic witness for each admitted source with its supplied H1--H4 datum. The comparison proposition then uses H5 for that same realized candidate. Historical obligation 66, historical Theorem 16.2, and all-records row 95 refer to this endpoint's lineage. Historical register row 66 is a different statement.

The \href{https://komorebi.invalid/b07/contract}{research contract in Section 1} and \href{https://komorebi.invalid/b07/contract_atlas}{atlas plate V01} make the quantifiers explicit. Supplying finite operators does not construct an arithmetic representation. Likewise, proving a factor identity for a different candidate does not identify the packet's realizer.
