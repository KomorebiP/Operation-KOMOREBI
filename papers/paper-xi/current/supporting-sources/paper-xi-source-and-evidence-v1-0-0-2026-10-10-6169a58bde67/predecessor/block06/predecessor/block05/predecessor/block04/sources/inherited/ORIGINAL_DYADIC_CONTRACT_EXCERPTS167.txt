                9. A restricted one break Galois envelope
     We now isolate a finite Clifford setting in which the quotient projective
action is defined on a multiplicity space. This avoids assigning a quotient action
directly to arbitrary representatives of G/N .
    Definition 9.1 (One break Clifford datum). A one break Clifford datum
consists of finite groups and representations
      N ◁ G,       A = G/N,         Π : G → GL(X ),       ρ : N → GL(W ),
with the following properties.
(1) The quotient A is a finite elementary abelian p group.
(2) The representations Π and ρ are irreducible.
(3) The isomorphism class of ρ is fixed by G.
(4) The restriction of Π to N is ρ isotypic.
(5) The quotient records one declared ramification break.
    The multiplicity space is
                              Mρ = HomN (W, X ).
    The isotypic condition gives an N equivariant evaluation isomorphism
                                  W ⊗ Mρ −→ X .
Choose a section s : A → G. For every a ∈ A, choose an intertwiner Ia from ρ
to its conjugate by s(a). Under the evaluation isomorphism, there is a unique
operator Ta on Mρ such that
                                 Π(s(a)) = Ia ⊗ Ta .
The operators Ta form a projective representation of A.
     Proposition 9.2 (Clifford multiplicity action). The projective equivalence
class of the action (Ta )a∈A is independent of the section, the intertwiners, and
the chosen isotypic evaluation isomorphism. The projective action is irreducible.
      Proof. Changing an intertwiner Ia by a scalar changes Ta by the inverse
scalar. Changing the isotypic evaluation isomorphism conjugates all Ta by one
element of GL(Mρ ). Changing the section multiplies s(a) by an element of
N . That factor is absorbed by the ρ action on W , leaving a scalar change on
the multiplicity operator by Schur’s lemma. Thus all choices give projectively
equivalent actions.
      If a nonzero proper subspace of Mρ were stable under all projective operators
Ta , then its tensor product with W would give a nonzero proper G stable
subspace of X . This contradicts irreducibility of Π.                            □
