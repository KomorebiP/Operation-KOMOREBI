## 1. Exact local fields and a regular character

Take compatible roots of unity and write

\[
 F=\mathbb Q_3(\zeta_3),\quad L=\mathbb Q_3(\zeta_9),\quad
 \varpi=\zeta_9-1,\quad \varpi_F=\zeta_3-1,\quad
 \beta=\varpi^{-10}.
\]

Then `L/F` is cyclic totally ramified of degree3, generated by `sigma(zeta9)=zeta9^4`. The relative Eisenstein polynomial is

\[
 X^3+3X^2+3X-\varpi_F.
\]

The residue fields are both `F3`. Normalize `v_L(varpi)=1`, so `v_L(3)=6` and `v_L(varpi_F)=3`. Its derivative at `varpi` is `3(1+varpi)^2`; the relative different exponent is6. Let `psi_Q3` be the standard additive character with kernel `Z3`, and fix

\[
 \psi_F(t)=\psi_{\mathbb Q_3}(\operatorname{Tr}_{F/\mathbb Q_3}(t)/3),
 \qquad \psi_L=\psi_F\circ\operatorname{Tr}_{L/F}.
\]

The different exponent of `F/Q3` is1, hence `psi_F` has conductor `p_F` in the BK convention. The trace character `psi_L` has conductor `p_L^{-3}`: it is trivial on that ideal and nontrivial on `p_L^{-4}`. This negative exponent is essential; using conductor `p_L` here would give incorrect unit levels.

Every element of `L^x` has a unique decomposition `varpi^a epsilon u`, with `a` integral, `epsilon` in `{1,-1}`, and `u` in `U_L^1`. Define

\[
 \chi(\varpi^a\epsilon u)=\psi_L(\beta\log u).
 \tag{1}
\]

The ordinary3-adic logarithm converges on `U_L^1` and is a continuous homomorphism there; its kernel can contain3-power roots of unity, which causes no problem. Thus(1) defines an actual smooth unitary finite-image character, without choosing an extension of a partially specified character. The exponential and logarithm are inverse bijections between `p_L^r` and `U_L^r` for `r>=4`, since `e(L/Q3)/(3-1)=3`.

For `r>=4`, the character on `U_L^r` is trivial exactly when `r-10>=-3`. Therefore

\[
 a_L(\chi)=7.
\]

The displacement satisfies

\[
 v_L(\sigma(\varpi)/\varpi-1)=2,
 \qquad v_L(\sigma(\beta)-\beta)=-8.
\]

The second equality follows by expanding `(1+t)^(-10)-1`; its leading coefficient is a3-adic unit and `v_L(t)=2`. Consequently the nontrivial conjugate quotient of `chi` has conductor5: on `U_L^4` it is nontrivial, and on `U_L^5` it is trivial. Replacing `sigma` by its inverse only exchanges the two nonidentity conjugates and does not affect regularity. Thus `chi,chi^sigma,chi^(sigma^2)` are pairwise distinct already on `U_L^4`.

The exact script `evidence/arithmetic/cyclotomic_check113.py` works in

\[
 \mathbb Q[X]/(X^6+6X^5+15X^4+21X^3+18X^2+9X+3)
\]

using rational arithmetic. It verifies16 identities and witnesses, including

\[
 \chi(\exp(\varpi^6))=\exp(2\pi i/3),\qquad
 \psi_L((\sigma\beta-\beta)\varpi^4)=\exp(4\pi i/3).

It does not numerically approximate the p-adic exponential: `log(exp(varpi^r))=varpi^r` in the convergent range is the separate mathematical reduction, and the script computes the resulting exact rational traces. For `u` in `U_L^1` and `y` in `U_a^6`, set

\[
 \theta_F(uy)=\chi(u)\psi_F\bigl(\operatorname{tr}_{\operatorname{End}_F(L)/F}

 \tag{3}

This is well defined. On the intersection `U_L^6`, logarithm differs from `u-1` by terms of valuation at least12; multiplication by beta leaves valuation at least2, on which `psi_L` is trivial. ## 4. Execute a nontrivial tame lift and descent

Set

\[
 K=F(i),\quad i^2=-1,\qquad M=KL.
\]

Because `X^2+1` is irreducible over `F3`, `K/F` is the unramified quadratic extension, so this is a nontrivial tame cyclic step of degree prime to3. It is an auxiliary step, not a claim that the Sylow construction intrinsically requires thisK. The extensionsL andK are linearly disjoint. Define

\[
 \chi_M=\chi\circ N_{M/L},\qquad
 \pi_K^0=\operatorname{AI}_{M/K}(\chi_M).
\]

The same regularity computation over the unramified fieldK makes this an actual totally-wild rank-three supercuspidal. The nonidentity element of `Gal(K/F)` fixesL and commutes with `N_M/L`, so it fixes the character `chi_M` and, by naturality of cyclic induction, the class of `pi_K^0`. Mackey restriction gives

\[
 \rho|_{W_K}\simeq\operatorname{Ind}_{W_M}^{W_K}\chi_M,
 \qquad \omega_{\pi_K^0}=\omega\circ N_{K/F}.
\]

BH1999 Theorem1.3(viii) gives a descent `pi_F` with algebraic tame lift `pi_K^0`. Its central character can be normalized to exactly `omega`, and this normalized descent is unique:

* If `pi_F'` is any descent, `delta=omega_(pi_F')/omega` is trivial on the norm subgroup. It has order dividing2.
* Twist `pi_F'` by `delta`. This leaves its lift unchanged and changes its central character by `delta^3=delta`, yielding exactly `omega`.
* Two normalized descents differ by a norm-trivial character `alpha`; equality of central characters gives `alpha^3=1`, while `alpha^2=1`. Hence `alpha=1`.

By the monomial base-change compatibility in BH1999§2.3 and the equality of algebraic tame lift with base change (Theorem1.8), this unique normalized descent is precisely the independently defined `pi_F^0` of(2). Thus the wild induction plus tame descent really has been instantiated on this example.

## 5. A proved arithmetic occurrence lemma

Let `a_K` be the actual `O_M` chain order in `End_K(M)`, use `psi_K=psi_F o Tr_K/F`, and construct `theta_K` from the same beta and `chi_M` exactly as in(3). The norm/log identity and the actual transfer construction identify this with the unramified tame lift of `theta_F` in BH1996§11. No identification of unrelated finite quotients is used.

**Occurrence lemma.** With the actual data above,

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{H_F^1}(\theta_F,\pi_F^0)\ne0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \operatorname{Hom}_{H_K^1}(\theta_K,\pi_K^0)\ne0.
 \tag{4}
\]

**Proof.** A nonzero theta-isotypic vector in a smooth representation generates a finite-dimensional representation of the compact groupJ1. Semisimplicity and BK5.1.1 make theta-occurrence equivalent to eta-occurrence. Retain the central-character datum in BH1996(14.9): on `N=N_K/F(K^x)`, use `omega|N`, which agrees with theta on `N cap H_F^1=U_F^1`. The corresponding extension of eta to `N J_F^1` occurs exactly when eta does, since the central character of `pi_F^0` isomega. Upstairs use `omega o N_K/F`; the same argument applies to `K^x J_K^1`. The field `K tensor_F L=M` is a field, the parameter has full degree3, and `pi_K^0` is Galois-invariant. Every hypothesis of BH1996 Corollary14.21(ii) is now satisfied. Its equivalence gives(4). This argument retains the central-character extensions of(14.9), rather than incorrectly citing14.21 as a bare finite-character statement. QED.

This is a genuine source-backed arithmetic transport lemma. It shows that the tame stage creates no new occurrence ambiguity once the actual theta and normalized candidate are fixed. It does not establish either side of(4). In particular, it cannot manufacture the missing occurrence at the wild degree-three step.

### The cubic self-twist test succeeds for the actual simple character

This next discriminator was executed, rather than left as a suggestion. Let `omega_cub` be the nontrivial cubic norm character of `F^x` attached to `L/F`, choosing the generator such that, for `u` in `U_F^2`,

\[
 \omega_{\rm cub}(u)=\psi_F\bigl((1/3)\log u\bigr).
\]

Define it concretely by `omega_cub(varpi_F)=omega_cub(-1)=1` and, for `u` in `U_F^1`, by `zeta3^((N_F/Q3(u)-1)/3 mod3)`. Norms of such units lie in `1+3Z3`, so this is a character of order dividing3. The logarithm of a member of `1+3Z3` differs from that member minus1 by `9Z3`, proving the displayed additive formula, in particular on `U_F^2`. It has conductor3 and is nontrivial.

Triviality on `N_L/F(L^x)` is also checked concretely. The uniformizer has normvarpi_F, and the sign has norm-1. On `U_L^4`, the logarithmic formula is automatically trivial by valuation. The remaining quotient `U_L^1/U_L^4` is generated by `1+varpi^r`, `r=1,2,3`. The script checks that all three exact values `Tr_L/Q3(log(1+varpi^r))/9` have zero3-adic fractional part. Thusomega_cub is trivial on the full norm subgroup. The local norm index is3, so its kernel equals that subgroup. This defines a particular one of the two cubic self-twist characters without an ambiguity over whether a chosen reciprocity generator is geometric or arithmetic.

Use the basis `1,varpi,varpi^2` ofL and letPi be the exact multiplication matrix

\[
 \Pi=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&\varpi_F\\1&0&-3\\0&1&-3\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Put

\[
 D=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,2),\qquad g=1+D\Pi^4.
\]

The exact rational matrix calculation gives

\[
 v_a(g\Pi^{-10}g^{-1}-\Pi^{-10}-(1/3)1)=-3.
 \tag{6}
\]

It also checks, for all six elements `u_r=1+varpi^r`, `1<=r<=6`, that

\[
 g^{-1}u_rg\in H_F^1,\qquad
 \theta_F(g^{-1}u_rg)=\theta_F(u_r).
 \tag{7}
\]

Every value in(7) is calculated from the actual formula(3). The script first finds an actualL-unit representative modulo `U_a^6`, then evaluates its logarithmic character and the remaining matrix trace character. The nontrivial representative at `r=1` is `1+varpi+2varpi^5`; all six resulting ratios are1. The logarithm is summed through degree26 with an exact tail proof: for every `k>=27`, `k-6 v_3(k)>=9`, so the omitted terms lie in `p_L^9`, which is more than sufficient after multiplication bybeta. This is a finite rational calculation with a uniform error bound, not floating p-adic numerics.

Here is the reduction from those computations to the whole simple character. The six `u_r` generate `U_L^1/U_L^7`, because every quotient `U_L^r/U_L^(r+1)` is the additive groupF3 and `u_r` generates that quotient. The remaining subgroup `U_L^7` is contained in `U_a^6`. The elementg belongs to `U_a^4`, so it normalizes `U_a^6`; by(7) it therefore conjugates all generators of `H_F^1` into `H_F^1`. Conjugation therefore induces an injective self-map of the finite group `H_F^1/U_a^6`; it is surjective, so this inclusion is equality. Thusg normalizes the actualH1.

For `y=1+X` in `U_a^6`, the error term in(6) multiplied byX lies in `P^3`, whose matrix trace lies in `p_F`. Hence

\[
 \theta_F(g^{-1}yg)/\theta_F(y)
 =\psi_F((1/3)\operatorname{tr}X)
 =\omega_{\rm cub}(\det y).
\]

The last equality follows from `log det(1+X)=tr log(1+X)`; the higher terms become trivial under `psi_F((1/3) - )`. Indeed for each `k>=2`, the order level of `X^k/k` is at least `6k-6v_3(k)>=12`, its trace hasF-valuation at least4, and multiplication by1/3 leaves valuation at least2. On `U_L^1` the twist is trivial because determinants areL/F norms, and(7), together with the preceding deep calculation on `U_L^7`, gives equality there. Therefore this particular example has the exact simple-character intertwiner

\[
 \boxed{\theta_F^g=\theta_F\, (\omega_{\rm cub}\circ\det),
 \qquad\theta_F^g(h)=\theta_F(g^{-1}hg).}
 \tag{8}
\]

There were27 diagonal candidates in the specified form `1+diag(a,b,c)Pi^4`; exactly the three cyclic permutations `(0,1,2),(1,2,0),(2,0,1)` passed the deep stratum shift test. Formula(8) is an ordinary computer-assisted exact statement about this one actual simple character. Its finite calculation and reduction are explicitly recorded so they can be independently checked.
