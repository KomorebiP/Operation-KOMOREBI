## 1. Exact local fields and a regular character

\[

 \psi_F(t)=\psi_{\mathbb Q_3}(\operatorname{Tr}_{F/\mathbb Q_3}(t)/3),

## 5. A proved arithmetic occurrence lemma

Let `a_K` be the actual `O_M` chain order in `End_K(M)`, use `psi_K=psi_F o Tr_K/F`, and construct `theta_K` from the same beta and `chi_M` exactly as in(3). **Occurrence lemma.** With the actual data above,

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{H_F^1}(\theta_F,\pi_F^0)\ne0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \operatorname{Hom}_{H_K^1}(\theta_K,\pi_K^0)\ne0.
 \tag{4}
\]

**Proof.** A nonzero theta-isotypic vector in a smooth representation generates a finite-dimensional representation of the compact groupJ1. Semisimplicity and BK5.1.1 make theta-occurrence equivalent to eta-occurrence. This argument retains the central-character extensions of(14.9), rather than incorrectly citing14.21 as a bare finite-character statement. QED.

This is a genuine source-backed arithmetic transport lemma. It shows that the tame stage creates no new occurrence ambiguity once the actual theta and normalized candidate are fixed.
