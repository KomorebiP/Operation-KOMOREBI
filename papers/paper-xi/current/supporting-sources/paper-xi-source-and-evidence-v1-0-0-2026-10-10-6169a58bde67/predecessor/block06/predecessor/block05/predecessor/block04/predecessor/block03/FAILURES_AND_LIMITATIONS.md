# Block03 — failures and limitations

## Source recovery failure
The complete Block02 working source ZIP could not be recovered from mounted files or the Library search/listing. Its reader ZIP and surfaced ledger/review/audit/handoff artifacts were recovered and hash-checked. The new cumulative reader preserves its 33 PDF pages unchanged and supplies a separate editable continuation. It is not a rebuilt or complete editable v0.2.0 source release.

## Formal receipt gap
The v0.45 production source exists, but the recovered logs do not contain successful compilation of its terminal module. The background log ends at 3890/3891 after FiniteTameComparison. This is not a demonstrated compiler error. No Lean/Lake executable was used in Block03. No blanket formal certification is inferred from filenames, source presence or preservation hashes.

## Execution failures and repairs
A streaming-session tool call failed before writing the intended files. A later combined replay timed out at the outer orchestration level after eight named gates printed success. It did not produce a complete nine-gate receipt. The driver now checkpoints each completed gate, supports --only, and the nine gates were rerun in three completed groups.

## Publication failures and repairs
A raw PyMuPDF insertion dropped the named internal links while preserving page text and pixels. Named destinations are now resolved, transformed and rewritten. All final destinations were checked. Underfull diagnostics and sparse spill pages were repaired. Incorrect Imai author/title/journal metadata in the first draft was corrected against the primary source before release.

## Reproducibility limit
The independently rebuilt PDFs match in page text and 100-dpi rendered pixels but not in complete bytes. Binary reproducibility is not claimed. The exact internal source of every binary difference was not determined.

## Mathematical limits
Original theta0 occurrence is excluded in its stated C113 setting. Corrected theta-prime passes the cubic self-twist obstruction but its occurrence in the same AI candidate remains unproved. General source-to-arithmetic realization, compatible actions/transports and same-candidate H5 remain open. Native independent markings and the direct-constructor conductor inputs await their own audit. The finite model tests are not universal arithmetic proofs. The three original XI readers are unchanged baselines, not freshly revised editions.

## Packaging adapter correction
The first final-baseline comparison assumed a dictionary manifest, while Block01 stores a list of path/hash records. It failed before archive creation. The adapter now recognizes the preserved list schema and converts it without altering the original manifest.
