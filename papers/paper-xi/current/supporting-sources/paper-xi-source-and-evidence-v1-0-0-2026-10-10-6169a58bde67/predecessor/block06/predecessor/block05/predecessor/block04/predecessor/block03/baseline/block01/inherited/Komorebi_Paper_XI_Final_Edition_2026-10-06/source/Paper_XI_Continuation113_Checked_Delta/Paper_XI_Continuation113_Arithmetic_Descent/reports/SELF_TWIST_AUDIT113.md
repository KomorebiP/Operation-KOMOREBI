# Independent audit of the original theta's cubic self-twist obstruction

**Claim audited.** For the specific original simple character theta_0 defined below, no irreducible supercuspidal representation of GL3(F) containing theta_0 has the cubic self-twist associated with L/F. Consequently no such representation is AI_{L/F}(chi_0).

**Primary verdict: proved as written.** This is an ordinary source-backed, computer-assisted proof for this one explicitly specified character. The finite calculations were replayed and the decisive scalar was recomputed without the author's averaged uniformizer matrix. The passage from that scalar to all possible intertwiners and all central-type extensions is proved below. This is not a Lean certificate, a refutation of Paper XI Claim 16.2, or a conclusion about a subsequently corrected character.

The previously completed `INDEPENDENT_AUDIT113.md` is unchanged. Its abstract Lean results and this arithmetic exclusion have different scopes.

## 1. Fixed objects and conventions

Let

\[
F=\mathbb Q_3(\zeta_3),\qquad L=\mathbb Q_3(\zeta_9),\qquad
\varpi=\zeta_9-1,\qquad \varpi_F=\zeta_3-1,\qquad \beta=\varpi^{-10}.
\]

The hereditary order a stabilizes the O_L-chain on the F-space L, P is its radical, and U_a^r=1+P^r. The additive character is

\[
\psi_F(x)=\psi_{\mathbb Q_3}(\operatorname{Tr}_{F/\mathbb Q_3}(x)/3),
\qquad \psi_L=\psi_F\circ\operatorname{Tr}_{L/F}.
\]

The original character, with no correction term, is

\[
\chi_0(\varpi^a\epsilon u)=\psi_L(\beta\log u)
\quad(a\in\mathbb Z,\ \epsilon\in\{1,-1\},\ u\in U_L^1).
\]

Set

\[
H^1=U_L^1U_a^6,\qquad J^1=U_L^1U_a^5,\qquad N=L^\times J^1,
\]

and prescribe

\[
\theta_0(uy)=\chi_0(u)\psi_F(\operatorname{tr}(\beta(y-1))),
\quad u\in U_L^1,\ y\in U_a^6.
\]

These are the data in section 3 of the original arithmetic report, preserved in `evidence/arithmetic/ARITHMETIC113_before_central_scalar.md`. The simple stratum is minimal and full degree because v_L(beta)=-10 is coprime to [L:F]=3. The two formulas agree on U_L^6, so theta_0 is the specified actual simple character. Its unique Heisenberg representation eta has degree three.

Write omega for the nontrivial cubic character of F^x that is trivial on N_{L/F}(L^x), with omega(varpi_F)=omega(-1)=1 and

\[
\omega(u)=\psi_F\bigl((1/3)\log u\bigr),\qquad u\in U_F^1.
\]

Its concrete norm formula and triviality on the full norm subgroup are checked by the raw self-twist script and the unit-generation reduction in the original report. It is not merely an unspecified cubic character. Put Omega=omega composed with determinant on GL3(F).

The conjugation convention in this audit is

\[
\rho^g(x)=\rho(g^{-1}xg).
\]

BK's definition immediately before Theorem 3.3.2 uses the opposite convention. The self-intertwining set is inversion-stable; the all-intertwiner argument below compares two types with the same character and thus avoids guessing a left-versus-right cross-intertwining coset.

## 2. Actual normalization of the three groups

In the basis 1,varpi,varpi^2, let Pi be multiplication by varpi and put

\[
\Pi=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&\varpi_F\\1&0&-3\\0&1&-3\end{pmatrix},
\qquad g=1+\operatorname{diag}(0,1,2)\Pi^4.
\]

The matrix g lies in U_a^4. The raw exact calculation establishes

\[
v_a(g\Pi^{-10}g^{-1}-\Pi^{-10}-(1/3)I)=-3
\]

and verifies theta_0(g^{-1}u_rg)=theta_0(u_r) for the six units u_r=1+varpi^r, 1<=r<=6. Each conjugate is explicitly decomposed into an L-unit times an element of U_a^6.

The reduction to the complete group is valid. The six units generate U_L^1/U_L^7, and U_L^7 lies in U_a^6. Conjugation by g preserves U_a^6. The tested conjugates therefore give g^{-1}H^1g contained in H^1. On the finite quotient H^1/U_a^6 the induced map is injective, hence surjective. Thus g normalizes H^1.

On a deep element 1+X, X in P^6, the displayed stratum error contributes trivially to psi_F: its product with X lies in P^3 and has trace in p_F. The remaining factor is psi_F((1/3)tr X)=Omega(1+X). Higher logarithm terms contribute trivially. On U_L^1 the determinant is an L/F norm, so Omega is trivial. This proves on the entire actual group

\[
\theta_0^g=\theta_0\,\Omega|_{H^1}.
\tag{1}
\]

For J^1, the standard order filtration gives [U_a^4,U_a^1] contained in U_a^5. Hence g and g^{-1} send U_L^1 into U_L^1U_a^5, and both preserve U_a^5. Therefore g normalizes J^1.

Finally define

\[
h=g^{-1}\Pi g\Pi^{-1}.
\]

The independent calculation places h in H^1. Since N is generated by Pi, J^1 and the central element -1, and g^{-1}Pi g=h Pi, it follows that

\[
g^{-1}Ng=\langle h\Pi,J^1,-1\rangle=N.
\tag{2}
\]

This proves normalization of N on actual generators. It does not infer it merely from a quotient action.

## 3. All eta intertwiners are scalar

Let

\[
u_0=1+\operatorname{diag}(1,0,0)\Pi^5,
\qquad u_1=1+\operatorname{diag}(0,1,0)\Pi^5.
\]

Their images form a basis of J^1/H^1. One way to verify independence is the computed nontrivial commutator theta_0([u_0,u_1])=zeta_3^2, combined with [J^1:H^1]=9. The quotient is the two-dimensional F3 Heisenberg quotient.

The independent check computes directly

\[
k_i=g^{-1}u_i g u_i^{-1}\in U_a^9\subset H^1,
\qquad \theta_0(k_i)=1,
\qquad \Omega(u_i)=1,
\quad i=0,1.
\tag{3}
\]

The values theta_0(k_i) use only the deep matrix-trace formula. They do not depend on choosing a Weyl matrix or an averaged uniformizer implementer. The logarithm linearization for Omega(u_i) is valid at level 5: for k>=2, the order level of X^k/k is at least 9, so multiplication of its trace by 1/3 is trivial under psi_F.

By (1), eta^g and eta times Omega agree on H^1. By (3), they agree on u_0 and u_1 as literal operators, since eta(k_i)=I. These elements generate J^1 with H^1. Consequently

\[
\eta^g=\eta\otimes\Omega|_{J^1}
\tag{4}
\]

on the same carrier. Every intertwiner between these two representations is therefore scalar, by irreducibility and Schur's lemma. This covers all linear intertwiners, not only the nine Weyl operators searched by the original script.

## 4. The uniformizer obstruction is independent of every extension choice

Let Lambda be any central type of N extending eta. No choice of central character or value at Pi is fixed for this argument. The independently computed decomposition is

\[
h=(1+2\varpi^4)y,\qquad y\in U_a^8,
\]

with character contributions

\[
\chi_0(1+2\varpi^4)=1,
\qquad \psi_F(\operatorname{tr}(\beta(y-1)))=\zeta_3.
\]

Thus

\[
\theta_0(h)=\zeta_3\ne1.
\tag{5}
\]

Every such Lambda satisfies

\[
\Lambda^g(\Pi)=\Lambda(h\Pi)=\zeta_3\Lambda(\Pi),
\qquad
(\Lambda\otimes\Omega)(\Pi)=\Lambda(\Pi),
\tag{6}
\]

because det(Pi)=varpi_F and omega(varpi_F)=1. Any N-intertwiner between these representations restricts on J^1 to an intertwiner in (4), hence is scalar. A nonzero scalar intertwiner cannot satisfy (6), since Lambda(Pi) is invertible. Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Hom}_N(\Lambda^g,\Lambda\otimes\Omega)=0
\quad\text{for every central-type extension Lambda of eta.}
\tag{7}
\]

Rescaling the candidate intertwiner does not change the discrepancy. Replacing Lambda(Pi) by any scalar multiple also leaves the factor zeta_3 unchanged. More generally, changing Lambda by a character of N/J^1 cannot help: g fixes that quotient because it fixes Pi modulo J^1 and fixes -1. Thus the argument includes all unramified and tame extension choices. It is not the failure of one arbitrarily selected implementer.

## 5. Eliminating every full-group intertwiner

BK Theorem 3.3.2, and explicitly the proof of Proposition 5.1.1, give

\[
I_G(\theta_0)=I_G(\eta)=J^1 L^\times J^1=N,
\tag{8}
\]

since the centralizer is the field L and L^x normalizes J^1. By (2), conjugation does not change this subgroup, so I_G(theta_0^g)=N.

The two central types Lambda^g and Lambda times Omega both restrict on H^1 to multiples of the same character theta_0^g. Any full-group intertwiner between them must therefore lie in I_G(theta_0^g)=N. Conjugation by an element of N is inner on an N-representation, so such an intertwiner would yield an N-equivalence between the two types. Equation (7) excludes it.

Equivalently, BH1999 section 3.1 Lemma 4 applies to their common simple character and the maximal centralizer order, and says that global intertwining would force equivalence. This explicitly disposes of all G-intertwiners. The finite search among 27 diagonal candidates is not being claimed to exhaust G.

BH1999 section 5.4 identifies all irreducible representations containing this full-degree totally wild simple character, with any fixed compatible central character, with its central types. They are compactly induced from N. This also follows from the maximal simple-type results BK6.2.2 after choosing the type. Thus every supercuspidal pi containing theta_0 is c-Ind_N^G Lambda for such a Lambda.

Since g normalizes N, c-Ind_N^G Lambda^g is isomorphic to c-Ind_N^G Lambda. Twisting commutes with compact induction. If pi times Omega were isomorphic to pi, the central types Lambda times Omega and Lambda^g would intertwine in G, contrary to the preceding paragraph. Hence

\[
\boxed{\pi\otimes\Omega\not\simeq\pi
\quad\text{for every supercuspidal pi containing theta_0}.}
\tag{9}
\]

The argument ranges over all compatible central characters. Twisting by Omega preserves the central character because Omega(zI)=omega(z^3)=1, so the fixed-central-character classification introduces no missing case.

## 6. Consequence for the independently constructed candidate

The original character chi_0 is regular for the cyclic extension L/F. The characteristic-zero cyclic monomial construction in BH1999 section 2.3 therefore supplies the supercuspidal

\[
\pi^0=\operatorname{AI}_{L/F}(\chi_0).
\]

The same section states compatibility of that construction with twisting by quasicharacters. Since omega is trivial on the norm subgroup,

\[
\chi_0(\omega\circ N_{L/F})=\chi_0,
\qquad \pi^0\otimes\Omega\simeq\pi^0.
\]

Combining this with (9) gives the claimed exclusion:

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Hom}_{H^1}(\theta_0,
\operatorname{AI}_{L/F}(\chi_0))=0.}
\tag{10}
\]

This changes the initial occurrence test from unresolved to false for theta_0. The obstruction is at the full central type, despite the successful simple-character self-twist (1). It does not invalidate the arithmetic induction construction or the abstract finite algebra from the other audit.
