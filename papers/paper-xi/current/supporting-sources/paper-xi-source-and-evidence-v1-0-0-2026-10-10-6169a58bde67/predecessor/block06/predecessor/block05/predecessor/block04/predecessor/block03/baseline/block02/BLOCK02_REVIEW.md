Section 8 distinguishes native trace one, the zero trace of an amplified earlier parameter, and the trace of the actual correction. It reconstructs character invisibility at positive valuation depth and the exact floor in the critical correction estimate.

The original quotient-dimension failures remain unchanged. Compact recovery, noncompact quotient recovery and the central-normalized finite projector are still separate statements.
