# An explicit dyadic arithmetic parameter constructor

Date: 2026-10-05. Ordinary proof. No character, quotient-dimension, source
attachment, pair-factor, or universal Paper XI closure is asserted here.

## 1. Statement

Let F=F_2((t)), let 0<h_1<...<h_d be odd positive integers, and set

    q_i=3h_i,  N=2^d.

The explicit recursion below constructs B_d in M_N(F) such that

    F[B_d] is a totally ramified maximal field normalizing
       the complete period-N lattice chain,
    v_A(B_d)=-2^(d-1)q_d,
    k_0(B_d,A)=-q_1,
    tr_F(B_d)=1.

There are explicit native constructors of ranks 2^j for the largest j
exponents. Their identity-amplifications give the actual defining
sequence with field degrees 2,4,...,2^d and critical levels

    n_j=2^(d-j)q_(d-j+1),  1<=j<=d.

Every parameter in this ambient defining sequence has the same leading
level n_1. The quantities n_2,...,n_d are its successive critical
approximation levels, not new leading levels of the ambient matrices.

Every native constructor has trace 1. Every correction from an
identity-2 amplification of the preceding native constructor to the
next native constructor also has trace 1. In full ambient rank 2^d,
the earlier amplified approximations instead have trace 0, because
their multiplicities are even. This distinction is essential.

## 2. The minimal degree-two building block

Let L be a complete discretely valued field of characteristic 2 with
residue field F_2, and choose a uniformizer t_L. For odd q>0
put a=(q+1)/2 and define

    E=L(alpha),  alpha^2-alpha=t_L^(-q),
    z=t_L^a alpha.

The Artin--Schreier polynomial is irreducible: an element x^2-x of
negative L-valuation has even negative valuation. Its derivative is 1,
and the valuation equation forces ramification index 2. Thus E/L is
separable and totally ramified, v_E(alpha)=-q, and alpha is minimal.

The element z has E-valuation 2a-q=1 and satisfies

    f(z)=z^2+t_L^a z+t_L=0.

Thus 1,z is a uniformizer power basis, and

    alpha=z/t_L^a,  Tr_(E/L)(alpha)=1.

## 3. An explicit normalized corestriction and lift

On E use the coefficient basis 1,z. Its trace-dual elements are

    z_0^*=(z+t_L^a)/t_L^a,
    z_1^*=1/t_L^a.

Let pr(T_ij)=z^i z_j^* be the trace corestriction. Put

    kappa=t_L^a/z=alpha^(-1),
    s=kappa pr,
    u_0=s(P0)=t_L/z^2.

Here P0 is constant-coefficient projection, applied separately to each
coordinate when acting on E^m. The identity for u_0 follows from f(z)=0.
It is a unit: v_E(t_L)=v_E(z^2)=2. More explicitly,

    s(T_i0)=z^i(1+t_L^a/z),
    s(T_i1)=z^(i-1).

Since v_E(t_L^a/z)=2a-1=q>0, these preserve the expected leading grades.
On End_L(E^m), take the complete interleaved chain in the basis
w_(mi+b)=z^i v_b. Its centralizer is M_m(E) with its complete E-chain.
The formulas give s(P^r) contained in Q^r for all integers r.

For C in M_m(E), define the explicit L-linear operator

    Lift_E(C)=u_0^(-1) C P0=(z^2/t_L) C P0.

B-bimodularity gives s(Lift_E(C))=C. The lift preserves exact valuation:
u_0^(-1) is an E-unit, and expansion in 1,z gives coefficient valuations
distinct modulo 2. Each coefficient entry has the same grade in the
interleaved L-chain and the E-chain. In particular s(P^r)=Q^r.

## 4. Exact trace transport

For every C in M_m(E), ordinary matrix traces satisfy

    tr_L(Lift_E(C))
       =Tr_(E/L)(z_0^* u_0^(-1) tr_E(C))
       =Tr_(E/L)(kappa^(-1) tr_E(C))
       =Tr_(E/L)(alpha tr_E(C)).                 (T2)

The first equality follows by taking the L-trace of each rank-one
operator yP0, which is the constant coefficient of y. This coefficient
is Tr_(E/L)(z_0^* y).

Thus the trace of a lift is 1 whenever the inner ordinary E-matrix trace
is 1. It is not asserted that arbitrary trace values are unchanged.

## 5. Recursive actual matrices and fields

Define B(L,t_L;h_1,...,h_d) as follows. For d=1 use the multiplication
matrix of alpha from Section2 with q=q_1. For d>=2 apply Section2 with
q=q_d, set m=2^(d-1), recursively construct

    C=B(E,z;h_1,...,h_(d-1)),

and put

    B(L,t_L;h_1,...,h_d)=alpha I_m+(z^2/t_L) C P0.       (D2)

This is a finite algebraic recipe. It uses specified separable quadratic
extensions and no unknown support, genericity choice, or correspondence
inverse.

The degree and simplicity proof is by induction. The base alpha is
minimal and generates E. At a later step let gamma=alpha I_m and
b=Lift_E(C). The outer minimal stratum has

    n=2^(d-1)q_d,  k_0(gamma,A)=-n.

The inner induction hypothesis gives an actual maximal field E[C]
normalizing the complete E-chain with

    R=2^(d-2)q_(d-1),  v(C)=-R,  k_0(C)=-q_1.

Because h_d>h_(d-1), we have n>2R. Therefore

    -2R > -n-q_1 = k_0(gamma,A)+k_0(C).

The derived stratum with parameter s(b)=C is pure, and its field is
maximal. Bushnell--Kutzko Remark2.2.7 consequently gives the field
generated by the actual gamma+b, not merely an equivalent replacement.
It has ramification and degree 2^d, residue degree one, normalizes the
complete chain, and has k_0=max(-n,-q_1)=-q_1. Since v(b)>v(gamma), its
level is n and [A,n,0,gamma+b] is simple.

The derived model E[C] is not identified with L[gamma+b]. In particular
no literal embedding of E into the latter field is claimed.

## 6. Trace 1 at every native stage

The base matrix has trace Tr_(E/L)(alpha)=1. Inductively tr_E(C)=1, so
(T2) gives tr_L(Lift_E(C))=1. For d>=2 the L-trace of alpha I_m is

    m Tr_(E/L)(alpha)=0,

because m is even. Hence tr_L(B)=1 at every native rank.

For any j>=1, let A_j denote the native rank-2^j constructor using a
fixed list of the largest j exponents. The next native rank-2^(j+1)
constructor A_(j+1) uses the same largest exponents and adds the next
smaller one. With their compatible interleaved bases, put

    Delta_(j+1)=A_(j+1)-A_j tensor I_2.

Then

    tr(Delta_(j+1))=1-2*1=1                    in characteristic 2.

This native trace identity is stronger and more relevant than a claim
obtained merely by amplifying to a rank divisible by two.

## 7. Full arithmetic defining sequence

For the final rank 2^d define

    B^[j]=B(F,t;h_(d-j+1),...,h_d) tensor I_(2^(d-j)).

All truncations use the same top q_d to select the first extension and
uniformizer, the same next q_(d-1) to select the next extension, and so
on. The coefficient projection and lift commute with amplification.
Thus these are literal truncations of (D2).

Their parameter fields have degree 2^j, and their critical levels in
the ambient complete chain are n_j=2^(d-j)q_(d-j+1). Valuation-preserving
lifts give

    v_A(B^[j+1]-B^[j])=-n_(j+1).

Later differences have strictly greater valuation, since
n_j>2n_(j+1). This proves that the displayed parameters form an actual
defining sequence of simple approximations.

Their ambient traces are

    tr_F(B^[j])=0 for j<d,  tr_F(B^[d])=1.

Consequently ambient corrections before the last step have trace 0,
and the last correction has trace 1. Every one of these trace values
is integral. On a scalar principal unit u, the additive correction
character has value psi_F((u-1)tr(delta)); this is 1 for an additive
character trivial on the maximal ideal. No assertion about full simple
character extension follows here without its separate intersection and
transfer argument.

## 8. Concrete rank-eight instance and limits

For h=(1,3,5), the construction gives actual approximation-field degrees
2,4,8 and critical levels 60,18,3. The supplementary exact program
constructs its Laurent-polynomial matrices, verifies the normalized
lift identities, and checks the native/ambient trace distinctions and
matrix grades. It does not prove field existence or simplicity by
bounded computation: those are consequences of Section5.

The odd-prime Heisenberg quotient and conductor counting from Block166
cannot simply be copied: q_1 is odd here. This note makes no dyadic
quotient, full-character, stabilizer, phase, source-attachment, or H5
claim. It also changes lower parameter conjugacy classes, as in the
corresponding odd-prime Artin--Schreier construction.
