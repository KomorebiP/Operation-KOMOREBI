## Mathematical scope

The article develops exact nonlinear packet coordinates, packet jets, a packet Faà di Bruno formula, convergent rooted tree expansions, graded nonlinear equations, refinement defects, canonical cokernel and mapping cone obstructions, relative obstruction under coarsening, nonlinear correction, Lyapunov Schmidt reduction, complete filtered lifting, spectral sequence refinement, nonlinear elimination, finite holonomy, and canonical smooth packet transport.

The paper does not claim that every obstruction vanishes or that every nonlinear problem has a finite obstruction space. Each obstruction object is defined relative to declared correction data.

## Verification boundary

The manuscript is internally complete and reproducible. The verification scripts support the displayed formulas and finite constructions. They do not replace independent proof review, literature comparison, or an editorial novelty assessment.
