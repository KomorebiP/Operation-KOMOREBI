## Proof chains reviewed in detail

### Exact nonlinear coordinates

Analysis and synthesis are inverse finite coordinate maps. Composition, dynamics, and changes of packet coordinates follow by conjugation.

### Packet jets

Every derivative is reconstructed by inserting complete packet identities into all input slots and the output slot. The Taylor formula uses the Banach space integral remainder. The packet Faà di Bruno formula follows from the ordinary higher chain rule and finite insertion of intermediate packet identities.

### Rooted trees

Picard iterates equal finite height tree sums. A scalar recursion majorizes the sum of tree norms. The contraction hypothesis gives both uniqueness and absolute convergence.

### Obstruction objects

The cokernel criterion is the universal quotient for a declared correction map. Naturality follows from the induced quotient map. The relative exact sequence is the snake lemma written out directly. The mapping cone result has the stated cohomological degree convention.

### Nonlinear correction

The right inverse theorem is a contraction on the closed selected complement. Lyapunov Schmidt reduction uses the implicit function theorem locally. The second order obstruction formula correctly removes terms containing the first derivative of the correction map.

### Filtered lifting

The revised hypotheses make depth classes and associated graded corrections well defined. One step lifting is an exact cokernel criterion. Compatible branches converge by completeness, and continuity places the limiting defect in every filtration submodule. Separatedness then forces the defect to vanish.

### Filtered complexes

The spectral sequence statement is used only in its standard finite filtered form. The manuscript no longer equates arbitrary first page cohomology with obstruction classes without a declared correction complex.

### Nonlinear elimination

The first and second Schur formulas follow by differentiating the implicit hidden equation. All derivatives are evaluated at the fixed base point.

### Holonomy

Spanning tree normalization is performed after a common model fiber or vertex frames have been fixed. Fundamental cycles provide the remaining edge data. Gauge classes are identified with representation classes of the graph fundamental group.

### Smooth packet transport

The generator is skew adjoint and its commutator with every packet projector equals the projector derivative. Exact transport follows by differentiating the conjugated projector. The direct rotation is the polar factor of the packet overlap operator. A uniform second order local estimate and a telescoping product bound prove convergence.
