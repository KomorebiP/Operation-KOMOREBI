2.3 From a transfer power to a finite walk sum
The transfer operator is 𝑇= ∑𝑒∈𝐸𝐸𝑒. Four related objects are kept separate:
1. wordExpansion recursively left-multiplies by 𝑇.
2. tupleWordOp converts a tuple Fin n →E to an execution list.
3. explicitWordExpansion sums every length-𝑛tuple.
4. walkExpansion keeps precisely the composable tuples.
At length zero there is one empty tuple, not zero tuples. Its contribution is the identity. This is
why the explicit sum agrees with 𝑇0.
To pass from length 𝑛to length 𝑛+ 1, the proof separates a tuple into its first 𝑛entries and its
final entry. Fin.snocEquiv supplies the finite indexing equivalence. Appending that final edge
left-multiplies the already executed prefix.

theorem explicitWordExpansion_succ
[Fintype E] [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V] [Semiring R]
(n : ℕ) :
explicitWordExpansion G S (n + 1) =
transferOp G S * explicitWordExpansion G S n := by
unfold explicitWordExpansion
rw [←(Fin.snocEquiv (fun _ : Fin (n + 1) ↦E)).sum_comp]
rw [Fintype.sum_prod_type]
change (∑final : E, ∑prior : Fin n →E,
tupleWordOp G S (n + 1) (Fin.snoc prior final)) = _
simp only [tupleWordOp_snoc]
unfold transferOp
rw [Finset.sum_mul]
simp_rw [Finset.mul_sum]
This is a finite distributivity argument. It uses neither an infinite geometric series nor a conver-
gence limit. The proof then identifies the recursive expansion with the explicit tuple sum by in-
duction on 𝑛.

theorem explicitWordExpansion_eq_walkExpansion
[Fintype E] [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V] [Semiring R]
(n : ℕ) :
explicitWordExpansion G S n = walkExpansion G S n := by
classical
unfold explicitWordExpansion walkExpansion
apply Finset.sum_congr rfl
intro word _
by_cases hwalk : Composable G (List.ofFn word)
· simp [hwalk]
· have hzero : tupleWordOp G S n word = 0 :=
wordOp_eq_zero_of_not_composable G S (List.ofFn word) hwalk
simp [hwalk, hzero]
When a tuple is composable, the filtered term is unchanged. When it is not, its unfiltered operator
was already zero. Combining this equality with the recursive power identity gives transferOp_p
ow_eq_walkExpansion.
