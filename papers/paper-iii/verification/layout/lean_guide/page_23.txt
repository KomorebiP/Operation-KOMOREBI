5.4 Extracting a packet-supported witness
Idempotence is needed for the stronger witness statement:
Source: KomorebiPaperIII/ReversibleConnections.lean.
theorem exists_packetWitness_of_block_ne_zero
(A : M →ₗ[R] M) (mu nu : I) (hne : P.block A mu nu ≠0) :
∃x, P.projector nu x = x ∧P.projector mu (A x) ≠0 := by
classical
have hexists : ∃y, P.block A mu nu y ≠0 := by
by_contra h
apply hne
ext y
by_contra hy
exact h ⟨y, hy⟩
rcases hexists with ⟨y, hy⟩
refine ⟨P.projector nu y, ?_, ?_⟩
· have hid := congrArg (fun L : M →ₗ[R] M ↦L y) (P.idempotent nu)
simpa using hid
· simpa [block] using hy

5.5 When a fixed packet pattern survives a gauge
Total obstruction always transforms by conjugation at the base vertex. For a fixed packet family to
transform block by block, the gauge must commute with every projector.
