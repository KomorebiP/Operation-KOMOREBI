theorem flat_iff_triangleHolonomy_eq_refl :
C.Flat ↔∀a b c,
C.triangleHolonomy a b c = LinearEquiv.refl R (F a) := by
constructor
· intro hflat a b c
rw [←C.cocycleDefect_eq_zero_iff_triangleHolonomy_eq_refl a b c,
C.cocycleDefect_eq_zero_iff_cocycleAt]
exact hflat a b c
· intro hhol a b c
rw [←C.cocycleDefect_eq_zero_iff_cocycleAt,
C.cocycleDefect_eq_zero_iff_triangleHolonomy_eq_refl]
exact hhol a b c
Here Flat means the pair-cocycle law at every triple of vertices. It is a global algebraic condition
on all pair transports. It is not a differential curvature condition obtained from a limiting process
or a local chart cover.
