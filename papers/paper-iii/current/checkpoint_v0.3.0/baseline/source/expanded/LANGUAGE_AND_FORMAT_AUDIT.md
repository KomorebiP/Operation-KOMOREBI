## Prose revision

The article avoids project status language, release terminology, generic promotional language, repeated summary boxes, and claims that computation replaces proof. Explanatory paragraphs are placed near the results they clarify. The paper distinguishes coordinates, defects, obstruction classes, elimination, and holonomy rather than treating them as one interchangeable idea.
