1 Reading the formal paper
Paper III turns three kinds of data into precise finite statements: directed packet operators, coor-
dinate and connection transport, and local obstruction certificates. Its Lean component is small
enough to read as a connected development. Seven mathematical modules contain the human-
authored constructions and proofs. An aggregate import exposes them together. A separate audit
module checks the compiled namespace.
This guide follows the definitions before using their theorem names. A declaration name is a
precise destination, but its type determines what has actually been proved. The authoritative map
has 41 rows and 143 distinct targets. Of these, 88 are theorem declarations and 55 are constructions.
A construction can be a structure, inductive type, definition, or abbreviation. The count does not
mean 143 independent mathematical theorems.
1.1 Three levels of evidence
The preserved report, dated 3 September 2026, records a successful 3,076-job aggregate build, the
theorem-map parity gate, and a namespace audit of 468 declarations including 229 entries of kind
theoremInfo. Paper III also preserves the kernel-audit transcript. That transcript contains one
axiom report for each of the 143 mapped targets and the final namespace PASS line.
For this edition, a Python reader reconciled all 41 map rows with their lexical source locations, all
143 explicit #print axioms directives, and all 143 transcript records. It checked the 16 preserved
formal-component files against the original checksum manifest. The theorem-map and transcript
hashes also match the values printed in the inherited verification report. These are fresh static
checks of the preserved evidence. They do not replace a new Lean execution, and no fresh compiler
run is claimed by this guide.
The expanded traditional companion includes additional ordinary mathematical proofs. The finite
v1.0.1 formal contract stays fixed. A final-paper section may combine a formally covered finite
result with a stronger ordinary theorem. The presence of a related Lean name does not make
every statement in that section part of the contract.
