LEAN GUIDE
For each source vertex, the proof splits on membership in the support. Inside the support, the
relevant transfer entry vanishes by unreachability. Outside it, the seed coordinate vanishes. Every
term of the matrix-vector sum is therefore zero.
The direction cannot be reversed without extra assumptions. A graph may contain a path whose
coefficient is zero. Distinct paths may cancel over a ring. Thus a zero orbit coordinate does not by
itself prove that the target is unreachable. The Lean theorem deliberately asks for unreachability
as a hypothesis and returns zero as a conclusion.
