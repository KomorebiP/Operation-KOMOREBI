\head{From the finite models to the broader paper}
The traditional paper continues with rooted tree expansions, nonlinear
refinement defects, obstruction objects, filtered lifting, elimination, and
smooth projector transport. Read the finite examples as concrete preparation.
The broader ordinary proofs keep their own hypotheses and are distinct from
the finite Lean contract.

\head{Read products in traversal order}
A path $e_1,e_2,\ldots,e_n$ acts as $L_{e_n}\cdots L_{e_2}L_{e_1}$:
the first traversed edge is the rightmost operator. Row indices are targets
and column indices are sources throughout the guide.
