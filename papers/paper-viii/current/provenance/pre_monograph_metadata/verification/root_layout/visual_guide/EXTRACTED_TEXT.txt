Three models to carry through the paper
The first is a six-dimensional operator A = D + uvT . Every entry of A is nonzero, yet three 2 × 2 diagonal
blocks and two thin vectors suffice to apply it exactly. Pages V – 3, V – 9, V – 10, and V – 27 reuse this
operator or its factors.
The second starts with redundant factors L and R. Actual range coordinates reduce a 4 × 3 product
to a 2 × 3 core. Pages V – 11–V – 12 connect rank, singular tails, and the geometric reason a small-core
optimum is also an ambient optimum.
The third is a seven-node candidate tree with exactly five prunings. Its budget optimum is not selected by
any Lagrangian penalty. Pages V – 21–V – 23 let readers inspect every feasible choice and every changed
subproblem.
How to use the guide
A source line beneath each title identifies the relevant final traditional result; the accompanying source
crosswalk records its label and page. The preserved examples use rational, Gaussian-rational, symbolic, and
finite-enumeration arithmetic. Fresh portable checks use exact rational and Gaussian-rational arithmetic;
the original SymPy suite remains inherited because SymPy is unavailable. Smooth drawing coordinates
are illustrations of stated formulas, not additional proof evidence.
Read this as: An exact representation can remove work before a device is chosen. It does not by
itself supply a fast kernel, a good approximation, or a fair timing comparison.

Conventions used in the examples
Matrices act on column vectors. A block Aβα goes from source α to target β. Products act from right to
left. Real examples use transpose; complex examples use adjoint unless a vectorization identity explicitly
requires transpose. Column-major vectorization stacks the columns of a matrix.
An arithmetic count follows the paper’s modeled multiply/add convention. It is not a count of executed
machine instructions. The tiny scheduling and amortization examples use declared synthetic units, not
measured GPU times. The original benchmark corpus remains historical data in the preserved source tree.
Evidence levels
The original theorem ledger records its inherited finite formal scope. The new exact checks validate
these particular examples. A plot samples a formula. A historical timing describes its recorded case and
machine. None of these evidence types can silently substitute for another.
Read this as: Before interpreting a number, identify its object, norm, output contract, and timing
scope. Most apparent contradictions disappear once those four fields are explicit.

What was avoided?
The global part does not require 36 independent coefficients. It depends on the input through one scalar,

What the closure permits
The directed transitive closure includes 0 →3, because it records existence of a route rather than the

Why the general proof is broader
This example happens to have a terminating Neumann polynomial. The general finite-dimensional theorem
does not require nilpotence or a small norm. For a fixed source coordinate, its reachable-coordinate
subspace is invariant under A. If A is invertible, the restricted map is injective. Equal finite domain and
codomain dimensions make it surjective, so the inverse preserves the same subspace.
The finite-dimensional and invertibility hypotheses matter. In an infinite-dimensional space, an injective
restriction can fail to be onto. A singular matrix does not have an inverse whose support can be tested at
all.
Read this as: The inverse can never transmit outside the reachable subspace under the theorem’s
hypotheses. Inside that subspace, coefficient cancellation can still remove a structurally permitted
interaction.

Why the complexity is pseudo polynomial
For a declared integer budget B, a table has B + 1 possible exact-weight states. A direct merge checks at
most (B + 1)2 state pairs. At B = 255, this means 256 states and 65,536 candidate pairs at a merge, even
though the budget fits in eight binary digits.
With a bounded number of children, the paper’s direct tree implementation therefore has quadratic budget
dependence at each node. Polynomial dependence on the numerical value of B does not mean polynomial
dependence on the binary input length. These are counts of declared states and pairs, not measured
runtimes or claims that every state is reachable.
Read this as: The dynamic program is exact for its integer data. Conservative conversion and the
numerical size of the budget are separate obligations that determine its accuracy and practical scale.
