Active mathematical clarifications and repairs:
  - Local packet maps use pi: X -> X_alpha and inclusion iota: X_beta -> X.
    Ambient projectors remain P_alpha = iota_alpha pi_alpha.
  - Physical divisions and reduction ratios have positive denominators. Work
    reduction ratios require positive right-hand-side count; zero-size count
    formulas remain valid without forming a ratio. Equal packet widths are
    positive, divide n exactly, and the band width satisfies w < p.
  - Fourier and Toeplitz order is positive. Tensor work counts describe reused
    intermediate slices and explicitly handle the empty tensor product.
  - Matrix powers enumerate directed walks, with repeated vertices permitted.
    Inverse and hidden Schur support use reflexive reachability. Empty task
    graphs have depth zero. Scheduling assumes unit tasks and unlimited
    concurrency. Support is an inclusion because paths may cancel.
  - Condition-number statements use a nonempty coordinate space, actual
    positive definite diagonal data and the actual inverse square root.
    Classical conjugate-gradient convergence is an exact-arithmetic input.
  - Continuous precision requires a finite nonempty edge set, positive costs,
    sensitivities and budget. Clipped allocation also has positive caps and a
    feasible budget. At maximal budget the allocation is unique, while the
    positive multiplier can be nonunique.
  - The raw generic input-backward rounding sentence is replaced by the
    componentwise forward bound |C_hat-AB| <= gamma_p |A||B| for ordinary real
    uniform-precision dot-product multiplication, p*u < 1 and no overflow or
    underflow. Other arithmetic/kernel models need separate constants.
  - Mixed-chain bounds use uniform operator certificates for fixed linear
    approximations. A bound at one input vector does not bound operator norm;
    a rounded action on varying inputs is not automatically one fixed map.
  - Small-core truncation uses min(k,d) directions and zero-padded tails.
    Compressing arbitrary ambient competitors supplies the global optimality
    bridge. Supplied ordered thin-SVD equations do not assume optimality.
  - Compiler stability controls solved branch scores with strict half-gaps;
    no robustness at ties is claimed. Local updates use valid unchanged sibling
    caches. Budget tabulation is a unit-cost pseudo-polynomial model in numeric
    B, excluding exact-arithmetic bit complexity and device instructions.
