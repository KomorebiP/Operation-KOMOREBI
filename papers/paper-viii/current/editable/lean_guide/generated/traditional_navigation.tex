\subsection{The open research boundary}
The traditional boundary discussion is on printed page 42 (PDF page 48). It retains the three broader targets: discovery of profitable structure for arbitrary operators, controlled compilation for overlapping decompositions and nonlocal costs, and a communication theorem for a specified packet memory architecture. Their finite special cases do not settle those broader targets.
