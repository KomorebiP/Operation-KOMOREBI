# Paper VIII visual monograph changes

The cover, reading page, fonts, headers, and worked-page typography now match the monograph series. All 32 worked pages and 30 vector plate identities are retained. Scientific model, generator, and checking programs remain byte-identical to the retrieved baseline.

The 32 source lines now cite actual printed pages from the current traditional PDF. The crosswalk separately records physical PDF pages, and pins the source, map, and both PDFs. Per-page navigation labels use independent phantom anchors. The integrated atlas omits external PDF locators and explains where the standalone reading crosswalk is supplied.

The first worked page now records the actual fresh SymPy replay instead of saying the package is unavailable. Exact portable and symbolic evidence remains separate from saved formal records and historical hardware measurements.

Two extension plate title coordinates were raised to prevent title/axis-label crowding: `complex_core.tex`, (0,3) to (0,3.4), and `quantization.tex`, (0,2.7) to (0,3.2). Their mathematical labels and models are unchanged. Original versions are archived.

The maintained XeLaTeX builder typesets an isolated full-source copy and retains bundled fonts. It does not regenerate mathematical source, vector plates, or computation results. An independent rebuild reproduced the final PDF byte for byte. All final pages were rendered into a directory identified by the final PDF hash and visually inspected.
